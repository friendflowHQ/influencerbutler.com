How Influencer Butler Works

The Chrome extension: scores, data sources and estimates. A quick reference to keep next to you while you explore.

Chrome extensionReference guide
At a glance

It adds a money layer to the page you are already on

On Amazon and Walmart search results and product pages, the Butler shows what a product could earn you and whether it is worth making content for, so you can decide in seconds instead of clicking into every listing.

1. Read
The page

Price, deals, ratings, reviews and Amazon's own "bought in past month" are read straight off the page you are viewing.

2. Look up
Rates and demand

Commission rates, campaign rates, stock, video counts and sales rank come from product pages, rate cards and our shared catalogue.

3. Score
0 to 100

Six signals blend into one Butler Score, so results can be ranked the way a creator would weigh them.

How each signal is judged

  • Commission: dollars per sale. Full points at $5 or more, scaling down below that.
  • Open slot: fewer existing influencer videos scores higher. At six or more, this part drops to zero.
  • Demand: Amazon's "bought in past month". Full points at 200 or more.
  • Price: full points at $20 or more.

Not known yet? No penalty.

If a signal has not loaded (for example video counts on a search tile), it gets half credit instead of dragging the score down. Open the product or tap Scan videos and the score sharpens.

Example: a $299.99 laptop at a 2.5% rate scores 83: commission 30 + open slot 12.5 (not scanned) + demand 20 + stock 5 (not checked yet) + price 5 + campaign 10.

Putting it to work

A quick way to pick what to film

  1. Sort by Butler Score using the toolbar above the results. Hot picks (70+) rise to the top.
  2. Look for Butler Approved or Likely fit. These are selling now, in stock and not crowded with other creators.
  3. Check the money chips: commission per sale, a Campaign chip for boosted pay, and Deal chips for real price drops.
  4. Open the product to confirm the video count, or tap Scan videos to check a whole page at once. The score sharpens as data loads.
Tip: hover the estimate chips for a plain-English note, and re-pick a sort in the toolbar after details load to apply the updated scores.

Estimates

How estimates are calculated

Estimated sales per month

  1. Amazon's own number first. When a listing shows "400+ bought in past month", that is used as a floor.
  2. Shared catalogue. Otherwise a sales estimate from sales rank, fitted per category using products where we can see both rank and Amazon's bought figure.
  3. Built-in rank model as the final fallback, adjusted by category.

Revenue is estimated units × price.

Your commission per sale

  1. Live SiteStripe rate when you are signed in and it is on the page.
  2. Associates rate card for the product's category (Walmart: its published category rates).
  3. Your saved default rate from extension settings.

A confirmed Creator Connections rate replaces the standard rate for that product.

Good to know

Reading the numbers well

  • Sales and revenue are estimates. A "~" means modeled, not reported by Amazon or Walmart.
  • "400+ bought" is a floor, so the real number is at least that.
  • Revenue is the product's total, not your earnings. Your earnings are the sales you drive times your rate.
  • Commission is an estimate. Associates rates vary by category and country, and special campaigns can pay more.
  • Data refreshes as you browse. Rates and product details are reused for up to a day.
  • Other countries: Global reach shows "not checked" until the Creator API is connected, instead of guessing.
  • Sites change. When Amazon or Walmart redesign a page, we ship a fix quickly.
Privacy

Your data stays yours

  • Reads pages in your browser. No account is needed for the core tools.
  • Optional sync sends your own findings to your dashboard, only if you turn it on.
  • Shared catalogue contribution is off by default. If you turn it on, only product facts (price, rank, category, bought count) are pooled, never personal data.
  • Desktop app link stays on your own computer.
Where it works

12 Amazon storefronts (US, CA, UK, AU, DE, FR, IT, ES, JP, IN, MX, BR) and Walmart.com (US). Non-English wording is best-effort.

FAQ

Quick answers

Why does a high score say "Likely fit" and not "Approved"?
The video count has not loaded for that result. Open the product or tap Scan videos and it resolves to Approved or removes the badge.
Why do some results have no chips?
Sponsored ad carousels and non-product blocks are not scored. Regular product results are.
Can I change the thresholds?
Yes. In the extension settings you can set the minimum bought count, maximum influencer videos, minimum price and your default commission rate.
Do I need the desktop app?
No. The scoring, chips and sorting work without it. The app adds your earnings, "already posted" markers and one-click sending to your butlers.
Is the estimated revenue what I will make?
No. It is the product's estimated revenue. Your earnings depend on the sales you drive and your commission rate.
Is the extension free?
Yes, the extension is free to install.
Glossary

Terms you will see

  • SiteStripe: Amazon's Associates toolbar at the top of Amazon pages. It shows your live commission rate.
  • Creator Connections: Amazon's program where brands offer boosted commission on specific products.
  • Sponsored Products for Creators: a pay-per-click campaign type. The Butler shows Amazon's own per-click forecast.
  • Best Sellers Rank: Amazon's sales rank within a category. A lower number means more sales.
  • Open slot: room for a new creator video, judged by how many influencer videos a product already has.
  • Rollback and Clearance: Walmart's markers for reduced prices.
