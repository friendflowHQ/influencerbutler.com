# Competitor intel: MVP Affiliate

Internal competitive research. Do not surface any of this, or the competitor
name, on public or affiliate-facing pages (see the competitor-naming policy).

- **Captured:** 2026-09-28
- **Site:** https://mvpaffiliate.io (app at https://app.mvpaffiliate.io)
- **Community:** public Facebook group "MVP AFFILIATE - Affiliate Online Marketing Tool"
  (https://www.facebook.com/groups/mvpaffiliate/), ~31 members as of capture.

## Who they are

Built by Seb and Michelle, the "Gominplanet" affiliate brand, who claim MVP runs
their own Amazon affiliate business past $3M/yr in revenue. "El Turco Boludo" /
Alejandro Gimenez are the faces marketing it inside the Facebook group and page.

Signals they are early stage and recruiting: tiny public community, and all
paid pricing is framed as "founder pricing, locked for life," i.e. pre-scale.

## Positioning

Core wedge is owned media. Headline: "Your Amazon storefront is rented. Build
the one you own." The pitch: you already film the reviews, and MVP turns each
video into an SEO blog post on your own WordPress domain, written in your real
voice (an AI trained on your own videos and edits), then finishes the upload
(description, tags, affiliate links, tested thumbnail) and fans it out across
9+ social channels plus TikTok.

Heavy trust messaging: "we never use or sell your data," "your WordPress site
stays yours forever," 30-day money-back guarantee.

## Pricing

Premium-priced relative to us. Two paid tiers plus a free tier. Regular prices
in parentheses; the lower number is the "founder" rate they are pushing.

| Plan | Founder | Regular | Scope |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| Free | $0 forever | - | Product research + Deal Radar free forever; 5 posts + 5 thumbnails/designs for 30 days, no card |
| Amazon | $99/mo | $179 | Storefront/influencer. No blog, no WordPress, no YouTube. 250 thumbnails/mo, social designs + auto-post (Pinterest/IG/FB), 150 deal posts/mo, Creator Connections, shoppable link-in-bio, 2 face models, 600 AI assistant msgs/mo |
| Pro | $199/mo | $399 | Everything in Amazon, plus WordPress blog (100 generations/mo), Video-to-Blog, Clip Factory, X/TikTok/LinkedIn/Threads/Bluesky/Telegram auto-post, up to 10 WordPress sites + 3 Virtual Assistant seats, multiple YouTube channels, newsletter (10k subs), 800 AI msgs/mo |

Takeaway: they sit at $99 to $399/mo. Our Pro tier is $69/mo for 3 seats, so
price is our clearest opening against them.

## Named features (and our nearest equivalent)

- **Passport**: free, unlimited geo-routing links, "never pay per click." Direct
  shot at GeniusLink / OneLink style tools that bill per click. Overlaps our
  GeniusLink-based routing and undercuts it on price.
- **Deal Radar**: price-history-verified Amazon deals (vs our Deals butler and
  the extension Deal Radar / Campaign Radar overlays).
- **Clip Factory**: long video to vertical shorts, auto-post to TikTok/IG (vs our
  Reload / Repost / YouTube / Shopping butlers).
- **Art Director**: one-click thumbnails and social designs with the creator's
  own face (Photobooth headshots, face models).
- **Creator Connections**: daily brand-deal digest pulling Levanta, PartnerBoost,
  and Walmart campaigns (vs our Campaign butler and Creator API work).
- **Co-Pilot / Liftoff / Encore**: writes YouTube title, description and tags;
  bulk-launches up to 10 videos to YouTube + storefronts in one press; auto-pins
  a comment with the affiliate link when a product goes on sale and removes it
  when the sale ends.
- Native publishing to WordPress, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads,
  Bluesky, Telegram, Pinterest, plus TikTok. Site clean-up / migration (404s,
  duplicates, stale affiliate links).
- "Real voice" AI writing trained on the creator's own videos and edits.

## Threat assessment

Where they are genuinely ahead of us:

- The owned-blog / SEO-article engine ("video to a ranked WordPress article in
  your voice") is more built-out than anything we ship, and it is their whole
  story.
- Free unlimited geo-links (Passport) is a sharp pricing wedge.
- "Trained on your own voice" writing is a real differentiator.
- Founder credibility: a real $3M/yr affiliate business as proof.

Where we are ahead:

- Desktop app that actually automates FB/IG posting.
- Chrome extension money-signal overlays across 12 storefronts.
- Walmart support, our own affiliate program, and the events system.
- They appear web-only, WordPress/blog-dependent, with a tiny community and
  premium pricing.

Overlap is high and growing: Deal Radar, geo-links, clip repurposing, brand-deal
matching, VA seats, and extension-style value. They are building the same
butler-shaped surface area, just packaged as a premium suite.

## Suggested angles for us

- Lean on the price gap ($69/mo vs their $99 to $399/mo).
- Emphasize the desktop + extension automation they do not have.
- Watch Passport: if free unlimited geo-routing gains traction, our
  GeniusLink-based routing needs a cost-competitive answer.
