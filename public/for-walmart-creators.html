Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Walmart Creator account to use this?

Yes, Walmart Repost signs in to your own Walmart Creator storefront to republish content there. Walmart Research works without a Walmart Creator sign-in since it only tracks public price and rank data.

Can I use this if I also work Amazon?

Yes. Most Walmart creators run Influencer Butler on both. Walmart Repost pulls directly from the Amazon storefront content you already have and republishes it to Walmart on an exact barcode match, so you are not filming everything twice.

What is the cross-retailer card?

The cross-retailer card shows, on a product you are already looking at, whether the exact same item is also available on Walmart or Target, matched by UPC/GTIN barcode, so you can decide whether it is worth a storefront listing on more than one retailer.
