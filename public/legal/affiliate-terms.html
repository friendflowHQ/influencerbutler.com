1) Program Overview

The Influencer Butler Affiliate Program lets approved Affiliates earn recurring commissions for referring paying customers to our software subscriptions. Referral tracking, commission accrual, and payout fulfillment are performed by our payment and affiliate processor, Lemon Squeezy. Influencer Butler does not directly process payouts to Affiliates.

2) Eligibility and Approval

a) You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction) and able to enter into a binding contract.

b) You must submit an application at influencerbutler.com/affiliates/apply and be approved by Influencer Butler. Approval is at our sole discretion and may be granted, declined, or revoked at any time, with or without cause.

c) You must complete affiliate setup in the Lemon Squeezy hosted portal (app.lemonsqueezy.com → Affiliate Hub) using the same email address on your application. Until your Lemon Squeezy affiliate record is active, you cannot earn commissions.

d) Employees, contractors, and immediate family members of The Social Media Posse LLC are not eligible to participate as Affiliates without prior written consent.

3) Commission Structure

a) Rate. Approved Affiliates earn thirty percent (30%) of the net subscription revenue (after refunds, taxes, and payment processor fees deducted by Lemon Squeezy) of every paying customer they refer.

b) Recurring for twelve months per customer. Commissions accrue on each paid period - monthly or annual - for the first twelve (12) months after the referred customer's first paid period. Once the referred customer has been a paying subscriber for twelve consecutive months, commission accrual on that customer ends, regardless of whether the customer continues to pay. There is no per-affiliate earning cap; the 12-month-per-customer window is the only commission expiration. Tier reductions are not applied during the 12-month window.

c) Attribution window. A 30-day attribution cookie is set by Lemon Squeezy when a user clicks your tracked link or applies your branded promo code. If a clicked user converts to a paying subscriber within 30 days, the resulting commissions are credited to you.

d) Last-click attribution. Where a customer clicks multiple Affiliate links before converting, Lemon Squeezy attributes the conversion to the most recent click within the 30-day window.

e) No discount stacking. A customer may apply either an Affiliate-tracked code (yours or another Affiliate's) or a site-wide welcome / promotional code at checkout - not both. When a customer arrives through an Affiliate link or applies an Affiliate-tracked code, any site-wide welcome promotional discount is suppressed at checkout in favor of the Affiliate code. Your commission is computed against the net amount actually charged after the applied discount.

4) Payouts

a) Processor. All Affiliate payouts are processed directly by Lemon Squeezy. Influencer Butler is not a party to the payment transaction between Lemon Squeezy and the Affiliate.

b) Schedule. Payouts are issued monthly, generally on the first of each month, for the prior calendar month's earned commissions.

c) Minimum balance. A minimum unpaid balance (currently ten US dollars (US$10), as set by Lemon Squeezy and subject to change) is required to trigger a payout. Balances below the minimum roll forward to the following month.

d) Payout method. You select your payout method (PayPal, Wise, bank transfer, or such other methods as Lemon Squeezy supports in your jurisdiction) inside your Lemon Squeezy portal. Influencer Butler has no visibility into and no control over your payout method or banking details.

e) Disputes and delays. Disputes regarding payout amount, timing, method, currency, or failure are between you and Lemon Squeezy and must be raised through Lemon Squeezy support. Influencer Butler will reasonably assist with reconciliation questions but is not a party to the payment.
