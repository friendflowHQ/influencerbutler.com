Build Week Official Rules

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

PLEASE READ CAREFULLY. These Official Rules ("Rules") govern the Influencer Butler Build Week event (the "Event"). By posting an idea, reacting to ideas, or submitting the claim form, you agree to these Rules. No purchase is necessary to take part. These Rules are written in English and the English version controls over any translation or summary. This Event is in no way sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Facebook or Meta.

These Rules supplement our Terms of Service, End-User License Agreement, and Privacy Policy. If these Rules conflict with the Terms of Service about the Event, these Rules control for the Event only.

1) Sponsor and Overview

a) The Event is run by The Social Media Posse LLC, 3556 S 5600 W #1-478, Salt Lake City, UT 84120, United States, doing business as Influencer Butler (the "Sponsor", "we", "us").

b) Members of the public post ideas for new Influencer Butler automation tools (each a "Butler") in the Influencer Butler Facebook group (the "Group"). Ten ideas are chosen (the "Featured Ideas"). During Build Week, we try to build each Featured Idea. If we do not build a Featured Idea to the Working Standard (Section 8) by the Build Deadline, the person who submitted it receives the Guarantee Prize (Section 9).

2) Eligibility

a) To be eligible for the Guarantee Prize you must be a legal resident of the United States (the 50 states and the District of Columbia), at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live, if higher), and a member of the Group in good standing.

b) Anyone who meets the Group's own rules may post ideas and react to ideas, but only eligible persons can receive a Guarantee Prize.

c) Employees, contractors, and members of the immediate households of The Social Media Posse LLC, and anyone who helps build or judge the Event, may take part in the Group conversation but are not eligible for a Guarantee Prize and cannot take a Featured Idea slot reserved for the community.

d) Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3) No Purchase Necessary

a) No purchase, subscription, payment, or license is required to take part or to be eligible for the Guarantee Prize. You do not need to be an Influencer Butler customer.

b) Taking part is free. Joining the Group is free. Nothing in these Rules gives an advantage to paying customers.

4) Event Dates

All times are Mountain Time (Salt Lake City, Utah), "MT".

  • Ideas open: Monday, October 12, 2026, through Wednesday, October 28, 2026, 11:59 PM MT.
  • Voting closes: Saturday, October 31, 2026, 11:59 PM MT (the "Snapshot").
  • Featured Ideas announced: on or about Sunday, November 1, 2026.
  • Build Week: Monday, November 2, 2026, 9:00 AM MT, through the Build Deadline.
  • Build Deadline: Sunday, November 8, 2026, 11:59 PM MT.
  • Results announced: on or about Monday, November 9, 2026.

We may change these dates as described in Section 17.

5) How to Submit an Idea

a) Post your idea as a top-level comment on the Event's pinned post in the Group, using the template in that post (what the Butler does, who it is for, which website or app it works with, and why it matters).

b) Each person may submit up to two (2) ideas. Extra ideas may be removed.

c) Your idea must be your own, original work. Do not submit anything that belongs to someone else, that someone else asked you to keep confidential, or that you are not allowed to share.

d) If two people submit essentially the same idea, we credit the earliest submission and may combine the others into it.

e) Ideas are posted publicly in the Group. Do not include anything you want to keep private (for example, passwords, personal data, or secret business details).

6) Eligible Ideas

a) To qualify for the Guarantee, an idea must be something we can reasonably build as a desktop Butler or a feature of the Influencer Butler app.

b) An idea is not eligible if, in our reasonable judgment, it: (i) breaks any law; (ii) would require us to break a website's or platform's terms of service; (iii) depends on approval, access, an account, an API, or a partnership that we cannot obtain by the Build Deadline; (iv) is offensive, deceptive, or harmful; (v) targets or harasses a person or business; or (vi) duplicates a Butler we already offer.

c) We decide whether an idea is eligible. If a chosen idea turns out to be ineligible, we may replace it with the next idea in line, and the replaced idea is not a Featured Idea.

7) Voting and Choosing the Featured Ideas

a) Seven by reactions. Seven (7) of the ten Featured Ideas are the eligible ideas with the most reactions (likes and other reactions on the idea comment, counted equally) from members of the Group, counted at the Snapshot.

b) Three staff picks. Three (3) of the ten Featured Ideas are chosen by us from the eligible ideas, whatever their reaction counts.

c) Ties. If ideas are tied for a reaction slot, the earliest submitted idea ranks first.

d) Counting. We use the reaction counts shown by the Group at the Snapshot. We may exclude reactions that we reasonably believe came from fake, duplicate, purchased, automated, or newly created accounts, or from accounts that are not members of the Group.

e) No manipulation. Do not buy, trade, swap, or automate reactions, and do not use fake accounts. Anyone who does may be disqualified, and their ideas removed.

f) Our decisions about eligibility, counting, and selection are final.

g) We may choose to feature more than ten ideas. We will announce that publicly. Any extra ideas get the same Guarantee as the original ten.

8) Build Week and the Working Standard

a) During Build Week we will try to build each Featured Idea. We will share progress in the Group.

b) A Featured Idea is built to the "Working Standard" when, by the Build Deadline, all of the following are true:

  • it is included in a released version of the Influencer Butler app that is available to the person who submitted it through the app's normal update process;
  • in our testing against the real website or app it is meant to work with, it performs the core function described in the submitted idea;
  • it does not crash and does not lose or corrupt the user's data during its core flow; and
  • it has a help page that explains how to use it.

c) Minor issues do not count. Cosmetic problems, small bugs outside the core flow, and missing extras that were not part of the submitted idea do not stop a build from meeting the Working Standard.

d) Access for testing. Each person with a Featured Idea gets access to test their build during Build Week, including a Pro trial if they do not already have access.

e) Outside our control. A Featured Idea is treated as meeting the Working Standard if it would have, except that the website or app it works with blocks it, changes, goes down, or otherwise stops working for reasons outside our reasonable control, or because of an event we could not reasonably prevent (for example, a major outage, a legal order, or a disaster).

f) How we decide. We decide whether the Working Standard was met using a written checklist applied by our team. If you disagree, email hello@influencerbutler.com within seven (7) days of the results announcement and we will have a second team member review it. The result of that review is final.

9) The Guarantee Prize

a) If a Featured Idea does not meet the Working Standard by the Build Deadline, the person who submitted it (the "Entrant") may claim: one (1) Lifetime Influencer Butler Pro Solo license for the Entrant, and one (1) for one friend the Entrant names (together, the "Guarantee Prize").

b) The friend must be at least 18 years old, must not be a member of the Entrant's household, and must not already hold an Influencer Butler license. The friend is named when the Entrant claims the prize. Naming, tagging, or sharing with a friend is not required to enter the Event.

c) If we build it, no Guarantee Prize is owed for that idea. We will publicly credit the Entrant for the idea.

d) Approximate retail value. A lifetime license has no fixed retail price. For information and tax purposes, we state its approximate retail value as US$468 per license (twelve months of Pro Solo at the monthly price of US$39 in effect when these Rules were published).

A Lifetime license lasts for as long as Influencer Butler's Pro Solo plan (or a successor to it) is offered by the Sponsor or its successor, and the license has not been ended under these Rules, the Terms of Service, or the EULA.

A buyer or successor may modify, replace, or end the plan, the product, or this program, as described in Section 12(d) of the Terms of Service. You have no vested right to the continued availability of any feature, plan, or program, and we cannot control what a future owner does.

c) Changes and discontinuation. We may change, add, remove, or discontinue features, and may stop offering the product, as described in Section 13(b) of the Terms of Service. Features that use third-party services still depend on those services, and AI features still use the user's own provider key at the user's own cost.

d) One device. Each Lifetime Pro Solo license is intended for one (1) device and one (1) person. It may not be shared, resold, rented, or used for a business or team other than the person it was issued to.

e) No cash value; no exchange. The Guarantee Prize has no cash value. It cannot be sold, traded, transferred, assigned, or exchanged for money, credit, or any other item, and it cannot be combined with another prize or offer. The Entrant's license cannot be transferred to anyone else, and the friend's license is only for the named friend.

g) Revocation. We may end a license, without refund and without liability, if we reasonably believe it has been used to commit fraud, abuse the service, break the Terms of Service or EULA, share the license, resell it, or manipulate this Event.

i) Taxes. The Entrant and the friend are each solely responsible for any taxes that apply to a prize. We may ask for a tax form if the law requires it.

b) If you do not claim within fourteen (14) days, or the information you give us is false, the prize is forfeited and we may not award it to anyone else.

c) The Entrant's license is delivered to the Entrant's email and the friend's license to the friend's email, normally within fourteen (14) days after we confirm the claim.
