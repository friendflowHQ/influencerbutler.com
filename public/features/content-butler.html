Watch the 4-minute walkthrough

Plan Your Filming Days: Content Butler. Recorded inside the app and narrated start to finish.

Read the video transcript

A backlog with no dates. You have a backlog of products you mean to film and no system for when. Content Butler is the calendar: each entry is a product parked on a day, and the sidebar holds everything you have not scheduled yet.

Power User feature. Quick note on plans. Content Butler is a Power User feature. On the Beginner plan it is hidden from the sidebar. Upgrade any time in Settings, under Subscription.

Three views. Monthly for the shape of the month, Weekly for what you are shooting this week, Daily for a busy shoot day, and List for a flat searchable list.

Back to the month. Back to the month. Each day holds a few entries, and overdue ones are marked. The Overdue button filters to them.

Tags. Tags track each entry's stage: ideation, ready to film, filming, editing, awaiting approval, ready to publish. Filter by tag or add your own.

Unassigned products. Unassigned products is the sidebar: what Orders Butler found you still need a photo, a vertical or a horizontal video for. Drag a card onto a day to schedule it, or drop it back to unschedule.

Quick add. Quick add a product adds one that did not come from your orders: a title, an optional ASIN, and shooting notes.

Income report. The income report, like Collab Butler's, lists the products brands sent you with their value, for tax time.

Export. Export CSV takes the plan to a spreadsheet. Delete All clears the calendar, so leave it alone.

Next step. Your next step: drag five products from Unassigned onto next week, and tag them ready to film.
