Watch the 4-minute walkthrough

Scan Your Amazon Storefront: Storefront Butler. Recorded inside the app and narrated start to finish.

Read the video transcript

Do this one first. If you set up one thing today, make it this one. Most of the Amazon butlers read from what Storefront Butler finds: Video Reload, Photo Reload, Retag, Benable, CC Check. Empty here means empty there.

Included in the Beginner plan. Quick note on plans. Storefront Butler is included in the Beginner plan, so everyone has it.

What it does. It opens your Amazon storefront in your signed-in session and writes down everything on it: every video, every photo, every idea list and media list, and every product tagged in them. Then it looks each product up for price, deal and stock.

Nothing to paste. There is no address to paste. It already knows your storefront from Settings. A Creators API key is recommended for prices and commission rates, but the scan runs without one.

Discovery switches. Two optional discovery switches: Related CC campaign discovery also notes the Creator Connections campaigns on your products, and Related products discovery pulls in the products Amazon shows beside yours.

Watch it run. Watch it run in the top bar shows the browser while it reads. Turn it off and it works quietly in the background. It applies to every butler.

Run. Then Run. It reads your storefront one section at a time, and you can stop any time without losing what it already found.

Content totals. The totals count up as it goes: videos, photos, idea lists, media lists, and products. This is the part you came for.

The results table. Then the table. One row per product on your storefront, with the content it sits in, the price, the deal, the commission rate, whether it has a Creator Connections campaign, and whether it is still in stock.

Search. Search narrows it by keyword or product code.

What is out of stock. The ASIN Status filter is worth checking weekly. Out of stock shows the products nobody can buy any more, which is exactly what Retag Butler fixes.

Keep prices fresh. Update Pricing and Deals refreshes prices and deals for every row, and Auto-refresh does it every six, twelve or twenty-four hours.

Send it on. Send selected to Benable Butler queues rows for a Benable list, and Export CSV takes the whole table to a spreadsheet.

Next step. Your next step: press Run once and let it finish. Then come back weekly, filter to Out of stock, and let the other butlers work from a fresh table.
