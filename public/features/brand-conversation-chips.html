Brand Conversation Chips

A chip on the product you're already browsing shows exactly where you stand with its brand: Messaged, Brand responded, You replied, or Brand messaged you. Click it to jump straight back into that Creator Connections conversation, free in the Chrome extension.

Screenshot: a "Brand responded" chip on an Amazon product card
Without this

You see a product worth pitching, but have no idea whether you already messaged that brand until you open the Messages drawer and search for its name by hand.

With Butler

A chip right on the product tells you before you click anything: already messaged, brand replied, or brand reached out to you first.

Know before you ask

  • Shows up wherever you're browsing products: Amazon search results, the Deals overlay, Idea List detail pages, Campaign Radar, and a dedicated "Brand conversation" section on the product page itself.
  • Four states, plain language: Messaged (you wrote, no reply yet), Brand responded, You replied (they wrote first, you answered), Brand messaged you (they wrote, you haven't answered yet).
  • Matches the product to a conversation by the page's own brand name, so it works without any manual setup.
  • Click the chip to open that thread in Creator Connections. If the desktop app is paired and already holds that thread, a second button opens it directly in Messenger Butler.

Covers Creator Connections conversations specifically. See the Creator Connections overview for the full cockpit.

Reads your whole inbox, not just the newest 100

  • Amazon's own Messages drawer only ever renders the 100 newest conversations, with no way to load more.
  • Brand Conversation Chips reads Creator Connections' own chat data directly, so a chip stays accurate no matter how many conversations you have, not just the ones the drawer happens to show.
  • The same full-inbox read also fixes the Messages drawer's own filter counts and adds a "more conversations" list for anything past the 100-row cap.

Pairs with the Messages drawer inside Creator Connections and the Campaign Butler cockpit.
