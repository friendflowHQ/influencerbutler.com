Two Walmart tools under one butler. Walmart Research builds price, rank, and sales history for the products you touch. Walmart Repost copies your Amazon storefront videos and photos to your Walmart Creator storefront, hands-free, only on an exact barcode match.

Without this

You pick Walmart products to feature on a hunch, and rebuilding your storefront on Walmart Creator means filming and editing everything a second time.

With Butler

Walmart Research keeps price, rank, and sales history for every product you look up, and Walmart Repost republishes the storefront content you already have, only when it is the exact same item.

Walmart Research: know what actually sells

  • Tracks price history, an approximate popularity rank, estimated monthly sales and revenue, and stock for every Walmart product the app looks up.
  • Fills up by itself as you harvest Walmart deals or run a repost, with a daily check that keeps every tracked product fresh.
  • Track a product by hand: paste its Walmart link, item ID, or UPC and it starts building history from the next check.
  • Open View history on any product for a price chart and a rank chart, so a real drop or a rising trend is easy to spot.

Read the Walmart Research guide →

Walmart Repost: copy your storefront, hands-free

  • Republishes your Amazon storefront videos and photos to your Walmart Creator storefront, only when it is the exact same item (matched by UPC/GTIN barcode).
  • Video Repost and Photo Repost are twins that share one Walmart Creator sign-in.
  • The Set it up wizard signs you in, then lets you review each match first or post automatically, cap how many post per run, skip out-of-stock items, and schedule daily, every 3 days, or weekly.
  • Post cleanup can re-post everything fresh or remove soundless and older posts from your Walmart storefront.
