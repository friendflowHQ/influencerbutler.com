Fix Your Dead Product Links: Retag Butler. Recorded inside the app and narrated start to finish.

Read the video transcript

Money quietly leaking. Products go out of stock. Your old posts keep getting views, and every one of those views now earns nothing. Retag Butler finds those dead links and adds a product people can actually buy.

Power User feature. Quick note on plans. Retag Butler is a Power User feature. On the Beginner plan it is hidden from the sidebar. Upgrade any time in Settings, under Subscription.

It adds, never removes. The reassuring part: it never removes your old tag. It adds the replacement alongside, so your history stays intact and the link points at something live.

What it needs. It reads which products are unavailable from Storefront Butler, so sync that first. Your storefront URL comes from Settings, and the status line here confirms it is set and signed in.

Your numbers. The overview: pieces scanned, stale products, untagged pieces, replacement candidates matched, and new products tagged.

After matching. The control that matters most. Manual review, then retag approved puts every suggestion in a list and waits for you. Start here. Auto-approve tags matches above the threshold on its own.

The auto-tag threshold. Auto-tag threshold is the confidence line for auto mode, seventy out of a hundred by default. Keep it at seventy or above unless you review results.

Min title similarity. Min title similarity decides which candidates appear in the list at all. Lower it to see more, including weak ones you can reject.

Pick from a list or single best. When a match is found, pick from a short list or take the single best. Amazon Visual Match uses Amazon's own image matching: more accurate, and slower.

Run. Run scans your storefront, finds the stale products, and matches replacements. Watch it run in the top bar keeps the browser on screen.

The queue. Then the queue, as cards or a table. Each one shows the content, the stale product, the candidate, and its score, with Approve, Skip and Re-match. Filter by status, type or reason.

Approve, then retag. Approve selected marks the ones you agree with, and Run retags on approved writes only those to Amazon. Export diff CSV keeps a before-and-after record.

On a schedule. Schedule recurring runs keeps it going, every few days, so new dead links get caught early.

Next step. Your next step: sync Storefront Butler, keep Manual review, press Run, and approve your first ten swaps. Then decide whether auto mode earns your trust.
