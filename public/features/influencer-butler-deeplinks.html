What it does

Pick Influencer Butler as your link provider under DeepLink Routing, and every link your tools build becomes a short, branded links.influencerbutler.com link that you own. Then the built-in Link Performance dashboard shows you how your audience is actually clicking, without logging into any outside service.

  • Branded short links on your own domain that you control
  • Set it once: every tool (Deal Posting, Orders Butler, and more) routes links through it, with no per-tool setup
  • Link Performance dashboard: total clicks, a day-by-day trend, and your top links
  • Audience insight: clicks broken down by country, device, and referrer
  • Pick a date range: last 7, 30, or 90 days
  • One link per product, so re-posting the same item keeps its clicks together instead of splitting them
  • No third-party signup and no API keys to paste. It is built in

What you can expect

  • Cleaner, on-brand links in your posts instead of long raw Amazon URLs
  • A clear picture of which products and which platforms drive your clicks
  • Your affiliate tag is preserved, so commissions still track the same way
  • Because Amazon does not report earnings per outgoing link, the dashboard shows click metrics, not revenue
  • Free on every plan, including Free forever. Just sign in and pick it

The trouble with Amazon links. A long Amazon link opens a browser tab and looks like spam. A short link you own opens the shopping app, which converts far better, and you can change where it points later. Link Butler mints those links.

Included in the Beginner plan. Quick note on plans. Link Butler is included in the Beginner plan, so everyone has it.

Your numbers. Total links, total clicks, how many are posted somewhere, and how many are healthy.

Paste a product link. Create a Butler Link: paste the product link. It reads the product so you recognise it at a glance.

Title and campaign. A title only you see, and a campaign so its clicks group together in Performance.

Add Butler Link. Add Butler Link mints the Calling Card. It is live the moment it is made, so I am not pressing it here. Add several at once takes one product link per line.

The ledger. Your Butler Links is the ledger: the product, the Calling Card with one-click copy, where it was posted, a two-week click trend, total clicks, a health pill that reads Live, Healed or Broken, and your notes and tags.

Search and filter. Search, filter by health, posted or not, retailer, campaign or tag, and Group by campaign for subtotals. Export Ledger takes it to a spreadsheet.

QR Studio. QR Studio turns any link into a QR code with your colors and logo, for packaging, print or a story.

Make one. Paste a link, pick the colors, and download it as PNG or SVG.

Performance. Performance charts the taps over time, by link and by campaign, so you know which placements earn.

Settings. Settings holds your default affiliate tag, link routing and providers like Mavely, and your own custom domain. It saves as you type.

Next step. Your next step: paste one product link, add it, copy the Calling Card, and share it in your next post. Come back in a day and read the clicks.
