What it does

Pick Influencer Butler as your link provider under DeepLink Routing, and every link your tools build becomes a short, branded links.influencerbutler.com link that you own. Then the built-in Link Performance dashboard shows you how your audience is actually clicking, without logging into any outside service.

  • Branded short links on your own domain that you control
  • Set it once: every tool (Deals Influencer Butler, Orders Butler, and more) routes links through it, with no per-tool setup
  • Link Performance dashboard: total clicks, a day-by-day trend, and your top links
  • Audience insight: clicks broken down by country, device, and referrer
  • Pick a date range: last 7, 30, or 90 days
  • One link per product, so re-posting the same item keeps its clicks together instead of splitting them
  • No third-party signup and no API keys to paste. It is built in

What you can expect

  • Cleaner, on-brand links in your posts instead of long raw Amazon URLs
  • A clear picture of which products and which platforms drive your clicks
  • Your affiliate tag is preserved, so commissions still track the same way
  • Because Amazon does not report earnings per outgoing link, the dashboard shows click metrics, not revenue
  • Free on every plan, including Free forever. Just sign in and pick it
