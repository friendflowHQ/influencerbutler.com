Photo Reload Butler

Your storefront photos, re-posted to every marketplace you are eligible for.

Start free See all tools
Without this

Re-upload the same photo to Canada, the UK, Australia, and Singapore by hand. Re-tag every one. Lose the photos Amazon removed.

With Butler

One run fans a photo out to every marketplace you are eligible for with fresh tags, and a saved copy means a deleted photo can always go back.

How it saves you time, money, and headaches

  • The same shoppable photo earns in four more storefronts, with no extra shooting or editing.
  • Every delete saves the original to your computer first, so a removed photo can always be reloaded.
  • Availability checks skip out-of-stock products, so you do not post dead links that drag down your metrics.

What’s in the box

  • Fan-out to Canada, the UK, Australia, and Singapore from your US photos in a single run
  • Preferred Formulas: three one-click mixes that fill in source, destinations, and filters for the most common reload paths
  • Delete-before-repost to refresh a photo on its own storefront, with the original downloaded to your computer first
  • Reload deleted photos from the Deleted from Amazon tab, each with its locally saved original
  • Skip-unavailable checks before posting and at tag time, both fail-open so an API error never blocks a post
  • Title-based matching that finds a product’s foreign listing by name and product image when the ASIN does not exist abroad
  • Duplicate filters by title, ASIN, both, or already-posted-to-that-marketplace
  • A Photo Log that tracks every post with its destinations and status
