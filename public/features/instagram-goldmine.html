Instagram Goldmine

Research-only Instagram creator discovery. Seed a few accounts, walk the suggested-creator graph, harvest qualifying posts with ad disclosures, paid-partnership labels, or brand mentions.

Setup: ~5-10 min

Start free See all tools
Screenshot: seed list + filter knobs + qualifying-posts table
Without this

Scroll Instagram for hours hunting creators in your niche. Manually copy down handles, follower counts, recent brand deals. Lose the list when your phone restarts.

With Butler

Drop in 3–5 seed creators. Butler walks the suggested-account graph, filters out the noise, and gives you a sortable table of creators with verified brand partnerships.

How it saves you time, money, and headaches

  • Find collab partners and brand-deal benchmarks in minutes, not days.
  • See exactly which brands a creator already works with - useful for pitching the same brand yourself.
  • Export the whole result set as CSV or JSON for your CRM, spreadsheet, or pitch tracker.

What’s in the box

  • Seed-and-walk discovery: paste usernames, Butler follows Instagram’s suggested-creator graph
  • Filters: min followers, min views, min comments, max posts per profile, verified-only, Reels-first
  • Disclosure filters: #ad/#sponsored hashtags, Paid-partnership labels, tagged brand mentions
  • Run modes: seeds + suggested, seeds only, suggested only
  • Two tables: Discovered accounts (with category, follower counts, source seed) and Qualifying posts (with views, likes, comments, hashtags, mentions)
  • Safe mode (2× human delays), screenshot-on-failure, manual sign-in window if Instagram challenges
  • Export to CSV / JSON; open exports folder; clear results

Watch the 5-minute walkthrough

Find Brands That Pay Creators: Instagram Goldmine. Recorded inside the app and narrated start to finish.

Read the video transcript

Who is paying?. The easiest brands to pitch are the ones already paying creators like you. Instagram Goldmine finds them: it starts from a few creators in your niche, follows Instagram's suggested-for-you trail, and collects every post tagged as paid.

Power User feature. Quick note on plans. Instagram Goldmine is a Power User feature. On the Beginner plan it is hidden from the sidebar. Upgrade any time in Settings, under Subscription.

It only looks. It reads public profiles and posts. No follows, no likes, no messages. Sign in to Instagram once here and every Instagram butler shares that session.

Seeds. Seed usernames: three to ten creators whose audience overlaps yours. Handles or profile links both work.

Run mode and size. Run mode decides how wide it goes, min followers sets the floor, and max suggested per seed and max profiles per run cap the walk. Keep the first run small.

What counts as paid. What counts as paid: posts with hashtag ad disclosures, posts with the paid partnership label, or posts with tagged or mentioned brands. Tick the ones that matter to you.

Safe mode. Safe mode doubles the human delays. Slower, and far less likely to get the session blocked. Leave it on.

Harvest emails. Harvest tagged brand emails pulls the contact email from each brand it finds, and Also check the bio-link website chases the email one step further. Slower, but that is the pitch list.

Watch it run. Watch it run in the top bar keeps the browser on screen. It applies to every butler.

Start Discovery. Start Discovery opens Chrome and walks the graph. It takes a while, so start it and come back. Not pressed today.

Qualifying posts. The qualifying posts table: the creator, their followers and engagement rate, the post, why it qualified, the brands mentioned, and the brand email when it found one. Search it, or filter by pitched status.

Send it on. Tick rows and Send to Pitch Butler carries the brand, the creator and the post across, or send the creators to Group Invite Butler. Export CSV keeps the lot.

Next step. Your next step: three seeds, twenty-five profiles, safe mode on, harvest emails on. Run it once, then pitch the brands that show up twice.
