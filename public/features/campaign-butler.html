One cockpit for the Creator Connections and SPCC grid. Campaign Radar scores every card with fill meters, Last Call alerts you before a good one closes, the Butler's Brief gives you a verdict on what to film, and Idea List Butler carries the same money signals into your saved items.

Without this

You scroll the Creator Connections grid by hand, guess which campaigns are worth accepting, and find out a good one filled up after it is already closed.

With Butler

Fill meters and the Butler's Brief tell you what to go after before you open a single listing, and Last Call alerts catch the ones about to close.

Campaign Radar: fill meters on every card

  • Scores every campaign card on the Creator Connections and SPCC grid with a fill meter, so you can see at a glance how close each one is to closing.
  • Last Call watch bells alert you when a campaign you are tracking is about to fill completely, so you do not lose it to the clock.
  • Works directly on the grid in the free Chrome extension, no extra setup beyond installing it.

Covers onsite creators specifically. See the Creator Connections overview for the full cockpit.

Butler's Brief: a verdict, not just a number

  • A plain-language call on whether a campaign is worth your time and what to film, based on its rate, fill, and history.
  • Pairs with CC Check and Campaign Deals auto-accept so the campaigns you take are the ones the Brief actually recommends.
  • Idea List Butler carries the same money-signal scoring into the items you capture while browsing, before they ever reach a campaign.
