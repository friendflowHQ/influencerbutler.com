Watch the 3-minute walkthrough

Daily Commission Butler: Accept Creator Connections Campaigns From Your Sales. Recorded inside the app and narrated start to finish.

Read the video transcript

Campaigns on what you already sell. Brands run Creator Connections campaigns that pay extra on products you are already selling. Finding them one by one every day is the job nobody does. Daily Commission Butler does it for you.

Included in the Beginner plan. Quick note on plans. Daily Commission Butler is included in the Beginner plan, so everyone has it.

What it does. It reads your recent sales, looks for a campaign on each of those exact products, and accepts the ones it finds. It also checks your storefront's best sellers, so a top product with an open campaign is never left unclaimed.

Accepting is a promise. Accepting a campaign is a promise to a brand, and it cannot be undone from here. So in this video I show every setting and press nothing.

Before you run it. Two things first. Run CC Check at least once so the campaign catalog is fresh, and have Orders Butler synced in the last couple of days. Those supply the sales it matches against.

Marketplaces. Marketplaces: tick every Amazon country where you earn. One run sweeps them all, and the storefront URL beside each one is optional.

Your earnings. The earnings cards: lifetime earnings from accepted campaigns, the rolling thirty days, and the total accepted. Show in lets you pick the currency.

Day range. Day range chooses how far back it looks at your sales. Today is the smallest, safest start. Seven days catches products that sold slowly, and thirty days goes wider still.

Watch it run. Watch it run in the top bar opens the browser so you can see it work. Handy the first time. It applies to every butler.

Run. Run starts it. It signs into Amazon, reads the sales for the range you ticked, and accepts every campaign that matches a product code exactly. Read the status badge while it works.

On a schedule. Once you trust it, put it on a schedule. A start time and a repeat interval, then Enable Schedule, and it says yes to brands while you get on with your day.

Enable Schedule. Enable Schedule turns the timer on. Stop Schedule turns it off and clears anything waiting.

What it accepted. The log is the record: every campaign it accepted, the commission, the deal details, and whether that product has a video carousel yet. Filter it, send products to Content Butler, or export it.

All Time. All Time swaps between the recent view and your whole history.

Next step. Your next step: run CC Check, tick Today, press Run once yourself, and read what it accepted. Then set the schedule to once a day.
