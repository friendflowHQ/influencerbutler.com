Without this

Refresh Amazon manually hoping to catch a $400 vacuum at $4 before someone else does.

With Butler

Scheduled scans across your chosen categories. Errors land in Daily Deals before you finish your coffee.

How it saves you time, money, and headaches

  • No more manually F5-ing Amazon hoping for a misprice.
  • Catches glitches the second they appear, not after the seller fixes them.
  • Auto-scheduled mode publishes to your social channels through Daily Deals while you sleep.
  • Per-scan toggle between “draft for review” and “auto-post” - choose your risk level.

What’s in the box

  • Threshold slider 50–99% (default 99% catches genuine pricing errors)
  • Multi-select Amazon category picker (~25 PA-API SearchIndex departments + "All")
  • Bait-keyword sweep per category - clearance, open box, closeout, overstock, plus your own
  • Schedule presets: 1h / 3h / 6h / 12h / 24h (defaults to 6h)
  • Per-scan hand-off mode: draft for review, auto-schedule, or no hand-off
  • Dedupes by ASIN within each run - never re-pushes the same deal
  • Counts toward the topbar Hours-Saved / Money-Saved tally at your hourly rate
  • Shares Amazon Daily Deals’s posting pipeline - same destinations, same auto-fire daemon
