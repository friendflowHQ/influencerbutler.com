Without this

Prime Day hits June 10 and your content calendar is empty. You scramble between Amazon, your storefront, your camera roll, and the Creators API trying to remember which products you’ve already covered and which deals are still worth chasing.

With Butler

One table. Every Prime Day deal that matches an ASIN you’ve already posted about, every deal on a product you already own, and every fresh discovery from your keyword filters — with CC/SPCC opportunity flags and one-click handoff to Amazon Daily Deals.

How it saves you time, money, and headaches

  • Reuse content you’ve already made — matched to every ASIN that’s on a Prime Day deal.
  • See products you already own that are about to drop in price, so you can shoot content NOW before everyone else does.
  • Discover fresh deals by keyword + filter (min % off, max price, deal type) — not just whatever Amazon shows on the front page.
  • Every row pre-checked for Creator Connections and SPCC opportunities, on both the ASIN and its parent ASIN.
  • One click to queue any deal into Amazon Daily Deals — no copy-paste, no re-typing prices, no missed posts.
  • Auto-unlocks June 10 with a banner + toast nudge. Auto-locks July 1 so it doesn’t clutter the rest of the year.

What’s in the box

  • Storefront content reuse — joins your Storefront Butler harvest with current Prime Day deals
  • Owned-product lookahead — cross-references your Amazon order history to flag products you have on hand
  • Fresh deal discovery via Creators API search + filters (keyword, min % off, max price, deal type)
  • CC + SPCC opportunity flags on every row — both ASIN and parent ASIN
  • Existing-posts counter so you don’t double-post the same product
  • Sortable, filterable table styled like Orders Butler — export to CSV anytime
  • One-click handoff to Amazon Daily Deals’s scheduler — no parallel queue to manage
  • Seasonal banner + first-launch toast, dismissable, re-enable from Settings
