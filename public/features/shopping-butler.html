Without this

Open Amazon in one tab and Walmart in another, compare prices and ratings by hand, then guess which one is worth a video.

With Butler

One search covers both stores. The best product to buy, and the best one to feature, are sorted to the top for you.

How it saves you time, money, and headaches

  • Find the cheapest, best-rated option without tab-hopping between two stores.
  • See at a glance which product pays you the most to feature, not just which is popular.
  • Spot the open lane: a great product that few creators have covered yet.

What’s in the box

  • One search across Amazon and Walmart, with live prices and star ratings
  • Three ranking modes: Best to buy (cheap and good), Balanced, and Best to feature (most commission, least crowded)
  • Your estimated commission per sale, including any Creator Connections or SPCC uplift, shown per row
  • Saturation check that counts the real number of creator videos already on a product’s Amazon page
  • Same product from both stores collapses to one row, with an “also cheaper at” note
  • Switch ranking modes instantly, with no new search
  • Reads your commission rates from the tools you already use, so there’s nothing extra to set up
