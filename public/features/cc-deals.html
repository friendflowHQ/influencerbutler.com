Campaign Deals

See what’s on sale in the campaigns you’re already in. No generic deals feed, just your products, sorted by biggest discount.

Start free See all tools
Your campaigns, filtered to what’s on sale right now
Without this

Search a generic deals site, then cross-check by hand whether each discounted product is even in a campaign you joined. Most of what you find isn’t.

With Butler

Open Campaign Deals. Every row is a product you can already earn on, on sale right now, sorted by the biggest discount.

How it saves you time, money, and headaches

  • Post the discounts your audience actually clicks, while they’re still live.
  • No more cross-referencing a deals feed against your accepted campaigns by hand.
  • Catch a price drop the moment it lands, then turn it into a post.

What’s in the box

  • One table of every product on sale in the Creator Connections campaigns you’re in
  • Discount percent, sale price, original price, and when the deal ends, per item
  • Your commission rate shown next to each deal
  • Sorted by biggest discount first, with search by brand or ASIN
  • Refresh Deals button for an instant, browser-free price check
  • Optional schedule so the list refreshes on its own in the background
  • Include available toggle to also surface deals from campaigns you could still join
  • Reads from CC Check, so there’s nothing extra to set up

Watch the 3-minute walkthrough

Which of Your Products Are On Sale? Campaign Deals. Recorded inside the app and narrated start to finish.

Read the video transcript

Post it while it is cheap. A product on sale sells far better. Campaign Deals tells you which products in your campaigns are discounted right now, so you post them while the price is low.

Power User feature. Quick note on plans. Campaign Deals is a Power User feature. On the Beginner plan it is hidden from the sidebar. Upgrade any time in Settings, under Subscription.

Where the list comes from. It does not go hunting. It reuses the price data CC Check already collects through the Creators API, so if this screen is empty, run CC Check first.

Two numbers. Up top: how many products are on sale now, and how many products are in your campaigns in total. Last refreshed tells you how fresh the prices are.

Include available. Include available adds on-sale products from campaigns you could join but have not yet. A quick way to spot a discount worth accepting a campaign for.

Refresh Deals. Refresh Deals asks Amazon for today's prices. It only reads, no browser opens, and nothing is accepted. Press it and the numbers move.

Three sources in one table. The tabs split the sources: Creator Connections campaigns, EPC deals, which are Earn on Clicks offers with a forecast per click, and Promo codes.

The table. Each row: the product, percent off, sale price, what it was, when the deal ends, your commission, the EPC, and a promo code if there is one. Deal ends is the column to respect.

Filters. Filters narrow it: minimum percent off, minimum commission, minimum EPC, a max price, or only products with a promo or coupon. Sort by percent off puts the biggest discount first.

Search. Or search by brand or product code when you know what you are after.

Social bonus and AI Post. Tick Social bonus on a row to remember a brand that pays extra for a social post. AI Post in the Actions column drafts a post for that product right away.

Refresh on a timer. Set a start time and how often to repeat, then Enable Schedule, and the prices refresh on their own.

Next step. Your next step: once a week, press Refresh Deals, sort by percent off, and post whatever is cheapest with a deal still running.
