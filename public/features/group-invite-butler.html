Without this

You built a list of creator emails but inviting them into your Facebook group means pasting addresses one by one, watching the clock so Facebook doesn’t flag you, and losing your place every time you close the tab.

With Butler

Load your list once. Butler invites each person at a natural, spaced-out pace, remembers who it already invited, and picks up right where it left off. You walk away.

How it saves you time, money, and headaches

  • Grow a community around your storefront without a full afternoon of copy-paste.
  • Stay under Facebook’s radar with human-paced timing and daily caps you control.
  • Never invite the same person twice - Butler keeps a durable record of who it already reached.

What’s in the box

  • Two ways to fill the queue: hand off emails from the Instagram or Amazon Goldmine, or load your own CSV (one email per row in the first column)
  • Human pacing: set the seconds between invites plus a random extra so the cadence looks natural
  • Sending limits: cap invites per day and per run, or leave them open
  • Live queue: pending, sent, and failed counts update as it works
  • No duplicates: a durable invited record means already-invited people are skipped automatically, even after you clear the queue
  • Retry and redo: requeue failed addresses, or re-invite a sent batch, in one click
  • Watch it run: bring the browser to the front to see each invite go out, or let it work in the background
