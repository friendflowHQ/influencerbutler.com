What is Instagram Like Butler?

Instagram Like Butler is a free auto liker for Instagram. You list up to 10 accounts, and it opens each profile in a Chrome window on your own computer, likes their most recent posts and reels at a pace you set, and skips anything you already liked. It runs inside the Influencer Butler desktop app for Windows and Mac and stays free forever: no license, no quota.

The point is reciprocity, not gaming a number. When you like a creator's or brand's posts consistently, your handle keeps appearing in their activity, and that familiarity pays off when you later comment, send a DM, or pitch them.

What it does not do

Many people search for "auto likes" hoping to get more likes on their own posts. This is not that. Instagram Like Butler does not buy likes, add followers, or place likes on your own posts. It likes other accounts' posts so the people you want to reach notice you. If you want more engagement on your own content, start with why saves and shares beat likes and check where you stand with the free engagement rate calculator.

Is it safe? An honest answer

Instagram's rules restrict automated activity, and no tool can promise your account will never be limited. Instagram Like Butler is built to be conservative: per-account caps, human-length delays, small scheduled runs, and a guard that stops the run cleanly when Instagram shows a "please wait a few minutes" or challenge screen. Start with the defaults, keep runs small, and use it on an account you can afford to pause.

If Instagram does block a run, wait a few hours, raise your delay, and run again.

Auto liking Instagram: common questions

How do I auto like Instagram posts?

Install the Influencer Butler desktop app, open Instagram Like Butler, add the usernames you want to engage with, set a max likes per run and a delay between likes, and click Start. The app opens each profile in a Chrome window on your computer and likes the most recent posts and reels, skipping any you already liked. Turn on the built-in scheduler to repeat the run.

Is there a free auto liker for Instagram?

Yes. Instagram Like Butler is free forever and stays unlocked on every Influencer Butler account, including after a trial ends or a subscription is cancelled. You need the free desktop app for Windows or Mac.

Is auto liking on Instagram safe? Will my account get banned?

No tool can promise that. Instagram's rules restrict automated activity, and accounts that act too fast can be rate-limited or asked to verify. Instagram Like Butler uses per-account caps, human-length delays, small scheduled runs, and a stop-on-block guard. Start with the defaults, keep runs small, and use it on an account you can afford to pause.

Will this get more likes on my own posts?

Not directly. It likes other accounts' posts so your handle shows up in the activity of creators and brands you want to work with. It does not buy likes, add followers, or put likes on your own posts.

How many likes should I give per day?

Start at 10 likes per run with an 8 to 12 second delay, and run once a day or every 12 hours. Raise the number gradually. Delays under 5 seconds invite throttling, and running more often than every 12 hours rarely adds value.

Does it work on a phone?

No. It runs inside the Influencer Butler desktop app for Windows and Mac, using a Chrome window on your own computer. The app needs to be open when a scheduled run starts.

What is the difference between Instagram Like Butler and Like Butler?

Instagram Like Butler likes posts and reels on Instagram. Like Butler is a separate tool that auto-likes Amazon creator storefronts. Both are free forever.
