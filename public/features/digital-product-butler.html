Without this

You guess at what to make, spend weeks writing and designing it, pick a price out of thin air, and still have to set up checkout, files and a listing image before anyone can buy.

With Butler

Answer a few questions. Butler reads the best sellers in your niche, builds a free sample and a paid product, prices them against real listings, and publishes them to your Foyer page in one pass.

What it does

  • Searches Etsy for your niche and reads the best sellers: what they cost, how big the winning bundles are and which formats sell.
  • Decides the product type for you (ebook, printable idea cards, planner or checklist pack), or uses the one you pick.
  • Builds a free sample to grow your email list, a paid product and an optional post-purchase upsell.
  • Designs every page in your topic’s theme: checklists, trackers, countdown calendars, certificates, bingo boards, word searches and coloring pages.
  • Packages each product as a PDF and an EPUB with an AI cover, plus a Pinterest-ready listing image.
  • Publishes to your Foyer page with Stripe checkout, so sales land in your own Stripe balance.

How it works

  • Enter your niche and, optionally, your audience.
  • Pick a product type and a free sample style, or let AI decide.
  • Choose colors that match the topic or your Foyer page.
  • Review the product, tweak the title or price, or use Ask for changes to add pages.
  • Publish: the product tile appears on your Foyer page automatically.
