Influencer Butler vs Oink for influencers

Oink is a genuinely capable Chrome extension: a drawer of 40+ in-browser Amazon tools you operate one click at a time. Influencer Butler is a connected desktop system that does the work in the background and reaches past Amazon into Instagram, YouTube, and Pinterest. Here is an honest look at where each one fits.

Start your free 14-day Pro trial

The short version: a drawer of tools vs a system that connects them

Both products cover a lot of the same ground on paper, and Oink is not a weak tool. The real difference is shape. Oink lives in your browser and gives you 40+ tools you reach for individually: a cross-check here, an earnings tracker there, a bulk Facebook post when you have time. It speeds up your clicking. Influencer Butler is a desktop app that connects those jobs so they run without you sitting there: outreach becomes a tracked collab card automatically, the inbox tags when a product ships and what to film next, and deals auto-post across five social platforms on a schedule.

Put simply, an extension makes you click faster. A butler does the clicking for you and hands you the result.

Where Oink is genuinely good

Oink deserves credit. It is a capable extension with a mature tool set, a free tier, and a lower entry price at $29.99 per month. If you spend your day inside Amazon in a single browser tab and you are happy running each tool by hand, Oink gives you a lot for the money: cross-checks, an earnings tracker, a collaboration tracker, brand messaging, a campaign scan, bulk Facebook posting, and cross-posting to Canada, the UK, and Australia, plus an AI assistant on its Pro plan. For a creator who wants a fast, in-browser Swiss army knife, it is a reasonable pick.

Where Influencer Butler pulls ahead

The gap opens up once you want the work done for you rather than made faster. Because Influencer Butler is a desktop app, it keeps running in the background and joins the steps together:

Deals track themselves

A brand deal moves from outreach to a tracked collab card to shipped to filmed to paid without you updating a sheet.

Your inbox does triage

The unified Amazon inbox tags when a product ships and tells you what to film next.

Deals post everywhere

Auto-post your deals across Instagram, Threads, Facebook, Telegram, and Reddit on a schedule, not just bulk Facebook.

Past the edge of Amazon

Reach into Instagram outreach, YouTube cross-posting, and Pinterest, which a browser extension does not touch.

Auto-accept is a good example. In Influencer Butler the Daily Commission Butler accepts the right Creator Connections campaigns based on what you actually sold yesterday, then the earnings intelligence tools total what those campaigns earn. It is one of 40+ connected tools, not a standalone action you trigger and watch.

Frequently asked questions

Is Oink or Influencer Butler better for Amazon creators?

It depends on how you work. Oink is a capable Chrome extension with 40+ in-browser tools, a free tier, and a lower entry price at $29.99 per month. If you live inside Amazon in one tab and are happy running each tool by hand, it does a lot. Influencer Butler is a desktop app that connects the same kinds of tools so the work runs in the background, and it reaches past Amazon into Instagram, YouTube, and Pinterest. Both have a free way to start.

What does Oink cost compared to Influencer Butler?

As of our August 2026 review, Oink starts at $29.99 per month with a free tier. Influencer Butler has a free-forever tier with no card required and Pro at $39 per month or $390 per year (a 17% saving), with a 14-day Pro trial that unlocks all 40+ tools. Influencer Butler also gives away a free Chrome extension separate from the paid app.

Is Influencer Butler an extension like Oink?

No. Oink is a Chrome extension. Influencer Butler is a desktop app for Windows and Mac, plus a free companion Chrome extension. The desktop app keeps running in the background, so it can carry a brand deal from outreach to a tracked collab card to filmed to paid, and auto-post your deals across five social platforms, instead of you operating each tool by hand in a browser tab.

Can Influencer Butler auto-accept Creator Connections like Oink?

Influencer Butler auto-accepts both Creator Connections and Sponsored Products Creator Connections campaigns based on what you actually sold, through its Daily Commission Butler. As of our last review, Oink offers a Creator Connections scan in the browser. In Influencer Butler, auto-accept is one of 40+ connected tools rather than a standalone action.
