Cha-Ching Automate review: what it does well, and where it stops

Cha-Ching Automate is a desktop app that does one thing for Amazon influencers, and does it well: it takes your Amazon Influencer videos, syncs them to YouTube, and cross-posts them across 13+ international Amazon marketplaces with per-country translation and captions. This is a fair look at where that strength is real and where the tool stops.

3.9/ 5 for a focused, single-job video tool

What Cha-Ching Automate is

As of our last review (August 13, 2026), Cha-Ching Automate is a desktop app priced around $20 per month. Its whole focus is video distribution. It syncs your Amazon Influencer videos to YouTube and cross-posts them to 13+ international Amazon storefronts, auto-transcribing, translating, and captioning each in the local language. It also handles auto affiliate links, thumbnail sync, playlists, and scheduling. It is a single-purpose specialist, and it is priced like one.

Where Cha-Ching is genuinely good

Credit where it is due, and this is a real strength. Video is Cha-Ching's entire focus: it cross-posts to 13+ international Amazon marketplaces and auto-translates and captions in the local language, and it does that one job well at an accessible price. If a creator's single goal is getting one video seen across many Amazon marketplaces and on YouTube, in the right language, at scale, Cha-Ching is strong at exactly that. The multilingual angle is the headline: instead of one video reaching one market, you get localized versions working for you across regions with very little manual effort. That is worth paying for if international video reach is where your growth is.

The honest limits

The flip side of a focused tool is narrow coverage. As of our last review, Cha-Ching does video only. It does not do photo reload to international storefronts. It does not do brand outreach. It does not auto-accept Creator Connections or Sponsored Products campaigns. It does not track your brand collaborations, post deals to social platforms, or give you a unified Amazon inbox. None of that is a knock on what it set out to do: it just means Cha-Ching is one part of a larger workflow, and you would pair it with other tools to cover the rest.
