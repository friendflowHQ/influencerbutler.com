Cha-Ching Automate review: what it does well, and where it stops

Cha-Ching Automate is a desktop app that does one thing for Amazon influencers: it takes your Amazon Influencer videos, syncs them to YouTube, and cross-posts them across 13+ international Amazon marketplaces with per-country translation and captions. Amazon now does the international part on its own, so this is a fair look at what Cha-Ching still adds and where the tool stops.

3.4/ 5 for a focused video tool whose main edge Amazon now covers

Update, October 2026: Amazon now does this natively

Amazon now lets creators add their content to other country marketplaces automatically, right from their own account, with translation and captions handled for you. That was the headline reason to pay for Cha-Ching, and it is now a built-in Amazon feature at no extra cost. We lowered our rating from 3.9 to 3.4 because of it.

What this changes: if you subscribed to Cha-Ching mainly for international reach on Amazon, you should re-check whether you still need it. What it does not change: syncing videos to YouTube with affiliate links, thumbnails, and playlists is not something Amazon's feature does, so that part of Cha-Ching still stands. We have not compared Amazon's translation quality against Cha-Ching's for every language, so spot-check a few videos in the languages that matter most to you.

What Cha-Ching Automate is

As of our last review (August 13, 2026), Cha-Ching Automate is a desktop app priced around $20 per month. Its whole focus is video distribution. It syncs your Amazon Influencer videos to YouTube and cross-posts them to 13+ international Amazon storefronts, auto-transcribing, translating, and captioning each in the local language. It also handles auto affiliate links, thumbnail sync, playlists, and scheduling. It is a single-purpose specialist, and it is priced like one.

Where Cha-Ching is still good

Credit where it is due. Video is Cha-Ching's entire focus: it syncs your videos to YouTube with affiliate links, thumbnails, and playlists, and it cross-posts to 13+ international Amazon marketplaces with auto-translated captions in the local language, at an accessible price. The international half used to be the headline. Now that Amazon adds your videos to other country marketplaces automatically, it is less of a differentiator, and the YouTube sync is the clearer reason to keep it. If you want your videos on YouTube without doing it by hand, it still does that job well. If you only wanted localized versions on Amazon, check whether Amazon's own feature already covers you before paying.

The honest limits

The flip side of a focused tool is narrow coverage. As of our last review, Cha-Ching does video only. It does not do photo reload to international storefronts. It does not do brand outreach. It does not auto-accept Creator Connections or Sponsored Products campaigns. It does not track your brand collaborations, post deals to social platforms, or give you a unified Amazon inbox. None of that is a knock on what it set out to do: it just means Cha-Ching is one part of a larger workflow, and you would pair it with other tools to cover the rest.
