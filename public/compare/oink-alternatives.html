Oink alternatives for Amazon influencers in 2026

Oink is a popular Chrome extension for Amazon creators, but it is not the only way to get in-browser product intel, scan Creator Connections, and track your earnings. This roundup covers the real alternatives, what each one is good at, and where an all-in-one desktop app does the parts a browser extension leaves to you.

What people actually want from an Oink alternative

Most creators searching for an Oink alternative are chasing one of three jobs. First, in-browser Amazon intel: seeing how many influencer videos a product already has, spotting content gaps, and knowing at a glance whether something is worth filming. Second, Creator Connections scanning: catching the campaigns that match what you sell so you do not miss a time-limited brief. Third, earnings tracking: knowing which products actually pay you. A good alternative should nail at least one of those, and the best ones connect all three instead of leaving you to stitch tools together.

In-browser intel

Video counts, content gaps, and a storefront check while you shop on Amazon.

Creator Connections

Scanning, or better, auto-accepting the campaigns that fit what you actually sold.

Earnings tracking

Lifetime earnings per product so you double down on winners, not guesses.

Cost

A free tier or trial so you can test it on your own account before you pay.

Where Oink is genuinely good

Credit where it is due. As of our last review (August 13, 2026), Oink is a genuinely capable Chrome extension. It packs 40+ in-browser Amazon tools including cross-checks, an earnings tracker, a collaboration tracker, brand messaging, auto-scan, bulk Facebook posting, and Canada, UK, and Australia cross-posting, plus an "Agent Oink" AI assistant on its Pro plan. It has a free tier and a lower entry price, listed around $29.99 per month. If you basically live inside Amazon in one browser tab and want a mature drawer of tools you operate yourself, Oink does a lot for the money. The trade-off is that it is a set of separate tools you drive one click at a time, and it stays inside the browser.

How to choose

If you want to stay entirely inside your browser and drive the tools yourself, Oink is a solid, lower-cost pick with a free tier.
