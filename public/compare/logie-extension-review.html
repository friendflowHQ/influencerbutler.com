Logie extension review: the AI Amazon tool, honestly assessed

Logie's browser extension is one of the more ambitious AI tools for Amazon creators. Its image generation is genuinely impressive and its Inbox CRM is real. But nearly every action is metered by credits and your Opportunity Score is capped by plan tier. This is a fair look at both sides.

3.8/ 5 strong AI, held back by credit metering

One clarification up front: this review is about Logie's browser extension as a software competitor. Logie is also an affiliate network, and Influencer Butler integrates with it as a public integration partner. Comparing the extension does not change that: Butler still pulls brand and program signals from partners like Logie into its pipelines.

What Logie's browser extension is

As of our last review (August 13, 2026), Logie is an AI-first Amazon suite delivered as a browser extension plus a web portal, with a free tier and paid plans up to around $166 per month. It packs a lot: X-Ray Super Search, an Opportunity Score and Sales Rank, Creator Connections visibility and reminders, a brand Inbox CRM, sample requests, AI shoppable photos, collages, and thumbnails, AI Pinterest posts, auto-publish to YouTube, Pinterest, and Amazon Canada and UK, smart deep links, and weekly Zoom training. The defining detail is that nearly everything runs on credits, and the Opportunity Score is capped by your plan tier.

Where Logie is genuinely good

Credit where it is due, and this is real. Logie's AI image generation, its shoppable photos, collages, and thumbnails, is a distinctive strength that Influencer Butler does not have today. If turning a product into scroll-stopping shoppable imagery matters to your content, that alone can justify a look. Beyond images, its brand Inbox CRM and its weekly live Zoom training are legitimate value too: the CRM keeps brand conversations in one place, and recurring live training is a real support perk that many tools do not offer. These are not throwaway features, and an honest review names them plainly.

The honest limits: credits and the score cap

Here is the catch a fair review has to name. As of our last review, Logie meters nearly every action by credits, and it caps your Opportunity Score by plan tier. The free browser intel tops out at 60 out of 100, which means the number the tool is built around is deliberately limited until you pay up a tier. Credit metering has the same effect across the suite: the more you use the AI features, the more the meter runs. That is a reasonable business model, but it changes how it feels day to day, and it is the opposite of an uncapped, no-meter free tool.
