Specialists vs the suite

Cha-Ching and Logie's extension are excellent at their chosen slice of the job. Cha-Ching is a video specialist. Logie's extension is an AI image and research specialist. A quick note on Logie, because it matters. Logie also runs an affiliate network, and that network is a public integration partner, not a competitor. Everything on this page compares Logie's browser extension specifically, and Influencer Butler works happily alongside the Logie network.

Influencer Butler

The connected suite. It reloads both videos and photos, auto-accepts campaigns, tracks deals end to end, and posts across five platforms, and its browser extension is free and uncapped with no credits and no score cap.

Frequently asked questions

What does Cha-Ching Automate do?

Cha-Ching Automate is a video specialist. It syncs your Amazon Influencer videos to YouTube and cross-posts them to 13+ international Amazon marketplaces, auto-transcribing, translating, and captioning per country, with auto affiliate links, thumbnail sync, playlists, and scheduling. For multilingual video-to-YouTube at scale it is strong, and it is priced accessibly at around $20 per month.

How is Logie's extension different from Influencer Butler?

Logie's browser extension is AI-first: X-Ray search, an Opportunity Score capped at 60 out of 100 on the free tier, AI shoppable photos and thumbnails, and other features that are credit-metered. Its AI image generation is a real, unique strength that Influencer Butler does not have today. Influencer Butler's browser extension is free and uncapped, with no credits and no score cap, and it feeds a desktop suite of 40+ connected tools. Note that Logie also runs an affiliate network, which is a public integration partner, not a competitor; this comparison is specifically about Logie's browser extension.

Which tool should I choose?

If your only goal is multilingual video-to-YouTube at scale, Cha-Ching is strong at that one thing. If you want AI-generated shoppable images, Logie's extension leads there. If you want the whole workflow in one connected desktop app, with both video and photo reloading, deal tracking, and multi-platform posting, plus a free uncapped browser extension, Influencer Butler is built for that.

Does Influencer Butler reload both videos and photos?

Yes. Influencer Butler has a Video Reload Butler that re-uploads storefront videos across Amazon marketplaces with translated titles and captions, and a Photo Reload Butler that re-posts storefront photos to Canada, the UK, Australia, and Singapore. Cha-Ching focuses on video only, and Logie's extension does not reload deleted videos, so you would need several tools to match one Butler.
