Oink vs Campaign Finder vs Influencer Butler

Auto-accepting Amazon Creator Connections campaigns is a niche of its own, and three tools approach it differently. Oink folds a campaign scan into a big in-browser toolkit. Campaign Finder is a Chrome extension focused on the auto-accept job. Influencer Butler is a desktop app where auto-accept is one of 40+ connected tools. Here is a fair look at all three.

Why auto-accept matters

Creator Connections is the program where brands invite creators to make paid product videos. Some campaigns are time-limited or fill quickly, so being at your screen at the right moment can be the difference between a paid brief and a missed one. Auto-accept tools watch for campaigns that match your criteria and accept them for you. All three tools here can help with that. The question is what else you get, and how much of the rest of the workflow you still have to do by hand.

Frequently asked questions

What is Creator Connections auto-accept?

Amazon Creator Connections is the program where brands invite creators to make paid product videos. Campaigns can be time-limited, so auto-accept tools watch for matching campaigns and accept them for you, so you never miss a paid brief while you are away from your screen.

How do Oink, Campaign Finder, and Influencer Butler differ on auto-accept?

Oink is a Chrome extension that includes a Creator Connections scan among its 40+ in-browser tools. Campaign Finder, as of 2026 and based on its public positioning, is a Chrome extension focused on auto-accepting Amazon Creator Connections campaigns. Influencer Butler is a desktop app where auto-accept, through its Daily Commission Butler, is one of 40+ connected tools and accepts campaigns based on what you actually sold.

Is a focused auto-accept extension enough, or do I need more?

A focused extension is great if auto-accept is the only job you want. Many creators find that accepting a campaign is only step one, and they still need outreach, deal tracking, commission totals, content reloading, and posting. Influencer Butler bundles those into one desktop app so auto-accept connects to the rest of the workflow instead of standing alone.
