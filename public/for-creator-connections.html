Frequently asked questions

Is this different from the offsite affiliate-link tools?

Yes. The Creator Connections cockpit is built for onsite creators who earn through Amazon's Creator Connections and SPCC programs rather than offsite affiliate links. It auto-accepts campaigns, scores the grid with fill meters and Last Call alerts, and gives a Butler's Brief verdict on what to film next. You can use it alongside the offsite tools if you do both.

Do I need the desktop app or just the Chrome extension?

The Chrome extension carries the Campaign Radar overlay, fill meters, Last Call watch bells, and Idea List Butler directly on the Creator Connections grid, free. The desktop app adds auto-accept scheduling, the Butler's Brief verdict, and CC Check across your whole campaign list.

What is the Butler's Brief?

The Butler's Brief is a plain-language verdict on a Creator Connections campaign: whether it is worth your time, what to film, and why, based on the campaign's rate, fill, and history.
