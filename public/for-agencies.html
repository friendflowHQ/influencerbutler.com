Frequently asked questions

How many creators can my agency manage on one plan?

Seats are per device. Pro Team covers up to 10 devices for $129 per month and Pro Agency covers up to 25 devices for $299 per month, roughly one seat per creator you manage.

What is the design-partner offer with 3 free months?

Do our creators each get their own account?

Yes. Each creator runs the desktop app on their own machine and connects their own Amazon, Instagram, and social accounts. Your agency holds the plan and its shared seat pool.
