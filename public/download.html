The top bar. The top bar is always there. Your hourly rate, the hours and money the butlers have saved you, your storefront id, and whether automations are paused.

Butlers live in the sidebar. Every butler lives in the sidebar, grouped by platform: Amazon, Walmart, Deals, Facebook, Instagram and more. Click a group name to open it.

The Dashboard is home. The Dashboard is home. It shows what is running, what is scheduled today, and your numbers, all in one place.

Your butlers at a glance. Lower down, one card per butler with its last run. Click a card to open that butler.
