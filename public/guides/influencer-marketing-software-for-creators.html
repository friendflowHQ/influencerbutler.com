Search "influencer marketing software" and nearly every result is built for the other side of the table: platforms that help brands find, pay, and measure creators. Useful, but you are the product in those tools, not the customer. What the creator's side actually needs

Being listed on brand-side platforms is passive income theater until a brand happens to pick you. The deals creators actually close come from being active, and that activity is exactly what software can carry:

Win retail media campaigns

Amazon Creator Connections pays commissions boosts for campaigns you accept. CC Check and Daily Commission Butler make sure the right ones never expire unanswered.

Find brands already spending

Goldmine Butler scans other creators' storefronts for disclosed partnership content; Instagram Goldmine finds brands running paid partnerships in your niche. These brands have budgets today.

Pitch and follow up

Pitch Butler is one CRM for every brand pipeline. Connect Gmail, send pitches, and let it track replies instead of a spreadsheet.

Prove your numbers

Orders Butler pulls your real commission history, so your media kit claims come from data, not vibes.

Keep earning between deals

Deals Butler posts commission-earning deals to your audience on a schedule, so sponsorships are a bonus, not the whole income.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between brand-side and creator-side influencer marketing software?

Brand-side software helps companies discover creators, run campaigns, and measure results; the brand is the customer. Creator-side software works for the influencer: it surfaces paid campaigns you qualify for, finds brands already paying creators in your niche, sends and tracks your pitches, and automates the posting work that earns commissions.

As a creator, do I need a brand-side platform account?

Often yes, but being listed in marketplaces is passive. The deals most creators actually close come from being active: accepting retail media campaigns like Amazon Creator Connections quickly, and pitching brands directly. Creator-side software automates both.

How does software help me get brand deals?

Three ways. Goldmine-style research finds brands already paying creators in your niche (by scanning for disclosed partnership content and sponsorship signals). A pitch CRM sends your outreach and tracks replies through Gmail. And campaign tools accept the retail media campaigns you already qualify for before they expire.

What does creator-side influencer marketing software cost?

Influencer Butler bundles the creator-side stack (campaign auto-accept, brand research, pitch CRM, deal posting, content recycling) for $39 per month or $390 per year, with a free-forever tier and a 14-day Pro trial. Brand-side platforms often cost hundreds to thousands per month because their customer is a company.

Is there software for agencies that manage creators?

Yes. Agencies and managers run Influencer Butler across their roster with multi-seat plans, so each creator's campaigns, pitches, and deal posting run under one roof. See the agencies page for details.
