Free influencer software in 2026: what you get without paying

"Free" in this space usually means a seven-day trial with a card on file. This page is the other kind: software that stays free. One desktop tier, one Chrome extension, a hub of web calculators, and an 11-part course, none of which ask for a card. Works on both Windows and Mac.

The free-forever desktop tier: 6 butlers

Influencer Butler's free tier is not a trial. These six butlers work indefinitely on any account, with no card required:

Orders Butler

Pulls your real Amazon purchase and commission history so you can see what actually sells instead of guessing what to film.

Storefront Butler

Syncs photos, videos, idea lists, and media collections from your storefront into one searchable feed for coverage audits.

CC Check

Checks your ASINs against live Creator Connections catalogs, and can accept campaigns and submit content from one results table.

Like Butler

Auto-likes brand Creator Connections storefront content on a cadence so brands keep seeing your name.

Benable Like Butler

Auto-likes other creators' Benable collections on a schedule with caps and delays.

Instagram Like Butler

Auto-likes Instagram posts on a schedule with the same safety caps.

The free Chrome extension: intel while you browse

The companion Chrome extension is free and needs no login. While you browse Amazon it shows influencer versus brand video counts on product pages, content gaps in your own orders, storefront checkups, a Creator Connections campaign radar with fill meters, a money-first search overlay, and per-product earnings badges. It works in any Chrome browser on Windows or Mac.

Free web tools and the free course

Two more things cost nothing and need no download. The Free Tools hub has browser-based calculators for creators, and the free 11-part Amazon Influencer course walks you from applying to the program through filming, storefront building, and reading your reports, sized for a few hours a week with zero experience assumed.

Frequently asked questions

Is there actually free influencer software, or just trials?

Both exist, and the difference matters. Influencer Butler's free tier is free forever with no card required: 6 See and Organize butlers plus a free companion Chrome extension. The 14-day Pro trial is separate and only matters when you want automation.

What do the 6 free butlers do?

Orders Butler pulls your real Amazon purchase and commission history. Storefront Butler syncs your photos, videos, and idea lists into one searchable feed. CC Check checks your ASINs against live Creator Connections catalogs. Like Butler, Benable Like Butler, and Instagram Like Butler auto-like content on a schedule with caps and delays to keep you visible.

What does the free Chrome extension show?

Without any login it shows influencer versus brand video counts on Amazon product pages, content gaps in your own orders, storefront checkups, a Creator Connections campaign radar with fill meters, a money-first search overlay, and per-product earnings badges.

Are there free web tools too?

Yes. The Free Tools hub at influencerbutler.com/tools has free browser-based calculators for creators, no download or account needed, alongside the free 11-part Amazon Influencer course.

What is the catch?

The free tier covers seeing and organizing: what sells, what has campaigns, what your storefront covers. The automation (auto-accepting campaigns, posting deals on a schedule, brand outreach, content recycling) is Pro, which has a 14-day trial and costs $39 per month or $390 per year after.
