How to auto accept Amazon Creator Connections

Creator Connections campaigns are paid and time-limited, and the good ones fill up fast. This guide shows you how to auto-accept the right campaigns on your own criteria, so you stop refreshing the page and stop leaving commission on the table.

What Amazon Creator Connections is

Amazon Creator Connections is the program where brands invite Amazon Influencer Program creators to make paid product videos. A brand posts a campaign with a set commission, a limited number of slots, and a deadline. You accept the ones that fit your storefront, film the content, and earn on the products the campaign covers. Because slots are capped and campaigns expire, the difference between catching a brief and missing it is often just who saw it first.

Why auto-accepting matters

Manually watching Creator Connections is a losing game. New campaigns appear at all hours, the best ones fill within minutes, and you cannot refresh the page while you sleep, film, or work a day job. Every campaign you match but miss is commission you will never see. Auto-accepting flips that: you define what a good campaign looks like once, and the right briefs get accepted the moment they match, day or night.

Step by step: auto accept Creator Connections

Influencer Butler is a desktop app for Amazon creators that runs on both Windows and Mac. Daily Commission Butler is the tool that does the auto-accepting. Here is the full setup.

1. Install the desktop app

Download the app from the download page and install it.

  • On Windows: run the downloaded installer, then open Influencer Butler from the Start menu. It keeps running in the system tray so butlers work in the background.
  • On Mac: open the downloaded .dmg, drag Influencer Butler into your Applications folder, then launch it from Launchpad. It sits in the menu bar so butlers keep running.

2. Connect your Amazon Influencer account

Sign in to your Amazon Influencer account inside the app. This lets it read your storefront, your order history, and the live Creator Connections catalog for your account. Nothing is accepted yet, this just gives the butlers accurate signal to work from.

3. Set your acceptance criteria

Decide what a campaign has to look like before it is accepted for you: a minimum commission rate, brand or keyword filters, and a daily cap on how many campaigns to accept. The daily cap is what keeps pacing sensible instead of accepting a flood at once.

4. Let Daily Commission Butler auto-accept

Turn on Daily Commission Butler. It auto-accepts the right Creator Connections campaigns based on what you actually sold yesterday, so it favors products with real demand on your account instead of accepting everything indiscriminately. Prefer to review before anything is accepted? Use CC Check to check your ASINs against the live catalogs and accept from a results table by hand.

5. Track the commissions

Let Orders Butler harvest your real Amazon purchase and commission history. Now you can see which accepted campaigns actually earned, and feed that back into what you film next. Orders Butler is free, so you can track earnings even without a Pro plan.

Stay within Amazon's terms and pace safely

Automation is a convenience, not a loophole. You are responsible for following the Amazon Influencer Program and Creator Connections terms on your own account. The app runs locally under your own sign-in and paces actions with a daily cap and human-like delays rather than firing off requests as fast as possible. Set a cap you are comfortable with, keep an eye on what gets accepted, and only accept campaigns you can genuinely deliver content for. The goal is to stop missing good briefs, not to accept more than you can fulfil.
