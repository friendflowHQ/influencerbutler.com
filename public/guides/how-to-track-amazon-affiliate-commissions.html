Guide: earnings tracking

How to track Amazon affiliate commissions

Most creators film, post, and hope. The ones who grow know exactly which products earn. This guide shows you a free way to harvest your Amazon commission history and see which ASINs pay, so you can film more of what works and stop guessing.

Start your free 14-day Pro trial

Why tracking commissions changes everything

Amazon pays you a commission on the products people buy after watching your videos, but the earnings are spread across dozens or hundreds of ASINs. Without a clear view, you cannot tell whether your best video is carrying your whole account or a handful of quiet products are quietly outperforming your favorites. Once you can see which products earn, every filming decision gets easier: you double down on winners and stop pouring hours into content that never converts.

The free way to do it

You do not need a paid plan to track commissions. Orders Butler is a free-forever butler and works on any account, even a lapsed one. Here is the full workflow.

1. Install the desktop app

Download the app from the download page and install it.

  • On Windows: run the downloaded installer, then open Influencer Butler from the Start menu. It stays in the system tray so harvesting can run in the background.
  • On Mac: open the downloaded .dmg, drag Influencer Butler into your Applications folder, then launch it from Launchpad. It sits in the menu bar while it works.

2. Connect your Amazon account

Sign in to your Amazon Influencer account inside the app so it can read your storefront and your order history. This is what gives the butlers accurate ASIN signal to work from.

3. Harvest your orders and commissions

Run Orders Butler. It pulls your real Amazon purchase and commission history into one searchable table, so the rest of the app, and you, are working from real numbers instead of guesses. Orders Butler is free.

4. Read Earnings Intelligence to find best-earning ASINs

Open Earnings Intelligence. It takes the harvested data and surfaces your best-earning products, so the ASINs quietly making you money rise to the top instead of hiding in a spreadsheet. This is where "which products earn" stops being a mystery.

5. Act on it and film more of what earns

Now the data pays for itself. Film more content for the products that already convert, and refresh or retag content for the ones that have gone quiet. Your effort follows the money instead of your gut. Pair this with Orders Butler feeding a content calendar and you have a simple, repeatable loop: track, rank, film, repeat.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track Amazon affiliate commissions?

Connect your Amazon Influencer account to Influencer Butler and run Orders Butler, which pulls your real purchase and commission history into one searchable table. Then read Earnings Intelligence to see which products and ASINs earn the most. Orders Butler is on the free-forever tier.

How can I see which Amazon products earn the most?

After Orders Butler harvests your commission history, Earnings Intelligence sorts it so your best-earning ASINs rise to the top. That tells you exactly which products to film more of and which content to refresh or retag.

Is commission tracking free?

Yes. Orders Butler is a free-forever butler and works on any account, even a lapsed one. Influencer Butler also has a free Pro trial with no auto-charge if you want the Pro tools, but you can track commissions on the free tier. See the pricing page.

Does it work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Influencer Butler is a desktop app for both Windows and Mac. The install and launch steps differ slightly by operating system, but the commission-tracking workflow is the same on both.

Why not just read Amazon's own reports?

Amazon's reports show the numbers but leave the analysis to you. Orders Butler harvests the same history into a searchable table, and Earnings Intelligence ranks your best-earning ASINs automatically, so you spend time acting on the data instead of reconciling spreadsheets.
