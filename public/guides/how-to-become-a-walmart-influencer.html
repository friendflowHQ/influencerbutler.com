How to become a Walmart influencer in 2026

Walmart is where a lot of your audience already shops, and the Walmart Creator program lets you earn commissions on the products you feature. This guide walks you through the whole path: how the program works, how to pick Walmart products that actually sell using a free Chrome extension, and how to reuse the Amazon storefront content you already have on Walmart, without filming it twice. Works on both Windows and Mac.

What the Walmart Creator program is

The Walmart Creator program gives you a creator storefront and lets you earn a commission when someone buys through your links. Commission rates vary by category, and the same creator habits that work on other retailers work here: feature things people genuinely want, be honest, and post consistently. You apply through Walmart's own creator portal; approval and payout are handled on Walmart's side. What Influencer Butler adds is everything around that: knowing which products are worth your time, tracking whether a deal is real, and getting your storefront built without hours of extra filming.

New to affiliate creating in general? The free 11-part influencer course covers the fundamentals of filming, storefronts, and reading your numbers. The skills carry straight over to Walmart.

The path, step by step

  1. Apply to the Walmart Creator program. Sign up through Walmart's creator portal to get your creator storefront and your commission links. There is no cost to apply.
  2. Add the free Chrome extension. Install the free Influencer Butler extension. It needs no account and turns walmart.com into a research tool while you shop.
  3. Find products worth featuring. Browse Walmart normally. On every product and search page you get money signals: price, ratings, an estimated commission, a monthly-revenue estimate, and a Butler Score, so you back winners instead of guessing.
  4. Build your Walmart storefront fast. If you already sell on Amazon, use Walmart Butler's Walmart Repost to copy your existing storefront videos and photos to Walmart Creator on an exact barcode match, hands-free.
  5. Track and refresh. Let Walmart Research keep price, rank, and sales history on the products you feature so you can re-post real deals and drop the duds.

Pick products that sell: money signals while you browse

The hardest part of any storefront is knowing what to feature. The free Influencer Butler Chrome extension reads the walmart.com page you already opened and puts the money signals right on it, so you never guess. It works with no login in Chrome, Edge, or Brave, and it does not run in the background, it only reads the page you are viewing.

Butler Score and seal

Each Walmart product gets a 0 to 100 Butler Score and the Butler Approved seal, a quick read on whether it is worth featuring.

Commission and revenue

An estimated commission plus a pooled monthly-revenue estimate, so you can see the earning potential before you film.

Price, ratings, break-even

Current price, rating and review counts, an out-of-stock warning, and a break-even calculator on the product page.

Search and browse grids

The same scoring rides along on Walmart search, browse, and rollback pages, so whole result grids get ranked at a glance.

A quick note on how deep this goes: the Walmart overlays are money-signal panels and scored grids. Some Amazon-only tools (influencer video counts, Creator Connections campaign intel, and per-product earnings from the desktop ledger) do not apply to Walmart. Copy-link builds a working Walmart link; connecting your Walmart Creator account is what makes it a commission-tracked one.

Reuse your Amazon storefront on Walmart, hands-free

If you are already an Amazon influencer, you have a library of storefront videos and photos. Rebuilding all of that on Walmart by hand is the reason most creators never start. Walmart Butler's Walmart Repost copies your Amazon storefront videos and photos to your Walmart Creator storefront automatically, but only when it is the exact same item, matched by UPC or GTIN barcode, so you never post a mismatched product. A setup wizard signs you in once, then lets you review each match first or post automatically, cap how many post per run, skip out-of-stock items, and schedule daily, every three days, or weekly.

Its twin, Walmart Research, keeps price, rank, estimated monthly sales, and stock history for every Walmart product the app touches, and refreshes it daily, so a real drop or a rising trend is easy to spot. Both live inside Walmart Butler, part of the desktop app.

The free Influencer Butler Chrome extension puts money signals right on walmart.com product and search pages: price, ratings, an estimated commission, a pooled monthly-revenue estimate, a Butler Score with the Butler Approved seal, a break-even calculator, and a one-click copy-link button.
