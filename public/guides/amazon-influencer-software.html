Amazon influencer software: the 2026 guide

The Amazon Influencer Program rewards consistency: answering campaigns before they expire, posting deals while they are live, and keeping your storefront content working in more than one marketplace. Software exists for exactly those jobs. Here is what Amazon influencer software actually does in 2026, and where to start without paying anything. Works on both Windows and Mac.

Two kinds of software, one workflow

Modern Amazon influencer software comes in two shapes that work together. A desktop app runs scheduled automations ("butlers") in the background: accepting campaigns, posting deals, re-uploading videos. A browser extension adds live intel while you shop and research: influencer versus brand video counts on product pages, content gaps in your own orders, campaign radar with fill meters, and per-product earnings badges. Influencer Butler ships both: the desktop app for Windows and Mac, and a free Chrome extension that needs no login.

Frequently asked questions

What is Amazon influencer software?

Amazon influencer software automates the repetitive parts of earning in the Amazon Influencer Program: checking your products against live Creator Connections campaigns and accepting the right ones, posting deals to your audience on a schedule, re-uploading storefront videos to other marketplaces, and finding and pitching the brands behind your best sellers.

Can software really auto-accept Creator Connections campaigns?

Yes. Influencer Butler's CC Check checks your ASINs against live Creator Connections catalogs and can accept campaigns and submit content from one results table, and Daily Commission Butler auto-accepts the right campaigns based on what you actually sold yesterday, so paid campaigns stop expiring unanswered.

Can my storefront videos earn in other countries?

Yes. Video Reload Butler re-encodes and re-uploads your storefront videos across 13 Amazon marketplaces with translated titles and local-language captions, and Photo Reload Butler re-posts storefront photos to Canada, the UK, Australia, and Singapore with fresh tags.

Do I need to pay to try Amazon influencer software?

No. Influencer Butler has a free-forever tier with 6 See and Organize butlers, a free companion Chrome extension that needs no login, and a free 11-part Amazon Influencer course. Pro automation comes with a 14-day trial.

Does it run on Windows and Mac?

Yes. The desktop app runs on both Windows and Mac, and the Chrome extension works in any Chrome browser. Many creators also run the app on an always-on cloud PC so butlers keep working while the laptop is closed.
