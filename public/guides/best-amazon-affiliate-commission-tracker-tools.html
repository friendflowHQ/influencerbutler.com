The real pain: no visibility into what earns

Amazon earnings reports pay you, but they do not connect each payout back to the specific video, post, or product that drove it. So you know your total is up or down, but not why. Which ASINs are carrying you? Which content was a waste of a filming afternoon? Without that link you are flying blind, and you end up making more of the wrong products. A good commission tracker restores the connection between the money and the product, so your next filming session is aimed at what works.

Frequently asked questions

Why is tracking Amazon affiliate commissions so hard?

Amazon reports pay you a commission but do not clearly connect each payout back to the video, post, or product that earned it. Without that link you cannot tell which ASINs actually make money, so you end up guessing what to film next. A tracker that harvests and totals your commissions restores that visibility.

What is the best way to track Amazon influencer commissions?

The most complete option is an all-in-one desktop app. Influencer Butler's Orders Butler pulls your real Amazon purchase and commission history so the app has accurate ASIN signal, and Earnings Intelligence totals commissions and surfaces your best-earning products. Because Orders Butler is free forever, commission visibility does not cost anything.

Can I just use a spreadsheet?

You can, and many creators start there, but a spreadsheet is manual reconciliation. It cannot pull your history automatically, it goes stale the moment you stop updating it, and it will not rank your ASINs by what earns. A tool that harvests the data does the tedious part for you and keeps it current.

Does commission tracking cost money?

In Influencer Butler the core commission visibility is free. Orders Butler is one of the free-forever butlers, so you can pull your Amazon order and commission history and see the totals without a card. Pro adds the deeper Earnings Intelligence workflow and the rest of the 40+ tools.

How do I find which products actually earn?

Once Orders Butler has harvested your real order and commission history, Earnings Intelligence totals it and spots your best-earning products. That tells you which ASINs to make more content for and which to stop spending time on, instead of treating every product as equal.
