The first three tools that matter

You do not need 40 tools on day one. You need three, and all three are free. Here is the order most beginners find natural.

1. CC Check (free)

Grab every ASIN from any page and check them against the live Creator Connections catalogs. This is how you learn which of your products have paid campaigns available.

2. Orders Butler (free)

Pull your real Amazon purchase and commission history so you can see what actually sells, instead of guessing what to film next.

3. Storefront Butler (free)

Sync your photos, videos, idea lists, and media collections into one searchable feed so you can audit your coverage and spot gaps.

Feature pages: CC Check, Orders Butler, and Storefront Butler. The free Chrome extension rounds this out by showing influencer versus brand video counts, content gaps, and storefront checkups while you browse Amazon.
