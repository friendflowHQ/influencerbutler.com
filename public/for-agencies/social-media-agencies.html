Frequently asked questions

How would a social media agency use Influencer Butler?

Agencies use it two ways. Some run it for creators and clients as a done-for-you affiliate service, handling brand outreach, links, and commission tracking on their behalf. Others give each creator on the roster a seat so the repetitive affiliate work runs in the background. Either way it is one plan with a shared seat pool.

How many seats do we get?

Pro Team covers up to 10 devices for $129 per month and Pro Agency covers up to 25 devices for $299 per month. Each device is one seat, so you can cover a team, a client roster, or both, and add more as you grow.

What is the design-partner offer with 3 free months?

Do we need to be an Amazon expert to use it?

No. The tools automate the mechanics of Amazon Creator Connections, affiliate links, and deal posting, and the free Chrome extension surfaces the intel. Your agency brings the creators and the strategy, and Influencer Butler handles the repetitive execution.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month to month with no long-term contract, and annual billing is available at roughly 17% off. You can start on a free trial with no card required.
