If you have heard creators talk about earning from recommendation lists and want in, this is the walkthrough for you. Learning how to make money on Benable is genuinely beginner-friendly, which is rare in the affiliate world. There are no applications to a dozen brands, no waiting weeks for approval, no hand-pasting tracking URLs. You build a list, share it, and you earn when people shop it. That's the whole loop. Let's walk through it start to finish so you can have your first earning list live this week.

Quick promise before we start: you do not need a big following to begin. You need a few products you genuinely like and a willingness to write a sentence or two about why. That's it. Your first commission will probably come from a list of stuff you already own and recommend in DMs for free.

## Step 1: Get set up on Benable

[Benable](https://benable.com/i/CJAQK) is an invite-only recommendation app, so your first step is getting in and creating your profile. Once you're in, the setup is light: pick a username, write a short bio that says who you are and what you recommend (beauty, home, travel, parenting, whatever your thing is), and add a profile photo. That's it. You are not configuring anything technical.

The part that makes Benable beginner-friendly is what happens automatically behind the scenes:

- **You're approved for 35,000+ brands instantly.** Nike, Sephora, Wayfair, eBay, Ulta, and tens of thousands more, no per-brand application.
- **Affiliate links are created for you.** When you add a product to a list, Benable generates the tracking link automatically.
- **You keep the full commission.** Benable takes no cut. Your earnings typically land between 2% and 15% depending on the brand.

So the scary, tedious parts of affiliate marketing are just handled. Your job is the fun part: deciding what to recommend.

## Step 2: Build your first list (start with what you already love)

Do not overthink this. The best first list is a collection of products you already own and already recommend to friends. Think about the last time someone asked "where did you get that" and answer it with a list.

Good beginner list ideas:

- **My everyday essentials** (the things you reach for daily)
- **What's in my [bag / kitchen / shower] right now**
- **Gifts under $50 for [a specific person]**
- **Beginner gear for [your hobby]**

Add five to eight products. For each one, write one or two real sentences: what it is, who it's for, and why you love it. This is the single most important habit for earning, so don't skip it. A list of bare product names earns far less than the same list with a little honest context under each pick. People shop your judgment, so show them your reasoning.

> The mental model: every list you build is a tiny, evergreen storefront. It keeps earning long after you publish it, every time someone discovers it and buys.

## Step 3: Title it so people can find it

Your list title is doing double duty as a headline and as SEO. Title it the way a real person would search for it. "Gifts under $50 for new dads" beats "gift ideas" because it matches what someone actually types into a search bar. A clear, specific title is the difference between a list that gets discovered for months and one that nobody ever sees.

A simple formula that works: **[who it's for] + [what they want] + [a qualifier]**. For example, "Cozy gifts for the homebody who has everything." Say it out loud. If it sounds like a question someone would ask a friend, you nailed it.

## Step 4: Share it where your people already are

Once your list is live, get it in front of people two ways.

First, **share it directly.** Drop the list link in your Instagram bio, in your stories, in a TikTok caption, in a group chat where someone asked for recommendations. Your existing audience is the fastest path to your first commission.

Second, **let discovery do the slow work.** Your lists are findable on Benable itself and through search engines, so a well-titled list keeps pulling in new shoppers long after you post it. That second stream is the one that compounds. The more lists you build and the more active you are on the platform, the more discovery you earn.

## Step 5: Repeat and let it compound

One list is a start, not a business. The earners build steadily: a few good lists a month, each focused on a specific person and question, each freshened when they find something better. Over a few months you go from one list to a library, and the library quietly earns while you sleep.
