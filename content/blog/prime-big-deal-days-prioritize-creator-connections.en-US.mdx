Two products can sell for the same price, take the same effort to film, and pay you wildly different amounts. During Prime Big Deal Days, when your time is the scarce resource, which deals you lead with matters as much as how many you post.

The move is simple: give your best slots to the deals that pay above the standard rate.

## Know the three tiers you are working with

- **Standard storefront commission.** The baseline category rate. Fine, but it is the floor.
- **Storefront Commission bonuses.** Amazon periodically boosts the rate on certain products for influencers. Same sale, bigger cut.
- **Creator Connections campaigns.** Brands offering a specific, often higher, commission or a flat bounty to promote their product. This is usually the best-paying work available to you.

The same forty-two dollar sale might pay you a few percent as a standard item, or a much larger campaign rate through Creator Connections. Filming both takes the same afternoon.

## Surface the high payers first

When you rank your event deals, the ones flagged as Creator Connections or Storefront Commission opportunities are the ones to film first. They earn more per post, so a slot spent on them is worth more than a slot spent on a standard item at the same price. Sort by estimated payoff and the tiers sort themselves.

## Watch the campaign deadlines

Creator Connections campaigns have windows. A campaign that ends mid-event is worth prioritizing before it closes, and one that runs past the event can wait until day two when the standard deals thin out. Do not let a high-paying campaign expire in your queue while you post standard items around it.

## Do not ignore the standard deals, order them

None of this means skip the ordinary deals. Your audience wants the genuinely good prices regardless of what they pay you, and trust is the whole business. It means order your day so the highest-paying work happens when your energy and your audience's attention are freshest, and the standard deals fill the time around them.

Same effort, same event, better math. During the busiest earning window of the season, spend your best hours on the deals that pay you the most.
