Instagram is where a lot of your audience already is, which makes it a natural home for the deals you post. The problem is the grind: opening the app, copying a link, writing a caption, and doing it again an hour later, all day, every day. That is the exact chore that burns people out of running a deals account. Deals Butler, a Pro feature, takes it off your plate. It filters your Amazon and Walmart deals, builds each post from your template, and posts them to Instagram on a schedule you set once.

## Three ways it reaches Instagram

Deals Butler does not treat Instagram as one destination. It can post to three different surfaces, and you can turn on any mix of them:

- **Instagram broadcast channels.** Your deals drop straight into your broadcast channel, the direct line to the fans who opted in. This is the highest-intent audience you have on Instagram.
- **Instagram feed posts.** A deal goes out as a single photo post on your own feed. Every feed post needs an image, so in your post settings you decide what happens when a deal has no photo: skip it, or generate one with AI.
- **Instagram Threads.** Deals post to your Threads feed too. It reuses your Instagram sign-in, so it is just a toggle.

Pick the ones that fit how you use Instagram. A channel plus the occasional feed post is a common, low-noise setup.

## One schedule runs all of it

You do not manage a separate schedule per platform. In the Deals Butler hub there is a single Scheduler: turn on "Post deals for me," pick one cadence, and every destination you connected posts on that shared pace, taking turns between your Amazon and Walmart deals.

One honest note on pacing: Instagram rate-limits much faster than something like Telegram. A cadence Telegram is perfectly happy with can trip an Instagram limit, so set your pace to what Instagram tolerates, not the fastest platform in your list.

## Your template does the writing

You are not typing captions. Your post template uses pills like the product title, price, discount, and deep link, and there are quick-start formulas that fill a complete caption in one click. The image chip lets you keep the Amazon product photo, upload your own, generate one with AI, or post text only. Set it up once, hit preview to see how a real deal renders, and every future post follows it.

## Setting it up

1. Open **Deals Butler** and go to **Social Destinations**.
2. Connect Instagram. For a **broadcast channel** you paste the channel's desktop link, a one-time step because Instagram hides that channel ID. Our [Instagram channel link guide](/help/tutorials/instagram-channel-link) walks through pulling it. For **feed posts** and **Threads**, there is nothing to paste: they reuse your Instagram sign-in, so you just toggle them on.
3. Do a deal harvest, then use **Post one now** to send a single deal end to end so you can see exactly how it looks before you turn the schedule on.

The full workflow lives in the [Deals Butler tutorial](/help/tutorials/deals).

## One thing to expect

If your channel or account messages someone who does not follow you, Instagram may route it to their message requests rather than their inbox. That is Instagram's own behavior, not the butler's, and it is worth knowing so it does not look like something went wrong.

Set it up once and your deals flow to Instagram on their own, while you spend your time on the content only you can make.
