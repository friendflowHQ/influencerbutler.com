Shoppable photos are the most underrated content you make. They take minutes, they keep earning quietly, and they never go out of style the way a trend-chasing video can. The catch is that most of your photos live in exactly one place: your main Amazon storefront. Two Pro butlers give them more homes, working from the storefront Storefront Butler already keeps synced for you.

## Photo Reload Butler: more Amazon marketplaces, fresh tags

Photo Reload Butler is the photo counterpart to Video Reload Butler. It takes the shoppable photos already on your Amazon storefront and re-posts them to other marketplaces with fresh tags, so the same photo starts working for shoppers in more countries.

One honest detail so you set expectations correctly: for photos, the destinations are Canada, the UK, Australia, and Singapore. That is a narrower list than the video butler's marketplaces, and it is the accurate one, so plan around those four.

Because photos do not need re-encoding, this butler is simpler than its video sibling. You choose the source marketplace and the destinations, set how many to process per run, decide how to handle duplicates and out-of-stock items, and run it. It can also bring back photos that were removed from Amazon, and as with videos, every original is saved to your computer before anything is deleted.

There are one-click formulas for the common moves, like fanning your US photos out to Canada, the UK, Australia, and Singapore in one pass, and it can match a product by title when the exact item does not exist in another marketplace.

## Photo Repost: the same photos on Walmart

Photo Repost is the twin of Video Repost inside Walmart Butler. It has its own setup button and the same steps, and it does for photos exactly what Video Repost does for videos: it republishes your Amazon storefront photos to your Walmart Creator storefront.

The same load-bearing rule applies. A photo is only reposted when it is the exact same item on Walmart, matched by UPC or GTIN barcode. Anything without an exact match is skipped, which is what keeps your Walmart storefront clean and correct. It shares the single Walmart Creator sign-in with Video Repost, so once you are set up for one, you are set up for both.

## Fast content, wider reach

Photos are the quickest thing you make, which is exactly why it stings to leave them earning in only one place. Photo Reload Butler spreads them across more Amazon marketplaces, and Photo Repost carries them to Walmart. Set both once and your quickest content starts pulling its weight everywhere you sell.
