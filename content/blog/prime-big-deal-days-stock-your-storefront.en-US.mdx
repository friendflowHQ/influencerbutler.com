Every deal post you make during Prime Big Deal Days points somewhere. For a lot of creators that somewhere is a storefront that has not been touched in weeks. The event is the wrong time to find out your best-earning list is buried three rows down.

Spend an hour now, before the traffic arrives, making the destination worth the click.

## Put your winners at the top

Open Storefront Butler and look at your Idea Lists the way a first-time visitor would. The lists that earned you the most this quarter should be the first thing people see, not the ones you happened to make most recently. Reorder so your proven sellers lead.

If a list is thin, fill it. A list with three products looks abandoned. A list with fifteen looks like a place to shop.

## Build one list for the event itself

Make a single Idea List named for the event, something like "Prime Big Deal Days Picks," and load it with the deals you actually plan to promote. When you post during the event, you can point people to that one list instead of scattering them across your whole storefront. One clean destination converts better than ten cluttered ones.

Pull the candidates straight from your ranked content plan if you already built one. The products at the top of that list are the same ones that belong at the top of this one.

## Check the boring things

- **Every list has a cover image and a real title.** "List 4" is not a title. A clear, keyword-friendly name helps both shoppers and search.
- **Dead products are gone.** Anything out of stock or discontinued makes the whole list feel stale. Cut it.
- **Your bio and links point to the right place.** If your link in bio still sends people to last season's list, fix it now.

## Do it once, benefit all week

This is not glamorous work, and that is exactly why most creators skip it. They spend the event driving traffic to a storefront they never prepared. You will spend one quiet hour today so that for the next two weeks every click lands somewhere that actually sells.

Stock the shelves before you open the doors. When Prime Big Deal Days traffic shows up, your storefront will be ready to earn from it.
