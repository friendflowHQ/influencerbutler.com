Prime Big Deal Days did not just earn you commissions. It sent a wave of new people past your storefront, your profile, and your links, more than any ordinary week. By this weekend, almost all of them will be gone, back to their own feeds, remembering nothing. Unless you give them a reason to stay. Capturing even a slice of that wave is worth more than the commissions, because a follower buys from you again and again.

## The spike is temporary, so act this week

New visitors decide fast whether you are worth following. The window is now, while the event is still fresh in their minds, not in three weeks when you finally get around to it. Whatever you do to convert them, do it this week.

## Give the new arrival somewhere obvious to land

Someone who liked one deal post will click your profile once. What do they find? If it is a clear bio, a working link, and an obvious next step, some of them stay. If it is a stale bio and a dead link, all of them leave. Make sure your profile reads like a place worth following, not just a place that posted one good price.

## Offer a reason to come back

A follow is a small ask. Make it easy by promising something specific: "I post the best genuinely-worth-it deals every week, no junk." People followed you during the event for deals; tell them the deals keep coming. Consistency is the promise that converts a one-time visitor into a regular.

## Move the best ones off rented land

Followers on any platform are borrowed; an email list or a broadcast channel is yours. This is the moment to invite the wave somewhere you own: a simple newsletter of your weekly picks, or a broadcast channel for deal drops. Even a small percentage of an event-sized wave is a meaningful list, and unlike an algorithm, a list shows up every time you send.

## Then keep the promise

None of this works if the follow leads to silence or to a feed of low-effort posts. The people you captured this week will judge you by your next month. Keep posting the genuinely good deals you promised, at a steady cadence, and the audience the event sent you becomes the audience that carries you through the holidays and beyond.

The commissions were this week's win. The audience is the one that pays for the rest of the year. Do not let the wave wash back out without keeping some of it.
