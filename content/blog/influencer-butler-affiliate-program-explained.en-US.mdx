If you already talk about the tools you use, you have probably mentioned Influencer Butler to another creator without thinking twice about it. Maybe it came up in a Discord, in a comment reply, or as an aside in a video about your Amazon storefront setup. That recommendation was free goodwill. The affiliate program is what happens when you turn it into income instead.

This is worth separating from something else the app already does. Influencer Butler has built-in tooling for your own Amazon affiliate links, helping you shorten, tag, and track the product links you share with your audience. That is not this. The affiliate program covers a different relationship entirely: you referring other creators to become Influencer Butler customers, and earning a cut of what they pay us, for as long as they keep paying.

## Why this is worth your time if you are already in this space

If your audience is other creators, sellers, or anyone trying to automate their side of the influencer hustle, you are already in the exact position this program is built for. You do not need a dedicated "review the software" video to make it work. A line in a resource page, a mention in a YouTube description, a pinned comment pointing people to your link: any of it counts, as long as someone actually clicks through.

The honest pitch is simple. If you genuinely use Influencer Butler and would recommend it anyway, there is no reason for that recommendation to stay unpaid. The program pays you **30% of every subscription payment your referral makes, every month, for the first 12 months** they stay subscribed. There are no per-affiliate caps on how much you can earn and no tier structure to climb. One referral who stays subscribed a full year is worth 12 months of commission, not a single flat finder's fee.

## How it actually works, step by step

The mechanics are deliberately simple, and all of it lives in your own dashboard once you are in.

1. **Apply.** You fill out a short application telling us about your audience and how you plan to promote. Most applications get reviewed within a day.
2. **Get your link.** The moment you are approved, your unique referral link and your stats dashboard go live. There is no second signup anywhere else, no separate affiliate network to join. You log in with the same account.
3. **Share it anywhere your audience already is.** A video description, a newsletter, a resource page, a pinned comment. Wherever you would naturally mention a tool you use.
4. **Earn recurring commission.** Every subscription that comes through your link pays you 30% a month for the first 12 months of that subscription, and we pay you directly.

The dashboard itself is not just a link generator. It shows your stats, a sparkline of referral activity over time so you can see whether a given push (a new video, a newsletter mention) actually moved clicks, and your running commission totals. You are not guessing whether something worked. You can watch it happen.

## The part that protects you: 30-day first-touch attribution

Here is the mechanic that matters most if you are not posting a link and getting an instant click-to-signup. Your referral link sets a cookie that lasts 30 days, and it uses **first-touch attribution**: whoever's link someone clicks *first* within that window is the one who gets credited, even if that person goes on to click other links, read other reviews, or take their time deciding.

That distinction matters more than it sounds like it should. In a lot of affiliate programs, if someone clicks your link today and then clicks a different affiliate's link next week before finally signing up, the last click wins and you get nothing for the work you actually did convincing them. First-touch flips that. You planted the seed, you get the credit, even if they wander before they commit.

## The standard rate, and why you might see a different number mentioned

Everything above, 30% recurring for 12 months, is the standard offer, and it is what you should expect when you apply. It is also the number on the public application page, so there is no bait-and-switch between what is advertised and what gets paid out.

You may occasionally hear about an affiliate earning a different rate, or a longer (even lifetime) commission window. That does happen, but it is a custom arrangement negotiated individually with a small number of established affiliates, not something available by default or something you should expect on application. If you are reading this as someone considering applying, plan around the standard 30%-for-12-months deal. If your referral volume grows to the point where a custom arrangement makes sense for both sides, that is a conversation to have with us directly once you have a track record, not an assumption to apply under.

## A realistic walkthrough

Say you run a YouTube channel about selling on Amazon, and you add your Influencer Butler referral link to the description of a video about managing your storefront. Someone watches the video today, clicks the link out of curiosity, and then does nothing for three weeks. They are not ignoring you, they are just a normal person who bookmarks things and gets back to them later.

Three weeks later, they finally sit down, remember the tool from your video, and subscribe. Because your link set that 30-day first-touch cookie the day they first clicked, and three weeks is well inside the window, the referral is credited to you, not to whatever other mention of the app they may have come across in between. You did not lose the sale to someone else's video just because this person took their time.

From there, it shows up in your dashboard as an active referral. Every month that customer's subscription renews, 30% of that payment is added to your balance, for up to 12 months. You do not have to do anything else to keep earning it: no re-sharing the link, no manual tracking, no chasing them down to make sure they stayed subscribed. The dashboard's sparkline shows the click, the stats show the conversion, and the monthly PayPal payout reflects the commission as it accrues.

## What to actually expect on payouts

Commissions are paid monthly, directly to you via PayPal, not through a separate network you have to manage. A given month's earnings are held through the following month as a short buffer (so refunds have time to settle before money goes out), then paid around the 1st of the month after that, once your unpaid balance is at least $10. If you are under that threshold, it simply rolls into the next month rather than getting lost. You will need your PayPal email and a completed tax form on file in the dashboard, since neither we nor PayPal can send a payout without both.

Worth saying plainly: if a referral cancels, or hits the 12-month mark, the commissions on that customer stop accruing from that point forward, but anything you already earned is yours regardless. And if they refund within the refund window, your unpaid balance adjusts down for that specific order rather than clawing back everything you have earned from them.
