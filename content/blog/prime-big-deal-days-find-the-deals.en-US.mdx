The first job for Prime Big Deal Days is getting every event deal into one place so you can pick the winners. Scrolling Amazon during the event is how good deals get missed. Load the whole catalog once, filter it down, and queue the best.

## Load the event deals

Open the Promo Code Hub inside Deals Butler and tick the blue "Prime Big Deal Days only" filter. That narrows the catalog to just the deals in the event window, so you are not sifting through everything else Amazon is running. The list refreshes from Amazon's own deals export, so it is the complete set, not a sample.

## Narrow to the winners

You do not want to post everything. You want to post the deals your audience will actually click. Stack a few filters on top of the event filter:

- **Discount depth.** Sort by percent off, or by "lowest all year," to surface the deals that are genuinely rare rather than a few dollars off.
- **Category.** Filter to the categories your audience buys from you. A tech audience does not need your kitchen picks.
- **Creator favorites and Prime exclusives.** These tend to convert well during the event, so they are worth a look first.
- **Price band.** If your audience skews toward impulse-priced items, cap the max price so you are not queuing a run of big-ticket products.

## Add them to your queue

As you find deals worth posting, use Add to post, or select several and bulk-add. Each one lands in your posting queue with its price, discount, and link already filled in, so there is nothing to copy by hand. If you have a promo code for a product, attach it and it rides along into the caption.

## Do this early

The best time to load and queue is during the run-up, not on the morning of the event. When you have a full queue built in advance, the event itself becomes a matter of letting your schedule run and topping up with anything new that drops. That is the whole point of preparing: turn two frantic days into a calm, planned week.
