Prime Big Deal Days is here. If you followed the run-up, most of your work is already done: your queue is loaded, your content plan is printed, and your schedule is armed. Today is about steady execution, not heroics. Here is the short list.

## Morning

- **Confirm the schedule is running.** Check that your queued posts are firing on the cadence you set. If it is, leave it alone.
- **Do a fresh deal pass.** Open the Promo Code Hub, filter to Prime Big Deal Days, and sort by newest or deepest discount. Add any strong new drops to the queue. The best new deals of the event often land on the morning of day one.
- **Post your top piece of content.** Lead with the strongest thing you filmed. Give your best deal your best slot.

## Through the day

- **Top up, do not grind.** Add new winners as they appear, but let the schedule carry the steady load. You built it so you would not have to sit and copy-paste for 48 hours.
- **Watch the countdown.** The in-app countdown tells you how much of the window is left, so you can save a push for the final hours when procrastinating shoppers finally buy.
- **Reply to comments.** The event drives more comments than usual. A quick reply with the link often converts a browser into a buyer.

## Day two

Day two is where prepared creators pull ahead. Most people burned out yesterday. Your schedule is still running, your queue still has deals, and the shoppers are still buying. Keep the cadence steady, refresh your deal pass once more, and save a final nudge for the last few hours before the event closes.

## After it ends

When the window closes, look at what worked. The deals that converted this week are your evergreen winners for the rest of the holiday season and into Black Friday. Note them, and you have a head start on the next big event.

That is the whole playbook. Prepare in the run-up, let the schedule run on the day, and Prime Big Deal Days becomes one of the calmest big earning windows on your calendar.
