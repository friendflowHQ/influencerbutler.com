Influencer Butler was built so you do not have to babysit your Amazon business. The butlers post your deals, accept your campaigns, and keep your data fresh while you get on with your life. But "automated" does not mean "ignored". The creators who earn the most with Butler all share one habit: a short daily check-in.

This is that check-in, written out as a repeatable checklist. It takes about ten minutes a day, split into a morning pass, a midday glance, and an evening wrap-up. Do it with your coffee, and your butlers stay fast, accurate, and profitable.

![Branded checklist card showing a ten minute daily routine for Influencer Butler users](/assets/blog/daily-influencer-butler-checklist.png)

## Why a daily routine beats "set and forget"

Every butler is designed to run on its own, and most days they do exactly that. But three kinds of things still deserve a human glance:

1. **Decisions**: some butlers hit a fork they should not decide alone, like a campaign with an unusual commission rate. Those land in your Action Queue and wait for you.
2. **Drift**: filters that were perfect last month slowly go stale. Prices change, categories cool off, a Facebook group changes its rules.
3. **Windfalls**: pricing errors and hot deals are time-sensitive. Ten minutes of attention at the right moment can be worth more than a week of passive posting.

A short daily loop catches all three. Here is the routine.

## Morning: the five minute pass

### 1. Clear the Action Queue

Open **Action Queue** in the left menu. This is the single inbox where every butler parks a decision it did not want to make for you: a Creator Connections campaign with an oddly high or low commission rate, a replacement product that needs your eye, an AI-written message that got flagged as borderline.

Each card shows which butler queued it, the one-line decision, and the context behind it. Work top to bottom with the three buttons: **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Defer** if you want to research first. The butlers pick up right where you left off on everything you approve.

![Cowork prompt: open the Influencer Butler desktop app, click Action Queue in the left menu, and take a screenshot of the queue showing a few pending decision cards with their Approve, Reject, and Defer buttons](/assets/blog/daily-checklist/01-action-queue.png)

An empty queue is a good sign, not a problem. It means your butlers had a fully autonomous night.

### 2. Confirm last night's deal posts went out

Open **Deals Influencer Butler** and check the **Scheduler** tab. You are looking for two things: the posts that were scheduled overnight show as sent, and the next batch is queued up for today.

![Cowork prompt: open the Deals Influencer Butler workspace in Influencer Butler, click the Scheduler tab, and take a screenshot showing recently sent posts and the upcoming queue](/assets/blog/daily-checklist/02-daily-deals-scheduler.png)

If a destination quietly skipped a post, this is where you spot it early. A single missed Telegram post is nothing; three days of missed posts is a signed-out profile or an expired bot token, and now you know before your followers notice the silence.

### 3. Review what Daily Commission Butler accepted

**Daily Commission Butler** reads your sales and accepts the Creator Connections campaigns that match what you actually sold. It runs once a day on its own, so your morning job is just to glance at the results: which campaigns were accepted, and whether anything was skipped by your rules.

![Cowork prompt: open the Daily Commission Butler workspace in Influencer Butler and take a screenshot of the most recent run results showing accepted campaigns](/assets/blog/daily-checklist/03-daily-commission.png)

If the list looks thin, the usual causes are a stale campaign catalog (run cc-check) or thresholds set too aggressively. Dropping the minimum commission rate for one run will show you what you are filtering out.

## Midday: the two minute glance

### 4. Check Pricecrash Butler's catches

Pricing errors do not wait for business hours. **Pricecrash Butler** scans Amazon on a schedule for products discounted 99% or more (a $400 vacuum at $4 kind of mistake) and hands the catches to Deals Influencer Butler as drafts or auto-scheduled posts.

Around lunch, open the workspace and scan the catch table. If you run it in draft mode, approve the good ones now: these deals disappear fast, and the whole point of the radar is acting before the seller fixes the price.

![Cowork prompt: open the Pricecrash Butler workspace in Influencer Butler and take a screenshot of the results table showing recent catches from a scan](/assets/blog/daily-checklist/04-pricecrash.png)

## Evening: the three minute wrap-up

### 5. Read the topbar numbers

The topbar is your scoreboard: running totals like **Hours Saved** and **Money Saved**, plus your storefront ID. Take ten seconds to look at it. If you have set your hourly rate, the savings numbers tell you exactly what today's automation was worth.

![Cowork prompt: take a screenshot of the Influencer Butler main window focusing on the topbar with the Hours Saved and Money Saved totals visible](/assets/blog/daily-checklist/05-topbar-totals.png)

While you are there, confirm **Data Refresh Butler** is active. Nearly everything else (Deals Influencer Butler, commission matching, campaign data) reads from the data it keeps fresh, so this one butler quietly underwrites all the others.

### 6. One small tune-up

End the day by adjusting exactly one thing, based on what you saw:

- Too many Action Queue items? Tighten the source butler's auto-thresholds so it decides more on its own.
- Deal posts getting weak engagement? Nudge your Deal Filters (higher minimum discount, or narrow the categories to your niche).
- Campaigns accepted but not converting? Raise Daily Commission Butler's minimum commission rate or recent-sales threshold.

![Cowork prompt: open the Deal Filters tab of the Deals Influencer Butler workspace in Influencer Butler and take a screenshot of the filter controls, including minimum discount and category options](/assets/blog/daily-checklist/06-tune-filters.png)

One small change a day compounds. Ten tiny tune-ups beat one big monthly overhaul, and you never lose track of what you changed.

## The checklist, all in one place

Copy this wherever you keep your routines:

**Morning (5 min)**

- Clear the Action Queue (Approve / Reject / Defer)
- Deals Influencer Butler Scheduler: overnight posts sent, today's queue loaded
- Daily Commission Butler: review accepted campaigns

**Midday (2 min)**

- Pricecrash Butler: scan the catch table, approve drafts fast

**Evening (3 min)**

- Topbar: Hours Saved, Money Saved, Data Refresh Butler active
- Make one small tune-up (filters, thresholds, or captions)

**Weekly bonus (10 min, pick a day)**

- Review which campaigns and deals actually earned, and tighten your rules around the winners
- Check every connected destination (Facebook profiles signed in, Telegram bot green, Reddit healthy)
- Skim Help & Tutorials for a butler you have not tried yet, then set one up
