Influencer Butler is a desktop app that does the repetitive parts of being an Amazon influencer for you: posting deals, accepting brand campaigns, keeping your storefront content alive, and messaging brands. You set it up once, and the butlers do the busywork in the background.

If you have just downloaded it (or you are still deciding), this is the walkthrough to follow. Fifteen minutes, no tech background needed, and you will finish with your first butler running on its own.

![Branded card showing the first fifteen minutes of setting up Influencer Butler](/assets/blog/how-to-get-started-with-influencer-butler.png)

## Step 1: Download and install the app

Head to the [download page](/go/download) and grab the installer for your computer. It is a normal desktop app for Windows (and Mac), and the download is free: you can start on the free plan and upgrade whenever a paid butler earns its keep.

![Cowork prompt: open influencerbutler.com/go/download in a browser and take a screenshot of the download page with the download button visible](/assets/blog/getting-started/01-download.png)

Run the installer like any other program. When it finishes, Influencer Butler opens on its own.

![Cowork prompt: take a screenshot of the Influencer Butler desktop app open on the first screen you see after a fresh install](/assets/blog/getting-started/02-first-launch.png)

## Step 2: Sign in with a magic link

The first time the app opens, it asks you to sign in. There is no password to invent or remember:

1. Enter the same email you used when you bought Influencer Butler (or signed up).
2. The app emails you a one-time sign-in link.
3. Open the email, click the link, and the app catches it and signs you in automatically.

![Cowork prompt: take a screenshot of the Influencer Butler sign-in screen showing the email field and the button that sends the sign-in link](/assets/blog/getting-started/03-sign-in.png)

If the email has not arrived after a minute or two, check your spam folder and make sure you typed the email you purchased with.

## Step 3: Pick your goal

Right after sign-in, a short setup walkthrough asks what you most want to do first: things like posting your videos and photos to other country marketplaces, or auto-accepting brand campaigns.

Pick whichever fits you best and do not overthink it. The choice only decides which butler gets set up first. You can change your goal or set up every other butler later.

![Cowork prompt: take a screenshot of the Influencer Butler setup walkthrough on the step where you pick your first goal](/assets/blog/getting-started/04-pick-goal.png)

## Step 4: Connect your Amazon storefront

Most butlers work from your Amazon Influencer storefront, so the app needs to be signed in to Amazon inside its own built-in browser.

The first time a butler needs Amazon, a browser window opens and asks you to log in. This is normal and expected. Log in the way you always do, including any code Amazon texts you. The app remembers the session, so you usually only do this once.

![Cowork prompt: take a screenshot of the Influencer Butler built-in browser window showing the Amazon sign-in page during setup](/assets/blog/getting-started/05-amazon-login.png)

One tip: log in slowly and finish every prompt Amazon shows you. If you close the window too early, the app has to ask again next time.

## Step 5 (optional): Connect Creator Connections for brand campaigns

If brand deals are your goal, you will want the app to accept Creator Connections campaigns and submit your content links for you. That needs Creator Connections access on your Amazon Influencer account plus a set of API credentials, which takes a few extra minutes to set up in the **API Integrations** section.

![Cowork prompt: open the API Integrations section in Influencer Butler and take a screenshot of the Creator Connections credentials card](/assets/blog/getting-started/06-creator-connections.png)

Not focused on brand campaigns yet? Skip this entirely. You can add it any time later, and the in-app Help & Tutorials section has a video that walks you through getting the credentials.

## Step 6: Let your butlers warm up

Once you are connected, the walkthrough starts the first run for you. The first time a butler runs, it gathers your storefront and recent activity, so give it a few minutes. Every run after that is faster.

You do not have to sit and watch. Close the walkthrough and let it work: the app emails you when it is done, or right away if a butler needs you for a moment.

![Cowork prompt: take a screenshot of the Influencer Butler setup walkthrough while it is running the first butler setup in the background](/assets/blog/getting-started/07-warm-up.png)

## Step 7: Get to know the main screen

A thirty second tour so nothing feels mysterious:

- **Left menu**: your butlers. Each one handles a different job (deals, campaigns, messaging, content). Click a name to open its workspace.
- **Top bar**: your running totals, including **Hours Saved** and **Money Saved**, plus your storefront ID. Set your hourly rate there so the savings numbers reflect what your time is worth.
- **Help & Tutorials**: a guide for every butler, plus a free beginner course on the Amazon Influencer Program itself.

![Cowork prompt: take a screenshot of the Influencer Butler main window showing the left menu of butlers and the topbar with running totals](/assets/blog/getting-started/08-main-screen.png)

## Step 8: Run your first real task

Open the butler the walkthrough set up for you and let it do one full run while you watch. Seeing a complete run once takes the mystery out of it: you will know what it does, how long it takes, and what it looks like when it needs you.

From then on, let it run on its own. That is the whole idea: you set the direction, the butlers do the busywork.

## What to do on day two

Once your first butler is humming, two things will keep it that way:

1. **Adopt a short daily routine.** Our [Daily Influencer Butler Checklist](/blog/daily-influencer-butler-checklist) is a ten minute morning-midday-evening loop that keeps every butler fast, accurate, and profitable.
2. **Add a second butler.** Amazon Daily Deals is the crowd favorite: it finds fresh deals and [auto-posts them to Facebook, Telegram, and Reddit](/blog/auto-post-daily-deals-facebook-telegram-reddit) on a schedule you choose.

And if you want your butlers working around the clock without leaving your laptop on, you can [run the app on a free cloud PC](/blog/run-influencer-butler-free-cloud-pc).
