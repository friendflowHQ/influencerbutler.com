Two days before Prime Big Deal Days is the best time to get set up. The event prices are landing, so your prep is based on real deals, and you still have room to fix anything that breaks without panicking. Work through this list today and the event itself becomes execution, not scrambling.

## The destination is ready

- Your storefront is refreshed and your best Idea Lists lead.
- You have one Idea List built for the event, loaded with the deals you plan to promote.
- Your link in bio points to that list, and you have clicked it yourself to confirm it works.

## The deals are loaded

- You have run a deal pass in the Promo Code Hub, filtered to the event, and pulled the strong ones into your queue.
- You ranked them by payoff and cut the shallow and redundant ones.
- The Creator Connections and Storefront Commission deals are marked so you lead with them.

## The posting is scheduled

- Your queued posts are set to fire across the whole window, not bunched into one hour.
- You know your cadence: how many posts per day, spaced how far apart.
- You have confirmed the schedule is actually armed and running, not saved as a draft.

## The words are written

- You have five or six deal captions drafted and saved, ready to drop product and price into.
- Your affiliate disclosure is on every template you will use.

## The measurement is set

- You wrote down your baseline: a normal week's earnings, your traffic, your follower count, your target.

## Print it, then walk away

Run down this list once. Fix what is not done. Then close the laptop. The single biggest mistake creators make in the last 48 hours is fiddling: rearranging the storefront a fourth time, second-guessing the schedule, rewriting captions that were already fine.

The work that earns during Prime Big Deal Days is the work you finished before it started. Get these boxes ticked today, and the event is the easy part.
