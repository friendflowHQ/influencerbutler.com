A full queue is not a content plan. During Prime Big Deal Days you have more deals than you can possibly film, so the question is which ones to make content on first. The answer is simple: the ones you have not already covered, ranked by what they are likely to earn.

## Let the butler rank it for you

Open Prime Day Butler and run it. It pulls the event deals and merges them with two things only you have: the products you already own, from Orders Butler, and the content you already made, from Storefront Butler. Then it ranks everything by estimated earnings and sorts each item into "already covered" or "not covered yet."

That last split is the whole game. The deals you have already posted about do not need new content right now. The deals you have not touched are your opportunity.

## Print the list

Press Print content list. You get a clean PDF of the best event deals you have not covered yet, ranked by estimated payoff, each with the discount, the sale and list price, an estimated commission, and a checkbox. Creator Connections and Storefront Commission opportunities are flagged so you can prioritize the deals that pay you more than the standard rate.

Tape it to your desk. As you shoot, check items off. It turns a vague "I should make some Prime Big Deal Days content" into a concrete list you can work down.

## Work from the top

Start at the top of the list, where the estimated payoff is highest. If you already own the product, that is your fastest content, film it today. If you have raw footage from an earlier shoot, the butler attaches it automatically when you schedule, so you do not redo work.

You do not need to cover the whole list. You need to cover the top of it well. Ten strong pieces of content on high-payoff deals beat fifty rushed ones.
