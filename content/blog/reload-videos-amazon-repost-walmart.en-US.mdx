Most of your videos had exactly one run. You posted them, the algorithm gave them a few days, and then they sank down your storefront and stopped earning. The footage is still good. The products are still selling. The only thing missing is a second look. Two Pro butlers give your existing videos more runs without you filming anything new, and they pull from the storefront you already have (Storefront Butler keeps that synced for you, for free).

## Video Reload Butler: a fresh run on Amazon

Amazon treats a re-uploaded video as a new post. That is the whole idea behind Video Reload Butler: it re-uploads videos already on your Amazon Creator storefront so they get another run in the algorithm, described in the app as the closest thing to a free engagement boost.

It does more than repost, though. It can fan a video out across Amazon marketplaces, up to thirteen of them (US, Canada, the UK, Australia, and Singapore are the verified ones, with more in beta), refreshing the title and adding local-language captions for each. Each marketplace is a separate storefront, so this is genuine new reach, not a duplicate.

A few things worth knowing so nothing surprises you:

- **Same-marketplace refreshes delete the old copy first.** Amazon's duplicate detection is per storefront, so to re-run a US video it removes the US original and posts fresh. The file is always downloaded to your computer before anything is deleted, and deletes require you to type a confirmation.
- **Your winners are protected.** You can tell it to skip videos above a view threshold, so a piece that is still performing is left alone.
- **The 31-second floor still applies.** Amazon monetizes videos of about 31 seconds and up, and the butler respects that.

## Video Repost: the same videos, now on Walmart

Walmart's Creator program is a second storefront most people leave empty. Video Repost, part of Walmart Butler, fills it from what you already have: it republishes your Amazon storefront videos to your Walmart Creator storefront, so Walmart builds itself out without a single new shoot.

The one rule that matters: it only reposts a video when the product is the exact same item on Walmart, matched by its UPC or GTIN barcode. No exact match, and it skips that video rather than guess. That is what keeps your Walmart storefront accurate.

Setup is a short wizard: sign in to Walmart Creator once, choose whether to review each match or auto-post, cap how many items go per run, and optionally schedule it to run daily, every few days, or weekly. Review mode holds each match for your approval, and it remembers what it has posted so nothing goes up twice.

## One library, many placements

The point of both butlers is the same. You did the hard part when you filmed. Video Reload Butler gets those videos a fresh run and international reach on Amazon, and Video Repost carries them to Walmart. Turn them on, and a library you already own quietly earns in more places than it did yesterday.
