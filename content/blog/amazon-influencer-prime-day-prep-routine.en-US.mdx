## Step 4: Fix your back catalog with Retag Butler 🏷️

Do not skip this one. Before you send a wave of Prime Day traffic to your storefront, make sure that traffic lands on products people can actually buy.

Over four years, I have made a lot of content, and some of it points to ASINs that went out of stock, got delisted, or were swapped by the brand. Every dead ASIN is a broken affiliate link that earns nothing, and Prime Day is the worst possible time to be leaking commission.

**Retag Butler** scans your storefront, finds the content pointing at unavailable products, looks up a replacement in the same category, and tags it. It adds the replacement alongside the original, so your history stays intact but the link now points to something a shopper can buy. Run it in Review mode the first time so you can approve each swap, then let Auto handle the obvious ones. By the time Prime Day hits, your entire back catalog is earning again instead of sending shoppers to dead ends.
