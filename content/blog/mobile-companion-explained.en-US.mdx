You are standing in a store, or scrolling Amazon's own app on your phone, and you see something worth putting in front of your audience. Your desktop, where Influencer Butler actually lives, is at home. Until now, that meant remembering the product, or emailing yourself a link, and dealing with it later. The mobile companion exists so you do not have to wait: pair your phone once, and from then on you can check on your Butlers and get a link made without sitting down at your computer.

It is not a second copy of the desktop app. It is a lightweight companion, a phone-sized window into the app that is already running on your desktop, built for the handful of things you actually want to do while you are out and about.

## Pairing is a one-time step

Setup lives in the desktop app: go to **Settings**, then **Mobile**, then **Show pairing code**. That displays a QR code on your screen. Scan it with your phone's camera, and it opens a pairing link that connects your phone to that specific desktop app. You do this once. After that, your phone and your desktop stay linked, and you will not need to scan anything again unless you are setting up a new device.

It is a deliberately small amount of ceremony. No account to create separately, no password to type on a phone keyboard, just point your camera at the screen and you are connected.

## Four tabs, each doing one job

Once paired, the companion is organized into four tabs, and each one earns its place rather than padding out a menu.

**Home** is your at-a-glance check. It shows whether the desktop app is currently reachable (useful before you plan on using Share, since that depends on your desktop being online), plus stats: hours and money saved, commission earned, and recent alerts. There is also a pause/resume-all control here, one switch that pauses or resumes every Butler at once, for the moments you need everything to stop without hunting through individual settings.

**Butlers** is where you get specific. You can run, pause, resume, or stop individual Butlers remotely from your phone, the same level of control you would have sitting at your desktop, just from your pocket.

**Share** is the tab you will actually reach for most days, covered in its own section below.

**More** is device management and a language picker, English, Spanish, or French.

## Share is the feature that earns its keep

Home and Butlers are useful for checking in. Share is the one that changes how you work. It lets you share a link from any shopping app on your phone, Amazon's own app is the obvious example, straight into Influencer Butler's tools: Make My Link, Check It, Add to Link Page, or Add to Deals Butler.

In practice, that means using your phone's native share sheet, the same one you would use to text a link to a friend, and instead picking Influencer Butler from the list. No copying a URL, no switching apps to paste it somewhere, no opening a browser tab to find the right tool. You see a product, you tap Share, you pick where it should go, and you are done.

This is the part of the mobile companion that is genuinely useful on a day-to-day basis rather than just nice to have. Checking stats on your phone is convenient. Being able to catch a product the moment you see it, without losing the thought by the time you get home, is the thing that actually changes behavior.

## What happens if your desktop is asleep

Here is a detail worth knowing before you rely on Share: if your desktop app happens to be offline when you share something, it does not fail and ask you to try again later. It queues the share, and processes it automatically once the desktop app is back online. You do not lose a product idea just because your computer happened to be off when you were out shopping. You can share in the moment and trust that it will show up once your desktop catches up.

## It is a web app today, native apps are planned

The mobile companion is a Progressive Web App, PWA for short, which in plain terms means it is a website you install to your phone's home screen rather than something you download from the App Store or Play Store. Open the pairing link once, add it to your home screen, and it behaves like an app from then on: its own icon, full screen, no browser bar.

It is explicitly planned to be wrapped for the App Store and Play Store later, using a tool called Capacitor that packages web apps for native distribution. But that is a future step, not a missing piece today. Shipping it as an installable web app first means there is no app-store review to wait on and no download gate between you and using it. You scan a QR code and you are in. That is a reasonable way to get something usable into your hands now, rather than holding the whole feature back for weeks of app-store approval, and it is worth saying plainly rather than apologizing for it: this is the deliberate shape of the release, not a corner cut.

## A realistic first use

Say you pair your phone one evening using the QR code in Settings, then More, on your desktop app. A few days later you are scrolling Amazon's app on your lunch break and spot a product you know your audience would want. You tap the native share icon, Influencer Butler shows up in the share sheet, and you send it straight to Make My Link. You do not open your laptop, you do not copy a URL anywhere, you just share it the way you would share anything else from your phone.

That evening, you open the companion again and check the Home tab. It shows the day's stats, hours saved, commission earned, recent alerts, and you can see the link you made at lunch already sitting where it should be. If you want to check that a particular Butler ran on schedule while you were out, the Butlers tab lets you look, and pause or resume it right there if something needs adjusting.

None of this replaces the desktop app. It is not meant to. It is meant to catch the moments that happen away from your desk, a product you spot in a store, a link you want made before you forget, a quick check that everything is still running, without asking you to wait until you are back at your computer.
