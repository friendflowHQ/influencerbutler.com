Uploading a video is only half the opportunity. If the product in that video has an open brand campaign through Amazon Creator Connections or the Storefront Commission program, you can earn extra on it, but only if you accept the campaign and submit your content before the window closes. Doing that by hand, video by video, is exactly the kind of chore that never gets done. YouTube Butler folds it into the upload.

## One toggle, done in the right order

Next to the upload button is an "Accept Creator Connections" checkbox, on by default. With it on, for every video it uploads YouTube Butler:

1. **Checks first.** Before uploading, it looks at whether that product has an open Creator Connections or SPCC campaign.
2. **Accepts the campaign.** If there is one, it opts you in.
3. **Submits the link after the upload finishes.** Once your video is live on YouTube, it goes back and submits that YouTube link as the campaign's content, so it actually counts.

The order matters: it submits the finished YouTube link, not the raw file, so the campaign is credited to real, published content.

You can see it happening. The inventory and upload tables gain a CC and SPCC column: a chip appears when a campaign is available, and a green CC or SPCC checkmark shows once it has been accepted and the link submitted. It only opens Amazon when it actually finds a campaign, and if your Amazon session happens to be signed out, it notes that and keeps uploading. The campaign step never causes an upload to fail.

## See what is already on YouTube, right on Amazon

There is a companion piece in the free Chrome extension worth knowing about. On your Amazon Creator Hub and storefront pages, the extension paints a small chip on each of your videos: "On YouTube" (green, linking to the YouTube video) or "Not on YouTube." When a video is not up yet, an "Upload to YouTube" button hands it straight to the desktop uploader, and the chip flips to green once it finishes.

It reads its status from the desktop app over a local connection, so the desktop app needs to be paired and running. When it is not, the chip shows a quiet "connect the app" state instead of guessing.

## Get seen and get counted

Getting your videos onto YouTube widens your reach. Letting YouTube Butler accept and submit your Creator Connections and SPCC campaigns as it goes means that wider reach is also credited to the brand deals that pay more. Flip the toggle once, and the earning side keeps up with the uploading side automatically.
