The most common Prime Big Deal Days mistake is going all-out on the first morning and having nothing left by the afternoon of day two. The shoppers at hour 30 are just as valuable as the ones at hour two. The creator who posts steadily across the whole window out-earns the one who sprints and crashes.

## Set a pacing plan

Decide your cadence before the event, while you are calm. A good default is a steady drip: a handful of posts spaced through each day, with your strongest deals placed at the hours your audience is most active. Leave room to add anything new that drops. A plan you made in advance beats a decision you make while frazzled at hour 14.

## Let the scheduler run it

You already built your queue and your content plan. Now set your schedule in Deals Butler so the queued posts fire automatically across the event days. Turn on your social destinations so each post is one click, or fully hands-off, from going live. Once the schedule is armed, the event mostly runs itself and you top it up with fresh deals as they appear.

## Write captions that fit the event

Your event posts should feel like the event. The caption token for the event name lets you write a line like "Prime Big Deal Days price" that fills in automatically and quietly disappears on any post that is not an event deal, so you can reuse one template all season. Lead with the discount, keep the link clean, and let the price do the selling.

## Trust the safety net

Deals move fast during a big sale, and a price can revert before your scheduled post fires. The butler rechecks each deal at post time and skips anything that is no longer a real discount, so you never post a full-price item by accident. That means you can schedule aggressively without worrying about a stale price going out under your name.
