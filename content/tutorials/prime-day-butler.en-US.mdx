## What it does

Prime Day Butler is a seasonal radar that surfaces deal-worthy Amazon products from three sources at once: content you've already posted to your storefront, products you've actually bought (from Orders Butler), and fresh discoveries pulled from the Creators API. Every row is ranked by **estimated earnings** (biggest first) and tagged with a bucket so you know whether it's a quick win or a last-minute pick. You filter by keyword, minimum % off, and max price; the table updates and you export the candidates to CSV.

## The two buckets

- **Quick wins**: deals on products you already have content for. The fastest path to a post. Just resurface what you made.
- **Last-minute**: deals on products you own (from Orders Butler) that have a live deal but no content yet. These are the on-hand picks you can shoot today.

A bucket tab above the table lets you view All, Quick wins, or Last-minute.

## Earnings prioritization

Each row shows three commission signals so you can post the biggest earners first:

- **Amazon %**: the Associate Rate Card commission for the product's category.
- **CC**: the best Creator Connections commission rate, when the product is CC-eligible.
- **SPCC**: the estimated earnings-per-click for a Sponsored Products for Creators offer.

The **Est. earnings** column picks the best per-sale estimate (Amazon vs CC) and the table sorts by it by default. Click any column header to re-sort.

## Prerequisites

- **Storefront Butler** has synced at least once so it knows what content you've made.
- **Orders Butler** has synced at least once if you want the "products I own" and last-minute signals.
- **Creators API key** configured in **API Integrations** for fresh discoveries, the owned-product deal check, and trending search.
- **Associate Rate Card** harvested (under Settings) so the Amazon-default earnings estimate can populate. Without it, that column shows a dash.

If any source is missing, the workspace still runs. It just shows fewer rows.

## First run

1. Open **Prime Day Butler** in the left nav (under the Amazon group).
2. Optional: build a **tracked-keyword watchlist**. Type a keyword and click **Track**. Tracked keywords run on every pass and on Find trending, so you don't retype them each time.
3. Set the filters:
   - **Keyword**: optional one-off search for this run.
   - **Min % off**: defaults to 20%. Bump to 40–50% for a tighter list.
   - **Max price ($)**: optional.
   - **Source toggles**: leave them on for the widest net.
   - **Check owned products for live deals**: turn on to build the last-minute bucket (uses extra Creators API calls).
   - **CC-eligible only**: filter to products where Creator Connections accepts the affiliate link.
4. Click **Run now**. The status line shows the count, split by quick wins and last-minute.
5. Use the bucket tabs to focus, and sort by **Est. earnings** to post the biggest earners first.
6. Per row: **Open** the product, **Download** your existing content, or **Add to Daily Deals** to queue a post. **Export CSV** dumps the whole filtered list, including the earnings columns.

## Find trending

Click **Find trending** to pull current Prime Day deals from the Creators API (a seed query plus your tracked keywords). New deals merge into the table with the same earnings ranking, so trending picks slot in next to your own.

## Gotchas

- **The list goes stale fast during Prime Day.** Re-run every 2–4 hours during the event window. Amazon's prices flip in real time.
- **Amazon-default earnings need the rate card.** If the Amazon % column is blank, harvest the Associate Rate Card under Settings.
- **The owned-deal check costs API calls.** Turn off "Check owned products for live deals" if you're rate-limited and only need quick wins.
- **CC eligibility lags.** A product can be CC-eligible today and gone tomorrow. The chip shows what was true at scan time.
