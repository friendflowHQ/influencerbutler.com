## What it does

Prime Day Butler is a seasonal radar that surfaces deal-worthy Amazon products from three sources at once: content you've already posted to your storefront, products you've actually bought (from Orders Butler), and fresh discoveries pulled from the Creators API. You filter by keyword, minimum % off, and max price; the table updates and you export the candidates to CSV for posting.

## Prerequisites

- **Storefront Butler** has synced at least once so it knows what content you've made.
- **Orders Butler** has synced at least once if you want the "products I own" signal.
- **Creators API key** configured in **API Integrations** if you want fresh discoveries beyond what you already have.

If any source is missing, the workspace still runs. It just shows fewer rows. The filters tell you which source each row came from.

## First run

1. Open **Prime Day Butler** in the left nav (under the Amazon group).
2. Set the filters:
   - **Keyword**: optional. Narrow to a niche, e.g. "kitchen".
   - **Min % off**: defaults to 20%. Bump to 40–50% for a tighter list.
   - **Max price ($)**: optional. Skip $200+ products if your audience won't bite.
   - **Source toggles**: leave the three on for the widest net; turn off "fresh discoveries" if you only want to repost what you've already made.
   - **CC-eligible only**: filter to products where Amazon Creator Connections accepts the affiliate link (highest commission paths).
3. Click **Run now**. The status line shows progress; the table fills as deals are found.
4. Review the results. Each row shows the ASIN, title, current price, % off, source, and (if CC-eligible) commission rate.
5. Click **Export CSV** to dump the candidate list. Most users paste these into Daily Deals Butler's post-builder, but a CSV works for any workflow.

## Tuning over time

- If the table is empty: drop the Min % off threshold to 10% and try again. Many "deals" Amazon flags are mid-range discounts.
- If the table is overwhelming: turn on **CC-eligible only** and bump Min % off to 30%+ to get the highest-leverage candidates.
- If you don't see your own storefront content: confirm Storefront Butler ran in the last 24 hours.

## Gotchas

- **The list goes stale fast during Prime Day.** Re-run every 2–4 hours during the event window. Amazon's prices flip in real time.
- **Fresh discoveries can be noisy.** The Creators API doesn't know your niche; pair the fresh-discoveries toggle with a tight keyword filter.
- **CC eligibility lags.** A product can be CC-eligible today and gone tomorrow. The status field tells you what was true at scan time.
