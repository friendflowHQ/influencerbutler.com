> **You stay in control.** Idea List Butler never creates anything on Amazon until you have reviewed the lists and clicked **Build on Amazon**. Lists it has already built are never built twice.

## What Idea List Butler does

A storefront that looks curated sells better than a pile of products. Idea List Butler does the refresh a pro would charge for:

- Gathers the products you already promote (your storefront, your best earners, live deals, or a pasted list).
- Groups them into **themed Idea Lists** that fit a one line style brief, like "Luxury, cool girl, unique items", and writes the descriptions.
- Makes a **styled collage image** for each list from its product photos and posts it as a **shoppable photo** with the same products tagged.
- Files each list and its photo into a **Curation** and **pins** a list and photo mix to the top of your storefront.

## Before you start

- Run **Storefront Butler** once so the butler knows what is on your storefront.
- Run the **storefront ID check** in Settings so it knows which storefront to build on.
- For AI themes, descriptions and the AI backdrop style, connect OpenAI in **API Integrations**. The grid and card image styles are free.

## Step by step

1. Open **Idea List Butler** under Amazon Butler.
2. **Products.** Tick the sources you want. Paste ASINs or one of your list links if you like. Click **Find products**.
3. **Theme the lists.** Type your style brief, pick how to group, how many lists and how many products per list. Click **Plan lists**.
4. **Review your lists.** Rename a list, edit its description, untick products or whole lists. **Preview image** shows the collage before anything is built. Lists that already exist on your storefront start unticked so you do not make duplicates.
5. **Collage images.** Choose the style (clean grid, aesthetic card, or AI backdrop) and whether the image goes up as a shoppable photo post or stays on your computer.
6. **Curations and pins.** Leave both on to get the full refresh, or turn either off.
7. Tick your storefront, keep **Watch it run** on the first time, and click **Build on Amazon**.

## Good to know

- Amazon shows up to 20 curations. The butler counts the ones you already have and stops at the limit.
- Posts you pinned yourself are never unpinned.
- If Amazon asks you to sign in, turn on Watch it run, click Build again and sign in when the browser asks. The run continues where it stopped.
- Amazon's product photos belong to Amazon. The shoppable photo option composes them into a collage; if you prefer to stay strictly inside Amazon's tools, use the **Amazon Collage** option once it is available in your build, or choose **Download only**.
