Telegram Butler posts to your Telegram channel through the Telegram Bot API, so no browser is involved. You write a message (and an optional image), post it now or schedule it for later, and edit or delete posts you have already sent, all from one place.

## Connect your bot (one time)

1. Open Telegram Butler and click **Connect & Settings** on the hub.
2. In Telegram, message **@BotFather**, create a bot, and copy the **bot token** it gives you.
3. Back in Connect & Settings, paste the **Bot token** and your **Channel**. For a public channel use its `@handle`.
4. Click **Test connection** to confirm it works.

This connection is shared with Deals Butler, so if you already post Telegram deals there, you are already connected here.

## Compose a post

Open **Compose** to build your post the same way as Deals Butler's Build Your Post:

- **Write your text** in the post box. Type freely, or drop in **Library pills** (Hook, CTA, Hashtags, Urgency, and hook styles). Click a pill to edit its lines; each post uses a different line, so your captions vary automatically.
- **Quick-start layouts** fill the post with a starting structure you can then edit.
- **More versions** lets you keep several variations of the caption; each post uses one, cycled.
- **Image**: drop the Image pill and pick a source. Paste an image URL, upload a photo from your computer, or generate one with AI. Or choose No image to post text only.
- **My Caption Voice**: paste 2 to 5 of your own posts and AI writes more versions in your voice, added to your list below.

When it looks right, click **Post to Telegram** and it lands in your channel right away.

## Schedule for later

Under the message, pick a date and time and click **Schedule**. Scheduled posts appear in the list below, each with a **Cancel** button, and Influencer Butler sends them automatically when their time comes. Scheduling respects the global pause, so a paused app will not fire scheduled posts.

## Manage posts

Open **Manage posts** to see everything you have sent from Compose. Edit the text of any post in place, or delete it. Telegram lets you edit and delete your own channel posts, so both actions work here.
