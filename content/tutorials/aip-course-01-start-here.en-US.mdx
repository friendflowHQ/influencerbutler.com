## Honest expectations

Before you invest hours into this, here is the truth most sales pages soften:

- **This is real, but it is not fast.** Most creators see their first onsite earnings within 1 to 2 months of getting approved and uploading consistently. Some see it in days, some take longer.
- **The money compounds.** Every video you upload is an asset that can earn for years. Creators who stick with it for 6 to 12 months are usually the ones posting the "I made $3k this month" screenshots.
- **You do not need an audience** for the onsite part of the program. Your videos show up on Amazon product pages, in front of people already shopping. That is what makes this program different from normal influencer work.
- **You do need consistency.** Ten videos will not change your life. A few hundred, built up over months, can become a meaningful income stream.

If you are looking for money this week, this program is not that. If you are willing to film products you already own for a few hours a week, keep going.
