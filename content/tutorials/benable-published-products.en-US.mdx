## What Published Products shows

Once Benable Butler publishes products to your lists, **Published Products** gives you one place to see exactly what went out and why. It is a filterable, sortable table with one row per published product, so you can answer questions like "which sponsored products are about to expire?" or "what did I publish into my Fall collection?" at a glance.

Each row shows:

- **Image** and **product** title, with the ASIN.
- **Reason** it was published: a short tag such as SPCC sponsored, Creator Connections, On sale, or a niche match.
- **CC** and **SPCC**: whether the product has an accepted Creator Connections campaign or a Sponsored Products for Creators offer.
- **Commission** rate and **Ends**: the campaign end date and whether it is active, ending soon, or ended.
- **Caption**: the note published under the product.
- **Collection**, **List**, and **Section** the product lives in.
- **Retailer**, **Published** date, and **Status**.

## Opening it

Open **Published Products** under Benable Butler in the left sidebar (nested under the Benable Butler toggle, next to List Publishing). The table loads the products from your most recent publish of each list.

Click **Refresh** any time to rebuild it from your latest runs. This view is read-only: it never changes your lists, so it is safe to open while a publish is running.

## Filtering and sorting

Use the toolbar to narrow the table:

- **Search** across product, ASIN, caption, list, and collection.
- **Collection** and **List** dropdowns to focus on one.
- **CC** and **SPCC** to show only sponsored (or only unsponsored) products.
- **Reason** to group by why each product was picked.
- **Campaign status** to find products that are active, ending soon, ended, or have no campaign at all.
- **Retailer** when your lists mix stores.

Click any sortable column header to sort by it, and click again to reverse. Your filters and sort are remembered for next time.

## Export

Click **Export CSV** to download the currently filtered table as a spreadsheet, including the reason, CC and SPCC status, campaign end, caption, and list for every row.

## How the reason is worked out

For products published recently, the reason is recorded at publish time. For products you published earlier, Published Products works out a best-effort reason from what it knows now: whether the product has an accepted Creator Connections or SPCC offer, whether it was on sale, and the niche the list was built around. The Creator Connections and SPCC status and the campaign end date always reflect the latest data, so a campaign that has since ended will show as ended here.

## Common gotchas

- **A row says "SPCC sponsored" and you have never touched Sponsored Products for Creators**: that chip describes the product, it is not something Butler did on your behalf. It means Amazon lists a Sponsored Products for Creators offer on that item, which is a brand paying a higher rate on it. No ad was created, nothing was scheduled, and nothing is charged to you. The chip beside it finishes the story: **not accepted** means Butler did not take the offer, so the product is published at the standard rate. Nothing in this table can spend money.
- **You would rather Butler did not lead with SPCC products at all**: open **Benable Butler**, then **Settings**. Turn off **Build from your SPCC campaigns first**, so lists are no longer led by your sponsored offers, and leave **Auto-accept SPCC campaigns while building** off, so no campaign is ever accepted for you. If **Publish only Amazon SPCC or Creator Connections products** is on, turn that off too, because it limits publishing to campaign products. Butler then picks on niche and price alone. A product it picks can still happen to carry an SPCC offer, and the chip still says so, which is worth knowing rather than hiding.
