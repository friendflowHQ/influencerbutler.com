## The problem this solves

Creator Connections campaigns fill fast. Bulk-accepting can claim every slot in a
campaign before you get to it, even when you already have great content for that
exact product on your storefront. You are locked out of a campaign you are a
perfect fit for.

Filled campaign recovery turns that dead end into an opening. It finds the full
campaigns that match content you already made, then helps you ask the brand to
open one more slot for you, with your content as proof.

## Where to find it

The same panel appears in three places, so it is right where you already work:

- **Campaign Alert Butler** (under the "Storefront matches" area)
- **CC Check**
- **Storefront Butler**

Open any of them and scroll to **Filled campaigns matching your content**.

## How it works

1. Click **Rescan**. The app compares every fully claimed Creator Connections
   campaign against the products you already have content for (your storefront
   videos, photos, and idea lists, plus anything you have posted). The first scan
   reads the full campaign catalog and can take a few minutes.
2. Each match shows the brand, the campaign, the commission rate, and which of
   your content covers it, along with a ready **Proof link** to that content.
3. Click **Message brand**. The app writes a short, friendly pitch for you: it
   names the campaign, includes your proof link, and asks the brand to open one
   more slot. The message is copied to your clipboard.
4. Open the brand's chat in Creator Connections and paste it in. You review and
   send, so you are always in control of what goes out.

## Tips for a good pitch

- **Lead with proof.** The message already includes your content link. That is
  the whole point: you are not asking cold, you are showing the brand you have
  already done the work.
- **One campaign at a time.** Each brand and campaign is tracked, so you will not
  accidentally send the same pitch twice. A different filled campaign for the same
  brand is still fair game.
- **Run a harvest first.** If the panel says there is no storefront content on
  file yet, run a Storefront Butler harvest so the app knows what content you
  have to match.

## Good to know

- The scan is on demand. Nothing runs in the background until you click Rescan, so
  it never slows the app down on its own.
- A campaign that shows as full in the catalog may occasionally have reopened. The
  panel flags those so you can double check before you pitch.
