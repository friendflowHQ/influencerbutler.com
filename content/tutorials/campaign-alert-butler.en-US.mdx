## The problem this solves

The best Creator Connections campaigns fill fast. By the time you happen to check
the catalog, the brand you love has already posted a campaign and it is full.
Campaign Alert Butler watches your favorite brands for you, tells you the moment
they post something new, and can accept those campaigns automatically so you are
one of the first in line.

## Follow the brands you care about

Open **Campaign Alert Butler** (under Amazon Butler) and add the brands you want
to watch.

- **Search and add.** Type a brand name in the search box and click **Add**. It
  is now on your followed list.
- **Follow all brands I have content for.** One click follows every Creator
  Connections brand you already have content for, pulled from your Storefront,
  Deals Butler, and YouTube. A fast way to start watching everything you work
  with.
- **Follow automatically when you accept.** Leave this on and any brand whose
  campaign you accept elsewhere is added to your list automatically, so you never
  lose track of a brand you work with.

The first check after you follow a brand quietly records its current campaigns as
a starting point. It does not alert on those, only on campaigns that appear
afterward.

## Choose how you hear about new campaigns

Under **Alert methods**, pick any mix of:

- **A popup in the app** (on by default)
- **A card on your Dashboard** marked as needing attention
- **An email** to your inbox

Every setting saves on its own, so there are no Save buttons to remember.

## Auto-accept new campaigns

This is what gets you in early. Turn on **Auto-accept new campaigns from brands I
follow** and Campaign Alert Butler will accept the new campaigns it finds for the
brands you follow, without you lifting a finger.

It is **off until you turn it on**, because accepting a campaign opts you into it.
Once on, you control exactly how it behaves:

- **Which campaigns.** Choose **All new campaigns** to grab every new campaign
  from a followed brand (best for getting in before a campaign fills), or **Only
  campaigns for products I have content for** to stick to products you have
  already made content for.
- **Most to accept per check.** A simple cap so one check never accepts more than
  you want. Leave it blank for the built-in default.
- **Watch it run.** Leave this on to see the browser work, which also lets it use
  your signed-in Amazon session.

### Run it on a schedule

Turn on **Run auto-accept on a schedule** and set how often to check (for example,
every 6 hours). Campaign Alert Butler will look for new campaigns from your
followed brands on that cadence and accept the ones that match your settings.

One honest note: new campaigns become visible only as often as our central
catalog refreshes, which is a few times a day. A very short interval will not
surface campaigns any sooner. The schedule is there to keep auto-accept running
on its own, and to let you pick the timing that suits you.

### Auto-accept by keyword, from any brand

Sometimes you want to catch campaigns from brands you are not following yet, for
example during a busy event like Prime Day when new campaigns drop and fill fast.
Turn on **Also auto-accept new campaigns matching keywords, from any brand** and
type the words you care about (one per line, or comma separated, for example
`prime day, kitchen`).

Campaign Alert Butler then accepts any new campaign whose title or brand contains
one of your keywords, across every brand in the catalog, not just the ones you
follow. A few honest notes:

- This looks across the whole catalog, so it turns on the catalog sync even if
  you follow no brands.
- It is a separate switch from the followed-brand auto-accept above. Use either
  one, or both together.
- Every guardrail still applies: the per-check cap, the never-accept-twice
  memory, the retry on a failed accept, and the global pause.
- Change your keywords whenever you like. Remove a keyword and it stops accepting
  matches for it right away.

## Good to know

- **Following a brand and accepting its campaigns are two different things.**
  Following a brand only puts it on the watch list, so you hear about its new
  campaigns. To have those new campaigns accepted for you as they appear, turn
  on **Auto-accept new campaigns from brands I follow** and pick **All new
  campaigns**. That is the setting people mean when they ask whether it can
  follow every new campaign from the brands they follow: yes, it can.
- **It never accepts the same campaign twice.** Each campaign it accepts is
  remembered, so a repeating schedule will not re-accept something you already
  have.
- **A failed accept retries on its own.** If Amazon reports a campaign as full or
  a check cannot finish, that campaign stays on the list and is tried again on the
  next check, until it lands or ends.
- **It respects your global pause.** When you pause all automation from the top
  bar, scheduled auto-accept pauses with it. Manual checks still work.
- **The first check per brand is silent.** It records that brand's current
  campaigns as a baseline and only accepts or alerts on campaigns that appear
  after you started following.
