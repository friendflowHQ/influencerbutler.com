## What Collage Templates does

Collage Templates is where you save the default look your collages start from. Instead of setting the layout, fonts, and colors every time you generate a collage, you set them once here and Collage Butler reuses them. Think of it as your collage style sheet: a layout, a background and text style, brand colors, and headline and call-to-action presets you can reuse across collages.

It is a settings panel, not a generator. There is nothing to run and nothing to schedule. Every change auto-saves as you make it, so you can adjust a color or preset and close the panel with no Save button to press.

## Before you start

- Collage Butler is currently **In Beta**. If the controls look locked, open **Settings** and turn Collage Butler on to unlock the panel.
- Have your brand colors handy (background, accent, and text) so your collages match your other posts.
- You can always override any of these defaults on a single collage in the AI Collage Generator. The template just sets the starting point.

## How to use it

1. In the left menu, open **Collage Butler**, then **Collage Templates**.
2. Pick a **Default layout**. This is the grid every new collage starts with, from **2 x 2 (4 products)** up to **3 x 3 (9 products)**. Choose the one that fits how many products you usually feature.
3. Choose a **Font** for your headline and call-to-action text: **Sans (Arial)**, **Serif (Times)**, **Monospace**, or **Display (bold)**.
4. Set the **Font size** to **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large** depending on how bold you want the text to read.
5. Set your three brand colors with the color pickers: **Background color** (the space behind the products), **Accent color** (highlights and price tags), and **Text color** (your headline and call-to-action).
6. Type a **Default headline**, the phrase that appears on top of most collages, for example "New arrivals are live". You can still change it per collage later.
7. Type a **Default call to action**, the nudge at the bottom of the collage, for example "Shop now".
8. That is it. Each field saves the moment you change it. Open the AI Collage Generator when you are ready to build a collage, and it will start from this look.

## Notes

- Everything here is a default, not a lock. Any collage can override the layout, colors, headline, or call to action at generation time.
- Changes apply to collages you generate next, not to collages already sitting in your Collage Gallery.
- The accent color is a great place to reuse your brand's signature color so price tags and highlights stay consistent across every collage.
- Because Collage Butler is still in beta, small details may change as the feature is polished.
