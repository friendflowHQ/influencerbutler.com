## What Facebook Group Email Collection does

Many Facebook group admins add a participation question to the join form, like "What is your email address? We'll send you daily deals to your inbox too." Every pending request then carries a real, opt-in email address, but reading them one by one and copying each into a list is slow.

Facebook Group Email Collection opens your group's **Participant requests** page, reads the email each person submitted, and drops it into a table. It can approve each request after capturing the email, and it hands the whole list to Pitch Butler, where every contact shows a Facebook source chip.

## Before you start

- **A Facebook account signed in** through Deals Butler.
- **You are an admin** of the group whose requests you want to read.
- Your group's join form asks for an email in a participation question.

## Running your first collection

1. Open **Facebook Group Email Collection** under Facebook Butler.
2. **Group participant requests URL**: paste your group's participant requests URL, for example `https://www.facebook.com/groups/yourgroup/participant_requests`. You can also paste the group's main URL and Butler adjusts it for you.
3. **Facebook account**: pick which signed-in account reads the requests.
4. **Approve requests after capturing email**: on by default. After reading each request's email, Butler clicks Approve to admit the person. Turn it off to only read emails and leave the requests pending.
5. **Send to Pitch Butler automatically**: off by default, since your group members are not brand prospects. Turn it on if you want it. At the end of each run, newly captured emails are added to Pitch Butler.
6. Keep **Watch it run** turned on in the top right of the app so you can see the browser work, then click **Start**.

A Chrome window opens on your group's Participant requests page. Butler reads each request's name and email, saves it to the table, and (if enabled) approves the request before moving to the next.

## Working the results

The results table has one row per captured email:

- **Name**: the person who requested to join.
- **Email**: the address they submitted.
- **Timestamp**: when it was captured.
- **Sent** toggle: flag whether you've reached out already.
- **Pitched**: shows whether this contact is already in Pitch Butler.

Top toolbar buttons:

- **Select visible** and **Clear selection**: pick rows for bulk actions.
- **Send to Pitch Butler**: push the selected rows (or all rows) to Pitch Butler now. This is the same handoff the automatic option does at the end of a run.
- **Mark as sent** and **Unmark sent**: flip the Sent flag on selected rows.
- **Copy table** and **Export CSV**: take the list into a spreadsheet or another tool.
- **Delete selected**, **Delete all**, and **Restore last data**: manage the list, with a one-click undo.

## Sending to Pitch Butler

Captured emails become brand or creator records in Pitch Butler tagged with a **Facebook** source chip, so you can pitch them alongside your other leads and filter by where they came from. Auto-send only adds contacts that aren't already in Pitch Butler, so re-running never creates duplicates.

## Adding emails to your Foyer newsletter

If you use Foyer Butler, you can add the people you collect to your Foyer newsletter list, so your next newsletter reaches them.

1. In the **Foyer newsletter** card, turn on **These people agreed to get emails from me when they gave their email**. Only turn it on if your group's join questions say their email will be used for your newsletter. Nothing is added until this is on, and you will be asked to confirm again every 30 days.
2. Check **How they agreed**. It is saved with your list as your record of consent, so keep it truthful. The default is "Gave their email in my group's join questions, which said it would be used for my newsletter".
3. Click **Add to Foyer newsletter** in the table toolbar. It adds the selected rows, or every row if none are selected.

Want it hands-free? Turn on **Add new emails after each run** to add new emails as soon as a run finishes. To run the whole thing on its own, turn on **Collect on a schedule** and pick how often, from every 3 hours to every 3 days. Each scheduled run collects (and approves, if that is on) and then adds the new emails to the newsletter.

The **Newsletter** column shows where each person stands: **Added**, **Checking** (large lists are checked automatically before they can receive emails, and this finishes on its own), or **Unsubscribed**. Each run adds at most 500 new emails; the rest are added on the next run. Emails to these contacts say they are receiving it because they joined your Facebook group, the first one opens with a short note about who you are and why they are getting it, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

### When someone unsubscribes

Their address goes on a do-not-contact list shared across Influencer Butler. They are skipped when you add emails to the newsletter, and **Pitch Butler will not email them or add them from this table either**. If someone who unsubscribed signs up again from your Foyer page and confirms by email, they come off the list. Someone whose address bounced, or who reported a message as spam, never does.

## Common gotchas

- **No emails found**: your group's join form has no email question, or the requests don't include a plain-text email in the answer.
- **Sign-in prompt or bot check mid-run**: keep Watch it run turned on in the top right of the app, finish the sign-in in the Chrome window, then run again.
- **Approved people leave the queue**: with Approve on, each captured request is admitted and removed from the pending list, so a re-run only sees new requests.
- **The Add to Foyer newsletter button is greyed out**: turn on the confirmation that these people gave you their email, and make sure **How they agreed** is not empty.
- **"Sign in to add emails" or "Set up your Foyer page first"**: the newsletter needs a signed-in Influencer Butler account and a Foyer page. Set up Foyer Butler, then try again.
- **"Held for review"**: a batch of more than 500 new emails is checked before it can receive emails. Contact support if you need help getting a large list cleared.
