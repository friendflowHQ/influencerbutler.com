## What the extension does

The Influencer Butler Chrome extension is a free companion that works while you browse Amazon like normal. It adds five tools:

1. **Video Scanner**: on any product page, a panel shows how many videos the product has and who made them: influencer, brand, or customer. That is the competition picture for the carousel you would be entering.
2. **Content Gap Finder**: on your Amazon order history, one click scans the products you already bought and flags the ones with few or zero influencer videos. You own those products, so they are the fastest videos you can film.
3. **Butler Approved seal**: a product earns the seal when it is actively selling, has an open influencer slot in the video carousel, is in stock, and clears your price floor. Each criterion shows pass or fail so you always know why.
4. **Storefront Checkup**: on your own storefront page, one click checks your videos for missing product tags, over-tagging, and tagged products that have gone unavailable.
5. **Orders Butler**: on your Amazon order history, sync your whole purchase history to your dashboard, the same pull the desktop runner does. It uses whichever Amazon account the browser is signed into, so it also works on an account you manage for someone else.

Everything works without an account. Connecting your license key is optional and adds sync to your web dashboard.

## Install the extension

1. Open the Chrome Web Store listing from influencerbutler.com/extension and click **Add to Chrome**
1. Pin the extension: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Influencer Butler
1. Open any Amazon product page; the Influencer Butler panel appears in the corner

## Read the video panel

1. On a product page, find the floating **Influencer Butler** panel (click its header to collapse or expand it)
1. **Video competition** shows the counts: influencer, brand, and customer videos, plus any the page did not classify
1. **Butler Approved** lists the four criteria with a pass or fail mark for each; tune the thresholds in the popup settings
1. **Break-even math** estimates sales and views needed to cover your time; adjust the commission rate and views to match your niche

## Scan your orders for content gaps

1. Go to your Amazon order history (Returns & Orders)
1. In the panel, click **Scan these orders** ; the extension checks each product one at a time, politely paced
1. Products with few or zero influencer videos get a red **content gap** badge: those are your easiest wins

## Sync your full order history (Orders Butler)

1. Go to your Amazon order history (Returns & Orders), signed into the account whose orders you want: your own, or one you manage such as a family member's
1. In the **Orders Butler** section of the panel, pick a scope: **Only new since last run** for a quick top-up, or **All years (full catch-up)** the first time
1. Click **Sync my orders**; the extension walks your order pages one at a time, politely paced, and records every item to your dashboard
1. Keep the tab open during a full catch-up. If Amazon asks you to sign in again, sign in on the tab and click **Resume**

## Check your storefront

1. Open your own storefront page (amazon.com/shop/yourhandle) and scroll so your videos load
1. Click **Check my storefront** in the panel
1. Review the results: untagged videos earn nothing, over-tagged videos dilute clicks, and unavailable products waste tags

## Sync to your dashboard (optional)

1. Click the extension icon to open the popup
1. Paste your Influencer Butler license key under **Account** and click **Connect** (trial keys work too)
1. Findings now sync automatically; see them under **Extension** in your dashboard at influencerbutler.com/dashboard/extension

## Tips

- The extension never crawls Amazon in the background. Scans only run when you click a scan button, and they pace themselves like a person browsing.
- Orders Butler syncs to your connected license key, so orders you pull while signed into an account you manage land in your own dashboard.
- Your license key is stored only in your browser's local extension storage.
- Set your storefront handle in the popup settings so the extension recognizes your own storefront instantly.
