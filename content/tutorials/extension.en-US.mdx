## What the extension does

The Influencer Butler Chrome extension is a free companion that works while you browse, and it reads the page you already opened so nothing crawls in the background. It layers money signals onto the pages where you decide what to post:

1. **On Amazon product pages**: the Butler Score and Butler Approved seal, Best Sellers Rank with an estimated monthly revenue, a price and rank history sparkline, the video panel (influencer, brand, and customer counts plus an open-slot indicator), deal signals, and your break-even math.
2. **On Amazon search, Best Sellers, deals, idea lists, and brand stores**: a per-tile score with commission-per-sale, estimated units, and a Campaign chip, plus a toolbar to sort and filter the whole grid.
3. **On Creator Connections**: Campaign Radar on the Affiliate+ grid and match scoring on the Sponsored Products for Creators (SPCC) tab, Last Call watch bells, The Butler's Brief, and message templates.
4. **On Creator Hub and your storefront**: Video Money per video, a YouTube status chip, earnings badges, and the Storefront Checkup.
5. **On Walmart and Benable**: the same money-first read, tuned to each site.
6. **Everywhere you post**: your own tagged Amazon link with Get link, optional branded short links, localized links for 12 marketplaces, and the Deal Sites Harvester.

Everything works without an account. Connecting your license key is optional and adds sync to your web dashboard. Pairing the desktop app is optional and adds one-tap campaign accept plus your real earnings on the money tools.

## Install the extension

1. Go to influencerbutler.com/extension and click **Add to Chrome**; the Chrome Web Store listing opens, click **Add to Chrome** again there and confirm
1. Pin the extension: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Influencer Butler
1. Open any Amazon product page; the Influencer Butler panel appears in the corner

The same install works in Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Brave on both Windows and Mac. There is nothing separate to download for either operating system.

## Score every product on a search page

1. Search on Amazon as normal, or open a category page
1. Every result gets a badge with its Butler Score, a rough commission per sale, estimated monthly units and revenue, and whether it is fit for content
1. Use the toolbar above the results to sort by Best Butler Score, to show **Campaign-eligible only**, or to set a minimum price
1. Click **Scan videos on this page** to add each product's influencer-video count, so you can spot the products with an open slot without opening them one by one

## Read the Butler Score, BSR and price history

1. On a product page, find the floating **Influencer Butler** panel (click its header to collapse or expand it)
1. **Butler Score** shows a 0 to 100 number with a hot, warm, or cool band; hover the info tip to see where the points came from
1. **Butler Approved** lists the criteria with a pass or fail mark for each: actively selling, an open influencer slot, in stock, and above your price floor; tune the thresholds in the popup settings
1. **Product snapshot** shows the Best Sellers Rank and its category at a glance
1. **Price history** draws a sparkline of the prices seen for this product and shows the estimated monthly units and revenue that rank implies; when the desktop app is paired it also draws the sales-rank history from the app's deeper time-series
1. **On sale** appears as a Deal chip when the listing shows a strikethrough or deal badge, so you can lead with a discount
1. **Break-even math** estimates sales and views needed to cover your time; adjust the commission rate and views to match your niche

## Read the video panel

1. **Video competition** shows the counts: influencer, brand, and customer videos, plus any the page did not classify
1. The open-slot line tells you whether the upper carousel next to the gallery still has room for an influencer video, which is where a new video actually surfaces
1. **Influencer videos** lists the creators already on the listing, and flags the ones that earn commissions on it
1. Click **Sweep every video** to page through Amazon's own video feed, classify every video it will serve, and split them by upper and lower carousel

## Use Trend Radar

1. Open Amazon's Best Sellers, New Releases, or Movers & Shakers pages
1. Every tile gets a badge with its Butler Score, estimated commission per sale, and video count, plus a **Watch** button
1. Use the toolbar above the grid to sort by trending, rank, Butler Score, or commission, and to show only products with an open video slot

## Spot deals and capture idea lists

1. On **Today's Deals** and Coupons pages, each deal card gets a Butler chip so you can tell a real content opportunity from a thin discount
1. On any creator's **idea list** (amazon.com/shop/handle/list/...), every saved item gets the same score and commission read, so you can size up a competitor's list at a glance
1. Open the **Deal Sites Harvester** from the popup to pull deal roundups from the aggregator sites into one scored list, and send the winners to your desktop app or dashboard

## Scan your orders for content gaps

1. Go to your Amazon order history (Returns & Orders)
1. In the panel, click **Scan these orders**; the extension checks each product one at a time, politely paced
1. Products with few or zero influencer videos get a red **content gap** badge: those are your easiest wins

## Sync your full order history (Orders Butler)

1. Go to your Amazon order history (Returns & Orders), signed into the account whose orders you want: your own, or one you manage such as a family member's
1. In the **Orders Butler** section of the panel, pick a scope: **Only new since last run** for a quick top-up, or **All years (full catch-up)** the first time
1. Click **Sync my orders**; the extension walks your order pages one at a time, politely paced, and records every item to your dashboard
1. Keep the tab open during a full catch-up. If Amazon asks you to sign in again, sign in on the tab and click **Resume**

## Campaign Radar on Creator Connections

1. Sign in at affiliate-program.amazon.com and open **Creator Connections**, then the **View campaigns** grid (the **Affiliate+ campaigns** tab)
1. Every campaign card gets a score badge with chips: products you already own, products you have already earned on, and which marketplaces carry the product; cards that clear your thresholds get an orange outline
1. Use the toolbar above the grid to set your thresholds live (minimum commission, days remaining, and budget) and to show passing campaigns only
1. The **fill meter** on each card shows how full the campaign is; click the **watch bell** to have the Butler watch it, and you get a Last Call notification when it is nearly full so you can accept before it closes
1. Open a campaign to read **The Butler's Brief**: a verdict, why to take it, what to film, and which product to lead with
1. In the campaign message composer, use the **Templates** toolbar to insert a saved message with the placeholders filled in, or save what you typed as a new template; **Brand Keywords** surfaces the terms a brand asked for so you can work them in

## Score the Sponsored Products for Creators tab

1. On the Creator Connections page, switch to the **Sponsored Products for Creators** tab
1. These campaigns have no commission rate; they pay per qualified click, so each card is scored on Amazon's own **Estimated EPC** (earnings per click) and its **budget availability**, plus whether you already own or have earned on the product
1. The **strong picks** filter keeps only the top band, so the best-paying products rise to the top of the tab
1. If a card shows no Butler badge, see the Troubleshooting section below: Amazon changes this tab often, and a score that is missing usually means the extension needs an update to match the new layout

## Accept a campaign from the extension

Accepting needs the desktop app paired today; the extension itself never accepts or declines a campaign on its own.

1. Open the Influencer Butler desktop app. **On Windows:** launch it from the Start menu, or click its icon in the system tray if it is already running. **On Mac:** launch it from Applications, or click its icon in the menu bar or Dock if it is already running
1. Click the extension icon in Chrome, scroll to the **Desktop app** section of the popup, and click **Connect the desktop app**; the desktop app shows a 6-digit code
1. Type that code in the popup and click **Pair**
1. Back on a product page, the **Campaigns** block at the bottom of the product snapshot shows whether the product has a Creator Connections or Sponsored Products campaign and an **Accept** button for each one that is available
1. Click **Accept**; the desktop app confirms the enrollment and the block switches to an **Enrolled** badge

## See what your videos earn (Creator Hub)

1. Open your Creator Hub at amazon.com/creatorhub/manage
1. Each video row gets a **Video Money** readout: what it has earned, its earnings per view, the live commission rate, and a demand badge, so you can tell which videos to reshoot or repost
1. A **YouTube status** chip on each row shows whether the video is already cross-posted to YouTube: green **On YouTube** links to it, and **Not on YouTube** offers an **Upload** button that hands the video to your desktop app to publish
1. The YouTube status and the earnings figures need the desktop app running and paired; the rest of the row works either way

## Check your storefront

1. Open your own storefront page (amazon.com/shop/yourhandle) and scroll so your videos load
1. Each product card picks up an **earnings** badge, and the panel offers **Check my storefront**
1. Review the results: untagged videos earn nothing, over-tagged videos dilute clicks, and unavailable products waste tags

## Walmart

1. On a Walmart product page (walmart.com/ip/...), the panel shows the price, the rating, an estimated commission, a Butler Score, and the Butler Approved checklist read from that page
1. Click **Copy Walmart link** for your affiliate link through Walmart Creator or Mavely; if your Walmart affiliate link is not set up yet, the panel says so and links you to set it up in Settings
1. Walmart search and category pages pick up the same money-first read where available

## Benable

1. Open a Benable list page (benable.com/handle/list); each card that points to an Amazon product picks up a Butler badge with its commission, campaign eligibility, and demand
1. Expand a badge to see enrollment, earnings, and ownership for that product, the same read you get on Amazon itself

## Deep links and branded links

1. On any product page, **Get link** sits pinned at the top of the panel: one tap builds your own tagged Amazon link for the product on screen and copies it
1. The link is a plain Amazon product link with your Associates tag or storefront handle, so on a phone it opens the Amazon app when the app is installed; this works with no account and no sign-in
1. **Scrub link** next to it copies the bare product URL with every affiliate tag and tracking parameter removed, for when you want a clean link
1. For a branded short link, sign in with your license key in the popup and choose Influencer Butler branded links in Settings; Get link then mints a short link you own
1. Open **Link Butler** from the popup to see the click ledger: clicks by day, country, device, and surface, and to repoint a link to a replacement product if the original goes unavailable

## Global Maximizer

1. On a product page, scroll to the **Global reach** section of the panel
1. It lists one row per marketplace (US, CA, UK, AU, DE, FR, IT, ES, JP, IN, MX, BR) with availability, local price, and an estimated commission per sale
1. Click **Get link** on a row for a localized affiliate link for that store, or **Copy all international links** for the whole set
1. Price and availability per market need the Amazon Creator API connected in Settings; the links work without it

## Settings and affiliate routing

Open the extension popup and click **Settings** to reach these.

1. **Tools**: every overlay has an on/off switch, so you can hide any signal you do not use. They are on by default
1. **Affiliate routing** (under Cross-platform): turn on routing to always build links with your best-paying option, set your Associates tag per marketplace, choose your default link provider, and pick Walmart Creator or Mavely for Walmart links
1. **Product pages**: tune the Butler Approved thresholds and the price floor the search and product scores use
1. **Campaigns**: set your Campaign Radar thresholds (minimum commission, days remaining, budget) and your Last Call alert level; auto-accept is off by default and has its own caps
1. **Storefront handle**: set it once so the extension recognizes your own storefront instantly; it is captured automatically the first time you open your Creator Hub
1. **Research and sync**: turn dashboard sync on or off, and choose whether to contribute to the shared product catalogue (off by default)

## Why scans never get throttled

One polite request at a time. Every scan runs through a single serialized fetcher with a 2.5 to 4 second jittered gap, watches for Amazon's robot check and backs off, and caps each run. You never see a screen of death.

- The panel itself fetches nothing: it reads the product page you already opened.
- Order, storefront, and grid scans queue through one fetcher and wait 2.5 to 4 seconds between pages, jittered so the traffic reads like a person browsing.
- If Amazon serves a robot check, the run stops and automatic enrichment pauses for ten minutes instead of hammering the block.
- Every run has a cap (20 orders, 25 storefront videos, 40 deal pages, 200 order-history pages), so a parsing miss can never turn into an unbounded crawl.

## Sync to your dashboard (optional)

1. Click the extension icon to open the popup
1. Paste your Influencer Butler license key under **Account** and click **Connect** (trial keys work too)
1. Findings now sync automatically; see them under **Extension** in your dashboard at influencerbutler.com/dashboard/extension

## Troubleshooting

- **The panel does not appear.** It only shows on pages the extension covers (product, search, Best Sellers, deals, idea lists, brand stores, Creator Connections, Creator Hub, your storefront, Walmart, and Benable), not on the Amazon home page. Reload the page, and confirm the extension is pinned and enabled.
- **A grid shows no badges after the page changed.** Amazon and Walmart redesign their pages often. When a retailer moves things, an overlay can stop matching until the extension is updated. Update the extension from the popup or the Chrome Web Store, and if it persists, use **Feedback Butler** in the popup to report the page so it can be fixed.
- **Earnings, ownership, or Video Money dollars are blank.** Those tools read from the desktop app. Open the Influencer Butler desktop app and pair it (see the "Accept a campaign from the extension" section above); the rest of each panel works without it.
- **Branded links or dashboard sync are missing.** Those need your license key. Paste it under **Account** in the popup and click **Connect**.
- **A scan stopped partway.** Amazon served a robot check, so the run paused for about ten minutes on purpose. Wait, or finish browsing and come back; nothing was lost.

## Tips

- The extension never crawls Amazon in the background. Scans only run when you click a scan button, and they pace themselves like a person browsing.
- Orders Butler syncs to your connected license key, so orders you pull while signed into an account you manage land in your own dashboard.
- Your license key is stored only in your browser's local extension storage.
- Set your storefront handle in the popup settings so the extension recognizes your own storefront instantly.
