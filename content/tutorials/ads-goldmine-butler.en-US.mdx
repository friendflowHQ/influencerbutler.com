## What Ads Goldmine does

Most of the time the question isn't "what products exist?" - it's "which of the products I have access to are *worth* featuring or pitching right now?" Ads Goldmine answers that. It scores each candidate ASIN across several signals:

- **Catalog candidates** (the row source): Ads Goldmine pulls brands and products from the Creator Connections and SPCC catalogs cc-check harvests, plus every ASIN you've personally harvested into your ledger (Daily Commission, Orders Butler, Storefront Butler). So the table fills up even if your own ledger is small.
- **Deal signals** (via the Creators API): Lightning Deals, Prime-exclusive deals, % claimed. The freshness of these is the strongest "post this now" indicator.
- **Creator Connections + SPCC**: read from the catalogs cc-check maintains and confirmed against the global lookup. ASINs with an active campaign or a sponsored offer score higher. This pass also fills in the product title, image, and authoritative SPCC status for catalog brands.
- **Brand-search density**: how many sponsored slots a brand owns on its own category search. Brands buying ad space at scale are signal-rich pitch targets.
- **PDP probe**: for top-ranked brands, walks the product detail page to detect a brand store and count sponsored-carousel slots.

The output is a sortable, filterable table of ASINs with a 0-100 composite score and a plain-language Reason for each row.

## Before you start

- **CC Storefront ID** in the topbar.
- **Creators API key** in API Integrations - required for Deal signals. Without it, the Deal column stays blank and scores rely on the other signals.
- **cc-check has run recently**: Ads Goldmine reads from the CC + SPCC catalogs cc-check maintains.

## Running your first refresh

1. Open **Ads Goldmine** under Amazon Butler.
2. Click **Refresh Now**.

The refresh runs in order:

1. Catalog candidates: enumerate promotable products from the CC + SPCC catalogs (no Creators API key needed).
2. Deal signals: Creators-API deal probe (6h freshness - won't re-run within 6 hours if already fresh; skipped entirely if no Creators API key).
3. Brand-search density (a capped batch of brands per session, 7-day freshness).
4. PDP probe for top-ranked brands (14-day freshness - detects brand stores + sponsored carousels).
5. Creator Connections + SPCC presence is resolved during the row build, no separate probe needed.

A first refresh on a fresh catalog takes several minutes. Subsequent refreshes are minutes-to-seconds because the freshness windows keep most data warm. Deal, Brand Store, and brand-search data fill in gradually across refreshes, since each run probes a capped batch of the highest-priority brands.

## Working the results

The catalog summary above the table tallies **ASINs scored**, **High score (≥50)**, **CC opportunities**, **SPCC opportunities**, and **Brands with stores**.

The results table columns:

- **Score** (0-100): the composite across all signals. Filter with the **Min score** slider.
- **Reason**: a plain-language summary of why the row surfaced and why it's worth pitching (for example, "In your Creator Connections catalog. Up to 8% commission"), composed from the same signals that drive the score.
- **Brand**, **Product**, **ASIN**: the brand, product title, and ASIN.
- **Image**: a product thumbnail. Use the **Image size** control (Small / Medium / Large) above the table to resize every thumbnail; your choice is remembered across restarts.
- **CC**, **SPCC**, **Brand Store**, **Deal**: per-signal columns showing which signals contributed to the score, so you can see at a glance whether a high score comes from a campaign, a sponsored offer, a brand store, or an active deal.
- **Actions**: **Send to Content Butler** queues that ASIN as an unassigned content task.
- **Pitched**: a badge that turns on once you have marked that brand as pitched in Pitch Butler (or moved it further along the pipeline). It updates automatically, so the table always shows who you have already reached out to.

The filter toggles narrow the list: **Only CC active**, **Only SPCC active**, **Has Brand Store**, and **Lightning Deal only**, plus a **Pitched** filter (Pitched / Not pitched), alongside the Min score slider. Filters and column layout auto-save and re-apply next launch.

What to do with high-score ASINs:

- **Send to Content Butler** straight from the table to queue content for it.
- **Schedule them in Deals Influencer Butler** if the score is driven by deal urgency (Lightning / Prime-exclusive).
- **Pitch the brand via Pitch Butler** if the score is driven by brand-search density or a brand store (the brand is spending on ads, so it has budget for influencers too).
- **Feature in Video Reload Butler / Voiceover Butler scripts** for any high-score ASIN.

## Tuning over time

- **Refresh cadence**: once a week is enough for most users. Deal signals have 6h freshness, brand-search/PDP have 7-14 days; weekly refreshes keep everything inside its window.
- **Min score filter**: start at 50 to see only "worth your attention" rows. Drop to 30 if you want a broader pool to evaluate.
- **Add a Creators API key** if the Deal column is empty and you want deal-driven scoring - it's the single biggest source of differentiation between otherwise-similar catalog brands.

## Where the data goes

Probe signals and the catalog candidate set live in the Ads Goldmine workspace's `runtime/` folder under your user-data directory: `ads-goldmine-butler_ads-signal-cache.json` (per-ASIN + per-brand probe results) and `ads-goldmine-butler_ads-goldmine-candidates.json` (the bounded CC + SPCC candidate set). The freshness windows keep file growth bounded.

## Common gotchas

- **"No products yet"**: you haven't clicked Refresh Now, or cc-check hasn't harvested the CC/SPCC catalogs yet. Run cc-check, then Refresh Now.
- **Deal column is blank**: Creators API key missing in API Integrations. Add it and re-refresh.
- **SPCC, Brand Store, or Deal still show "-" for most rows**: those signals fill in gradually. SPCC is resolved on the next refresh; Brand Store and Deal probe a capped batch of the highest-priority brands per run, so coverage grows over several refreshes.
- **Lots of rows scoring 15**: those are brands that are only in your Creator Connections catalog with no other signal yet. Refresh again (and add a Creators API key) to layer on deal, brand-store, and brand-search signals that differentiate them.
