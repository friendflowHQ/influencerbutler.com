## What AI Clone Butler does

AI Clone Butler trains a personal AI model, a "clone," from photos of you. Once trained, it can generate new photos of that clone in any scene you describe, holding or wearing a real product. The product itself isn't AI-hallucinated: your actual product photo (pulled from the ASIN or Walmart item ID you give it) is composited into the generated scene, so the item stays accurate.

This is a manual, on-demand tool. It creates and saves images for you to download; it doesn't post anything on its own.

## Before you start

- A fal.ai API key, added in **AI & Data Keys** in Settings. Training a clone and generating each photo runs on your own key, so both cost you a small amount directly.
- 10 to 20 clear photos of yourself for training.

## Training your clone

1. Open **Your clone**, create and name a clone.
2. Click **Add photos** and upload 10 to 20 clear photos of your face and body.
3. Check the likeness-consent box.
4. Click **Train this clone**. Training runs in the background and takes a few minutes.

You can train more than one clone if you want different looks or personas.

## Creating a photo

1. Open **Create a photo** and pick a trained clone.
2. Describe the scene: what you're doing and where. The more specific, the better the result.
3. Add your product's Amazon ASIN or Walmart item ID, or upload or paste a product photo URL.
4. Click **Create photo**.

Optional settings under **Options** let you adjust shape, quality, and upscaling before you generate.

## A note on cost

Every trained clone and every generated photo uses your own fal.ai key's credits. Check fal.ai's own pricing for current rates before training or generating in bulk.
