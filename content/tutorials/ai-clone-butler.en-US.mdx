## What AI Clone Butler does

AI Clone Butler makes a personal AI "clone" from photos of you. It can then create new photos of you in any scene you describe, holding or wearing a real product. The product itself isn't AI-invented: your actual product photo (pulled from the ASIN or Walmart item ID you give it) is used in the scene, so the item stays accurate.

There is no training step. Your photos are used directly every time you create a picture, so a new clone is ready to use as soon as you add a photo.

This is a manual, on-demand tool. It creates and saves images for you to download; it doesn't post anything on its own.

## Before you start

- An OpenAI API key, added in **AI & Data Keys** in Settings. Each photo you create runs on your own key.
- Optional: a fal.ai key in the same place, if you want finished photos enlarged to about 4K.
- A few clear photos of yourself: face close-ups, waist-up and full-body shots. More and clearer photos give a stronger likeness.

## Setting up your clone

1. Open **Your clone**, create and name a clone.
2. Use **Add photos** under each slot (Face close-ups, Waist up, Full body) to add clear photos of yourself from different angles and lighting.
3. Check the likeness-consent box.

That's it. You can make more than one clone if you want different looks or personas, and photos you add later apply to your very next picture.

## Creating a photo

1. Open **Create a photo** and pick a clone.
2. Describe the scene: what you're doing and where. The more specific, the better the result.
3. Add your product's Amazon ASIN or Walmart item ID, or upload or paste a product photo URL.
4. Click **Create photo**.

Optional settings under **Options** let you adjust shape, quality, and upscaling before you generate.

## Moving your clone to another computer

You never have to add your photos again on a new computer.

1. On the computer that has your clone, pick it in **Your clone** and click **Export clone**. Save the file somewhere you can reach from the other computer, like a USB drive or your own cloud folder.
2. On the other computer, open AI Clone Butler, click **Import clone**, and pick that file.

The clone arrives with all its photos, ready to use. Importing costs nothing and makes no AI calls. If that computer already has the same clone, the copy gets a new name instead of replacing it.

The file holds your photos, so keep it private. To move your whole setup at once, including every clone, use **Move to a new computer** in Settings, under **Data & Backup**.

## A note on cost

Each photo you create uses your own OpenAI key's credits, and the optional 4K enlargement uses your own fal.ai key. Making a clone, adding photos, and exporting or importing a clone cost nothing. Check OpenAI's and fal.ai's own pricing for current rates before creating photos in bulk.
