## What Pitch Butler does

If you're running outreach across Amazon Creator Connections, Levanta, and Instagram DMs, your brand pipeline lives in five different places - each platform's inbox, plus whatever you scribbled in a spreadsheet. Pitch Butler is the workspace that pulls them into one CRM.

Each brand becomes a card. The card moves through stages: **Prospect → Pitched → Replied → Negotiating → Closed**. You log every touchpoint, queue follow-up messages, and search/filter across the whole pipeline. It is a real CRM underneath: a brand is an **account** with its own **contacts** (the people you work with) and **deals** (opportunities with amounts and stages), plus **tasks** with reminders and a **reports** dashboard.

Pitch Butler tracks the pipeline so nothing falls through the cracks. Work it as a list or as a drag-to-advance board, and every message you send from any workspace (Messenger included) rolls up into the brand's history. It can also *send* pitches directly once you connect Gmail (see below), and it auto-advances a brand's stage when a reply lands.

## Before you start

You need at least one source of brands populated. Pitch Butler imports from:

1. **Amazon Creator Connections**: brands you've connected with via CC. Requires CC Storefront ID in the topbar.
2. **Levanta**: brands you've worked with on Levanta. Requires a Levanta account.
3. **Instagram emails**: brand contact emails extracted from your Instagram DMs (via the Instagram Email Collection workspace).

You don't need all three - start with whichever has the most brands. You can add the others later.

## Importing your first brands

1. Open **Pitch Butler** in the left nav.
2. Click **Import CC brands** (the most common starting point). A Chrome window opens, walks your CC dashboard, and pulls each brand you've engaged with - automatically tagged with their current campaign status, last-message date, and any commission rate visible.
3. Or click **Import Levanta emails** for the Levanta side. Same flow.
4. Brands appear as cards in the main grid. Each card shows brand name, source, current stage, and last-touch date.

The first import is the slowest because it walks your full history. Subsequent imports are incremental.

## Send contacts straight from your harvest tables

You don't have to import in bulk. Any results table that harvests brand or creator contacts now has a **Send to Pitch Butler** button and a **Sent to Pitch Butler** column:

- **Instagram Goldmine**: select the creators you want (or send them all), click **Send to Pitch Butler**.
- **Ads Goldmine**: each brand row has its own **Send to Pitch Butler** action.
- **Levanta email extractor**: select rows and send the brands over.

The **Sent to Pitch Butler** column then reflects each contact's real state - **No → Queued → Sent → Replied** - pulled live from Pitch Butler. It's also editable: click the little status dropdown on any row to set it by hand, handy as a lightweight reminder before the real send happens.

Every contact you send arrives tagged with two chips: **where it came from** (Instagram, Ads Goldmine, Levanta, Creator Connections) and **how it was found** (Hashtag Search, Seed profiles, Suggested profiles, Ads Goldmine). Those chips show on the brand card and in the detail pane, so months later you still know the origin of every lead.

## Working a brand card

Click any card to open its detail view:

- **Status**: Prospect → Pitched → Replied → Negotiating → Closed → Dead. Set it by hand, in a bulk action, or by dragging the card between columns in the board view (below).
- **Message history**: every touchpoint shows here as a timestamped row, now pulled from every workspace you actually message in, not just Pitch Butler. A brand you messaged in Messenger shows its Messenger touchpoints here with a short preview. Rows that came from a real thread are clickable: click one to jump straight into that conversation in the source workspace.
- **View in Collab**: when a brand you pitched replies and a collaboration card gets created, the two are linked. The brand card gets a **View in Collab** button that jumps to the board.
- **Open in Instagram / Open in Messenger**: when a brand has an Instagram handle or an existing message thread, a one-click hand-off takes you to the workspace that sends, ready to message.
- **Contacts**: a brand is an account, so add the people you work with (name, role, email, phone). Everything auto-saves as you type.
- **Deals**: track opportunities as real records, not just a single number. Each deal has a name, amount, stage (Prospect, Qualified, Negotiating, Won, Lost) and an expected close date. The **Pipeline** total above the list sums the amount of every open deal, so it reflects what is actually in play.
- **Deal value**: a quick single-number estimate for brands you have not built deals for yet. Once a brand has real deals, those take over the pipeline math.
- **Queue message**: drafts a message in your default template. Type `{{brand}}` and it fills in the brand name when queued; wrap wording choices in `{a|b}` and Pitch Butler picks one so no two pitches read identically. Pitch Butler tracks "queued" as a touchpoint, and sends directly once you connect Gmail (below).
- **Tags**: free-form labels you apply across brands. Useful for filtering (e.g. "hot lead", "seasonal", "spring 2026").

## Four ways to work: List, Board, Tasks, Reports

The tabs at the top of the grid switch between four views, and your choice is remembered next time you open Pitch Butler:

- **List**: the searchable, filterable roster of every brand.
- **Board**: a pipeline with one column per status and a count on each. Drag a brand card from one column to the next to advance it (drop it on **Negotiating** and it's negotiating).
- **Tasks**: your to-do list across every brand (see below).
- **Reports**: the dashboard (see below).

## Tasks and reminders

Open the **Tasks** tab to see everything you owe, grouped into **Overdue**, **Due today**, and **Upcoming**, with a **Done** section underneath. Add a task with a title, a type (to-do, call, email, meeting, note), and a due date; leave **Remind me** checked and Pitch Butler pops a desktop notification when it comes due, even if the app is in the background. Click the notification and it jumps you straight back to the Tasks tab. New tasks attach to the brand you have selected, and the tab shows a running count of what is overdue or due today so nothing slips.

## Reports dashboard

The **Reports** tab is your pipeline at a glance:

- **KPI cards**: pipeline value, open deals, deals won, win rate, average deal size, average sales-cycle length, response rate, and tasks due.
- **Conversion funnel**: how many deals sit at each stage from Prospect through Won, so you can see where things stall.
- **Deals over time**: a twelve-week trend of deals created versus deals won.

Everything updates live as you add contacts, move deals, and close business.

## Import from CSV

Already have a lead list in a spreadsheet? Click **Import CSV** and pick the file. Pitch Butler reads common column names automatically (name/brand/company, email, Instagram handle, role, phone, tags, deal value, deal stage, and status), so an export from almost anywhere lands without fiddly column mapping. Each row becomes a brand, plus a contact and a deal when those columns are present, and you get a count of what came in. It pairs with **Export CSV** for a clean round-trip.

Add a **Status** column when you've already pitched some of these brands outside the app. Pitch Butler reads the six pipeline statuses (Prospect, Pitched, Replied, Negotiating, Closed, Dead) and also understands common wording, so "Sent" or "Contacted" becomes Pitched, "Won" becomes Closed, and "Lost" becomes Dead. Rows with a blank or unrecognized status simply start as Prospect. If the brands are already in Pitch Butler, you don't need a re-import: select them in the list and use the bulk **Set status** action instead.

## Add a brand by hand

Not every brand comes from an import. Click **Add brand** in the header, type a name (plus an optional email or Instagram handle), and it drops into the pipeline as a manual prospect, ready to work like any other card.

## Never miss a follow-up

Turn on the **Needs follow-up** toggle in the filter bar to see only brands you pitched, that never replied, and that have gone quiet for more than three days. The toggle carries a live count so you can see the size of the pile at a glance. Open one and click **Send follow-up now** to queue the nudge in a single step.

## Export to CSV

Click **Export CSV** to download the brand list you're currently looking at (filters and all) as a spreadsheet: name, status, emails, sources, tags, deal value, and the last touch with its channel. Handy for a quick pipeline review outside the app or for sharing with a manager.

## Filtering and search

The top filter bar lets you slice the brand list by:

- **Source** (CC / Levanta / Instagram / Ads Goldmine / manual).
- **Stage** (any of the five).
- **Tag** (any custom tag you've applied).
- **Last-touched window** (within X days).
- **Free-text search** across brand name, notes, and tag content.

Useful workflows:

- "All brands tagged 'hot lead' that haven't been touched in 7+ days" → who to follow up with this week.
- "Source = CC, Stage = Replied" → who's actually responding from your CC outreach.
- "Source = Levanta, Stage = Negotiating" → active deals to close.

Plus the **Needs follow-up** toggle (see above) for brands that have gone quiet after a pitch.

Select several cards with their checkboxes to run a **bulk action**: set the same stage across everyone you picked in one click (e.g. move a whole batch of fresh leads to Prospect, or a set of dead ends to Dead).

## Connect Gmail (beta)

Connect your Gmail account and Pitch Butler can send pitches from your own address, so replies come back to *you* and the thread lives in your normal inbox and Sent folder.

1. In the Pitch Butler header, click **Connect Gmail**. Your browser opens Google's sign-in; approve the access.
2. Once connected, pitches you queue can send through Gmail, and **Sync now** scans your recent mail to match replies and sent messages back to the right brand.
3. When a brand replies, its stage advances to **Replied** automatically, and the **Sent to Pitch Butler** column on your harvest tables updates to match.

We ask only for permission to send mail and to read message headers (sender, recipient, subject, date) so we can match replies. We never read message bodies, and nothing but the matched status is stored.

This feature rolls out gradually: while Google reviews our access request it's limited to invited test accounts, so you may see a "coming soon" note until it reaches you.

## Tuning over time

- **Re-import cadence**: once a week is plenty. Imports are incremental and only pick up new brands or new touchpoints since last sync.
- **Tags**: invest 5 minutes setting up a small tag vocabulary (3-5 tags) that maps to your workflow. More than that becomes noise.
- **Notes per touchpoint**: short, factual. "Asked for rates" or "Sent product samples June 12" reads better in a year than "Good call!"

## Where the data goes

Pitch Butler stores in the preseed cache. Backups happen automatically with the rest of the app's settings backup flow.

## Common gotchas

- **"Import CC brands" doesn't see anything**: your CC Storefront ID isn't set in the topbar. Set it and re-import.
- **Duplicate brand cards after a Levanta import**: Pitch Butler dedupes on brand name; if Levanta and CC list a brand with slightly different spellings ("Acme Co" vs "Acme Co."), they'll appear as two cards. Merge manually (drag-to-merge in the card view).
- **Instagram email import shows no brand name**: Instagram emails sometimes don't expose the brand name in the DM thread. Edit the card and add the brand name manually.
