## Welcome

Influencer Butler is a desktop app that does the repetitive parts of being an Amazon influencer for you: posting your storefront content, accepting brand campaigns, harvesting commissions, and messaging brands. You set it up once, and the Butlers do the work in the background.

This page walks you through everything a brand-new user needs to get running. Take it one step at a time. You can come back to any step later.

- [ ] I have the app installed and open {#installed}

## 1. Sign in

The first time you open the app it asks you to sign in.

1. Enter the same email you used when you bought Influencer Butler.
2. We email you a one-time sign-in link. Open that email and click the link.
3. The app catches the link and signs you in automatically. You do not need a password.

If the email does not arrive in a minute or two, check your spam folder and make sure you used your purchase email.

- [ ] I signed in with my purchase email {#signed-in}

## 2. Pick your goal

Right after sign-in, a short setup walkthrough asks what you most want to do first, such as posting your videos or photos to other country marketplaces, or auto-accepting brand campaigns. Pick the one that fits you best.

Do not worry about getting this perfect. It just decides which Butler we set up first. You can change your goal or set up the others any time later.

- [ ] I picked a goal in the walkthrough {#goal}

## 3. Connect your Amazon storefront

Most Butlers work from your Amazon Influencer storefront, so the app needs to be signed in to Amazon inside its own built-in browser.

The first time a Butler needs Amazon, a browser window opens and asks you to log in. This is normal and expected. Log in the way you always do, including any code Amazon texts you. The app remembers the session, so you usually only do this once.

Tip: log in slowly and finish every prompt Amazon shows. If you close the window too early, the app has to ask again next time.

- [ ] I logged into Amazon inside the app when it asked {#amazon-login}

## 4. Connect Creator Connections (optional, for brand campaigns)

If you want the app to accept brand campaigns and submit your content links for you, you need Creator Connections access on your Amazon Influencer account, plus a set of API credentials. This one takes a few extra minutes and is worth it if brand deals are your goal.

## 5. Let your Butlers warm up

Once you are connected, the walkthrough starts the first run for you. The first time a Butler runs it gathers your storefront and recent activity, so give it a few minutes. After that first pass, everything is faster.

![The setup walkthrough running your Butlers in the background, with a note that you can step away and we will email you when it is done](/assets/setup/onboarding_walkthrough_auto_setup_view.png)

You do not have to sit and watch. You can close the walkthrough and let it work. We email you when it is done, or right away if a Butler needs you for a moment.

## 6. Get comfortable with the main screen

A quick tour so nothing feels mysterious:

- **Left menu**: your Butlers. Each one handles a different job. Click a name to open it.
- **Top bar**: your running totals, including **Hours Saved** and **Money Saved**, and your storefront ID. Set your **hourly rate** there so the savings numbers reflect what your time is worth.
- **Help & Tutorials** in the left menu: this guide, plus a short how-to for every Butler and a free beginner course on the Amazon Influencer Program.

## 7. Run your first real task

Pick one Butler that matches your goal and let it do a full run. For example, open the Butler the walkthrough set up for you and start it. Watch it work once so you know what to expect, then let it run on its own from then on.

That is the whole idea: you set the direction, the Butlers do the busywork.
