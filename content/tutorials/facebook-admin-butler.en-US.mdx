## What Facebook Admin Butler does

Facebook Admin Butler finds Facebook groups that have no active admin and helps you become their admin. It searches Facebook by your keywords, filters the results by group size and type, joins the groups you are not in yet, and when a group offers you the admin role (Facebook does this for adminless groups you belong to), it accepts the offer so you become an admin. It uses the same Facebook session as Deals Butler, so you sign in once.

Owning admin of an active niche group is valuable: you control the posts, the pinned content, and the audience, which is a natural home for your deals and links.

## Before you start

- **Sign in to Facebook in the app first.** Butler acts from your real Facebook session. Sign in to your accounts in **Deals Butler** under Social Destinations, then they appear in the **Facebook account** picker here.
- **Use a personal profile, not a Page.** A Page or business account cannot become a group admin, so Facebook rejects the request. Pick a personal Facebook profile in the **Facebook account** field.
- **Becoming admin can take a day or two.** Facebook does not always hand over the role instantly. Groups that are still waiting show under **Awaiting grant** and are re-checked automatically on every run.

## How to use it

1. In the left menu, open **Facebook Butler**, then **Facebook Admin Butler**.
2. Confirm **Facebook Admin Butler is active** is checked. Turning it off pauses both manual and scheduled runs.
3. In **Facebook account**, choose the personal profile that should act.
4. In **Keywords**, type the terms that describe your niche, one per line (for example: chicken keeping, urban homestead, backyard gardening). These are used both to search for new groups and to filter the groups you already belong to.
5. Under **Group size**, set **Minimum members** and **Maximum members** to focus on the groups you want. Leave either at 0 for no limit.
6. Under **Group type**, keep **Public groups** and **Private groups** checked, or uncheck one to skip it.
7. Under **How it finds groups**, choose your passes: **Scan groups I already joined** looks for admin offers in your existing groups, and **Search by keyword and join new groups** finds fresh ones. **Join groups I am not in yet** lets it join automatically, and **Also request to join private groups** adds private groups (those joins stay pending until approved).
8. Under **Daily limits**, set **Max admin accepts per day**, **Max joins per day**, and **Results to check per keyword**. Lower numbers are safer for your account.
9. Turn on **Watch it run** (the eye icon) if you want the browser to come to the front so you can follow along.
10. Click **Start Facebook Admin Butler**. Watch progress in the live status line and the **Activity** log, and stop any time with **Stop**.
11. Optional: under **Scheduler**, set a **Start time** and a **Repeat every** interval, then click **Enable Schedule** to keep it running on autopilot.

## Notes

- **Facebook Admin Butler is in beta.** Some steps depend on Facebook's current layout, which changes often, so results can vary. If something looks off, run it once with **Watch it run** on so you can see what happens.
- **Account safety first.** Facebook limits accounts that join or act too fast. Keep the daily limits modest, spread big keyword sweeps across several runs rather than checking thousands at once, and add more keywords instead of raising **Results to check per keyword** too high.
- **Awaiting grant is normal.** Adminless groups often take a day or two to finalize the handover. Leave those groups alone and let the next run re-check them.
- **Your progress at a glance.** The summary shows **Groups you admin**, **Awaiting grant**, and **Joined, watching**. Turn on **Email me a daily summary** to get a once-a-day recap, and set a different address in the email field if you want it sent elsewhere.
- Every setting auto-saves as you type, so there is no Save button.
