## What it is

Butler AI is an on-demand assistant that knows Influencer Butler. The Chrome extension gives you a shortcut to it, so when a question comes up while you are browsing Amazon you can get an answer right away instead of hunting through help pages. It pulls its answers from the same tutorials you are reading now, so the steps it gives you are the real ones.

It is an AI assistant, not a person. It is great for "how do I..." and "show me..." questions. When you want a human, it points you to book a real demo or support call.

## Open it from the extension

1. Click the **Influencer Butler** icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the popup
1. Click **AI Assistant**
1. A new tab opens with the assistant. Type your question in the box and press Enter

The assistant is the same brain used in your web dashboard and the desktop app, so the answers match wherever you ask.

## Ask right on the page

You do not have to leave Amazon. A round **💬** button sits in the bottom-right corner on the pages the extension works on. Click it to chat with Butler AI in a compact panel, right there on the page, without opening a new tab. The full-tab assistant, with voice, is still one click away from the popup.

## What it can answer

- **How a tool works**: "What does the Video Scanner tell me?" or "How does Butler Approved decide?" It explains the extension's tools and where to find them.
- **Exact setup steps**: "How do I sync my orders?" or "Where do I paste my license key?" It looks up the real click path and walks you through it.
- **Show you the screen**: when a tutorial has a screenshot of what it is describing, the answer includes that screenshot so you can see where you are going.
- **Pricing and plans**: what is free, what Pro unlocks, and how the trial works.

## Talk it through by voice

Prefer talking to typing? Click the **Voice** button in the assistant. Your browser asks for microphone access, and permission to reach the voice service, the first time. Allow both, then just start talking and the assistant talks back. Voice works best in a recent Chrome on a computer with a microphone. If the microphone is blocked, keep typing instead.

## Report a bug or idea

Open the chat bubble and click **🐛 Report a bug** under the chat. Pick a type (bug, feature, or question), add a title and details, and optionally attach screenshots: click **Attach screenshots**, paste an image with Ctrl+V, or click **Capture this page** to grab the current tab. Leave **Attach diagnostic details** on so we get the context that speeds up a fix, add your email if you want a reply, and send. It goes straight to the team, and anonymous reports are welcome too.

Your past reports are under the **My reports** tab in the same view. You can also still send a quick note from the extension popup's Feedback Butler.

## What it cannot do

- It cannot click things for you or sign in to your Amazon account. It gives you the exact click path and you do the clicking.
- It does not give financial or investment advice.
- If it is not sure, it says so and offers a tutorial link or a human call rather than guess.

## When you want a person

At any time, use the **Talk to a human** link in the assistant to book a demo or support call with the team. The assistant also offers this whenever it reaches the limit of what it can help with.

## A note on privacy

Butler AI answers your questions and never touches your Amazon or Instagram logins. A short summary of the conversation may be saved so the team can follow up if you asked for something they need to handle. Voice sessions have a time limit so a session cannot run on forever.
