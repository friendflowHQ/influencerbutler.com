## What AMZ Deals Finder does

AMZ Deals Finder is the Amazon store inside Deal Posting. It searches Amazon for deals that match your keywords and your Filter Recipe, and it adds Amazon-only tools for bringing in deals from deal pages, spreadsheets, coupons and your own Idea Lists. Captions, destinations and the posting schedule are shared with every store, so they are covered in the [Deal Posting tutorial](/help/tutorials/deals).

## Set it up from zero

1. **Connect Amazon.** Open **Amazon Butler › Settings**, fill in your Amazon Creators API fields and press **Test Connection**. The **Partner Tag** must be your Amazon Associates tracking ID (it looks like yourtag-20), not a storefront ID. This is all the keyword search needs. It does not use your Amazon sign-in. If Amazon has not approved your account for the Creators API yet, the same Settings page has a backup credentials button under the Test Connection result. See the [Amazon Butler tutorial](/help/tutorials/amazonbutler) for the rest of that page.
2. **Check your link tags.** In **Amazon Butler › Settings** set your storefront ID and tracking ID, and in **Deeplink Butler › Settings** pick your DeepLink Routing. A deal whose link would earn you nothing is held, not posted.
3. **Sign in to Amazon in the app browser (only for the browser tools).** Promotions, coupons, Search Deal Pages, Auto Idea Lists, Idea List Rotation and the Promo Code Hub scan open Amazon pages. Run the storefront ID check in **Amazon Butler › Settings** to sign in. If a sign-in window does not show, turn on **Watch it run** in the top bar, sign in once, then run again.
4. **Add keywords.** Open **Amazon Butler › AMZ Deals Finder › Keywords**. Press **Suggest keywords by niche** to fill months from 17 starter niches (about 20 per niche each month), or type your own. Each store keeps its own keyword lists.
5. **Run a first search.** Open **Amazon Butler › AMZ Deals Finder › Search Deals**, keep the Filter Recipe defaults and press the run button at the top.
6. **Send and post.** Send the deals you like to the queue (or leave Send mode on Auto). Turn on **Post deals for me** in **Deals Butler › Deal Posting › Scheduler**. New to all of it? [Guided Setup](/help/tutorials/deals-guided-setup) does these steps for you.

Optional: AI keyword generation and AI captions use your own OpenAI key. [How to get one](/help/tutorials/openai-api-setup).

## Every setting

Starting values below are what a fresh panel shows. A value you saved earlier, or one Guided Setup chose, wins.

### Search Deals: the run

- **Source** starts on Amazon. Other choices mix in Walmart or TikTok Shop. Leave it on Amazon for this workspace.
- **Send mode** starts on Auto: found deals go to the Deals workspace at harvest and post on your Scheduler settings. Pick Manual if you want to review and send them yourself.
- **Top up** refills the list toward your Max deals harvested target. It never adds a deal below your Min percent off.
- **Search for new deals on a clock** (auto-search) starts off. Turn it on, then choose how often (1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours, or a time and days of your choice). Picking a time alone does not start it. Each store has its own schedule, and a month with no keywords makes every scheduled run skip.
- **Search marketplaces** lets you also search other Amazon countries. A country unlocks only when its own Creators API credentials are connected. Marketplace mix sets how the results are shared between countries.
- **Include Amazon promotion deals** (off) reads Amazon promotion pages found from your keywords or from links you paste. **Include Amazon coupon deals** (off) reads Amazon's coupons page. Both need Amazon signed in, and coupons are never clipped for you.

### Filter Recipe

- **Optimizing for** (Clicks, Commissions or Both) sets several filters in one click.
- **Min price** 5 and **Max price** 9000: sale prices outside the range are skipped. Both labels show in English in every language.
- **Minimum percent off** starts at 35. It is a hard floor, and it is checked again right before posting. Set it to 0 to keep every deal.
- **Maximum percent off** starts blank (no cap). Use it to drop discounts that are too good to be true.
- **If a deal changes before it posts** starts on Skip it quietly. Hold it for review keeps it in your Upcoming list instead.
- **Min review count** 100 and **Min star rating** 4.0. Reviews and stars arrive in a second lookup, so a deal can show them blank for a moment.
- **Skip low stock**, **Verify every deal before auto-posting** (off) and **Hold upcoming deals until they go live** (on, so deals Amazon has not made live yet wait).
- **Real discounts only** (on) drops products with no real markdown. **Avoid 0-commission products and categories** (on). **Block adult content** (on).
- **Only CreatorConnections and SPCC deals** (off) and **Minimum commission ($)** (blank). Creator Connections and SPCC deals are always kept when you set a minimum commission.
- **Exclude third-party sellers** (on) keeps items sold by Amazon. If Amazon does not report a seller, the deal is kept.
- **Mix in full-price finds** (off, under Full price finds) lets undiscounted products in, up to your Max price. It does nothing until a Max price is set.
- **Keyword include** and **Keyword exclude**: include keeps only titles with one of your words, exclude drops titles with any of them. Exclude starts with a long list (used and refurbished terms and some risky brands). **Skip ebooks (Kindle and other digital books)** and **Add brand-risk pack** edit that list for you.
- **Skip recently posted (days)** starts at 2, **Skip recently harvested (days)** at 0 and **Skip deals you deleted (days)** at 7. Raise the first if the same deal comes back.
- **Max deals per keyword** starts at 2. **Total max keywords** follows the size of your biggest keyword list until you type a number. **Max deals harvested** only trims a search, it never adds to one.
- **Top up to reach your target** (off) fills a short harvest with the best deals that nearly missed. **Remove repeat look-alike products** (on, 55 percent) drops look-alike titles.
- **Result priority** ranks SPCC, Creator Connections, Highest commission and Best Deal. Drag a chip below the Not prioritized divider to stop using it.
- **Subscribe & Save check** (off) opens product pages in the browser to look for extra discounts, up to 25 pages per search.

### Keywords

- Twelve month boxes, one phrase per line or separated by commas. Each search uses the current month, or today's day bucket when that day has its own list.
- **Use this month's keywords** (on) and **Use custom keywords** (off) choose the sources.
- **Suggest keywords by niche** fills the months you pick from the 17 niches. **Seasonal Keywords** adds packs for seasons, US and UK holidays and Prime Day, to a month or a custom list.
- AI keyword generation needs an OpenAI key. Export and import keywords moves lists between computers (.json keeps everything, .csv keeps month, day and keyword).
- Keywords belong to a workspace. AMZ Deals Finder, WAL Deals Finder and each clone keep separate lists. **Copy keywords** moves them across, and **Restore my saved setup** shows only when an older Deal Posting hub still holds yours.

### Search Deal Pages

Open **Amazon Butler › AMZ Deals Finder › Tools › Search Deal Pages**. Paste up to 20 Amazon deal page links (or product links, which are used directly) and press **Search pages**. It needs Amazon signed in and the Creators API connected. Only products that are on sale are added. **When** starts on Save as drafts to review, so nothing posts until you press Schedule all drafts in the Deals table. Pick Queue to post on the next round to skip that step.

### Import list

Open **Amazon Butler › AMZ Deals Finder › Tools › Import list**. It reads .csv, .xlsx and .xlsm files with an asin column or a /dp/ link per row (up to 10,000 rows and about 8 MB). Your prices are trusted as written, so there is no live price check. Extra columns are ignored, and promo code columns are read.

- **Import a new list on a clock** (off) watches a folder you choose and imports the newest .csv or .xlsx file on a schedule (7:00 every day to start). A file already imported is skipped, and **Import now** forces it. Deals arrive as drafts unless you change **When imported**.

### Auto Idea Lists and Idea List Rotation

- **Auto Idea Lists** builds Amazon Idea Lists from your harvested deals. **Build Idea Lists after each search** starts off. Choose one list per keyword, one top-deals list, or let AI group them (10 products per list to start). It needs Amazon signed in on the chosen country site and Creator Connections.
- **Idea List Rotation** posts your existing Idea Lists on a schedule (off to start). **Don't repeat a list for at least** starts at 20 hours. The order starts as In order, and the caption starts as your own text with the list name and link.
- **Seasonal list refresh** is in beta and is not available to you yet.

### Promo Code Hub

Open **Amazon Butler › AMZ Deals Finder › Tools › Promo Code Hub**. Add promo codes one at a time, paste or upload many, or scan the Creator Connections campaigns you accepted. The Deals Hub list below is a copy our team publishes before and during each deal event. Reload fetches the latest copy, and **Add to post** or **Copy my link** use your own tag.

### Always-Post and Add a Deal

Always-Post keeps chosen products on a repeating schedule, and **Add a Deal** adds one product by link or ASIN. Both work the same as in the other stores, see the [Deal Posting tutorial](/help/tutorials/deals).

## Common gotchas

- **Deals below my Minimum percent off keep showing up, or good ones disappear.** The floor is exact. A deal under it is dropped at search time and held at posting time as Held: below your min discount. Top up never adds one below it. Lower the slider, or set it to 0, to see more.
- **A deal is held as full price though it is on sale.** The hold uses what was saved when the deal was found. Right before posting, Butler asks Amazon for the live offer and posts with today's numbers if it is on sale now and meets your Minimum percent off and Min price. If it is on sale but below your floor, it stays held. **Post at full price** releases it as it is.
- **Few results, most from Amazon itself.** Exclude third-party sellers is on by default. The search card says when most results were removed by it.
- **The same deal showed up twice.** A product already waiting in your queue (draft, scheduled or held) is not added again when you send it from the extension. After it posts, **Skip recently posted** (2 days) decides when it may return. Raise it if it returns too soon.
- **A deal from the Chrome extension is only a draft.** Extension and phone deals start as drafts unless the extension's own settings ask for another place. Change the default on **Deals Butler › Deal Posting › Scheduler**, under **Deals from the Chrome extension** and **Deals from the phone app**. Nothing moves a scheduled deal back to a draft on its own.
- **A date with a ~ in the Deals table.** It is an estimated send time from the deal's place in the queue and your schedule, not a fixed time.
- **My keywords are gone after an update.** Keyword lists belong to one workspace. Check that you are in AMZ Deals Finder and not the Deal Posting hub or WAL Deals Finder, then use **Restore my saved setup** if it shows.
- **Auto-search runs but finds nothing.** The month probably has no keywords. A notification and a note under the auto-search card say so.
- **Amazon pages stopped loading.** If Amazon asks to confirm a person, the Amazon butlers pause (idea lists, deal pages, Creator Connections). Deal and social posts keep going. Click **Open Amazon** in the top banner and tick the box.
- **Deals from a list or deal page do not post.** They are saved as drafts by default. Use Schedule all drafts.

## If you see this message

- **"Amazon is not connected yet"**: the Creators API fields are empty. Fill them in on Amazon Butler › Settings and press Test Connection.
- **"Amazon blocked every search (error 403)"**: Amazon refused every request. Check that Partner Tag is your tracking ID (yourtag-20), not a storefront ID. If it is right, Amazon may not have approved your account yet, so try the backup credentials button.
- **"Your search text is too long for Amazon"**: shorten the text in the Keyword include box (the message calls it Title must include) or clear it.
- **"Amazon paused your searches for a moment"**: Amazon limits searches over a short time. Wait a few minutes or lower Total max keywords. A smaller note says Amazon paused some of your keywords.
- **"Could not reach Amazon"**: press Test Connection to see the exact error.
- **"No keywords to search"** and the status Needs keywords: add keywords for this month on the Keywords tab.
- **"Nothing survived your filters"**: loosen price, percent off, reviews or stars. The full breakdown button shows which filter removed the most.
- **"Most results came from third-party sellers"**: turn off Exclude third-party sellers to see them.
- **"A sign-in page blocked this search"** and **"A "verify you're human" check blocked this search"**: turn on Watch it run, sign in or tick the box once in the window that opens, and search again.
- **Auto-search is on, but this store has no keywords for this month, so each scheduled search will skip. Add some in this store's Keywords tab.**
- **Marketplace not connected**: a country you ticked has no Creators API credentials. Connect them, or untick it.
- **Held: full price**: no real discount. Press Post at full price if you want it anyway.
- **Held: below your min discount** and **Held: above your max discount**: its discount is outside your Minimum or Maximum percent off. Change the filter, or press Post anyway.
- **Held: deal changed**: the price, stock or discount changed since it was found. Review it, or press Post anyway.
- **Held: off theme**: its keyword is not in this workspace's list. Add the keyword, or press Post anyway.
- **Held: link not attributed**: fix your storefront ID or tracking ID. "A deal was not posted: its link opened Amazon without your affiliate tag, so it would have earned you nothing. Set your storefront ID or tracking ID." and "A deal was not posted: its link opened the Amazon home page, not the product. Check your affiliate tag." mean the same family of problem.
- **Saved for review**: you added it as a draft. It waits until you schedule it.
- **"Amazon asked you to sign in or showed a robot check. Clear it once in the window that opened, then search again."**: clear the check once in the window that opened.
- **"No deals added. None of the products on those pages are on sale right now."**: nothing on those pages is discounted today.
- **"No deals added. Make sure each row has an Amazon ASIN (an asin column or a /dp/ link)."**: the file needs an asin column or /dp/ links.
- **"That file was already imported. Drop a new list and try again."**: the same file was imported before. Use Import now to force it.
- **"Every list is still cooling down. Nothing to post right now."**: every list is inside its cooldown (20 hours to start). Lower the cooldown.
- **"Amazon is not signed in on the Butler browser, so your storefront could not be read. Open Amazon Butler, go to Settings, and run the storefront ID check to sign in. Then try Refresh my idea lists again."**
- **"Estimated send time, based on your schedule."** is the tooltip on a ~ date.

In the Console Log (which is always in English), the lines worth knowing are `Creators API credentials not configured`, `skipped, Amazon returned an eligibility block (HTTP 403)`, `skipped, Amazon is rate limiting product searches (HTTP 429)`, `Search complete. Found N unique deal(s)`, `Filter breakdown: skipped N candidate(s)` and `Top up skipped N near-miss deal(s) below your Min % off`.

## Short FAQ

- **Do I need to be signed in to Amazon to search keywords?** No. The keyword search uses your Creators API credentials only. Sign-in matters for the browser tools in step 3.
- **Can I search other countries?** Yes. Tick them under Search marketplaces once their credentials are connected.
- **Does an Amazon pause stop my posts?** No. It pauses Amazon page tasks only.
- **Where do I turn posting on?** In the Deal Posting hub, not here: **Deals Butler › Deal Posting › Scheduler**.
- **Why can I not see Seasonal list refresh working?** It is in beta and locked for now.
