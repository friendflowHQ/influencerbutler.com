## What Walmart Repost does

Walmart Repost takes the content you already have on your Amazon storefront and republishes it to your Walmart Creator storefront, so your Walmart storefront fills itself in without extra filming or editing. There are two butlers that work the same way:

- **Video Repost** (nav label "Video Repost") for your storefront videos.
- **Photo Repost** for your storefront photos.

The one rule both follow: a product is only reposted when it is the **exact same item** on Walmart, matched by the product's barcode (UPC/GTIN). If there is no exact match, it is skipped. You never post a video or photo against the wrong product.

## Before you start

Repost pulls from your Amazon storefront content. If your feed looks empty, harvest it first with **Storefront Butler**, then come back.

You also need to be signed into **Walmart Creator** (creator.walmart.com). This is a one-time step, and the same sign-in is shared by Video Repost, Photo Repost, and your Walmart deal links.

## The fastest way: Set it up

Each Walmart Repost panel has a **Set it up** button in the top row. Click it and the guided wizard walks you through everything:

1. **Sign in to Walmart Creator.** The wizard checks whether you are already signed in. If you are not, it opens a normal Chrome window so you can sign in. If Walmart shows its Robot or human check, press and hold in that window until it passes. When you see your Creator dashboard, close the window (or click I'm signed in) and the app confirms your sign-in.
2. **How to post.** Choose whether to review each match before it posts, or let matches post automatically.
3. **How many per run.** Set a cap so each run posts at most that many items.
4. **Skip out-of-stock.** Skip items that are out of stock on Walmart instead of trying to post them.
5. **Watch it run.** Leave this on to see the browser work the first time so you can step in if needed.
6. **Schedule.** Optionally have it run once a day, every 3 days, or once a week, all on its own.

The wizard fills in the same settings you see on the panel, so you can fine-tune anything later. You can also ask the AI Assistant to set up Walmart Repost, and it opens the same wizard.

## Reviewing matches before they post

If you chose to review, each run gathers the exact matches it found and holds them for you. Open the review list, keep the ones you want, and click **Approve and post**. Approved pairs publish to your Walmart storefront and are remembered so they are never posted twice.

If you chose automatic posting, matches publish as they are found, still only on an exact barcode match.

## Signing in again later

You normally sign in once. If reposting ever says you are signed out, use the **Walmart sign-in** button in the panel, finish signing in, then close the window or click **I'm signed in**. Your session is saved so you do not have to repeat it every run. If Walmart keeps showing its robot check, or posting still says you need to sign in, click **Reset Walmart sign-in** in Walmart Butler, then Settings, and sign in once more in the fresh window.

## Photo Repost works the same way

Photo Repost is the twin of Video Repost for your storefront photos. It has its own **Set it up** button and the same steps. Both share your single Walmart Creator sign-in, so once you are signed in, both are ready.
