## Who the Power User plan is for

The Power User plan is for creators who already know the Amazon Influencer Program and want the whole toolbox: every butler, every hub, unlimited AI, and direct access to the team. If you chose **I know what I'm doing** when you signed in, this is your plan.

## What you get

- **Every butler, all 50+**: the Amazon, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Walmart, YouTube, Benable, Levanta, Pitch and Foyer families, the Goldmines, Earnings Intelligence, Retag and Relink, Social Posting, the AI Influencer Butler, Deals clones, and everything that ships later.
- **Unlimited AI on your own OpenAI key**: no monthly cap. You pay OpenAI directly for what you use, which for most creators is a few dollars a month.
- **1:1 calls with the team**: book a setup or strategy call from your online dashboard.
- Everything the Beginner plan has: the guided fast tracks, the extension, the phone app, the Pro Lounge and chat support.

## Add your OpenAI key first

AI features (keywords, captions, hooks, voiceover scripts, images, transcription) need your own key on the Power User plan. It takes five minutes:

1. Follow [Set up your OpenAI API key](/help/tutorials/openai-api-setup) to create a key and add a small amount of credit.
2. In the app, open **AI & Data Keys** in the left menu, paste the key, and click **Test**.

Until a key is added, AI buttons show a lock with a link to that panel. Nothing else in the app waits on it.

## Pick a fast track by goal

The first-run walkthrough asks what you want to do first and builds the setup steps for that goal: posting deals, messaging brands, growing a Facebook group, or working Creator Connections. Each track sets up only what that goal needs, in order. Rerun it, or pick a different goal, from **Settings > Onboarding**.

Two recommended first stops once the basics are in:

- [Getting started with Influencer Butler](/help/tutorials/getting-started-influencer-butler) for the lay of the land.
- [Ask the AI concierge](/help/tutorials/ai-concierge): it knows every butler and can walk you through any of them on the spot.

## Book a 1:1 call

Open your online dashboard and choose **Book a call**, or go to [influencerbutler.com/dashboard/book](https://www.influencerbutler.com/dashboard/book). Pick a time and you get a calendar invite. Calls are for setup help, strategy, and anything you would rather talk through than type.

## Changing plans later

Your plan, billing and license key live at [influencerbutler.com/dashboard/subscription](https://www.influencerbutler.com/dashboard/subscription). See [Manage or cancel your subscription](/help/tutorials/manage-subscription).
