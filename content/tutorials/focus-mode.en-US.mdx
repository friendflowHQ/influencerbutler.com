## What Focus Mode does

InfluencerButler packs a lot of butlers into one window. That power is the point, but on a busy day a wall of workspaces, badges, and motion can be a lot, especially if you have ADHD or just get pulled in ten directions at once.

Focus Mode is the calm switch. Turn it on and the HUD quiets down to the workspace in front of you, so you can finish one thing before the next one grabs your attention. It pairs with a few other calm-by-design settings so the whole app feels lower-distraction, not just one screen.

The point: keep your attention on the task you chose, not on everything the app could be doing.

## Turn it on

You have two ways:

- **Keyboard**: press **Ctrl+.** from anywhere in the app to toggle Focus mode on or off.
- **Settings**: open **Settings > Appearance** and flip the **Focus mode** toggle.

Changes apply instantly and persist across launches, so once you set it the way you like, it stays that way the next time you open Butler.

## The rest of the calm-by-design settings

Focus mode works best alongside the other Appearance and Performance options:

- **Motion**: set it to **Reduced** (or **Auto (follow OS)**) so panels don't slide, fade, or pulse while you read. If you've turned on reduced motion at the OS level, Auto respects it.
- **Theme**: choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **Auto (follow OS)**, whichever is easiest on your eyes.
- **Performance**: turn on running automations **one at a time** so the window stays responsive on small or virtual machines. Fewer things happening at once means less visual noise and a smoother feel.
- **Language**: English, Spanish, and French are all supported, so the calmer view reads naturally in your language.

## Tips

- Make **Ctrl+.** a habit: tap it the moment you sit down to work, and tap it again when you want the full lineup back.
- If the app ever feels overwhelming, reach for Focus mode plus **Motion: Reduced** together. That combination removes both the extra panels and the movement.
- On a laptop or a virtual machine, the **one automation at a time** setting is the single biggest win for keeping the window smooth.
