## What Collage Butler does

Collage Butler turns a handful of Amazon products into one designed collage and posts it for you. The default **Editorial board** looks like a magazine page: a framed card, a big title, the products cut out and arranged at sizes that suit each one, a small seasonal sticker, and your signature with "earns commission" at the bottom. Every post carries one link per product, tagged with your Associates ID. Instagram gets the same words with a note that the items are linked in your bio.

## Before you start

- **Collage Butler is an opt-in beta for Power User plans.** Turn it on in **Settings > Beta features** (Collage Butler). Until then its panels stay locked.
- Connect the **Amazon Creators API** in **Settings > AI & Data Keys**. Collage Butler uses it to find products.
- Sign in to the places you want it to post (Instagram, your Facebook group or page, Pinterest). They are set up in **Deal Posting > Social Destinations**.

## Set it up in a few questions

1. Open **Collage Butler** in the left menu. On the **Overview** tab, click **Start posting for me**.
2. **What do you want to share?** Pick topics (Home Decor, Kitchen, Fashion and more) and add your own search words. Collage Butler takes turns between them, one collage at a time.
3. **Price range.** Only products in this range go into your collages.
4. **Pick a style.** **Editorial board** (recommended) or **Classic grid** (product photos in tidy squares).
5. **How many products** on each collage: 4, 6 or 8.
6. **Headline style.** New finds, Gift ideas, Must haves, or your own words.
7. **Pick your colors.** Match the season (it changes on its own), pick a season, or use your brand colors.
8. **Your signature.** Your name or brand, written at the bottom of every collage.
9. **Where to post** and **how often** (collages a day, between which hours, on which days).
10. **Quick check.** Collage Butler checks the Creators API and your sign-ins.
11. Click **See an example first** to make one collage without posting it, then **Start posting for me**.

## Every day after that

- The **Posting for you** card on the Overview tab shows today's posting times and which collages are lined up.
- Use **Make today's collages now**, **Post the next one now**, **Pause**, or **Edit my answers** from the same card.
- Lined-up collages sit in your posting queue, where you can delete any of them before it posts.
- **Collage Gallery** lists every collage you have made. **Keywords & Filters** holds your search words and price range.

## Shape the caption

Open the **Build Your Post** tab to choose the words every collage posts with, the same way Build Your Post works in Fashion Looks.

- It opens filled in with the caption Collage Butler has always used: your headline, your call to action and one line per product with its link.
- Click or drag chips from **Insert** on the right: **Headline**, **Call to action**, **Product links**, **Keyword theme** and **Number of products**, plus your library lines (hooks, hashtags) and hook styles.
- The **First comment** tab adds a comment right under the post. Put the product links there if you prefer a short caption.
- The **Live preview** shows the result with your newest collage. Changes save on their own and apply to the next collages made.

## Notes

- **Cut-outs are free for most products.** Product photos shot on a white background are cut out automatically. A photo that can't be cut out (a room scene, for example) shows as a rounded photo tile instead.
- **Editorial board and real-photo grids cost nothing to make.** The AI and Hybrid grid looks generate new imagery, carry a small cost per image, and stop at your monthly AI limit (**Collage Butler > Settings**).
- Turning the beta off in Settings also pauses the daily posting.
