> **Heads up on costs:** the AI parts of this butler run on your own keys (fal.ai and OpenAI), so you pay those services directly, not Influencer Butler. Add your keys on the **AI and Data Keys** page. New to OpenAI keys? [Set one up in 5 minutes](/help/tutorials/openai-api-setup). The Milestone video needs your fal.ai key, and each video costs a few dollars.

## What AI Influencer Butler does

You pick a style, like "New England preppy" or a ready-made pack, and AI Influencer Butler builds a finished outfit post for you: your model wearing the themed look, the real products shown beside them, and a title band with your branding on top. Then you can post it, add the products to your storefront, or approve it and keep it in your library.

It does two jobs:

- **Outfit looks.** One image per look, made on demand with **Create this look** or on a daily schedule with autopilot.
- **Milestone videos.** A short vertical celebration video of you, like a champagne pop with a big "1k Members" on the end.

The look is clearly styled, but it is not a real photo shoot. That is fine for outfit posts, and the AI-generated label (see Autopilot below) keeps you honest with your audience.

## Before you start

- **Your keys are added.** Open **AI and Data Keys** and add your fal.ai key (needed for the AI model and for Milestone videos) and your OpenAI key (needed to generate new styles with AI). Using your own photo as the model does not need a fal.ai key.
- **You are signed in where you want to post.** Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and TikTok are the platforms available under Autopilot. Sign in to the ones you plan to use.
- **Optional: a trained clone.** If you want the model to look like you, train one first in **AI Clone Butler**. It takes a little while, so start it early.

## Pick your model

Under **Model and style**, the **Model** list has three kinds of choice:

1. **Generic AI model.** A made-up model. The quickest way to try the butler, and a good fit if you do not want to be in the pictures.
2. **My clone.** Any AI clone you have trained shows up here with the prefix "My clone:". The model then looks like you. If you have not trained one yet, click **Create a model of you**. It opens AI Clone Butler, where you add your photos and start training. When training finishes, your clone appears in this list.
3. **Use my own photo.** Click **Upload a photo** and pick a clear photo of yourself. Your photo is placed in the look as it is, so no AI model and no fal.ai key are needed for this option. It is the cheapest way to get a look that really is you.

If you choose a clone that is still training, **Create this look** will ask you to train it first or switch to a generic model.

## Pick a style: style packs and AI styles

The **Style pack** list holds ready-made looks. Pick one and you are set.

Want something fresher? Open **Generate styles with AI**:

1. Type an **Aesthetic**, like "New England preppy".
2. Pick a **Season** (Any, Spring, Summer, Fall or Winter).
3. Click **Suggest looks**. After a moment you get a list of outfit ideas. Click one to select it.

This step uses your OpenAI key. If it cannot generate, you will be told to add your key in AI and Data Keys.

## Products and branding

**Products** decides which real items appear in the look. The product photos are placed into the image as they are, so what people see is what they can actually buy.

- **Stores**: tick **Amazon**, **Walmart**, or both.
- **Pieces per look**: how many products to feature, from 1 to 9. The default is 4.
- **Max price**: only use pieces up to this price. Leave it at 0 if you do not want a limit.

**Branding** is the title band across every look:

- **Title**: something like "The Perfect Finds by You".
- **Subtitle**: for example "Amazon Fashion".

Leave the colors alone and each style pack uses its own palette. Everything on this page saves as you type, so there is no Save button.

## Create this look

Once you have a model and a style, click **Create this look**. It takes about a minute. When it is done, the finished image appears with four buttons:

- **Post now**: sends the look to the platforms you ticked under Autopilot and posting.
- **Add to storefront**: adds the products from the look to your Amazon storefront list. You need to be signed in to Amazon.
- **Approve**: saves the look to your library as one you are happy with.
- **Show file**: opens the image file on your computer.

Every look you create is kept under **Recent looks**, newest first.

If you see "Pick a style pack or generate a style first", you have not selected a style yet. If you see a message asking for your own photo, choose **Use my own photo** under Model and upload one.

## Autopilot and the daily spend cap

Open **Autopilot and posting** to have the butler create and post looks for you every day.

1. **Pick your platforms.** Tick Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and/or TikTok. Next to each one is an **Auto** box. With Auto on, finished looks post to that platform on their own. With Auto off, looks wait for you to approve them first.
2. **Set Posts per day.** The default is 2.
3. **Set the Daily spend cap ($).** This is the most autopilot may spend in a day on your fal.ai and OpenAI keys. When the day's spending reaches the cap, autopilot stops until tomorrow. It never goes over. The default is $5. A line under the settings shows how much you have spent today and how much is left.
4. **Choose the extras.**
   - **Also make a Reel/TikTok video** turns each look into a short video too.
   - **Add an AI-generated label to captions** is on by default. We recommend leaving it on.
5. **Turn on autopilot.** Tick **Turn on autopilot: create and post looks automatically each day**.

Not ready to switch it on? Click **Run one look now** to create and post a single look and see exactly how it goes. Start there, and keep a low spend cap for your first few days.

The app has to stay open for autopilot to run, and you need to stay signed in to the platforms you chose.

## Milestone video

Hit a follower or member milestone and want to celebrate? The **Milestone video** section makes a short vertical video of you, like a champagne pop, with a big number such as **1k Members** shown at the end.

The butler works in two steps so you never pay for a video before you have checked the picture it starts from.

### Fill in the details

1. **Photo of you**: click **Upload a photo** and pick a clear, well lit, front-facing photo of your face. The video keeps your face and hair from this picture.
2. **Outfit and hat photos**: click **Add outfit photos** and add pictures of the clothes you want to wear in the video, like a vest or a top hat. You can add up to 6. Crop out phone screens and other clutter so only the clothes show.
3. **Outfit description**: say in words what you are wearing, naming the colors and pieces. This is what makes the clothes come out exact. If you leave it blank, the default outfit is used: an orange satin vest, white shirt, black trousers and a black top hat.
4. **Video style**: choose **Champagne pop with a bat** or **Champagne pop by hand**.
5. **Big number or text**: type what you want shown big at the end, like **1k Members**. The butler adds this text itself after the video is made, so it is always spelled exactly the way you typed it.
6. **Small line under it (optional)**: a smaller line under the number, like "Thank you!". Leave it empty for none.

### Video options (optional)

Open **Video options** if you want to fine tune. The defaults work well, so you can skip this.

- **Ending**: **Black and white splash card** fades the last moment into a black and white card with your text. **Text over the video** keeps the full color video and puts the text on top of it.
- **Text position**: Top, Middle or Bottom.
- **Text color**: white looks best on the splash card.
- **Video model**: Seedance 2.0 is the default and also makes the pop sound. If a model stops working, try another one.
- **Quality**: 720p or 1080p. 1080p is sharper but costs more and takes longer. The saved video is always 1080 by 1920.
- **Also show the video model all my photos**: gives the video model your photo and outfit photos as well as the start picture, which can keep the clothes more exact. Use it with a Seedance "all photos" model.

### Step 1: check the start picture

Click **Create the start picture**. This makes a quick, low cost picture of you in the outfit, with the approximate cost shown next to the button. This picture is what the video is built from, so if it looks right, the video will too.

Not happy with it? Change the photos or the outfit description and click the button again. It is far cheaper to retry the picture than the video.

### Step 2: make the video

When the start picture looks right, click **Make my video**. The cost shown next to the button is the most you can expect to pay. A progress bar walks through the stages: the start picture, the video itself, and adding your text. It takes a few minutes, and you can click **Cancel** at any time.

### When it is finished

Your result appears with a preview strip and these buttons:

- **Play video** and **Save video**: watch it, and save a copy wherever you like.
- **Update the text**: change the big number, the small line, the ending style, the position or the color, and the butler re-applies the text to the video you already have. You do not pay again, because the expensive video part is not re-made.
- **Make another version**: makes a fresh video from your details, which does cost again.
- **Show file**: opens the video on your computer.

Under **Your milestone videos** you will find everything you have made, newest first. Each one can be opened or deleted.

### What a video costs

Each milestone video costs a few dollars, billed by fal.ai to your own account. Use the start picture check and **Update the text** to avoid paying for redos. If you see "Add your fal.ai key in AI and Data Keys, then try again", the key is missing or was not saved.

## Common gotchas

- **Nothing happens when I create a look**: check that you picked a style, and that your model choice is ready (a clone must finish training, and your own photo must be uploaded).
- **"Could not generate looks"**: styles from AI need your OpenAI key. Add it in AI and Data Keys.
- **The clothes in my video look wrong**: tighten the outfit description, crop clutter out of your outfit photos, and recheck the start picture before you make the video again.
- **The text is in the wrong place**: use **Update the text** to move or recolor it for free.
- **Autopilot stopped for the day**: the daily spend cap was reached. It resumes tomorrow, or raise the cap if you are comfortable spending more.
- **"A video is already being made"**: only one video builds at a time. Wait for it, or cancel it.
- **"Could not add the text because the video tools are missing"**: reinstall the app and try again.
