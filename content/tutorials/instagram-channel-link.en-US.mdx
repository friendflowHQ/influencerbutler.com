## Why you need this

Deal Posting can post your deals straight into an Instagram broadcast channel. To do that it needs a special desktop link for the channel. Instagram never shows that link, so the app finds it for you. All you do is copy the link Instagram gives you on your phone.

You only have to do this once per channel.

## Before you start

- You manage an Instagram **broadcast channel** (the owner account).
- You are signed in to Instagram in the app. If you are not, the walkthrough opens the sign-in window for you.

## Step 1: Copy the channel link on your phone

1. Open the Instagram app on your phone.
2. Go to your broadcast channel and tap its name.
3. Tap **Share** and send the link to yourself (email or a message works).
4. On your computer, open that email or message and copy the link.

## Step 2: Run the walkthrough

1. In the app, open Deal Posting and go to **Social Destinations**.
2. On the Instagram card, click **Find my channel link**.
3. Paste the link you copied and click **Next**.
4. Click **Find my channel link**. The app looks up your channel in the background, which can take up to a minute.
5. When it says it found your channel, click **Next**, then finish the walkthrough.

The channel link is added to the Instagram box on its own and saves automatically. Deal Posting now knows which channel to post to.

## Common gotchas

- **It asks you to sign in:** a window opens so you can sign in to Instagram. Sign in there, close it, then click **Find my channel link** again.
- **It says it could not find your channel:** make sure you are signed in to the Instagram account that owns the channel, then copy the share link again and retry.
- **It says that is not a channel link:** the link must start with instagram.com or ig.me. Copy the share link from the channel itself, not from a profile.
- **You already have the long link:** if you have a link that looks like instagram.com/direct/t/ followed by a number, you can paste it straight into the Instagram box in Social Destinations.
