## What Keywords & Filters does

Collage Butler builds scroll-stopping, multi-product images from real Amazon product photos. This screen is where you tell it what to put in them. The keywords and filters you set here run against the Amazon Creators API, and the products that come back are the ones Collage Butler arranges into your collage.

Think of it as the shopping list. The generator handles the layout and the look; this panel decides the merchandise.

## Before you start

Open Collage Butler in Settings and turn it on. Collage Butler is in beta, so the controls on this screen stay locked behind an "In Beta" note until you enable it.

You do not need anything else here. Keywords and price filters are optional refinements, so an empty screen is a valid starting point.

## How to use it

1. In the left menu, open **Collage Butler**, then **Keywords & Filters**.
2. In the **Keywords** box, type the search terms for the products you want, separated by commas (for example: summer dresses, beach bags, sandals). Each term is a search, so a few focused keywords usually beat one broad one.
3. In **Category** (optional), name a department to narrow the search, such as Fashion or Home. Leave it blank to search across everything.
4. Set **Minimum price** to skip products cheaper than that amount. Leave it blank for no floor.
5. Set **Maximum price** to skip products above that amount. Leave it blank for no ceiling.
6. That is all the setup this screen needs. Everything you type saves on its own, so head to the **AI Collage Generator** tab to pick a layout and build the collage from these products.

## Notes

- Collage Butler is in beta. The screen may change as it is polished, and the controls stay locked until you enable Collage Butler in Settings.
- Every field auto-saves as you type. There is no Save button, and your keywords and filters are waiting for you the next time you open the screen.
- Keywords and filters are shared by every collage you generate, so updating them here changes what future collages pull in.
- Prices use your storefront's currency. Leave a price field blank rather than entering 0 if you do not want that limit.
- Products come from the Amazon Creators API, so results depend on what is available there for your terms. If a collage comes back thin, try broadening a keyword or clearing the category or price filters.
