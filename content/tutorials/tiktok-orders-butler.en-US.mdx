## What TikTok Orders Butler does

TikTok Orders Butler is a read-only dashboard for your TikTok Shop affiliate earnings. It pulls the affiliate orders your content generated, deduplicates them, and shows three numbers at the top: how many orders you have, your projected commission, and your settled (paid) commission. Below the cards is a table of recent orders with the product, its status, when it was ordered, and the commission on it.

It does not change anything on TikTok. It only reads your affiliate order data so you can see how your posts are converting without logging into the TikTok Shop dashboard.

## Before you start

- **Connect TikTok Shop.** Open **API Integrations**, find the **TikTok Shop** card, and click **Connect TikTok Shop**. You approve access once in your browser. Orders Butler reads from that connection.
- You need a TikTok Shop account with affiliate access. If the card shows "not set up yet," TikTok Shop has not been configured on your build.

## Loading your orders

1. Open **TikTok Orders Butler** under TikTok Butler.
2. Click **Refresh orders**.

The dashboard fetches your latest affiliate orders and merges them with what it already stored, so an order that later settles updates in place rather than duplicating. The KPI cards and the table refresh together.

## Reading the numbers

- **Projected commission** is what you are estimated to earn across all pulled orders, including orders that have not settled yet.
- **Settled commission** is what TikTok has finalized as paid or payable.
- The gap between the two is commission still in flight (orders that can still be returned or adjusted).

Refresh whenever you want the latest. Orders you have already pulled stay in the dashboard between refreshes.
