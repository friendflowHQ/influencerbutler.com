## What Best Seller Butler does

Some products just keep working. Best Seller Butler finds them for you. On a schedule, it reads the engagement (reactions, comments, shares) on your posts, figures out which Amazon product each post was promoting, and scores every product over a recent time window. The proven winners get promoted into your always-post pool in Deals Butler, so they cycle back into your feed on a cadence without you having to remember which links converted.

Today it measures **Facebook groups and pages**. Telegram, Instagram, and Threads are marked "Coming soon" in the panel. You can weigh your own posts more heavily than other people's, cap how many products get promoted per scan, and watch the running leaderboard of your best sellers by engagement.

## Before you start

- **Pro subscription.** Best Seller Butler is a Pro workspace (available during your free trial).
- **Facebook signed in.** The scan uses your Deals Butler posting profile, so sign in to Facebook first from **Social Destinations** in Deals Butler.
- **Google Chrome installed.** The Facebook scan runs on genuine Chrome. If it is missing, the scan skips Facebook and tells you.
- **Some posts to measure.** You need a history of posted deals (yours, and optionally other people's) for there to be engagement to read.

## How to use it

1. In the left menu, open **Deals Butler**, then **Best Seller Butler**.
2. Turn on **Turn on Best Seller Butler** to let it scan on your schedule. You can also skip the schedule and just click **Run scan now** whenever you want.
3. Keep **👀 Watch it run** checked to watch the browser work, or uncheck it to scan quietly in the background.
4. Under **How often**, pick a preset (**6 hours**, **12 hours**, **24 hours**, **2 days**) or set a custom interval with **Or every**. Use **Start at** to delay the first scan, or leave it blank to start now.
5. Under **Where to look**, keep **Facebook groups and pages** checked (the other surfaces are coming soon).
6. Under **Facebook**, choose to read engagement on your own posts, other people's posts, or both. Paste the **Group IDs or URLs to scan** and **Page names or IDs to scan** (one per line), and set **Most posts to read per group or page**.
7. Under **Scoring and promotion**, set the recency window (**Only count engagement from the last (days)**), the **Promote a product when its score reaches** threshold, **Most products to promote per scan**, and **Post promoted products every (days)**. Tune **Weight for my own posts** and **Weight for other people's posts** to decide whose engagement counts more.
8. Watch the **Best sellers by engagement** table fill in after a scan. Each row shows the product, its **Score**, how many **Posts** it appeared in, your posts (**Mine**) versus **Others**, and a **Promoted** badge once it enters your always-post pool.

## Notes

- **Everything auto-saves.** There is no save button. Every toggle, number, and pasted list is stored as you change it and restored next launch.
- **Status at a glance.** The pills near the top show whether it is **Off**, **On**, or **Scanning**, plus the next and last scan times.
- **It shares Facebook with Deals Butler.** If a Deals post is publishing when a scan starts, the scan waits for the post to finish, then runs. If Facebook is not signed in, sign in from Social Destinations in Deals Butler and run the scan again.
- **Promotion goes to Deals Butler.** Promoted best sellers land in your always-post pool and repost on the cadence you set, so proven products keep showing up on their own.
