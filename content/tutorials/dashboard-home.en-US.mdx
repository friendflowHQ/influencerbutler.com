## The three cards at the top

Open **Dashboard** and the first thing you see is three cards side by side (stacked on a narrow window):

- **Right now**: every butler that is working at this moment, what it is doing and how long it has been going. Click one to open that butler. When nothing is running, the card says so.
- **Needs attention**: anything that stopped a butler or wants your eyes, such as an error, a site that needs you to sign in, or a paused posting account. Each line has a button that takes you to the fix. When you are caught up, the card says "You are all caught up."
- **Next up**: the next runs on your schedules, soonest first.

Your earnings, today's totals and the workspace cards sit below these three.

## Change a schedule from Next up

Each row in **Next up** shows the butler, when it runs next and how often. Click the row to open the butler, or click **Edit** on the row to open that butler and jump to its schedule so you can change the time or turn it off.

If nothing is left to run today, Next up shows the runs coming in the next 24 hours instead, so it is not blank late in the evening.

## Group dashboards

Each group in the sidebar (Amazon Butler, Walmart Butler, Instagram Butler, Deal Posting, and the rest) has its own dashboard with the same layout:

- **Site sign-in chips** at the top show whether you are signed in to each site that group uses, and when it was last checked. Click a signed-out chip to sign in.
- **Right now** for the butlers in that group.
- **Numbers with change arrows** compare the last 7 days (or the range you pick) to the period before.
- **Activity chart**, **Up next** and **Recent activity** for the group.

If a number looks empty, the butler may not have run yet in that range. Run it once and check again.

## Tips

- Treat **Needs attention** as your to-do list for the day. If it is empty, nothing needs you.
- Use **Edit** on **Next up** when you want a butler to post at a different time without searching for its schedule.
- The group dashboards refresh on their own every half minute, so you can leave one open while you work.
