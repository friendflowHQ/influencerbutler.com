## What Shopping Butler does

Shopping Butler lives under **Deals Butler › Shopping Butler**. It is a shopper's tool, not a poster: type what you are looking for, and it searches **Amazon and Walmart** at once and ranks everything it finds by four things at the same time:

- **Price**: which is the cheapest or the best deal right now.
- **Product quality**: the best-performing product, from its star rating and review count.
- **Commission**: how much you would earn if you featured it in a storefront video, including any Creator Connections or SPCC uplift.
- **Saturation**: how crowded a product already is, measured by the real number of creator videos on its Amazon page. A great product nobody has covered is a better opening than an equally good one that is already in a hundred videos.

It answers two questions in one view: *which product should I actually buy* (cheap and good), and *which one is the most lucrative and least crowded to post*.

It is a UI-only workspace. There is no runner to start and nothing schedules. You search, switch how the list is ranked, and optionally check saturation.

## Before you start

- **Connect your Amazon keys** in **AI & Data Keys** to include Amazon in the search. Without them, results are Walmart only, and the panel tells you so.
- **Walmart** works once you are signed in to your Walmart Creator account; no keys are needed.

## Searching

1. Open **Deals Butler › Shopping Butler**.
2. Type what you are shopping for in plain words, for example `laptop for my son`, and press Enter or click **Search**.
3. The results table fills in right away with products from both stores, each showing its price, rating, the commission you would earn, and a score.

When the same product is sold in both stores, the two rows collapse into one, with an "also cheaper at" note so you can see the better price at a glance.

## Rank for what you care about

Three presets change how the list is ordered. Your choice is remembered for next time.

- **Best to buy**: leans on price and product quality. Use this when you are shopping for yourself.
- **Balanced**: a bit of everything. This is the default.
- **Best to feature**: leans on commission and low saturation, with price and quality as light tiebreakers. Use this when you are deciding what to post.

Switching a preset re-orders the products you already have. It does not run a new search, so it is instant.

## Checking saturation

Saturation, the number of creator videos already on a product, is the one signal that needs a look at the live Amazon page, so it is a separate step you choose to run.

1. After a search, click **Check saturation** in the toolbar.
2. Shopping Butler opens Amazon and counts the creator videos on your top picks. It reads its own cache first, so picks you have checked before come back instantly.
3. When the counts arrive, the **Creator videos** column fills in and the list re-ranks, so the Best to feature and Balanced scores move to reflect what is still an open opening.

You can watch this run and cancel it at any time. If Amazon is resting your account, the check pauses and resumes on its own; the panel tells you when that happens.

## Reading the table

- **Price**: the live price, with an "also cheaper at" note when the other store is cheaper.
- **Rating**: the star rating, with the review count beside it.
- **You earn**: your estimated commission per sale. A **campaign** chip means the product is in a Creator Connections campaign, so it earns more.
- **Creator videos**: the saturation count, shown as "not checked" until you run the saturation step.
- **Score**: the blended score for the preset you picked. Higher is a better fit for that preset.

## Common gotchas

- **Amazon results are missing**: your Amazon keys are not connected. Open AI & Data Keys and add them.
- **Creator videos all say "not checked"**: that column only fills in after you click Check saturation.
- **Saturation would not run**: Amazon is resting your account for a bit. It resumes on its own, so try the check again later.
