## What Guided Setup does

Guided Setup is a question-driven wizard inside Deals Influencer Butler. Instead of visiting each tab yourself, you answer a short series of questions and Butler applies the settings for you:

1. **Where to post**: pick any mix of Facebook Groups, Facebook Pages, Telegram, Reddit, Instagram Channels, Instagram Threads, and Benable. You only set up the ones you pick.
2. **Keywords**: choose your niches and every month gets pre-filled with proven seasonal keywords.
3. **Your post**: start from the recommended caption template (percent off plus your link) or build your own later in Build Your Post.
4. **Destinations**: for each channel you picked, the wizard walks you through exactly what that channel needs: Facebook sign-in and your group links, a Telegram bot token, a Reddit app, Instagram channel links, or Benable list links.
5. **Schedule**: choose Steady (every 2 hours), Active (every hour), or Max (every 15 minutes). The wizard also switches on auto-send and a recurring deal search.
6. **First run**: run your first deal search and send a test post to any destination.

Every answer is applied through the same settings the tabs use, so you can fine-tune anything later in the normal panes.

## How to start it

There are three ways in:

- **The Guided Setup chip** at the end of the Deals Influencer Butler tab bar.
- **The banner** that appears at the top of the workspace when nothing is configured yet.
- **First run**: when you first open the Keywords tab, the setup chooser offers **Set this up for me**.

You can also ask the AI Assistant to set up deal posting for you: it opens the same wizard.

## Skipping and resuming

Every step has a **Skip this step** button, and closing the wizard never loses your place: reopen it and choose **Resume setup** to continue where you left off, or **Start over** for a clean run. The final summary lists anything you skipped, with an **Open** button that jumps to the right tab so you can finish it whenever you are ready.

## Notes per channel

- **Facebook Groups and Pages** need a one-time Facebook sign-in. Paste up to 5 group links per profile, one per line.
- **Telegram** posts through your own bot: create one with @BotFather, add it to your channel as an admin, and the wizard tests the connection before turning the channel on.
- **Reddit** uses your own Reddit app (type: script) and a one-time sign-in window.
- **Instagram** posts into your broadcast channels: paste the channel links that look like `instagram.com/direct/t/...`.
- **Instagram Threads** needs no link at all: it posts to your own feed and asks you to sign in the first time it posts.
- **Benable** takes your list links; sign in from the Benable Butler workspace first.
