## What Content Planner does

Content Planner is a visual week for your socials. It shows a Monday to Sunday grid where you can write your own plan into any day, colour-code it, and see everything your butlers have already scheduled for that week alongside your notes.

There are two layers in the grid:

1. **Your own entries**: notes you type yourself, each with an optional time, a longer note, and one of eight colours. These are just your plan, nothing posts from here.
2. **The scheduled overlay**: a read-only view of what your automations will fire that week, such as Roundup Campaigns, Scheduled Deals, Always-Post, Best Seller Butler, and the interval butlers like Messenger and Instagram.

It is a UI-only workspace. There is no runner to start and nothing posts from this screen. It is a planning surface: you write, you look, you navigate.

## Before you start

- Nothing to connect. Content Planner is free and visible to everyone, with no key or subscription required.
- The overlay only has things to show once you have scheduled something in another butler (a roundup, a deal cadence, an interval run). An empty overlay just means nothing is scheduled yet, not a problem.
- Your entries are saved automatically. There is no Save button anywhere in this panel.

## How to use it

1. In the left menu, click **Content Planner** (a top-level item).
2. Read the week you land on. The current week shows first, with today's column marked. Use the left and right arrows to move a week back or forward, and **This week** to jump home.
3. To add a plan, click the **+** on any day, or use **Add entry** in the toolbar (that one drops onto today). You can also click any empty day.
4. In the dialog, fill in **Title** (what you are planning), an optional **Time**, optional **Notes** for details, links or reminders, and pick a **Colour**. Click **Add** to save it onto that day.
5. To change a plan later, click the entry to reopen the dialog. Edit any field and click **Save**, or click **Delete** to remove it.
6. Leave **Show scheduled automations** ticked to overlay what your butlers will run that week. Each scheduled item shows its time and name, and a small count like ×3 when several runs collapse into one chip.
7. Click any scheduled chip to jump straight to the butler that owns it, so you can adjust its schedule at the source.
8. Open **Choose which automations to show** to fine-tune the overlay. Untick a source to hide its chips without changing its schedule, and tick it again to bring them back.

## Notes

- Everything auto-saves as you type, including your entries, your colours, the overlay toggle, and your source filters. Close the app and come back, and your week is exactly as you left it.
- The overlay is read-only. Hiding a source in the filters only hides its chips in this grid, it never pauses or changes the actual automation.
- Scheduled chips are a forecast based on each butler's current schedule. If you change a schedule elsewhere, the overlay updates the next time it refreshes.
- Your notes are personal planning. Nothing you write here is posted or sent anywhere.
