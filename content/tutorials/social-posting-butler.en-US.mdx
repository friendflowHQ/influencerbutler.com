> **Heads up:** The AI features in this butler (AI-generated images and AI caption help) use your own OpenAI API key (you pay OpenAI directly, usually pennies; a ChatGPT subscription does not work), unless you are on the Beginner plan, where a limited hosted allowance covers you. The rest works without it. [Set it up in 5 minutes](/help/tutorials/openai-api-setup).

## What Social Posting Butler does

Social Posting Butler is where you load your own posts and let them go out on a schedule, to every social destination you already use. Instead of a butler that finds deals for you, this is a butler you feed: your photo (or one you generate with AI), your caption, your link, and when it should post. It then publishes through the same posting engine the rest of the app uses, so Facebook groups and pages, Instagram, Threads, Telegram, Reddit, and Pinterest all work the same way here.

It is also the hub other butlers hand their posts to, so everything you have scheduled shows up in one place.

## The two kinds of post

Every post you compose is one of two kinds:

- **Schedule once**: publishes a single time at a date and time you pick, then it is done. Turn on **Repost this automatically** and pick how many days, and once it posts it quietly becomes an evergreen post on that cadence instead of retiring, so a post worth repeating does not have to be rebuilt by hand.
- **Evergreen**: re-posts on its own cadence forever, until you turn it off. Pick a simple interval (every few hours or days) or specific days of the week at a set time. This is the "set it and forget it" option for content that stays fresh, like a recurring reminder, a signature tip, or a always-relevant recommendation.

You can also **Post now** to publish immediately without saving it to your library.

## Composing a post

1. Open **Social Posting Butler** and go to the **Compose** tab.
2. Write your **caption**, or click **AI Caption** for help: give it a topic and tone (or leave both blank for general ideas), or turn on **Write in my voice** once you have sent a couple of posts to get captions styled like your own past writing. Pick one of the variants to drop it straight into the caption box.
3. Click **Suggest hashtags** to add a ready-made set (your own saved defaults plus a few seasonal and topic-relevant tags) to the end of your caption. It never repeats a hashtag already in your text.
4. If your caption runs long, watch for the length warning under the caption box: it flags when you are over Instagram's, Threads', or Telegram's caption limit for whichever destinations you have picked.
5. Choose a **photo**:
   - **Upload your own**: pick one or more images from your computer (up to 10). They are copied into the app so they stay available even if you move the originals.
   - **Generate with AI**: describe the image you want and Butler creates one for you.
   - **No photo**: a text-only post.
6. Add your **link**, if any. Turn on **Post the link in the first comment** if you would rather the link land as the first comment than sit in the caption. This is a common trick to keep the caption clean and avoid a platform down-ranking a post with an outbound link.
7. Pick your **destinations**, or leave them all unchecked to use every destination you have enabled.
   If you have more than one Facebook account, the **Accounts** box lets you pick which ones this post goes out from (up to 5). Each picked account posts only to the Groups and Pages it manages, so no group gets the same post twice. Leave them all unticked and every Group or Page posts from its usual account.
8. Choose **when to post**: Post now, Schedule once, or Evergreen.
9. Turn on **Watch it run** in the top right of the app if you want to see the browser window while it posts. Turn it off there to run in the background.
10. Click **Save to library** (for scheduled or evergreen posts) or **Publish now**. If your seat cannot approve content (see **Team approvals** below), this button reads **Submit for approval** instead, and the post waits for an owner or manager to approve it before it ever goes out.

## Your posts: folders, calendar, cards, and table

The **Posts** tab holds every post you have scheduled, plus every post that already went out. Use the **All / Upcoming / Archive** buttons at the top to choose which ones you see, then pick a view:

- **Folders**: your posts filed by year, month, and day, like folders on a computer. Open a year, then a month, then click a day.
- **Month**: a calendar with each day's posts and a small count per platform. Click a day number to open that day.
- **Week**: the same calendar, one week at a time.
- **Cards**: a photo-forward grid of every post.
- **Table**: a sortable list. Click a column heading to sort by time, post, type, or status.

**Open a day to see everything on it.** Clicking a day (from Folders or the calendar) shows every post for that day grouped by account, so you can see at a glance what each Facebook account, Instagram, Pinterest, and so on is posting. Switch that day between Cards and Table, and use the arrows to step to the day before or after.

**Preview and edit without leaving the page.** Click any post to open its preview. Change the caption, add, remove, or reorder photos, set a first comment or link, change the date and time, or pick different accounts. Every change saves on its own. Use **Open in full editor** for formatting or the schedule of a repeating post.

**Move a post to another day** by dragging it on the Month or Week calendar. It keeps its time of day. Repeating (evergreen) posts follow their own schedule, so change those in the editor instead.

**Filter and work on many posts at once.** Search captions and links, or filter by platform, account, status, one-time or repeating, and a date range. Tick the box on any post (or **Select all in view**) to pause, resume, publish now, move by a number of days, or delete them together.

### Importing a content calendar

Click **Import CSV** on the Posts tab for two ways to bulk-load posts:

- **Content calendar**: bring any spreadsheet with a date column plus a caption, link, or image path. Column names are matched automatically, so Date, Caption, Link, Image, Destinations, and Title all work in any order, under almost any header wording. Each row becomes a one-time scheduled post; re-importing the same file updates matching rows instead of duplicating them, and a row you have since hand-edited is left alone.
- **Butler Challenge queue**: the fixed-format queue used for Butler's own posting challenges, targeting a single Facebook group.

## Archive: see what actually landed

Switch the Posts tab to **Archive** to see everything that already went out. Each post shows a chip for every account it was sent to: a check for delivered, a cross for failed (hover it for the reason). **Retry failed** re-sends only the ones that did not go through, and **Post again** schedules a copy you can change.

A post that keeps failing (for example, a dead login or a broken link) stops retrying silently forever: after several failed attempts in a row, Butler pauses it automatically so it is not hammering a destination that will not accept it. Resume it from the Posts tab once you have fixed the underlying problem.

## Quiet hours

Social Posting Butler's scheduler honors the same quiet hours (or active-hours window) you set for Deals Butler, so an evergreen post never fires in the middle of the night just because this workspace did not know about your schedule. The **Settings** tab shows a read-only summary of your current quiet hours, with a link to manage them in Deals Butler.

## Team approvals

If you run this app on a Team or Agency seat with per-seat permissions turned on, an owner or manager can require that a VA's posts be approved before they go out. A seat without the **Approve posts before they go out** permission has its saves land in the new **Approvals** tab instead of posting directly; an owner or manager reviews each one there and approves or rejects it. Solo installs, and any install that has not turned on seat permissions, are unaffected: everything you save posts normally, the same as always.

## Destinations

The **Social Destinations** tab shows which destinations you have enabled. You enable and configure them (sign-ins, API keys, group and channel targets) in Deals Butler, and Social Posting Butler honors the same set. Use the **Manage destinations** button to jump there.

## Where the data goes

Your post library and its schedule live on your computer, alongside the app's other data. Posts publish through the shared posting engine, so a post you send here shows up in your posting history the same as any other.

## Common gotchas

- **"No destinations enabled yet"**: enable at least one destination in Deals Butler first, then come back.
- **AI help fails**: set your OpenAI key in API Integrations (or confirm you are on a plan with hosted AI allowance remaining). The same key powers every AI feature in the app.
- **A scheduled post did not fire**: check that the post is enabled, has not been auto-disabled after repeated failures, and is not sitting in Approvals waiting on a decision. Also confirm automation is not globally paused and you are not inside your quiet hours. Evergreen posts skip a missed time (for example if the computer was asleep) rather than posting it late.
- **The link is not in the caption**: if you turned on "Post the link in the first comment," the link is posted as the first comment instead, by design.
- **My post says "Submit for approval" instead of "Save to library"**: your seat does not have permission to approve content. An owner or manager needs to approve it from the Approvals tab.
