> **Heads up:** The AI features in this butler (AI-generated images and AI caption help) use your own OpenAI API key (you pay OpenAI directly, usually pennies; a ChatGPT subscription does not work), unless you are on the Beginner plan, where a limited hosted allowance covers you. The rest works without it. [Set it up in 5 minutes](/help/tutorials/openai-api-setup).

## What Social Posting Butler does

Social Posting Butler is where you load your own posts and let them go out on a schedule, to every social destination you already use. Instead of a butler that finds deals for you, this is a butler you feed: your photo (or one you generate with AI), your caption, your link, and when it should post. It then publishes through the same posting engine the rest of the app uses, so Facebook groups and pages, Instagram, Threads, Telegram, Reddit, and Pinterest all work the same way here.

It is also the hub other butlers hand their posts to, so everything you have scheduled shows up in one place.

## The two kinds of post

Every post you compose is one of two kinds:

- **Schedule once**: publishes a single time at a date and time you pick, then it is done. Turn on **Repost this automatically** and pick how many days, and once it posts it quietly becomes an evergreen post on that cadence instead of retiring, so a post worth repeating does not have to be rebuilt by hand.
- **Evergreen**: re-posts on its own cadence forever, until you turn it off. Pick a simple interval (every few hours or days) or specific days of the week at a set time. This is the "set it and forget it" option for content that stays fresh, like a recurring reminder, a signature tip, or a always-relevant recommendation.

You can also **Post now** to publish immediately without saving it to your library.

## Composing a post

1. Open **Social Posting Butler** and go to the **Compose** tab.
2. Write your **caption**, or click **AI Caption** for help: give it a topic and tone (or leave both blank for general ideas), or turn on **Write in my voice** once you have sent a couple of posts to get captions styled like your own past writing. Pick one of the variants to drop it straight into the caption box.
3. Click **Suggest hashtags** to add a ready-made set (your own saved defaults plus a few seasonal and topic-relevant tags) to the end of your caption. It never repeats a hashtag already in your text.
4. If your caption runs long, watch for the length warning under the caption box: it flags when you are over Instagram's, Threads', or Telegram's caption limit for whichever destinations you have picked.
5. Choose a **photo**:
   - **Upload your own**: pick one or more images from your computer (up to 10). They are copied into the app so they stay available even if you move the originals.
   - **Generate with AI**: describe the image you want and Butler creates one for you.
   - **No photo**: a text-only post.
6. Add your **link**, if any. Turn on **Post the link in the first comment** if you would rather the link land as the first comment than sit in the caption. This is a common trick to keep the caption clean and avoid a platform down-ranking a post with an outbound link.
7. Pick your **destinations**, or leave them all unchecked to use every destination you have enabled.
   If you have more than one Facebook account, the **Accounts** box lets you pick which ones this post goes out from (up to 5). Each picked account posts only to the Groups and Pages it manages, so no group gets the same post twice. Leave them all unticked and every Group or Page posts from its usual account.
8. Choose **when to post**: Post now, Schedule once, or Evergreen.
9. Turn on **Watch it run** in the top right of the app if you want to see the browser window while it posts. Turn it off there to run in the background.
10. Click **Save to library** (for scheduled or evergreen posts) or **Publish now**. If your seat cannot approve content (see **Team approvals** below), this button reads **Submit for approval** instead, and the post waits for an owner or manager to approve it before it ever goes out.

## Compose in three steps

Compose walks you through a post in three short steps, so you never face one long form: **1 Write** (title, caption, AI help), **2 Photo and where** (photo or video, link, destinations, accounts), and **3 When and folder** (post now, schedule once, or evergreen, plus the folder to save it in). Use **Next** and **Back**, or click a step at the top to jump.

## Your Library: folders you name

The **Library** tab (open it from the **Tools** menu in the tab strip) holds every post you have saved, in folders you create yourself.

- Click **+ New folder** to make one. Use the pencil to rename it, or the **x** to delete it. Deleting a folder never deletes its posts: they move back to **Unfiled**.
- **Drag a post onto a folder** to file it. Every post also has a **Move to** menu if you would rather not drag.
- Drag the folders themselves up or down to put them in the order you like.
- When you compose a post, pick **Save into folder** in step 3. In **Content Packs**, pick **Into folder** next to **Add to schedule** and the posts you add land there.

## Content Packs: a year of ready-made posts in one click

**Content Packs** is a library of thousands of ready-to-post pictures with a caption already written: recipes, crafts, memes, quotes, polls and seasonal fun. The first time you open it, a short tour explains the tab and ends with two choices: **Choose for me** or **I'll pick and schedule myself**. You can replay the tour any time with **Take the tour**.

**Browse and pick yourself.** Search, or narrow by **Topic**, type, season or color, then select the pieces you like and use **Add to schedule**. Nothing is saved to your computer when you schedule: each picture is fetched when its post goes out. Every caption is reshuffled for you, so your posts never read the same as anyone else's. Recipe and craft steps, ingredient lists and the words on the picture are never reworded.

**See how fresh a piece is.** Each piece shows how many times other Butler users scheduled it, plus a green **Fresh** badge when hardly anyone has. Use **Usage** to show only Fresh or Popular pieces, and **Sort** to put the least or most used first. If the counts cannot load, these controls simply stay hidden.

**Swipe-through toys and games.** The **Toys and games** type holds homemade toys and games built from cheap things at home, like a cardboard marble run or a backyard foam party. Each one is a single post with several photos: a cover that says "swipe to learn", a "what you need" photo, then one photo for every step. Pieces show how many photos they have, and the preview lets you page through them. When one goes out, all of its photos post together in order.

**Choose for me** schedules your whole year from a few questions. Open **Content Packs**, choose **Choose for me**, then:

1. Pick your topics and the kinds of posts. Everything starts switched on, including PG-13 and Naughty Elf, and you can switch off anything that is not for you.
2. Set how many posts a day (4 to start).
3. Set the timing: by default a post every 2 to 4 hours between 7 am and 9 pm, on every day.
4. Pick the months to cover (all 12 to start).
5. Choose where the posts go. Leave **Remind me to refill when a month gets low** on if you want a nudge before the calendar runs out.
6. Check the summary and click **Schedule my year**.

Your calendar fills with real posts you can open, edit, move or delete like any other. Holiday pictures land in their own month, no picture repeats within 90 days, and pieces few other users have scheduled are picked first. You can **Pause** the plan or **Remove its upcoming posts** at any time; posts you wrote yourself are never touched.

**Refill reminder.** About a month before your scheduled posts run out, you get a notification, a message in the HUD and a row in the bell. Click **Refill my calendar** to reopen the walkthrough with your earlier answers already filled in. **Remind me later** snoozes it for a few days.

## Your Calendar: today's deals and your own posts together

The **Calendar** tab opens on today. It shows two lanes side by side: the **deal posts** going out from Deals Butler, and your own **evergreen and scheduled posts**. Below them, **Already sent** shows what went out today.

Pick a view with the buttons at the top: **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **List**, or **By date**. For Day, Week, and List, choose **Cards** or **Table** (Week also has a **Calendar** layout). Use the arrows to move to the previous or next day, week, or month, and **Today** to come back.

- **Hover any post** to see its time, where it goes, and its status. Click it to edit the time and caption right there.
- **Open a sent post.** Click a post that already went out to see its photo and where it posted. Each destination has an **Open post** link that takes you straight to the live post in your browser.
- **Deal posts are marked Deal.** Anything you change on one (its time, caption, or Hold) is saved in Deals Butler too, so Deals Butler and the calendar always agree. A deal with no exact time posts in queue order, so the calendar shows an estimated time with a **~**. Saving a time pins it.
- **Drag a post to another day** to move it (it keeps its time of day). Saved posts with no date wait in a strip above the calendar: drag one onto a day, then pick its time.
- **Open a day** to see everything on it grouped by account. **Filter** by words, platform, account, status, or type (Once, Evergreen, or Deal). Tick posts to pause, resume, publish now, move by a number of days, or delete them together.


## Your dashboard, one card per Deals Butler

The **Social Posting Butler** dashboard shows posts sent, deliveries and failures with a chart, what is coming up next, and a card for each Deals Butler (for example Happy House and Elf On The Shelf) so you can see how each one is doing. Click a card to open its page.

## Your own Social Posting Butler workspaces

Under **Social Posting Butler** in the left menu you will find one page for each Deals Butler you run, such as Happy House and Elf On The Shelf. They appear on their own, so there is nothing to add. Each page has its own posts, folders, and calendar, shows only that Deals Butler's posts, and what you publish there goes out through its queue. You can rename a page from the banner at the top. The main **Social Posting Butler** page shows every Deals Butler together.

### Importing a content calendar

Click **Import CSV** on the Calendar tab for two ways to bulk-load posts:

- **Content calendar**: bring any spreadsheet with a date column plus a caption, link, or image path. Column names are matched automatically, so Date, Caption, Link, Image, Destinations, and Title all work in any order, under almost any header wording. Each row becomes a one-time scheduled post; re-importing the same file updates matching rows instead of duplicating them, and a row you have since hand-edited is left alone.
- **Butler Challenge queue**: the fixed-format queue used for Butler's own posting challenges, targeting a single Facebook group.

## Archive: see what actually landed

Switch the Calendar tab to **Archive** to see everything that already went out. Each post shows a chip for every account it was sent to: a check for delivered, a cross for failed (hover it for the reason). **Retry failed** re-sends only the ones that did not go through, and **Post again** schedules a copy you can change.

A post that keeps failing (for example, a dead login or a broken link) stops retrying silently forever: after several failed attempts in a row, Butler pauses it automatically so it is not hammering a destination that will not accept it. Resume it from the Calendar tab once you have fixed the underlying problem.

## Quiet hours

Social Posting Butler's scheduler honors the same quiet hours (or active-hours window) you set for Deal Posting, so an evergreen post never fires in the middle of the night just because this workspace did not know about your schedule. The **Settings** tab shows a read-only summary of your current quiet hours, with a link to manage them in Deal Posting.

## Team approvals

If you run this app on a Team or Agency seat with per-seat permissions turned on, an owner or manager can require that a VA's posts be approved before they go out. A seat without the **Approve posts before they go out** permission has its saves land in the new **Approvals** tab instead of posting directly; an owner or manager reviews each one there and approves or rejects it. Solo installs, and any install that has not turned on seat permissions, are unaffected: everything you save posts normally, the same as always.

## Destinations

The **Social Destinations** tab shows which destinations you have enabled. You enable and configure them (sign-ins, API keys, group and channel targets) in Deal Posting, and Social Posting Butler honors the same set. Use the **Manage destinations** button to jump there.

## Where the data goes

Your post library and its schedule live on your computer, alongside the app's other data. Posts publish through the shared posting engine, so a post you send here shows up in your posting history the same as any other.

## Common gotchas

- **"No destinations enabled yet"**: enable at least one destination in Deal Posting first, then come back.
- **AI help fails**: set your OpenAI key in API Integrations (or confirm you are on a plan with hosted AI allowance remaining). The same key powers every AI feature in the app.
- **A scheduled post did not fire**: check that the post is enabled, has not been auto-disabled after repeated failures, and is not sitting in Approvals waiting on a decision. Also confirm automation is not globally paused and you are not inside your quiet hours. Evergreen posts skip a missed time (for example if the computer was asleep) rather than posting it late.
- **The link is not in the caption**: if you turned on "Post the link in the first comment," the link is posted as the first comment instead, by design.
- **My post says "Submit for approval" instead of "Save to library"**: your seat does not have permission to approve content. An owner or manager needs to approve it from the Approvals tab.
