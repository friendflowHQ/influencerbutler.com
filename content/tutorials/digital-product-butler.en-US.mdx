## What Digital Product Butler does

Digital Product Butler figures out what digital product you should sell, writes it, and gets it ready to sell from your Foyer page, all in one pass. Tell it your niche and audience, and it comes back with a free sample (a lead magnet to grow your email list) and a paid product, each written as a full ebook, with AI-generated cover art and chapter images, packaged as both a PDF and an EPUB file. Before it suggests anything, it searches Etsy for your niche plus "digital" and reads the best sellers: what they cost, how many are on sale, how big the winning bundles are and which formats sell. The idea, page count and price are based on that, so the product is made to compete with what buyers already pay for. If Etsy can't be checked, pricing falls back to an AI estimate and the card says so.

This is a Power User feature: it uses your own OpenAI key for every generation step, and the finished products sell through the same Stripe-powered commerce system already built into Foyer Butler.

## Creating a product

1. Open **Digital Product Butler** and click **Start**.
2. Tell it your **niche or topic** (for example, "Elf on the Shelf ideas") and, optionally, your **audience**.
3. Pick a **type of digital download**, or leave it on **Let AI decide** so it picks whatever format sells best for your niche (an ebook, printable idea cards, a planner, or a checklist pack).
4. Choose how the **free sample** should relate to the paid product: a sample chapter, a mini version, a companion checklist, or let AI decide.
5. Turn on **Also create a post-purchase upsell** if you want a third product offered to buyers right after they purchase the main one.
6. Pick **Colors and style**: **Match the topic** (recommended) gives a Christmas kit red, green and gold, a Halloween kit orange and purple, and so on, with fonts and borders to match. Or choose **Use my Foyer page colors**.
7. Pick how many **Coloring pages** to include. **Automatic** adds 4 for kids and holiday topics and none for others. Each one is an AI drawing, so it adds a few cents to the cost.
8. Review the summary and generate. Your new campaign appears in the queue below, with a line showing what's selling on Etsy (tap **What's selling on Etsy** to see the top sellers and their prices).

## What's inside a paid product

A paid product is built like the kits that sell on Etsy: a short, friendly guide plus a stack of designed printable pages, each filling a full Letter page and printed from home. Depending on the topic you get checklists with big tick boxes, star trackers, countdown calendars, cut-out note cards, certificates, bingo boards, word searches, planners and coloring pages, all in the product's theme. There is a designed cover with a page-count badge and a "What's inside" page.

Want something different? Use **Ask for changes** on a card, for example "add 5 printable tracking sheets, one per page". It builds the pages for real, then says when it's done, with a **See your changes** button that opens the new PDF.

## Reviewing and publishing

Open a campaign from the queue to generate its chapter content, cover and interior art, and its PDF and EPUB files. Once everything is generated, use the **Review and tweak** step to adjust a product's title or price before publishing. When you are happy with it, publish: each product (the free sample, the paid product, and the upsell if you added one) is created in Foyer Butler's commerce system, its files are uploaded, its listing image is hosted (a Pinterest-ready 2:3 image showing the cover, a few of the real pages and the page count, also saved next to the PDF so you can pin it), and a product tile is added to your Foyer page automatically.

Selling a priced product requires your Stripe Connect account to be set up under Foyer Butler, Monetize. A free sample sells with no Stripe setup needed at all.

## Your products: previews, Archive and Delete

Every product you create shows as a card under **Your campaigns**, with its cover art on the left. Hover over a cover (or tab to it) to see a bigger preview: the title, price, description, chapter list and a few of the inside pages. Click the cover to open the PDF.

Use the **Active** and **Archive** tabs at the top of the list to keep it tidy:

- **Archive** moves a product to the Archive tab. Nothing changes on your Foyer page. If the product is published, you can also tick **Also pause its posting and email funnels**. Click **Restore** on an archived card to bring it back.
- **Delete** removes the product for good, including its chapters, covers, PDF and EPUB. If it is published, it is also taken off your Foyer page so it stops selling, and its funnels are paused. People who already bought it keep their copy. You need to be signed in to delete a published product.
