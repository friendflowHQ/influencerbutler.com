## What it is

The phone app is the pocket half of Influencer Butler. It pairs with the desktop app on your computer so you can check in on it, pause or resume your butlers, send it products you find while you shop, and ask Butler AI a question, without going back to your desk. It installs to your home screen like a normal app and works on both iPhone and Android.

Your computer still does the work. The phone is a remote control and a window into it, so most actions need the computer to be on and reachable.

## Pair your phone

Pairing links one phone to one computer. You only do it once.

1. On the computer, open Influencer Butler and go to **Settings**, then **Mobile**, then **Show pairing code**
1. On the phone, open the camera and scan the QR code. It opens the app and pairs by itself
1. No camera handy? On the computer click **Copy pairing link**, send it to yourself, and open it on the phone

That is it. The app remembers the pairing, so next time you just open it.

Your phone never receives your license key. It gets its own device token that you can remove at any time, and the pairing key that secures the connection stays on the phone and is never sent to a server.

## The four tabs

- **Home**: whether the computer is reachable, your hours and money saved, your commission, and any alerts that need you (a sign-in, new campaigns, an Amazon cooldown, or a butler that stopped). One tap pauses or resumes everything.
- **Butlers**: every butler with its current state and the controls that fit it: Run (where the computer allows it), Pause, Resume, Stop, and Stop everything.
- **Share**: paste a product link, or share one straight from a shopping app (on Android the app opens here already filled in). From a link you can make your affiliate link, check it for campaigns and earnings, add it to your link page, or add it to Deals Butler. If the computer is off, link and deal requests wait in line and finish when it comes back.
- **More**: which computer you are paired to, what this phone is allowed to do, your language (English, Spanish, or French), how to add the app to your home screen, and a button to remove this computer.

## Ask Butler AI

Tap the chat button to ask Butler AI anything about setting up or using Influencer Butler, right from your phone. It answers with real steps, points you to the matching tutorial video, and can even book a setup call for you. It is the same assistant you get on the desktop and in the Chrome extension, so the answers match wherever you ask.

Because the assistant runs through your computer, it answers when your computer is reachable. If the Home tab shows the computer is off, turn it on and ask again.

## Add it to your home screen

Make the app one tap away:

- **On iPhone**: open the app in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **On Android**: open the app in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The **More** tab also has a reminder of these steps.

## Good to know

- One phone pairs to one computer at a time.
- Sharing a link into the app works on Android. On iPhone, use the **Paste** button on the Share tab instead, since iPhone cannot share into a web app yet.
- Butler names show in English on the phone even when your app language is Spanish or French.
- To unpair, open **More** and remove the computer. You can pair again any time from **Settings**, then **Mobile** on the computer.
