> **Nothing switches unless you say so.** Your regular Walmart posting keeps running exactly as it is. Your Walmart posts only come from your Black Friday list if you turn on the switch, and the switch names the exact days before you agree.

## What Black Friday Event Finder does

Walmart runs its own holiday sales: **Early Black Friday Deals (Nov 12 to 15)**, **Black Friday Deals (Nov 23 to 29)** and **Cyber Monday Deals (Nov 30 to Dec 2)**. Black Friday Event Finder gives you a separate list for them, next to WAL Deals Finder under Walmart Butler.

- **Search finds only Walmart's event deals.** It reads Walmart's Black Friday and Cyber Monday deal pages, checks each item's live price, and keeps only the ones that are really on sale. Your keywords and filters narrow the results, the same way they do in WAL Deals Finder.
- **Prep ahead of time.** Queue deals now. They wait in this list until the event.
- **Starts with your setup.** The first time you open it, it copies your WAL Deals Finder keywords, filters and captions. Use **Copy my WAL Deals Finder setup** to copy them again later.

## Step by step

1. Open **Walmart Butler**, then **Black Friday Event Finder**.
2. Check your keywords, filters and captions. Change anything you want for Black Friday. It does not change WAL Deals Finder.
3. Click **Search Deals**. Turn on **Watch it run** in the top bar if Walmart asks you to prove you are a person, so you can clear it in the Walmart window.
4. Queue the deals you want to post.
5. To post this list during the event, click **Set up the switch**. Read the days, tick **I understand which days my Walmart posts switch over**, and click **Turn on the switch**.

## How the switch works

- On **Monday, Nov 23 at 12:00 AM Eastern**, your Walmart posts start coming from your Black Friday list instead of WAL Deals Finder.
- It runs through **Wednesday, Dec 2**. On **Thursday, Dec 3** you are switched back to WAL Deals Finder automatically.
- **Your Amazon posts are not affected.** They keep posting on your normal schedule.
- If your Black Friday list runs out, your regular Walmart deals fill in, so your Walmart posts never go quiet. Untick **When this list runs out, keep posting my regular Walmart deals** to post only your Black Friday list.
- You can **turn the switch off** at any time. If you turn it off during the event, it stays off.

## Where you will see it

- A banner at the top of the app reminds you before the event, tells you when the switch turns on, and tells you when you are back on WAL Deals Finder.
- While the switch is on, WAL Deals Finder shows a short note that your Walmart posts are coming from Black Friday Event Finder.
- You also get a desktop notification when the switch turns on and when it turns off.

## Good to know

- Posts go to the same destinations and on the same schedule as your Deal Posting.
- Every Walmart deal is checked again right before it posts. A deal that is no longer on sale is held, not posted.
- Walmart decides what appears on its event pages. If a search finds nothing, Walmart may not have posted the deals yet, so try again later.
