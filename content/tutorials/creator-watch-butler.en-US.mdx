## What Creator Watch Butler does

Instead of searching Amazon yourself for products to promote, Creator Watch Butler lets you watch other creators' storefronts and let their picks come to you. Add the creators you trust, and whenever one of them adds a new product, it shows up in your feed, with products you can also earn on (through Creator Connections or Storefronts Plus Commission) ranked first.

The first check after you add a creator just records what they currently promote; it doesn't alert you on anything from that first pass. After that, anything new they add triggers an alert.

## Watching a creator

1. Open **Watch a creator** and paste a creator's storefront link (like `amazon.com/shop/theirhandle`) or just their handle.
2. Click **Add creator**.

Your watched creators appear in the list below, and their current picks start filling the **What your creators are promoting** feed after the first check.

## Checking for new picks

Click **Check now** at any time to run a check immediately. Or leave **Check my creators automatically** on (it's on by default) and Creator Watch Butler checks on its own while the app is open, on the interval you choose under **How often**, every 3, 6, or 12 hours, or once a day. More frequent checks catch new picks sooner but visit Amazon more often; every 6 hours is a good balance.

## Getting notified

Under **How you get told**, choose how you want to hear about a new pick:

- **Show a pop-up when a creator adds a product**: on by default.
- **Add a card to the Dashboard for new picks**: off by default.

## Reviewing new picks

The **New picks** section lists everything new since your last visit. Click **Mark all read** once you've reviewed them.
