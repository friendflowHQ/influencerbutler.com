> **It fills up by itself.** There is nothing to run. Every Walmart product the app looks up while you harvest deals or repost is tracked here, and a daily check refreshes each one. You can also add specific products by hand.

## What Walmart Research does

Picking a Walmart product to feature is a guess unless you know whether it actually sells and how its price moves. Walmart Research answers that with your own history, built over time:

- **Price history**: every sale price and list price the app sees for a product is stored, so you can tell whether today's price is really a low.
- **Rank history**: a best-effort popularity rank for the product over time. Walmart does not publish a true best-seller rank, so this is approximate: it comes from where the product shows up in search results when a keyword check finds it. Treat it as directional, not exact.
- **Estimated monthly sales and revenue**: a rank-based model estimates monthly units and revenue (shown with a "~" so you know it is an estimate). Because the rank is approximate, treat the estimate as a rough guide rather than a hard number.
- **Availability**: whether the product was in stock at each check, so you can spot items that keep going out of stock.

## How products get tracked

Three ways, and the first two need no effort from you:

1. **Automatically**, whenever the app already looks a Walmart product up, for example while you harvest Walmart deals or run a repost. No extra work and no extra lookups.
2. **A daily check** refreshes every tracked product so its history keeps growing even when you are not actively working.
3. **By hand**, when there is a specific product you want to follow. Paste its Walmart link, item ID, or UPC into the "Track a product" box and it starts building history from the next check.

## Reading the table

Open **Walmart Research** under Walmart Butler. Each row is a product being tracked:

- **Price / List / % Off**: the latest observed sale price, list price, and discount.
- **Rank**: the latest approximate popularity rank, when one is known.
- **Est. sales/mo and Est. revenue/mo**: the model's rough estimate, shown with a "~".
- **In stock**: whether it was available at the last check.
- **Last seen**: when the app last refreshed the product.

Use the search box to filter by item ID, title, brand, or UPC.

## Reading a product's history

Click **View history** on any row:

- The **price chart** shows every recorded price, so a real drop is easy to see.
- The **rank chart** is drawn so up means selling better. A rising line is a product gaining momentum. Remember the rank is approximate.

## Tips

- The history gets more useful the longer it runs. It costs nothing and keeps itself up to date.
- Add the products you are considering to the watchlist a few weeks before a season so the trend has time to build.
- Use **Check now** in the top bar if you want to refresh everything right away instead of waiting for the daily check.
