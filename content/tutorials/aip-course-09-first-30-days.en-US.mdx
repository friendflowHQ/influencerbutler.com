## 🗺️ How to use this plan

Each week has a handful of concrete actions. If a week takes you two weeks, fine: the order matters more than the calendar. Approval wait times will shift things around; when you are blocked waiting on Amazon, pull actions forward from later weeks (there is always filming to do).

Budget assumption: 3 to 5 hours per week.

## 1️⃣ Week 1: apply and stock the pipeline

- [ ] Apply to the program with your strongest social account (Module 3) {#w1-apply}
- [ ] Build your product list: walk through your home and list 20+ products you own and can review {#w1-product-list}
- [ ] Write bullet scripts for your first three videos (Module 5) {#w1-scripts}
- [ ] Film your first three videos, horizontal, in daylight {#w1-film-three}

## 2️⃣ Week 2: pass the onsite gate

- [ ] Submit your review videos for onsite approval the moment the program lets you (Module 4) {#w2-submit-onsite}
- [ ] While waiting, film your next batch of 5 videos {#w2-batch-five}
- [ ] Set up your storefront basics: handle, photo, banner, bio (Module 7) {#w2-storefront-basics}
- [ ] Publish your first idea list {#w2-first-idea-list}

## 3️⃣ Week 3: build the habit

- [ ] Pick your weekly batch-filming slot and put it in your calendar as a recurring block {#w3-batch-slot}
- [ ] Film and upload 5 more videos with specific titles and careful product tags (Module 6) {#w3-five-more}
- [ ] Add your storefront link to every social bio {#w3-bios}
- [ ] Do your first ten-minute stats check-in and record baseline numbers (Module 8) {#w3-baseline}

## 4️⃣ Week 4: reach 20 and review

- [ ] Get your library to 20 uploaded videos {#w4-twenty-videos}
- [ ] Publish a second idea list, ideally seasonal {#w4-second-list}
- [ ] Weekly check-in: which videos get views? Film more products like those {#w4-review-doubledown}
- [ ] Decide your ongoing weekly quota (5 to 10 videos per week is the compounding zone) {#w4-set-quota}

## 🏁 The 30-day finish line

If you did the above, you now have: program membership, onsite placement (or a resubmission in flight), a live storefront with two idea lists, a 20-video library that earns around the clock, and a weekly rhythm. That puts you ahead of the vast majority of people who join the program and stall.

The next 11 months are the same loop with bigger numbers. Module 10 covers what to add once the loop feels easy.
