## ⬆️ Where uploads happen

You upload through your influencer dashboard on Amazon: on desktop via your storefront's creator hub, or with the **Amazon Creator** mobile app (formerly part of the shopping app flow), which is the fastest path from phone footage to live video. Sign in, choose upload video, and follow the prompts.

## 🏷️ Tagging: the step that determines your earnings

When you upload, you tag the product(s) shown in the video. Tagging is not paperwork; it is literally how Amazon decides **which product pages your video appears on** and which sales you get credited for.

- **Tag the exact product you reviewed.** Search by the product name or paste the ASIN (the 10-character ID in every Amazon product URL, like B0ABC12345).
- **Watch out for variations**: if the product has sizes and colors, tag the exact variation you show when the listing structure allows it, and know that Amazon groups some variations under one parent listing.
- **Tag every product that appears** if your video covers more than one (comparison videos can earn from both pages).
- **Do not tag unrelated popular products** to chase traffic. It fails review, and shoppers who feel baited do not buy.

## ✍️ Titles that get clicks on a product page

Your title shows in the video carousel on the product page, next to other creators' videos. The shopper is deciding which one to watch. Generic titles ("My review!") lose to specific ones:

- "Honest review after 6 months of daily use"
- "Testing it on frozen fruit: does it survive?"
- "Watch this before buying the larger size"

Formula: **the question a buyer has + the promise you answer it.** Keep it under about 60 characters so it does not truncate.

## 🖼️ Thumbnails

Amazon lets you pick or upload a thumbnail. Clear product shot, good light, minimal text. If the auto-generated frame is sharp and shows the product, it is fine: do not burn 20 minutes per video in a thumbnail editor.

## 📆 Upload cadence: steady beats heroic

The library effect is the entire business model: every uploaded video is a little earner that compounds. A sustainable target for a beginner:

- **Weeks 1 to 4**: 3 to 5 videos per week (from your weekly batch session)
- **Once comfortable**: 5 to 10 per week if you are treating this as a real income stream

One caution: quality still gates everything. A hundred careless videos that fail review or bore shoppers earn less than thirty good ones.
