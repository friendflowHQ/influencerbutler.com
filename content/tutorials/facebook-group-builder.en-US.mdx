## What Facebook Group Builder does

Facebook Group Builder helps you stand up a niche Facebook group and grow it without doing the busywork by hand. You give it a niche; it uses your OpenAI key to write SEO-friendly group names, a description, a welcome post for new members, and a batch of engagement posts people actually comment on. It designs a cover graphic sized for Facebook's group-cover crop, in the same look as your invite graphic, so nothing gets clipped on upload. Then it helps you create the group and registers it with Deals Butler as a posting destination, so your daily deals start flowing into it automatically.

It is built for creators who want more than one place to post their storefront deals, and who would rather approve AI-written content than write every post themselves.

## Before you start

- **Connect OpenAI.** The written content is generated with your OpenAI key. Open **API Integrations**, add your key, and run the connection test. Until it is connected, this workspace stays locked. (The cover and invite graphics have a built-in mode that needs no key.)
- **Be signed in to Facebook in the app.** Building the group drives your real Facebook in the browser, using the same session as Deals Butler. Sign in to Facebook there once first.

## Step 1: Plan the content

In the **Blueprint** section, enter your niche or topic, pick a tone, and choose how many groups and engagement posts you want. Turn on the spicy-posts option if you want a few debate-sparking posts that get people commenting. Click **Generate content plan**.

In a few seconds you get an editable plan: SEO name candidates, a description, a welcome post, and your engagement posts. Everything is editable. Change any wording you like before you build.

## Step 2: Generate a cover

In the **Cover image** section, click **Generate cover**. The cover is a designed graphic in the same look as your invite graphic, with your group name as the headline, fit to Facebook's group-cover crop with a safe center so the name is not cut off when Facebook re-crops it on upload. Choose how the center illustration is drawn, the same three ways the invite graphic offers:

- **Built-in illustration**: a flat illustration picked to fit your niche. It is instant and needs no OpenAI key.
- **AI illustration**: generated with your OpenAI key for any niche.
- **Upload my own image**: use a photo or graphic of your own.

The colors are chosen to fit your niche automatically, or turn that off to set your own brand color, so your cover and your invite graphic read as one set.

## Make an invite-friends graphic

In the **Invite friends graphic** section, Butler makes a ready-to-post 1080x1080 welcome image that asks members to invite 3 friends, plus a warm caption written for your niche. Enter your group name and niche, then choose how the center illustration is drawn:

- **Built-in illustration**: a flat illustration picked to fit your niche. It is instant and needs no OpenAI key.
- **AI illustration**: generated with your OpenAI key for any niche.
- **Upload my own image**: use a photo or graphic of your own.

The colors are chosen to fit your niche automatically, or turn that off to set your own brand color. Click **Generate graphic** to see the image and its caption. From there you can copy the caption, open the saved image, or post the image and caption straight to one of the groups you have already created.

## Step 3: Build the group

Choose a **Creation mode**:

- **Assisted** (recommended): Butler opens Facebook's Create Group page with the name pre-filled and lets you click the final Create yourself. Safest for Facebook's limits.
- **Full auto**: Butler drives the whole create step for you.

Keep **Pause for my approval between steps** on while you are getting comfortable, and keep the daily group cap low. Facebook has no group-creation API, so this works by driving your real Facebook; creating many groups quickly can trip Facebook's limits.

> Group creation is in beta. Content and cover generation are ready to use now; the automated create, cover upload, and invite steps are still being verified against live accounts.

## Step 4: Grow on autopilot

In the **Grow** section you can schedule recurring viral posts and invite people who react to your group's posts to join. Welcome, engagement, and viral posts are scheduled through the shared posting queue and spread across the coming days so a new group looks active without a burst.

## Tracking and Deals Butler

Every group you build gets a unique tracking source id and, when you are signed in, a branded tracked link, so you can see which group drives clicks. The group is also added to Deals Butler's destinations automatically, so your scheduled deals post there with no extra setup.
