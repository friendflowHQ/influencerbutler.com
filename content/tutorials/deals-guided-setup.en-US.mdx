## The 3-step Quickstart

The first time you open Deals Butler, it starts you in a short Quickstart that gets Amazon deals posting to one Facebook group in three steps. Everything else is set to sensible defaults you can change later.

1. **Connect your Facebook group.** Sign in to Facebook once, then paste the link to the group you want to post to (it looks like `facebook.com/groups/...`). This is the one thing Butler cannot guess for you, and it is the step people most often miss: if no group is connected, nothing posts.
2. **Pick your niche.** Choose the categories you post about. Butler fills in proven, seasonal keywords for each one, so your deal search knows what to look for.
3. **Press Start.** Butler runs your first deal search, sets up a caption for you, and turns on auto-posting on a steady schedule with a gentle warm-up (5 deals on day 1, then 1 more each day, up to 14 on day 10) so a newer profile eases into posting. Your deals start going out on their own. You can untick the warm-up on the Start step.

That is it. You will see "Step 1 of 3" at the top so you always know where you are.

## Where to find it

- **First run**: the Quickstart opens on its own the first time you open Deals Butler with nothing set up.
- **The banner** at the top of the workspace when nothing is configured yet: press **Quickstart: 3 steps**.
- You can also ask the AI Assistant to set up deal posting for you.

## Nothing posting? Check these two things

Almost every "it is not posting" case is one of these:

- **No group is connected.** A destination that is switched on but has no link added cannot post. Add your group link in Social Destinations. Butler will also show a "No place to post yet" note on the scheduler when this is the case.
- **The scheduler is off.** Auto-posting has a master switch (**Post deals for me**). The Quickstart turns it on for you; if you set things up by hand, switch it on yourself.

## Advanced setup

Want Walmart deals too, more destinations (Pages, Telegram, Reddit, Instagram, Threads, Benable), or finer control over what you optimize for? Press **Advanced setup** on the banner. It is the full question-driven wizard, and it walks you through everything the Quickstart defaults for you:

1. **What you are going after**: clicks, commissions, or both. This sets which Amazon deals Butler looks for and which go out first. Clicks posts only your Sponsored Products and CreatorConnections deals, highest earning per click first. Commissions leads with the deals that pay the most per sale and skips the ones worth a few cents. Both keeps the balanced order. You can change it any time from Optimizing for on the Search Deals tab.
2. **Where to post**: pick any mix of Facebook Groups, Facebook Pages, Telegram, Reddit, Instagram Channels, Instagram feed posts, Instagram Threads, and Benable. You only set up the ones you pick.
3. **Keywords**: choose your niches for each store (Amazon and Walmart), and every month gets pre-filled with proven seasonal keywords.
4. **Your post**: start from the recommended caption template (percent off plus your link), write it in your own voice (Butler learns your style from a few of your own posts), or build your own later in Build Your Post.
5. **Destinations**: for each channel you picked, the wizard walks you through exactly what that channel needs: Facebook sign-in and your group links, a Telegram bot token, a Reddit app, Instagram channel links, or Benable list links.
6. **Schedule**: choose Steady (every 2 hours), Active (every hour), or Max (every 15 minutes). The wizard also switches on auto-send and a recurring deal search, and turns on the warm-up by default (5 deals on day 1, then 1 more each day, up to 14 on day 10). Untick **Warm up my posting (recommended)** to skip it.
7. **First run**: run your first deal search and send a test post to any destination.

Every answer is applied through the same settings the tabs use, so you can fine-tune anything later in the normal panes.

## Skipping and resuming

Every step in Advanced setup has a **Skip this step** button, and closing the wizard never loses your place: reopen it and choose **Resume setup** to continue where you left off, or **Start over** for a clean run. The final summary lists anything you skipped, with an **Open** button that jumps to the right tab so you can finish it whenever you are ready.

## Notes per channel

- **Facebook Groups and Pages** need a one-time Facebook sign-in. Paste up to 5 group links per profile, one per line.
- **Telegram** posts through your own bot: create one with @BotFather, add it to your channel as an admin, and the wizard tests the connection before turning the channel on.
- **Reddit** uses your own Reddit app (type: script) and a one-time sign-in window.
- **Instagram** posts into your broadcast channels: paste the channel links that look like `instagram.com/direct/t/...`.
- **Instagram Threads** needs no link at all: it posts to your own feed and asks you to sign in the first time it posts.
- **Benable** takes your list links; sign in from the Benable Butler workspace first.
