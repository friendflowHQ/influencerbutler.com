> **Read-only.** Walmart Performance only reads the Performance and storefront pages of your Walmart Creator account. It never posts, edits or deletes anything there.

## What Walmart Performance does

Walmart Creator keeps your numbers on four pages. Walmart Performance opens them in the Butler browser, reads them and shows them in one place:

- **Sales** for this month, split into **All**, **Storefront** (collections, posts and your storefront link) and **Affiliate links** (the links in your deal posts), plus the category and platform split and every item Walmart credited to you.
- **Commission** earned in the last 30 days.
- Your **storefront**: how many clicks, sales and commission your video and photo posts have, and every collection with its item count and whether Walmart flags items in it as out of stock.
- **Traffic**: visits, orders and conversion for the last 7 and 30 days, and visits per week.

## Sales, commissionable sales and commission

These three numbers are easy to mix up, and the difference is where the money is:

- **Sales** is the full value of every order Walmart credited to you, whole carts included. When someone buys a cat bed from your collection and adds paper towels and bleach to the same order, it all counts here.
- **Commissionable sales** is the part of those orders in categories that pay a commission. Grocery, pharmacy and household items pay 0%, so this is often much smaller than Sales.
- **Commission** is what you actually earn: commissionable sales times each category's rate (apparel and beauty pay the most, pets and personal care only a few percent).

The **Sales by item** table shows the rate and the earned amount for every item, so you can see at a glance which categories are worth featuring.

## Before you start

1. Sign in to Walmart Creator once from **Walmart Butler** > **Settings**. The Butler browser keeps that sign-in.
2. Open **Walmart Butler** > **Performance**.

## Reading your numbers

- Click **Read now**. The read takes about a minute. Turn on **Watch it run** in the top bar to watch the browser move through the pages.
- Turn on **Read every day** to refresh the numbers once a day in the background. It pauses with **Pause all automations**.
- If your Walmart session has expired, the read stops and shows **Sign in to Walmart**. Sign in, then read again.

## Good to know

- A page Walmart has changed is skipped, not fatal: the status line names the pages that could not be read and the rest of the numbers still land.
- Every read is kept, so the last good numbers stay on screen even if the next read fails.
- A collection flagged **Items out of stock** can be cleaned up from **Collection Builder** with **Remove out-of-stock items**.
