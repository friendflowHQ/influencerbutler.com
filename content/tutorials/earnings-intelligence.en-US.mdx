## What Earnings Intelligence does

Earnings Intelligence cross-references the ASINs your butlers have harvested against your real earnings data. Instead of guessing which products are worth featuring again, you get a clear picture of which ones actually paid, which just drove clicks, and how your commissions trend month to month.

Everything is read-only. It does not run a browser or change anything on Amazon. It reads from the data your other butlers already collect (Daily Commission, Storefront Butler, Orders Butler, cc-check) and pivots it six ways through the tabs across the top:

- **ASIN Explorer**: row-by-row detail for every earning ASIN (commission, units, active days, last seen, marketplaces).
- **Top Performers**: your lifetime winners, ranked.
- **Recovered Winners**: past earners that dropped out of the Creator Connections catalog and later came back.
- **Harvest Funnel**: a source-type breakdown, from harvested ASINs down to earning ASINs.
- **Monthly Breakdown**: your commissions by month, split across every revenue source.
- **Conversion Leak**: posted deals that went dead, and the clicks and commission you likely lost.

## Before you start

- **Earnings Intelligence is a Pro feature** (see Notes). Confirm you are signed in on a Pro subscription.
- **Your butlers have already harvested some data.** The tabs draw on Daily Commission, Storefront, Orders, and cc-check runs, so let those run at least once first.
- For **Recovered Winners**, run a Creator Connections catalog refresh so Butler can tell which past earners left the catalog.
- For **Conversion Leak**, post some deals from Deals Butler with Butler Link routing on, so clicks are measurable.

## How to use it

1. In the left menu, open **Amazon Butler**, then **Earnings Intelligence**.
2. Pick a time window. Use a **Quick date ranges** chip (**Today**, **This week**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month**, **Year to date**, **All time**), or set **Date from** and **Date to** by hand.
3. Narrow the view with the filters if you want: **Source type** (for example dailycommission or storefront), **Marketplace** (an ISO country code like US or UK), **Storefront** (your CC storefront slug), and **Tag** (an Amazon affiliate tag).
4. Click **Refresh** to re-query. The filters and date range apply to every tab, so always Refresh after you change them.
5. Switch between **ASIN Explorer**, **Top Performers**, **Recovered Winners**, **Harvest Funnel**, **Monthly Breakdown**, and **Conversion Leak** to pivot the same filtered data.
6. In **ASIN Explorer**, read the summary bar (total commission, total units, ASIN count) above the table, then scan the rows. The **CC** and **SPCC** markers flag ASINs with an active campaign or a sponsored offer.
7. Use the row quick actions to jump straight to work: **Open in Daily Commission** or **Open in Storefront** for a promising ASIN.
8. To keep a record, click **Export CSV** or **Export Excel** (both the main table and the Monthly Breakdown have their own export buttons).

## Notes

- **Pro subscription required.** Earnings Intelligence is a Pro feature and is available after your free trial. The nav entry shows a **Pro** pill.
- The numbers are only as complete as your harvests. If a tab looks empty, run the butler that feeds it (Daily Commission, Storefront, Orders, or cc-check) and Refresh.
- **Recovered Winners** stays empty until a Creator Connections catalog refresh has run, since that is how Butler knows which earners left the catalog.
- **Conversion Leak** counts deals without a Butler Link as unmeasurable. Turn on Butler Link routing for deals so every posted deal shows up.
- Nothing here posts, edits, or deletes. It is a reporting view, safe to open any time.
