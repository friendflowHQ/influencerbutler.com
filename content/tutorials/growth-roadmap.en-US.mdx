## Start from your Dashboard

Open **Dashboard**. Under your setup card you will see **Your next steps**. It shows up to three butlers worth your time next, each with a one-line reason.

For each one you can:

- **Open** (or **Finish setup** if something is still missing): takes you to that butler. Opening a butler never starts a run on its own.
- **Show me how**: opens that butler's tutorial in Help & Tutorials.
- **Not now**: hides that step for 14 days. It comes back after that.

## The full roadmap

Click **See my full roadmap** to see every step in four stages. Work from the top, one butler at a time.

- **Earn**: your storefront, Creator Connections campaigns, brand messages and daily deals.
- **Build an audience**: scheduled posts, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube.
- **Own your audience**: branded links, your bio page and Launch Butler, so followers land on pages you control.
- **Multiply**: Walmart, bulk link tag changes, earnings numbers, a digital product and Support Circle.

Each step has a status: **Needs setup**, **Ready, not used yet**, **In use**, **Not used lately** (nothing in 30 days) or **Not tried yet**. For a few butlers the app cannot see whether you have used them, so they count as done once you have opened them from the roadmap.

## Make the order fit you

The order follows the goals you picked. Click **Change my goals** on the card to pick different ones, and the next steps update. If your plan does not include a butler, its step shows **Unlock with Power User** instead of Open.

## Booking a call?

When you start booking a 1:1 call, the booking card has a **See my growth roadmap** button. Most "am I using this fully?" questions are answered there.
