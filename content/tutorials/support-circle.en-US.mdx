## What Support Circle does

Support Circle is a reciprocal-engagement network for Influencer Butler members. You share your Amazon storefront and social links, then opt in to having your own Like and Follow butlers automatically support other members' content across Amazon, TikTok, Instagram, Benable, and YouTube. Once you've shown you're actively supporting others, your own links start appearing to the rest of the Circle, so you get engagement back.

Everything is opt-in, platform by platform. Every action comes from your own account using your own butlers, nothing posts or likes on your behalf from someone else's device. You can pause or leave the Circle at any time.

## Sharing your links

Open **Share your links** and add your name plus a link for each platform you want to share (Amazon, TikTok, Instagram, Benable, YouTube). Each link has its own **Share** checkbox, so you choose exactly what goes into the Circle. This saves automatically as you type.

## Supporting other members

In **Support other members**, turn on **Include Support Circle members in my butlers**, then tick which of your own platforms' Like and Follow butlers should add Circle members' links to their runs. Your butlers run on their own existing schedule; Support Circle just adds the Circle's members as targets alongside whatever else they're already set to support.

New members see a quick-start banner with two options: **Set it all up for me** (turns on support and schedules your Like and Follow butlers to run daily) or **Walk me through it** (a guided setup).

## Seeing what's happening

The **Directory** lists members currently sharing links. Click **Refresh** to pull the latest list. The activity tabs show what your butlers have done for other members, and what support you've received back.

## Leaving the Circle

Click **Remove me from the Circle** at any time to pull all of your links out. Your butlers stop supporting other members, and your own links stop appearing to them.
