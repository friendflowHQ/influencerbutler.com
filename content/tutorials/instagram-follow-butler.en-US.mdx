## What Instagram Follow Butler does

Instagram Follow Butler grows your account the slow, steady way: it finds accounts in your niche that are already growing, follows their newest followers (people who are clearly active and interested in your topic), and then unfollows them a few days later so your following count stays tidy. The idea is simple. When you follow someone, they get a notification and many take a look back.

## Before you start

- Sign in to **Instagram** in the app. Follow Butler acts as you in your own browser session.
- Decide the **accounts in your niche** you want to pull followers from. Pick a few that are active and a good match for your audience.
- Keep your daily numbers modest at first. Slow and steady keeps your account safe.

## Set it up

1. Open **Instagram Follow Butler** in the left menu.
2. Add the **source accounts** in your niche. Follow Butler looks at their newest followers.
3. Set how many people to **follow per day** and how many days later to **unfollow** them.
4. Choose your **active hours** so it spreads actions through the day like a person would.
5. Start it. Follow Butler follows during your hours, then comes back to unfollow on schedule.

## Good to know

- **Go gently.** Instagram limits how fast anyone can follow and unfollow. Smaller daily numbers are safer and still work.
- Follow Butler keeps its own list of who it followed and when, so it never forgets to unfollow and never double-follows the same person.
- Use **Watch it run** in the top bar if you want to see it work in the browser.
