> **Heads up on costs:** the video runs on your own fal.ai key, so fal.ai bills you directly, not Influencer Butler. Each video costs a few dollars. Add your key on the **AI and Data Keys** page. The page shows an estimate next to each button before you spend anything.

## What Milestone Video does

Hit a follower or member milestone and want to celebrate? Milestone Video makes a short vertical video of you, like a champagne pop, with a big number such as **1k Members** shown at the end.

It lives under AI Influencer Butler, next to Setup, Fashion Looks and Your Clone. It works in two steps, so you never pay for a video before you have checked the pictures it is built from.

## Before you start

- **Your fal.ai key is added.** Open **AI and Data Keys** and add it. If you see "Add your fal.ai key in AI and Data Keys, then try again", the key is missing or was not saved.
- **A clear photo of you.** Milestone Video uses its own photos, separate from the ones you train your clone with, so add them here.

## Fill in the details

1. **Photo of you**: click **Upload a photo** and pick a clear, well lit, front-facing photo of your face. The video keeps your face and hair from this picture.
2. **Face photos** (optional): add 3 to 5 sharp, well lit photos of just your face from different angles. They help the key pictures and the video look like you. Without any, your main photo is used.
3. **Full-body photos** (optional): add 1 to 3 photos that show you from head to toe, standing straight. They help match your real body and proportions.
4. **Outfit and hat photos**: add pictures of the clothes you want to wear in the video, like a vest or a top hat. You can add up to 6. Crop out phone screens and other clutter so only the clothes show.
5. **Outfit description**: say in words what you are wearing, naming the colors and pieces. This is what makes the clothes come out exact. Leave it blank to use the default outfit: an orange satin vest, a white shirt, black trousers and a black top hat.
6. **Video style**: **Champagne pop with a bat** or **Champagne pop by hand**.
7. **Big number or text**: what to show big at the end, like **1k Members**. The text is added by the app after the video is made, so it is always spelled exactly the way you typed it.
8. **Small line under it** (optional): a smaller line such as "Thank you!". Leave it empty for none.

## Video options (optional)

Open **Video options** to fine tune. The defaults work well, so you can skip this.

- **Ending**: **Black and white splash card** fades the last moment into a black and white card with your text. **Text over the video** keeps the full color video and puts the text on top.
- **Text position** and **Text color**: white looks best on the splash card.
- **Video model**: Kling 2.5 Turbo is the default because it accepts photos of real people, but it has no sound. Seedance adds sound but may refuse photos of real people. If a model stops working, try another.
- **Add a champagne pop sound**: adds a bat crack, cork pop, fizz and a deep boom, timed to the moment of the hit. It is only used when the video model makes no sound of its own, like Kling.
- **Quality**: 720p or 1080p. 1080p is sharper but costs more and takes longer. The saved video is always 1080 by 1920.
- **Also show the video model all my photos**: hands the video model your photos and outfit photos too, which can keep the clothes more exact. Use it with a Seedance "all photos" model.
- **Template video** (optional): a saved video of moves you want to copy, up to 30 seconds. Your video re-performs its choreography. Without one, the video is built from the key pictures instead.
- **Swap my real face into the video**: after the video is made, your real face is placed onto it. Only used with a template video.

The template video and face swap options are the least tested parts of this feature. If the result looks off, make the video again without them.

## Step 1: check the key pictures

Click **Create the key pictures**. This makes quick, low cost pictures of you in the outfit, one for each moment of the video, like the setup, the batter's stance and the swing. The video moves between them, so if they look right, the video will too. The approximate cost is shown next to the button.

Not happy with them? Change the photos or the outfit description and click the button again. It is far cheaper to retry the pictures than the video.

## Step 2: make the video

When the key pictures look right, click **Make my video**. The cost shown next to the button is the most you can expect to pay. A progress bar walks through the stages, and it takes a few minutes. You can click **Cancel** at any time. Only one video builds at a time.

## When it is finished

- **Play video** and **Save video**: watch it and save a copy wherever you like.
- **Update the text**: change the big number, the small line, the ending style, the position or the color. The app re-applies the text to the video you already have, and you do not pay again because the expensive video part is not re-made.
- **Make another version**: makes a fresh video from your details, which does cost again.

Under **Your milestone videos** you will find everything you have made, newest first. Each one can be opened or deleted.

## Common gotchas

- **The clothes look wrong.** Tighten the outfit description, crop clutter out of your outfit photos, and recheck the key pictures before you make the video again.
- **The text is in the wrong place.** Use **Update the text** to move or recolor it for free.
- **The video model refuses my photo.** Switch to Kling, which accepts photos of real people.
- **"A video is already being made."** Wait for it to finish, or cancel it.
- **"Could not add the text because the video tools are missing."** Reinstall the app and try again.
