## What the global controls bar does

Influencer Butler runs a lot of butlers at once. Sometimes you want to stop all of them fast, or decide in one place whether their browser windows show on screen. The slim bar just under the header at the top of the window gives you two controls that reach every butler at once, without hunting through each workspace.

It has two parts: a **Pause all** button and a **Watch it run (all)** toggle.

## Pause everything at once

Click **Pause all** and every butler that is currently running stops where it is. It also freezes scheduled runs, so nothing new kicks off on its own while you are paused. This is the fastest way to bring the whole app to a calm stop, for example if your computer is busy or you need the browser windows to hold still.

Hover over the button and a little panel shows you what is **Running** and what is already **Paused**, so you can see the state of everything at a glance.

## Resume just the ones you want

Once you are paused, the button changes to **Resume**. Click it and you get a checklist of everything that was paused, with every butler checked. Leave the ones you want to start back up checked, uncheck any you would rather keep paused, and click **Resume selected**.

The butlers you leave checked pick up where they left off, and scheduled runs start working again. Anything you uncheck simply stays paused until you come back for it.

## Show or hide every browser at once

Each butler has its own "Watch it run" toggle that decides whether you see its browser window on screen or it works quietly in the background. The **Watch it run (all)** toggle on the global bar flips that setting for every butler at the same time.

- Turn it **on** to have every butler show its browser window, so you can watch the work happen.
- Turn it **off** to have every butler run hidden in the background, which keeps your screen clear.

Flipping it updates every butler's own toggle right away. It applies the next time each butler runs. A butler that is already in the middle of a run keeps its current window until it starts its next run, because a browser window cannot switch between shown and hidden once it has opened.

## Tips

- The **Pause all** button and the **Automations** pill near it work together. Pausing everything also switches Automations to **Paused**, and resuming turns it back on.
- Use **Pause all** as a quick "stop everything" button when your computer feels busy, then resume one butler at a time so the window stays smooth.
- If you only ever want your butlers to run quietly, flip **Watch it run (all)** off once and every butler stays hidden from then on.
