## What Campaign Deals does

Campaign Deals sits under CC Check and answers one question: which products in
the Creator Connections campaigns you're in are **on sale right now**? Instead
of scrolling a generic deals catalog, you see only the items tied to campaigns
you've joined, with the discount, the sale price, the original price, when the
deal ends, and your commission rate, all in one table sorted by biggest
discount first.

## Where the deal data comes from

You don't have to hunt for prices. CC Check already looks up each of your ASINs
through Amazon's Creators API, which returns current sale prices and deal
details. Campaign Deals reuses that same data and surfaces just the items that
are actually discounted. No browser window opens for this; it's a quick API
lookup.

## Refreshing the list

There are three ways the list stays current:

1. **Refresh Deals** button: re-checks current sale prices for your campaign
   ASINs on demand. Fast, no browser.
2. **Automatic**: any time you run **Check ASINs** in CC Check, the deal data it
   collects flows straight into Campaign Deals.
3. **Schedule**: set a start time and a repeat interval to refresh the on-sale
   list in the background (for example, once a day). The app must stay open for
   scheduled refreshes to fire.

## Accepted only, or available too

By default the table shows only campaigns you've **accepted**, the deals that
are most relevant to you. Turn on **Include available (not yet accepted)** to
also see on-sale items from campaigns that are available to you but that you
haven't joined yet, a quick way to spot a discounted product worth accepting a
campaign for.

One thing to weigh before you accept: if a product also has an **Earn on Clicks**
campaign (Sponsored Products for Creators), accepting its **Affiliate+** campaign
means Amazon pays you the Bonus Commission on that product, not the per-click
earnings, because the two don't stack. See the [Daily Commission Butler
tutorial](/help/tutorials/dailycommissionbutler) for how to decide which to take.

## Flag products that pay a social bonus

Some brands pay a higher rate when you post a product on your socials. Amazon
doesn't share that in the deal data, so the **Social bonus** column lets you
mark it yourself: tick the box on a product's row and add a short note (for
example, "+3% on IG"). Your marks are saved automatically and stay put across
refreshes, so you always know which discounted products are worth an extra post.

## Gotchas

- **Empty table**: if you haven't accepted any campaigns yet, or nothing in
  your campaigns is currently discounted, the table is empty. Click **Refresh
  Deals**, or turn on **Include available** to widen the net.
- **Prices look stale**: deals change quickly. Click **Refresh Deals** for the
  latest, or arm the schedule so it updates on its own.
