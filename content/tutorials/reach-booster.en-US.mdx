## What Reach Booster does

When Amazon flags one of your published videos or photos "Improve reach," that content is held back from appearing on product detail pages, which is the highest-traffic placement for Amazon content. The content still shows on your Storefront, but it misses out on the reach a detail-page placement would bring, and earns far less as a result.

Reach Booster collects every flagged item in one place so you can see what's held back and understand why, instead of hunting through Amazon's Creator Hub one item at a time.

## Before you start

Reach Booster works passively: install the Influencer Butler browser extension, then open your Amazon Creator Hub or **My content** list while signed in. Every flagged item you browse past gets recorded here automatically. There's nothing to configure.

## Using it

Click **Refresh** to pull in the current flagged list. The table shows each flagged item and the reason Amazon gave for holding it back.

## Fixing a flagged item

Editing a flagged item in place does not retrigger Amazon's review. To fix reach on a flagged item, reshoot or re-edit it to meet Amazon's quality bar and upload it as new content. Amazon rechecks new uploads within 48 hours.
