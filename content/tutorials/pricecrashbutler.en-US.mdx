## What Pricecrash Butler does

Sellers sometimes forget a decimal point. Catalog teams sometimes miss a flag. When they do, a $400 vacuum shows up at $4, a $2,000 lens at $20, a $89 air fryer at $0.89 - and a smart creator can post the deal before Amazon's automated guards or a human cleanup pull it down.

Pricecrash Butler is the radar. It runs on a schedule, hits the Amazon Creators API for the categories you care about, post-filters every result by discount percentage, and the moment a ≥99%-off product surfaces, the deal gets pushed into **Deals Influencer Butler** as either:

- a **draft** in the Search tab (you approve before it posts), or
- an **auto-scheduled post** that publishes right away (or waits for your next scheduled post time, your choice).

The threshold is yours - 99% is the default because that's where real pricing errors live, but you can dial it down to 75–85% to widen the net for clearance, overstock, and outlet pricing.

## First run

1. Open **Pricecrash Butler** in the left nav (it lives under Orders Butler in the Amazon group).
2. Pick at least one **category** to scan. "All" sweeps every Amazon department; otherwise multi-select the ones that match your niche.
3. (Optional) Under **Marketplaces to scan**, tick the Amazon regions you want to sweep (UK, DE, FR and more). Leave them all unchecked to scan only your home marketplace. A box unlocks once you connect that region's Creators API credentials in Settings: US and CA use your default credentials; UK, DE, FR, IT and ES use your EU credentials; and JP, AU and IN use your FE credentials.
4. Decide what should happen when a deal is found:
   - **Draft for review**: safer; sits in Deals Influencer Butler → Search until you approve it.
   - **Auto-schedule**: fastest; drops straight into Deals Influencer Butler → Schedule. Pick the timing below it: **Post right away** publishes each catch the moment it's found (even with the auto-fire schedule off), or **On the next scheduled deal post time** queues it for your Deals Influencer Butler posting schedule.
   - **No hand-off**: just log it in the panel; you act manually.
5. Choose which deals workspaces receive the catches under **Send finds to**. Tick your base Deals Influencer Butler and any extra deals workspaces you have added. Each ticked workspace delivers the catch to its own channels. The post copy always comes from your Pricecrash Butler **Build Your Post** composer (see below), so one pricing error can go out across several audiences at once in one consistent voice. This row is hidden when you pick No hand-off.
6. Click **Run**. With the **Bait keywords** field empty, the runner browses each selected category by Amazon's root browse-node sorted price-ascending - no title-text filter, so it catches mispriced items even when the seller never typed "clearance" in the listing. Hits land in the table at the bottom of the panel.

## Recurring scans

Below the run-settings card is the **Auto-scan schedule** row. Pick a preset - `1h`, `3h`, `6h`, `12h`, or `24h` - or set your own with the **Or every** field (any number of minutes, hours, or days), then flip the **Run automatically** switch. The runner will fire on that cadence even when the panel is closed, and your choice (preset or custom) is remembered across restarts.

The 6h default is a deliberate compromise: frequent enough to catch a glitch before the seller fixes it, infrequent enough to stay polite to the Amazon Creators API rate limit (which is shared across every Butler that talks to PA-API - Daily Commission, CC Check, Deals Influencer Butler, Storefront, Levanta, and now Pricecrash).

## How "99%" actually works

The Amazon Creators API `SearchItems` endpoint does not expose a "minimum discount" server-side filter. Pricecrash Butler works around that by:

1. Iterating each selected category with `sortBy: Price:LowToHigh` - the cheapest 10 items per page bubble up first, exactly where pricing errors hide.
2. Parsing the offer block on every result for `savingsPercent` (already computed by Amazon).
3. Discarding everything below your threshold and deduplicating by ASIN within the run.

True 99%-off matches are rare on purpose - Amazon's automated guards remove flagrant errors from search results within minutes. Realistic catch rate at the 99% default is 0–2 deals/month over a typical scan footprint. Dial the threshold down to 75–85% if you'd rather treat the workspace as a general "steep discount" radar.

## Bait keywords

The bait-keyword textarea controls what the runner searches for in each category. **Leave it empty** for the widest net: the runner scans each category by its root browse-node sorted price-ascending, with no title filter at all - the cheapest items in the category bubble up first, and any one of them with a savings percentage at or above your threshold becomes a hit, regardless of whether the listing calls itself "clearance" or anything else.

Paste your own list (comma- or newline-separated) when you want to **narrow** the sweep - useful for brand stems ("anker", "dyson"), product types ("router", "monitor"), or generic discount cues ("clearance", "open box", "closeout", "overstock", "warehouse deal") that bias the first page toward known-mispriced inventory. The runner applies your keywords to every selected category.

If Amazon's API rejects a keyword-less browse for one of your categories (rare - it's usually a stale browse-node mapping), Pricecrash stops the run and tells you which category needs a manual keyword. Add at least one bait keyword for that category or unselect it, then run again.

## Build Your Post

Pricecrash Butler has its own **Build Your Post** composer, right in the panel, so every catch posts in one consistent voice no matter which deals workspaces receive it. It looks and works exactly like the one in Deals Influencer Butler:

- **Drag a pill, or click it.** Color-coded pills across the top drop values into your caption and first comment: deal fields (Title, Sale price, Was price, % off, Link, Reviews, Stars, Ends in), rotating lines (Hook, Call to action, Hashtags), and the red **Price-crash hype** pill. Drag a pill into the editor, or click it to drop it at your cursor. Dropped pills become colored chips you can drag around or click to remove.
- **The Image pill.** Drop the purple **Image** pill, then click it to choose what publishes with each post: the caught deal's **Amazon** photo (the default), an image you **Upload**, or **None** for a caption-only post.
- **Price-crash hype.** The red hype pill drops a high-urgency line with the real (cents-masked) sale price, like "HURRY! THE SELLER DIDN'T KNOW THEY LISTED THIS FOR $4.xx!!!". When it is in your caption it carries the post. Click the pill (note its small down arrow) to open the line editor: view every built-in hype line, turn lines off, delete them, add your own (write `{price}` where the sale price should go), and choose whether posts rotate through the enabled lines or always use a single line you pick.
- **Variants.** Add alternate captions or comments under each editor and choose whether posts rotate through them in order or at random, so your feed does not repeat the same wording.
- **Copy from another workspace.** Use **Copy caption & comment from** at the top to pull the caption, first comment, and their variants from your base Deals Influencer Butler or any deals workspace clone, then tweak from there.
- **Live preview.** The preview at the bottom shows a sample catch rendered with your current caption and comment.

## Hand-off to Deals Influencer Butler

Each found deal is written into Deals Influencer Butler' `posts.json` with `source: "pricecrashbutler"` and a `dealRef` containing the ASIN, list price, sale price, and percent-off. The post copy is built from your Pricecrash Butler **Build Your Post** composer (above), so the pills you set there drive exactly what Pricecrash posts say. From there:

- **Draft mode**: the post lands with `status: "draft"`. Open Deals Influencer Butler → Search, review, set destinations, hit Publish.
- **Auto-schedule mode**: the post lands with `status: "scheduled"`. With **Post right away** (the default), Pricecrash publishes it immediately to every enabled destination, so a catch goes out within seconds even when the auto-fire schedule is off. With **On the next scheduled deal post time**, the post waits for your Deals Influencer Butler posting schedule's next run.

The mode and the timing are **per-scan** - flip them between scheduled runs without re-opening Deals Influencer Butler.

## Topbar tally

While Pricecrash is running, the topbar **Hours Saved** and **Money Saved** counters credit the run time at your configured hourly rate - same accounting Orders Butler and Daily Commission Butler use.

## Gotchas

- **"Creators API not configured" in the log**: open Settings → API Integrations → Creators API and fill in your partner tag, access key, and secret. Pricecrash uses the same credentials as Deals Influencer Butler.
- **`429 RequestThrottled`**: too many Butlers hit PA-API in the same minute. Pricecrash caps scheduled ticks at one page per category (or one page per keyword when keywords are set) for this reason; the manual "Run now" allows multi-page sweeps and is more likely to throttle.
- **Only seeing amazon.com (US) deals when you sell on another marketplace?**: open Settings → API Integrations → Creators API and set the **Marketplace** dropdown to your store (for example amazon.co.uk), then save. Your main credentials already cover your own marketplace, so you do not need a separate "EU" or "FE" credential set just to scan it. Pricecrash then sweeps your marketplace by default; use the **Marketplaces to scan** tick-boxes only when you want to sweep additional regions.
- **Empty runs at 99%**: expected. The whole pitch is that 99%-off errors are rare. Lower the threshold.
- **Posts not appearing in Deals Influencer Butler**: confirm Deals Influencer Butler is enabled in Settings; the hand-off writes to that workspace's `posts.json`, so if Deals Influencer Butler isn't visible the deal still lands on disk but you can't see it.
