## What Pricecrash Butler does

Sellers sometimes forget a decimal point. Catalog teams sometimes miss a flag. When they do, a $400 vacuum shows up at $4, a $2,000 lens at $20, a $89 air fryer at $0.89 - and a smart creator can post the deal before Amazon's automated guards or a human cleanup pull it down.

Pricecrash Butler is the radar. It runs on a schedule, hits the Amazon Creators API for the categories you care about, post-filters every result by discount percentage, and the moment a ≥99%-off product surfaces, the deal gets pushed into **Deals Butler** as either:

- a **draft** in the Search tab (you approve before it posts), or
- an **auto-scheduled post** that publishes right away (or waits for your next scheduled post time, your choice).

The threshold is yours - 99% is the default because that's where real pricing errors live, but you can dial it down to 75–85% to widen the net for clearance, overstock, and outlet pricing.

## First run

1. Open **Pricecrash Butler** in the left nav (it lives under Orders Butler in the Amazon group).
2. Pick at least one **category** to scan. "All" sweeps every Amazon department; otherwise multi-select the ones that match your niche.
3. (Optional) Under **Marketplaces to scan**, tick the Amazon regions you want to sweep (UK, DE, FR and more). Leave them all unchecked to scan only your home marketplace. A box unlocks once you connect that region's Creators API credentials in Settings: US and CA use your default credentials; UK, DE, FR, IT and ES use your EU credentials; and JP, AU and IN use your FE credentials.
4. Decide what should happen when a deal is found:
   - **Draft for review**: safer; sits in Deals Butler → Search until you approve it.
   - **Auto-schedule**: fastest; drops straight into Deals Butler → Schedule. Pick the timing below it: **Post right away** publishes each catch the moment it's found (even with the auto-fire schedule off), or **On the next scheduled deal post time** queues it for your Deals Butler posting schedule.
   - **No hand-off**: just log it in the panel; you act manually.
5. Choose which deals workspaces receive the catches under **Send finds to**. Tick your base Deals Butler and any extra deals workspaces you have added. Each ticked workspace delivers the catch to its own channels. The post copy always comes from your Pricecrash Butler **Build Your Post** composer (see below), so one pricing error can go out across several audiences at once in one consistent voice. This row is hidden when you pick No hand-off.
6. Click **Run**. With the **Bait keywords** field empty, the runner browses each selected category by Amazon's root browse-node sorted price-ascending - no title-text filter, so it catches mispriced items even when the seller never typed "clearance" in the listing. Hits land in the table at the bottom of the panel.

## Recurring scans

Below the run-settings card is the **Auto-scan schedule** row. Pick a preset - `1h`, `3h`, `6h`, `12h`, or `24h` - or set your own with the **Or every** field (any number of minutes, hours, or days), then flip the **Run automatically** switch. The runner will fire on that cadence even when the panel is closed, and your choice (preset or custom) is remembered across restarts.

The 6h default is a deliberate compromise: frequent enough to catch a glitch before the seller fixes it, infrequent enough to stay polite to the Amazon Creators API rate limit (which is shared across every Butler that talks to PA-API - Daily Commission, CC Check, Deals Butler, Storefront, Levanta, and now Pricecrash).

## How "99%" actually works

The Amazon Creators API `SearchItems` endpoint does not expose a "minimum discount" server-side filter. Pricecrash Butler works around that by:

1. Iterating each selected category with `sortBy: Price:LowToHigh` - the cheapest 10 items per page bubble up first, exactly where pricing errors hide.
2. Parsing the offer block on every result for `savingsPercent` (already computed by Amazon).
3. Discarding everything below your threshold and deduplicating by ASIN within the run.

True 99%-off matches are rare on purpose - Amazon's automated guards remove flagrant errors from search results within minutes. Realistic catch rate at the 99% default is 0–2 deals/month over a typical scan footprint. Dial the threshold down to 75–85% if you'd rather treat the workspace as a general "steep discount" radar.

## Bait keywords

The bait-keyword textarea controls what the runner searches for in each category. **Leave it empty** for the widest net: the runner scans each category by its root browse-node sorted price-ascending, with no title filter at all - the cheapest items in the category bubble up first, and any one of them with a savings percentage at or above your threshold becomes a hit, regardless of whether the listing calls itself "clearance" or anything else.

Paste your own list (comma- or newline-separated) when you want to **narrow** the sweep - useful for brand stems ("anker", "dyson"), product types ("router", "monitor"), or generic discount cues ("clearance", "open box", "closeout", "overstock", "warehouse deal") that bias the first page toward known-mispriced inventory. The runner applies your keywords to every selected category.

If Amazon's API rejects a keyword-less browse for one of your categories (rare - it's usually a stale browse-node mapping), Pricecrash stops the run and tells you which category needs a manual keyword. Add at least one bait keyword for that category or unselect it, then run again.

## Build Your Post

Pricecrash Butler has its own **Build Your Post** composer, right in the panel, so every catch posts in one consistent voice no matter which deals workspaces receive it. It is laid out exactly like the one in Deals Butler, with three pages: **Caption**, **First comment** and **Options**.

- **Pick a style.** The first step on the Caption page offers ready-made layouts (Deal post basics, Telegram and Deal drops). Click one to fill your caption and first comment, then edit anything. You can also drag a style onto any editor, and save your own current post as a formula with **Save current as formula**.
- **Insert chips.** The **Insert** panel on the right holds the chips: deal fields (Image, Title, ASIN, Link, Sale price, Was price, % off, Store, Reviews, Stars, Ends in), rotating lines (Hook, Call to action, Hashtags, Urgency) and the red **Price-crash hype** chip. It starts with the essentials; click **More chips** to see everything and search by name. Click a chip to add it where your cursor is, or drag it in. Dropped chips can be dragged around or clicked to remove. There is no promo code chip here: a pricing error never comes with a code or a coupon.
- **The Image chip.** Drop the purple **Image** chip, then click it to choose what publishes with each post: the caught deal's **Amazon** photo (the default), an image you **Upload**, or **None** for a caption-only post.
- **A photo under your first comment.** On the First comment page, use the **Photo** control to post an image below the pinned comment: **Deal photo** uses the caught product's Amazon image, or **Upload** to add your own. Leave it on **None** to keep the comment text-only.
- **Price-crash hype.** The red hype chip drops a high-urgency line built around the deal's discount percentage, like "HURRY! THE SELLER DIDN'T KNOW THEY MARKED THIS DOWN 91%!!!". When it is in your caption it carries the post. Click the chip (note its small down arrow) to open the line editor: view every built-in hype line, turn lines off, delete them, add your own, and choose whether posts rotate through the enabled lines or always use a single line you pick. Every hype line is percentage-based (write `{percent}` where the percent off should go); no price ever shows, keeping every catch compliant with Amazon's and Influencer policy against posting exact prices.
- **Advanced caption options.** Open this section under the caption for **More caption variants** (alternate captions that rotate in order or at random so your feed does not repeat itself), **Caption by store** (turn on Amazon or Walmart to word that store's finds differently; stores you leave off use your main caption), and **My Caption Voice** (paste 2 to 5 posts you wrote, and the AI adds caption variants in your voice; needs your OpenAI key). The badge on the section shows how many of these are on.
- **Options.** The Options page holds **Where your affiliate link goes** (first comment, caption, or alternating, with the order) and **Shorten titles with AI**.
- **Live preview.** The preview at the top of the right panel shows a sample catch rendered with your current caption and comment, with a **Deeplink created at posting** badge whenever a link chip is present. When you add variants, use the arrows on the preview to step through each version.
- **Copy setup and Paste setup.** The two buttons in the header move your whole setup between Build Your Post panels: click **Copy setup** here, open any Deals workspace (or the other way round) and click **Paste setup**. It carries the caption, first comment, variants, store boxes, link placement and the rest, asks before replacing, and skips anything the other panel does not have. The **More** menu still offers **Copy from another workspace** to pull just the caption and first comment from a Deals workspace, and **Clear all text boxes**.

## Posting limits and best first

A big scan can find dozens of deals at once. The **Posting limits and best first** card keeps that under control:

- **Posts per scan, per hour and per day.** Set any of the three, or leave them blank for no limit. The per-hour and per-day numbers look back over the last 60 minutes and the last 24 hours across all your scans, so a scan that runs every few hours will not go past them. A deal sent to several deals workspaces counts as one post.
- **Pick the best by.** When a limit leaves room for only some of the catches, this decides which go first: **Best deal** (weighs the percent off and the dollars saved together, so a big saving on a real product beats a 99% cut on a 50 cent item), **Cheapest price**, **Biggest % off**, or **First found** (hand them off in the order the scan found them, which is how Pricecrash worked before).
- **What happens to the rest.** Deals that miss the cut stay in the results table with a **Held by limit** pill, and the **Send to schedule** button stays on the row so you can push any of them yourself. They are not marked as posted, so the next scan ranks them again against new finds.

With a ranking selected (the default is **Best deal**) or any limit set, Pricecrash waits until the scan finishes before handing deals off, so it can pick the best ones from the whole pool. With **First found** and no limits, deals are handed off one by one as they are found.

The line under the limits shows the most extra posts a day your settings can add. Only **Auto-schedule** with **Post right away** posts outside your Deals Butler schedule, so that is the only combination that adds to it. Deals Butler counts it too: in the Scheduler tab, the posts-per-day tally lists Pricecrash Butler (as "up to" the number your limits allow) and counts it toward the Facebook posting warning, and the **How often** card shows the same extra.

## Hand-off to Deals Butler

Each found deal is written into Deals Butler' `posts.json` with `source: "pricecrashbutler"` and a `dealRef` containing the ASIN, list price, sale price, and percent-off. The post copy is built from your Pricecrash Butler **Build Your Post** composer (above), so the pills you set there drive exactly what Pricecrash posts say, even when that workspace has a caption of its own: a Pricecrash find always publishes in the Pricecrash voice, while the destination workspace still decides where it posts and how its links are built. From there:

- **Draft mode**: the post lands with `status: "draft"`. Open Deals Butler → Search, review, set destinations, hit Publish.
- **Auto-schedule mode**: the post lands with `status: "scheduled"`. With **Post right away** (the default), Pricecrash publishes it immediately to every enabled destination, so a catch goes out within seconds even when the auto-fire schedule is off. With **On the next scheduled deal post time**, the post waits for your Deals Butler posting schedule's next run.

The mode and the timing are **per-scan** - flip them between scheduled runs without re-opening Deals Butler.

## Topbar tally

While Pricecrash is running, the topbar **Hours Saved** and **Money Saved** counters credit the run time at your configured hourly rate - same accounting Orders Butler and Daily Commission Butler use.

## Gotchas

- **"Creators API not configured" in the log**: open Settings → API Integrations → Creators API and fill in your partner tag, access key, and secret. Pricecrash uses the same credentials as Deals Butler.
- **`429 RequestThrottled`**: too many Butlers hit PA-API in the same minute. Pricecrash caps scheduled ticks at one page per category (or one page per keyword when keywords are set) for this reason; the manual "Run now" allows multi-page sweeps and is more likely to throttle.
- **Only seeing amazon.com (US) deals when you sell on another marketplace?**: open Settings → API Integrations → Creators API and set the **Marketplace** dropdown to your store (for example amazon.co.uk), then save. Your main credentials already cover your own marketplace, so you do not need a separate "EU" or "FE" credential set just to scan it. Pricecrash then sweeps your marketplace by default; use the **Marketplaces to scan** tick-boxes only when you want to sweep additional regions.
- **Empty runs at 99%**: expected. The whole pitch is that 99%-off errors are rare. Lower the threshold.
- **Posts not appearing in Deals Butler**: confirm Deals Butler is enabled in Settings; the hand-off writes to that workspace's `posts.json`, so if Deals Butler isn't visible the deal still lands on disk but you can't see it.
