## What Group Invite Butler does

Group Invite Butler invites people into a Facebook group you manage, one email at a time, on a natural schedule. You give it a list of email addresses and the group you want them in; it opens the group's email-invite form, adds each address, and paces itself so the activity looks human and stays under Facebook's limits.

It's built for creators growing a community around their storefront or niche. The emails usually come from the Instagram or Amazon Goldmine (other creators you found), but you can also load your own list from a CSV.

## Before you start

Two things need to be in place:

- **You're signed in to Facebook in the app.** Group Invite Butler uses the same Facebook session as Amazon Deals Butler. If you haven't signed in yet, open Amazon Deals Butler first and sign in to Facebook there once.
- **You manage the group.** You can only send email invites for a group where you have invite permissions. Paste that group's URL into the settings.

## Filling the invite queue

There are two ways to get emails into the queue.

### From a Goldmine

1. Open the **Instagram Goldmine** or **Amazon Goldmine** and run a harvest.
2. Click **Send to Group Invite Butler**. The harvested emails jump straight into the queue, deduped against anyone already there.

### From a CSV

1. In the Invite Queue, click **Load CSV**.
2. Pick a `.csv` file. The format is simple: **one email per row in the first column (column A)**. A header row and any extra columns are ignored, so a spreadsheet you exported from somewhere else usually works as-is.
3. Butler validates each row, drops anything that isn't an email, and adds the rest. The info tooltip next to the button spells out the format if you need a reminder.

Either way, emails that were **already invited** (or already in the queue) are skipped automatically, so you never invite the same person twice.

## Setting your pacing and limits

Under **Run settings**:

- **Facebook group URL**: the group you're inviting people into. Required; Butler won't start without it.
- **Seconds between invites** plus **Random extra seconds**: the gap between each invite, plus a random amount on top so the timing looks natural. Slower is safer.
- **Invites per day**: a daily cap that carries across runs. `0` means no daily cap.
- **Invites per run**: how many to send in a single run. `0` means send the whole queue.

Everything auto-saves as you type. There's no Save button.

## Running it

1. Set your group URL and pacing.
2. Click **Start Group Invite Butler**.
3. Leave **Watch it run** on to bring the browser to the front and watch each invite go out, or turn it off to let it work in the background.

The queue shows live **Pending**, **Sent**, and **Failed** counts as it works. You can stop at any time; the next run picks up where it left off.

## Running it on a schedule

If you'd rather not click Start every time, set a schedule and Butler runs itself on a repeating cadence, using the same run settings above.

Under **Schedule**:

1. Pick a **Start time** for the first automatic run.
2. Choose how often to **Repeat every**: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1, 2, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours, or pick **Custom** to type your own number of hours.
3. Click **Enable Schedule**. The card shows the next scheduled run, and the pill flips to "Schedule on".

Each scheduled run sends up to your **Invites per run** limit and respects the **Invites per day** cap, so the schedule can't blow past your limits. Click **Stop Schedule** to turn it off. The app has to stay open and signed in to Facebook for scheduled runs to fire.

## After a run

- **Retry failed**: moves every failed address back into the pending queue so the next run tries them again. Useful after a hiccup where nothing actually sent.
- **Redo sent**: moves already-sent addresses back to pending to re-invite them. Useful if a run marked people sent that Facebook didn't actually accept.
- **Clear queue**: empties the pending list. Your durable invited record stays intact, so cleared-then-reloaded emails still show as already invited.

## Common gotchas

- **It won't start**: check that the Facebook group URL is filled in and that you're signed in to Facebook (via Amazon Deals Butler).
- **My CSV loaded zero emails**: make sure the email addresses are in the **first column**. Extra columns and header rows are ignored on purpose, but if column A holds something other than emails, nothing matches.
- **Someone shows as Sent that I didn't invite this run**: that's the durable invited record doing its job. Once anyone has been invited, they stay marked so they're never invited twice.
- **Go slow at first**: high volume with short gaps is what trips Facebook's limits. Start with a modest daily cap and a comfortable delay, then loosen up once you see it running clean.
