> **Go slowly on purpose.** Facebook's rules do not allow automated activity, and group admins remove promotion. Community Share Butler works with small daily limits, a warm-up after you join, and one list per group. Keep **Watch it run** on in the top bar so you can see what it does.

## What Community Share Butler does

You have lists (Benable lists, Walmart collections) and no audience. Facebook groups are where the audience already is, but a post with a shopping link is often deleted. This Butler tries to do it the way a helpful member would:

1. **Collects your lists.** Every public Benable list and Walmart collection you make appears under **Your shareable lists** by itself. Tags for topic, audience and place are added so lists can be matched to groups.
2. **Finds groups.** It searches Facebook for groups about your topics and places (for example "pets deals" or "austin moms"), then reads each group's About page and rules.
3. **Reads the rules.** Each group is marked **Links allowed**, **Links in comments**, **Links discouraged** or **No links**, and flagged when admins approve posts or ask membership questions.
4. **Joins the groups you pick.** You tick the groups, then click **Join ticked groups**. Public groups are joined, private groups get a request.
5. **Shares one list per group.** After a warm-up, it matches a list to a group by topic and place and queues a friendly post through Deals Butler, with the link in the first comment unless the group's rules allow it in the post.
6. **Checks the result.** A day later it looks at each post. If a group removed it, the Butler never shares there again.

## Before you start

1. Sign in to the Facebook account you post with from **Deals Butler** (Sign in manually). This Butler uses the same logins.
2. Make at least one list or collection in **Benable Butler** or **Collection Builder**, or click **Add my lists** to bring in the ones you already have.

## Step by step

1. Open **Facebook Butler** > **Community Share Butler**.
2. Under **Find groups**, pick your Facebook account. Leave **Search phrases** on **Auto, from my lists**, or choose **Manual only** and type your own phrases, one per line.
3. Click **Find groups**. It reads each new group slowly, one at a time. The table fills with each group's size, fit score and rules.
4. Tick the groups you want to join. Groups that ask membership questions are marked **Needs your answers**: open them on Facebook, answer yourself, then tick them again. The Butler never answers those questions.
5. Click **Join ticked groups**. Joins are capped per day (5 by default).
6. After the warm-up (3 days by default), click **Plan today** under **Share plan**. It shows which list would go to which group and why.
7. Tick the shares you want and click **Share selected**. A group's first share always needs your tick. The posts are queued through Deals Butler, which applies its posting hours and limits.
8. Turn on **Run every day** to let the Butler check earlier shares and queue new ones each day.

## Limits that protect your account

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Joins per day | 5 |
| Shares per day | 6 |
| Minutes between shares | 45 |
| Days before sharing to a group again | 7 |
| Warm-up days after joining | 3 |

A group's rules always win: a group that bans links gets no share, and one removed post stops all sharing to that group.

## Fully automatic mode

**Fully automatic mode** is off by default. When it is on, the Butler also ticks good groups, joins them, searches weekly, and sends first shares by itself, within the same limits. It never answers membership questions. This raises the risk of a Facebook limit or a group ban, so keep the limits low and **Watch it run** on.

## AI and cost

The Butler can use OpenAI to tag your lists, write search phrases, score how well a group fits, and word each post. Set **AI budget per month** to cap it (the default is $3). At 0, AI is off and everything still works with simple rules. Every post mentions your commission honestly when the list earns through affiliate links.

## Good to know

- Group names and rules come from Facebook and are only read, never edited.
- If Facebook shows a limit or checkpoint, the Butler stops and pauses posting everywhere, the same as Deals Butler does.
- A group already in your Deals Butler list is tagged **Already in Deals Butler** so you can tell it is not new.
- If a run says sign-in is required, turn on **Watch it run**, sign in from Deals Butler, then run again.
