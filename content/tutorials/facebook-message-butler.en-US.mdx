## What Facebook Message Butler does

Facebook Message Butler reaches people on Facebook for you in two ways. First, it sends a private message to the members of a group you are in, or the followers of a Page, one person at a time, with a natural delay and a different wording for each person so nothing looks copy-pasted. Second, it runs comment-to-DM automations: when someone comments a keyword on one of your posts, Butler automatically sends them a Messenger message (and, if you want, a public reply on the comment too). It uses the same Facebook session as Deals Butler, so you sign in once.

It is built for creators who want to start conversations at scale without sitting at the keyboard, while staying inside limits that keep the account safe.

## Before you start

- **Sign in to Facebook in the app.** Butler messages from your real Facebook session. In the **Facebook account** section, pick the account to send from, click **Sign in**, finish signing in the window that opens, close it, then click **Verify sign-in**. A green **Signed in** badge means you are ready. To add more accounts, add them in Deals Butler under Social Destinations and they appear in this list.
- **Go slow at first.** Facebook watches for burst messaging. The built-in presets start conservative on purpose. Newer accounts should pick the most cautious one.

## Part 1: Message a group or Page

### Step 1: Choose who to message

In the **Who to message** section, pick a source:

- **Group members**: paste the URL of a group you belong to. Its members become your recipients. Turn on **Also message group admins** only if you mean to.
- **Page followers**: paste a Page URL. Real profiles are messaged; follower Pages are skipped.
- **Paste or CSV**: load a CSV of profile URLs or names, or hand recipients over from another workspace.

Click **Find recipients** (or **Load CSV**) to fill the queue. Anyone you have already messaged is skipped automatically.

### Step 2: Write your message

In **Your message**, write what you want to say. Put choices in braces like `{Hi|Hey|Hello}` and Butler picks one at random for each person, so every message reads a little differently. Use `{firstName}` to drop in their name. The toolbar can insert a greeting, a sign-off, or their name for you, and **Preview variations** shows a few sample messages so you can see the spread before you run.

### Step 3: Pick how to send and how fast

Under **How to send**, keep **Individual messages** (the safest option). Group chat is available but Facebook limits it heavily, so it is easier to get blocked.

Under **Pacing and limits**, pick a preset: **Cautious** for a new account, **Conservative** (recommended) for most, or **Moderate** if the account is well established. Choose **Custom** to set your own delay range, messages per hour, and messages per day. **Ease in gently** adds extra spacing for the first few messages, and **Pause overnight** stops sending late at night.

### Step 4 (optional): Put it on a schedule

Turn on **Run automatically on a schedule** to have Butler run itself every so many hours or days, starting at a time you pick. Click **Apply Schedule**.

### Step 5: Start

Click **Start Facebook Message Butler**. Leave **Watch it run** on to bring the browser to the front and watch the messages go out; turn it off to run quietly in the background. The queue shows Pending, Sent, and Failed counts as it works.

## Part 2: Auto-DM people who comment (comment to DM)

This is the hands-off part. Open the **Automations: comment to DM** section and turn on **Turn on automations**. Set **Max messages per hour** to a comfortable ceiling. Then add one or more rules with **Add rule**. Each rule is three simple steps.

### Step 1 of a rule: which post and words

- **Pick a post.** Enter your Facebook Page or profile URL and click **Load recent posts**. Your recent posts appear as cards with a thumbnail and caption. Click the one you want to watch. If nothing loads, paste a post link directly in the box below.
- **Choose how a comment triggers the rule.** It can *contain* any of your words, *start with* them, *exactly match* one, or match *any comment* at all. Then type your keywords, separated by commas.

### Step 2 of a rule: the auto message

Write the Messenger message Butler sends when the rule matches. Spintax like `{Hey|Hi}` and `{firstName}` work here too. Add a link if you want it included. Use **Preview** to see a sample with the spintax resolved, and **Send test to me** to send yourself the real message first so you can check it before turning the rule loose.

### Step 3 of a rule: public reply (optional)

Turn this on to also reply on the comment publicly, for example "Sent! Check your DMs." Leave it off to only send the private message.

Each rule shows a small activity line (how many it sent today, and when it last fired) so you can see it working. **Run a pass now** checks your watched posts immediately instead of waiting for the background check.

## Part 3: Collect email replies

If your message asks people something back, for example "reply with your email to join our daily deals newsletter," Butler can gather those answers for you. Open the **Check for replies and collect emails** section.

### How it works

Click **Check for replies**. Butler opens the conversations of the people you already messaged, reads what they wrote back, and saves any email address it finds. Collected emails appear in **Facebook Butler**, under **Facebook Group Email Collection**, where you can push them to Pitch Butler with one click. Butler remembers who it has already checked, so running it again only looks at new or still-unanswered conversations.

### Tune it

- **Conversations to check per run**: how many chats Butler opens in one pass. Keep it modest so a single run stays quick.
- **Recheck a quiet chat after (hours)**: how long Butler waits before reopening a conversation that has not produced an email yet. Once it captures someone's email, it never reopens that chat.

### Check automatically (optional)

Turn on **Check for replies automatically on a schedule** to have Butler sweep for new replies every so many hours or days, then click **Apply Schedule**. This runs on its own, separate from your sending schedule, so you can message on one cadence and collect replies on another.

## Tips for staying safe

- Start with the **Conservative** or **Cautious** preset and low daily caps, then raise them slowly over days, not hours.
- Keep messages genuinely varied with spintax. Identical messages are the fastest way to get flagged.
- If Facebook shows a block or a checkpoint, stop and let the account rest before running again.

## Troubleshooting

- **The Signed in badge is not green.** Click **Sign in**, finish in the window, close it, then click **Verify sign-in** again. If it still fails, sign in to Facebook once in Deals Butler.
- **No recipients found.** Make sure the group or Page URL is correct and that you are a member of the group. Some Pages hide their followers.
- **The post picker shows no posts.** Paste the post link directly in the box below the picker. The picker needs your correct Page or profile URL and an active Facebook sign-in.
- **A test DM did not arrive.** Confirm you are signed in, then try again. Test messages go to your own inbox as a note to yourself.
- **Check for replies found nothing, or Facebook asks to restore your chats.** Facebook Messenger sometimes shows an "Enter your PIN to restore your chats" screen that hides your conversation history. Open Messenger, clear that prompt once (enter your PIN, or choose not to restore), then run **Check for replies** again. Also remember that only emails people actually typed in a reply are collected.
