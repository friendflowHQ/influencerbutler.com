## What Link Butler does

Link Butler (the Influencer Deeplink Butler) turns any product link into a short, branded "Calling Card." When a shopper taps it, the link opens the Amazon shopping app instead of a browser tab, which converts far better, and every tap is tracked. Because the Calling Card routes through Influencer Butler, you can re-point it later and the printed or posted link never goes stale.

This is the same job Geniuslink and urlgenius do. Link Butler goes further: it ties each link back to the product, to where you posted it, and to its live health, in one ledger.

## Minting your first Calling Card

1. Open **Link Butler**, then **Influencer Deeplink Butler**, under the Link Butler group.
2. On the **Links** tab, paste a product link into **Product link**.
3. Optionally add a **Title** (only you see it) and a **Campaign** so its clicks group together in Performance.
4. Click **Add Butler Link**. Your Calling Card appears in the ledger below.

To mint many at once, open **Add several at once** and paste one product link per line.

## The ledger: more than a list of links

Every Butler Link is a row in **Your Butler Links**. Each row shows:

- **Product**: the product photo, title, and its brand, ASIN, and retailer, resolved automatically so you recognize the link at a glance.
- **Calling Card**: the short branded link, with one-click copy.
- **Posted to**: a badge for each place the link was shared (Facebook, Threads, Telegram, Reddit, and more). Click a badge to open the live social post. Links you post through Deals Butler are added here automatically, so you always know which link went where.
- **Trend**: a small sparkline of clicks per day over the last two weeks, so you can spot a link heating up without opening the drill-down.
- **Clicks**: total taps.
- **Health**: a pill that reads Live, Healed, or Broken. The Butler re-routes behind your link to keep it working; this tells you at a glance if anything needs attention.
- **Notes & tags**: your own note and colored tags for the link. Both save as you type, no Save button.

The **Overview** cards at the top total your links, clicks, posted links, and healthy links.

## Managing your links

- **Click any product** in the ledger to open that link's own analytics.
- **Search and filter** by health, posted / not posted, retailer, campaign, or tag, and **Group by campaign** to see subtotals per campaign.
- **Broken links** surface in a banner at the top; one click sends a dead link to a new destination.
- **Edit a link's destination** any time. The short link and its QR code stay the same, so anything you already posted keeps working.
- **Copy** a link as a plain URL, Markdown, HTML, or a ready-to-post caption with an #ad disclosure.
- **Select several** links to tag, export, download their QR codes, or delete them in one go.

## QR Studio

The **QR Studio** tab turns any Calling Card into a branded QR code with your colors and an optional center logo. Print it once: because it points at your Calling Card, you can re-route behind it and it never goes stale. Scans show up as a "qr" campaign in Performance.

## Performance

The **Performance** tab is your click dashboard: totals, app-open rate, self-heal rate, top links, and breakdowns by platform, referrer, campaign, country, region, city, device, and more, plus a map and a best-time-to-post heatmap. Amazon does not report earnings per link, so these are click metrics.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab holds account-wide defaults for new links: your default associate tag, the confirmation page ("The Doorman"), and optional retargeting pixels ("The Doorbell") that fire when a shopper opens a Calling Card.

## Use your own domain (paid plans)

On a paid plan you can put your own brand on your links, so they read as your domain instead of `influencerbutler.com`. In **Settings**, open **Your own domain** and enter a domain you own, like `go.yourbrand.com`. Add the two DNS records we show you at your domain provider:

- a **CNAME** record that points your domain at our link service, and
- a **TXT** record that proves you own the domain so we can issue its security certificate.

We keep checking until both records are live. This can take a few minutes to a couple of hours, depending on your DNS provider. Once your domain is active, every new Butler Link uses it in place of `influencerbutler.com`. Links you already posted keep working, and you can remove or change your domain any time.
