## What Collage Gallery does

Collage Gallery is the history of every collage image Collage Butler has generated for you. Each collage lands here automatically the moment it is created, shown as a thumbnail with a few details: how it was built (Template, AI, or Hybrid), the layout, and its post status (whether it was queued to Social Posting Butler). It is a read-only view, so nothing here changes or deletes your collages. Think of it as your library: a place to look back at what you have made, confirm what went out, and spot a look worth building again.

## Before you start

- **Collage Butler ships in beta.** You unlock it in the left menu under **Settings**: turn on **Collage Butler (beta)**. Until then the whole group, including the gallery, stays hidden.
- Generate at least one collage first. The gallery reads from what Collage Butler has already produced, so a brand-new install starts empty.

## How to use it

1. In the left menu, open the **Collage Butler** group, then click **Collage Gallery**.
2. Browse the grid. Every collage you have generated appears newest first, each as a thumbnail image.
3. Read the detail line under each thumbnail. It shows how the collage was built (**Template**, **AI**, or **Hybrid**), the layout, and its status so you can tell at a glance which ones were queued to Social Posting Butler.
4. Use the gallery to decide what to reuse. When you find a collage you want again, note its keywords, layout, and headline, then go back to the main **Collage Butler** panel and rebuild it, or repost the finished image through **Social Posting Butler**.
5. To lock in a look you like across future collages, open **Collage Templates** and save its layout, font, colors, and headline and call-to-action presets.

## Notes

- **This view is read-only.** The gallery is for browsing and reference. You create, edit, and post collages from the main **Collage Butler** panel, not from here.
- **Collage Butler is in beta** and still being polished, so expect a few rough edges while the generator wiring lands.
- If the grid says **No collages yet**, that is expected until you generate your first one in Collage Butler.
- The gallery keeps your most recent collages and caps the list so the history cannot grow without limit, so very old entries may roll off over time.
