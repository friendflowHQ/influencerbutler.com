## What TikTok Comment Butler does

TikTok Comment Butler visits the TikTok accounts you choose and posts a comment on their recent videos, rotating through a pool of comments you write. It keeps a history of what it posted per creator, so on later runs it can skip videos it already commented on. Use it to stay visibly active in your niche without opening TikTok and typing the same lines over and over.

## Before you start

- **Sign in to TikTok once.** Keep **👀 Watch it run** on (it is on by default). The first time the browser opens, log in to your TikTok account in that window so the butler can post as you.
- Have a short list of TikTok usernames you want to engage with, and a few comment lines ready.

## How to use it

1. In the left menu, open **TikTok Butler**, then **TikTok Comment Butler**.
2. Under **TikTok accounts**, add each creator you want to comment on. Enter a username (like @creator) or a profile URL. Use **Add another TikTok account** for more.
3. Decide whether to tick **Comment on every recent video**. On: it comments on every recent video, even ones seen before. Off (default): it skips posts it already commented on.
4. Under **Comment pool**, type one comment per line. The butler rotates through them, so keep them varied and natural.
5. Under **Run settings**, set **Max comments per run**, **Delay between comments** (in seconds), and **Recent videos per account**. The speed presets **Fast**, **Normal**, and **Careful** set the delay for you.
6. Click **Start TikTok Comment Butler** to run now. Use **Pause** or **Stop** to interrupt.
7. (Optional) To run on a cadence, set a **Start time** and **Repeat every** value under **Scheduler**, then click **Enable Schedule**. Click **Stop Schedule** to unlock the controls again.

## Notes

- TikTok Butler is in beta, so some behavior may still be rough while we build it out.
- While the scheduler is active, the account list, comment pool, and run settings are locked. Click **Stop Schedule** to edit them.
- The **TikTok Comment Butler totals** panel tracks **Total comments posted** (lifetime) and **Comments this run**. The **History** section lists activity by creator and lets you **Download CSV**.
- Comments that add an un-attributed shopping link are skipped automatically.
- Keep your comment count and speed reasonable. Very fast, high-volume commenting looks automated to TikTok and can get your account limited.
