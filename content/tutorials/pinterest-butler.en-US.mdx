> **Roadmap workspace.** Pinterest Butler is scaffolded in the dashboard but not yet functional. The nav button is visible so you can see what's coming; the workspace renders a "coming soon" panel rather than a working UI. Don't expect production behavior yet.

## What Pinterest Butler will do

When it ships, Pinterest Butler will be Pinterest's equivalent of Deals Influencer Butler - an end-to-end content pipeline for the Pinterest side:

1. **Source products**: read from your Amazon Idea Lists (curated lists you've built on Amazon) and from Storefront Butler's catalog.
2. **Prioritize**: rank products by active Creator Connections campaign status. Products you can actually monetize get priority over generic catalog content.
3. **Generate pins**: produce pin images from niche-aware templates. Templates respect category (food, fashion, home, fitness) and adapt fonts, colors, and layout per category.
4. **Write SEO copy**: pin title + description optimized for Pinterest's search algorithm, with affiliate-tagged URLs.
5. **Schedule**: post on a cadence respecting Pinterest's rate limits, with built-in jitter and per-board distribution.

In other words: the full pipeline from "I have a product catalog" to "pins are landing on Pinterest with affiliate links" without you touching the Pinterest UI.

## Why it's roadmap and not live

The scoring + scheduling pieces in InfluencerButler are general-purpose enough to power Pinterest - they already run for Deals Influencer Butler, Benable Butler, and the Instagram stack. What's missing is:

- **A Pinterest OAuth flow** in API Integrations.
- **A pin-generation module** (the visual template rendering is more involved than for text-based platforms).
- **A poster module** that drives the Pinterest API or Puppeteer-controls the web UI.

These are tractable; they just haven't been built. Pinterest Butler shipping means all three of those exist.

## What you can do today

Nothing in Pinterest Butler itself. While you wait:

- **Use Deals Influencer Butler** for the closest existing equivalent on other platforms. Its post-template + scheduler patterns are what Pinterest Butler will model.
- **Curate your Pinterest source data**: keep Amazon Idea Lists in good shape. The cleaner that source is, the better Pinterest Butler's first runs will look when it ships.
- **Set up your Pinterest account profile**: if you don't have a creator account yet, set one up now. The OAuth flow Pinterest Butler will use requires creator-tier permissions.
