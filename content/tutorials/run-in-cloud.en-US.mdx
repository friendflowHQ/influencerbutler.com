## Why do this

If your computer is slow or old, or you travel and do not want your butlers interrupted every time you close your laptop, you can run Influencer Butler on a **free** cloud computer instead. It lives in Amazon's data center, never turns off, and you connect to it from your own laptop or phone. Set it up once and your butlers run 24/7 while you get on with your day.

It is genuinely free to start: new AWS accounts get free credits (currently up to $200) plus a free usage window, which covers a small always-on computer for months. This tutorial is the short checklist version. For the full, screenshot-by-screenshot walkthrough, read the [complete guide on our blog](https://www.influencerbutler.com/blog/run-influencer-butler-free-cloud-pc).

## Before you start

- [ ] I have about 30 minutes (you only do this once) {#have-time}
- [ ] I have my Influencer Butler login handy {#have-login}
- [ ] I have a card for AWS sign-up verification (you will not be charged inside the free limits) {#have-card}

## Create your free cloud computer

- [ ] Go to aws.amazon.com and click **Create a free account**, then finish the free sign-up {#signup}

![Creating a free AWS account](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/01-signup.png)

- [ ] Sign in and find your **free credits** under the billing or Free Tier area {#free-credits}

![Your free AWS credits](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/02-free-credits.png)

- [ ] In the AWS search bar, type **EC2** and open it (EC2 just means cloud computers) {#open-ec2}

![Opening EC2](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/03-open-ec2.png)

- [ ] Click the orange **Launch instance** button {#launch-instance}

![The Launch instance button](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/04-launch-instance.png)

- [ ] Name it (for example **Butler PC**) and pick a **Windows** option marked Free tier eligible {#pick-windows}

![Naming it and picking Windows](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/05-name-windows.png)

- [ ] Choose **t3.medium** (4 GB of memory). Do not use the tiny 1 GB **t3.micro**: it is painfully slow for a browser app. t3.medium is not always-free, but your free credits cover it for months (after that it is about $44 a month running around the clock) {#choose-size}

![Choosing t3.medium](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/06-instance-size.png)

- [ ] Click **Create new key pair**, download the file, and keep it safe {#key-pair}

![Creating a key pair](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/07-key-pair.png)

- [ ] In network settings, make sure **Allow RDP** (Remote Desktop) is checked; limit it to **My IP** if offered {#allow-rdp}

![Allowing Remote Desktop](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/08-allow-rdp.png)

- [ ] Before launching, scroll to **Configure storage** and set the disk to **60 GB** (the default ~30 GB is too small for Windows plus the app, and you will run out of space installing it) {#storage}
- [ ] Click **Launch instance** and wait for the green **Success** screen {#launch}

![The success screen](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/09-launch-success.png)

## Connect and install

- [ ] Open your instance, click **Connect > RDP client > Get password**, upload your key file, and copy the password and the address (Public DNS / IP) {#get-password}

![Getting your password](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/10-get-password.png)

- [ ] Open **Remote Desktop** on your own device (built into Windows; free Windows App from the App Store on Mac), paste the address, and log in as **Administrator** with the password {#remote-desktop}

![Connecting with Remote Desktop](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/11-remote-desktop.png)

- [ ] You now see your cloud Windows desktop; you are controlling it remotely {#connected}

![Your cloud desktop](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/12-cloud-desktop.png)

- [ ] On the cloud desktop, open the browser, download Influencer Butler, and run the installer (if Windows warns, click **More info > Run anyway**) {#install}

![Downloading Influencer Butler](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/13-download-ib.png)

- [ ] Open Influencer Butler, log in, and start your butlers {#start-butlers}

![Influencer Butler running](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/14-running.png)

## Leave it running and keep it free

- [ ] When done, just **close the Remote Desktop window** (do not shut the PC down). The cloud computer keeps running your butlers 24/7 {#leave-running}
- [ ] In the AWS search bar, open **Budgets** and create a small ($1) budget with your email so you get a heads-up long before any charge {#billing-alarm}

![Setting a billing alarm](/assets/blog/cloud-pc/15-billing-alarm.png)

- [ ] Check your Free Tier balance every few weeks; if the free period ever ends, a small cloud PC is only a few dollars a month, or you can **terminate** it to stop all costs {#stay-free}

## That is it

Your butlers now run on an always-on cloud computer. Close your laptop, travel, lose your wifi: they keep working. For the full walkthrough with every screenshot, see the [complete guide on our blog](https://www.influencerbutler.com/blog/run-influencer-butler-free-cloud-pc).
