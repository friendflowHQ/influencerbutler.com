## What TikTok Creator Stats does

TikTok Creator Stats is a read-only dashboard for the TikTok account you connected. It reads your profile and shows four numbers: your follower count, your total likes, how many videos you have posted, and your average views across your recent videos. Below that is a table of recent videos with views, likes, comments, and the date each was posted.

It only reads your public creator data through TikTok's official Display API. It never posts or changes anything.

## Before you start

- **Connect TikTok.** Open **API Integrations**, find the **TikTok Account** card, and click **Connect TikTok**. Creator Stats reads from that connection.
- If the dashboard shows the connect prompt, link your account first, then come back.

## Loading your stats

1. Open **TikTok Creator Stats** under TikTok Butler.
2. Click **Refresh stats**.

The dashboard pulls your profile and your most recent videos, then fills the cards and the table. The name at the top confirms which account is connected.

## Reading the numbers

- **Followers, Total likes, Videos** come straight from your TikTok profile.
- **Avg views (recent)** is averaged across the recent videos it pulled, so it reflects how your latest content is doing rather than your all-time average.
- The table is sorted newest first. Use it to spot which recent videos are outperforming so you can lean into what is working.

Refresh whenever you want the latest figures.
