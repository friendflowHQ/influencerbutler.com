## What Messenger Butler does

Messenger Butler is the outreach arm of the Instagram stack. Instagram Butler discovers creators that fit your niche; Messenger Butler reaches out to them. The two are split deliberately - discovery is read-only, outreach is write-side, and you can tune them independently.

## Prerequisites

- **Instagram Butler** has populated the queue with at least one creator. Discovery has to run first.
- You're signed in to Instagram in the Chrome window Butler spawns. There is no separate auth flow - Butler uses your existing session.

## First run

1. Open **Messenger Butler** in the left nav.
2. In **Message templates**, pick a template (or write one). Use the `{handle}` and `{followers}` placeholders for personalization - they're substituted per-creator at send time.
3. Set the **Daily DM cap** conservatively for the first day: 10 DMs is a good starting point.
4. Click **Start Messenger Butler**.

The runner pauses between sends with jitter to look human. You'll see each send in the **Recent activity** stream.

## Tuning safely

Instagram is aggressive about DM volume - pushing too hard gets your account flagged. Some rules of thumb:

- **Stay under 50 DMs/day on a fresh account.** Older, more active accounts can push 80–100.
- **Vary message templates**: Instagram detects identical-text DMs. Keep three or four templates rotating.
- **Watch the topbar API Health badge.** If it turns yellow, slow down - that means Butler detected rate-limiting signals.

## Common gotchas

- **"Send button is greyed out"**: usually means Instagram is showing a verification prompt. Open the Chrome window Butler spawned, complete the prompt, and resume.
- **Messages going to message requests instead of inbox**: that's an Instagram-side decision based on whether the creator follows you. Not something Butler can fix.

## Brand cross-check on message cards

Each brand card in **Messages** carries a brand-at-a-glance panel so you can size up a brand before you reply. Alongside the Creator Connections and SPCC catalogue badges, the card now shows whether you have **already made content for that brand**, so you do not pitch the same brand twice without realizing it.

- **Content badge (collapsed card)**: a "Content" chip with a count and small colored dots, one per platform you have posted to (Amazon, Facebook, Telegram, Reddit, Instagram, Benable, YouTube).
- **Content you have made (expanded card)**: open the card to see each piece - its type (video, photo, idea list, media list, deal post), the platform, the date, and an **Open** link straight to the post.

The cross-check pulls from three places you already use: Storefront Butler content coverage (Amazon videos, photos, idea lists, and media lists), Daily Deals posts (Facebook, Telegram, Reddit, Instagram, Benable), and YouTube Butler uploads. Butler matches them to the brand by the brand's ASIN set, so nothing new to configure. If a brand shows no content badge, you have a clean slate to pitch.
