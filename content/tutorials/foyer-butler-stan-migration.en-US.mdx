## Before you start

This guide walks you through moving your link-in-bio page from Stan Store to Foyer Butler, then canceling Stan Store on your own, directly on stan.store. Influencer Butler never asks for, stores, or uses your Stan Store login. Every step below happens either in Foyer Butler or on Stan's own site, where only you can sign in.

## What you have in Stan, and its Foyer Butler equivalent

| In Stan Store | In Foyer Butler |
| --- | --- |
| Storefront / link-in-bio page | Your Foyer page at `links.influencerbutler.com/@yourhandle` |
| Product links | Product tiles, added by pasting a link |
| Free lead magnets | A product tile priced at $0, unlocked by email instead of payment |
| Booking / calendar links | A Booking tile, linking to your existing Calendly, Cal.com, or other scheduler |
| Digital products, courses, and paid memberships | Not yet available in Foyer Butler. Keep selling those through your current tool until this ships, and only cancel the Stan Store plan that covers your storefront once you've moved everything you need |
| Stanlytics (click analytics) | The Page Analytics card on your Foyer Butler panel |
| Email list / lead capture | The Email list card, with the same subscribe-and-broadcast setup |

## Rebuilding your page

1. Open **Foyer Butler**, under the Link Butler group.
2. Set your **title**, **handle**, **bio**, and an optional **avatar** to match your Stan page.
3. Pick a **theme**, or set your own brand colors in the custom panel.
4. Re-add each product from Stan by pasting its link into **Add a product**. Any free lead magnet becomes a product tile priced at $0.
5. If you had a booking link in Stan, add it under **Booking link** with your existing Calendly or Cal.com URL. It shows with its own calendar icon.
6. Turn on **Email list** if you were collecting emails in Stan, so your page keeps growing your list.
7. Add an **Announcement banner** for anything you'd pin to the top of your Stan page, like a current sale or a new drop.
8. Click **Publish** when your page looks right in the preview pane.

Everything saves as you type, no Save button, so you can rebuild your page at your own pace over more than one sitting.

## Pointing your bio link

Once your Foyer page is live, update the link in your Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and any other bio to `links.influencerbutler.com/@yourhandle` (or your Pro custom domain, if you've set one up). Your followers land on the new page from that point on; your old Stan link keeps working until you cancel Stan Store, so there's no gap.

## Canceling Stan Store

Once your page is fully rebuilt and your bio links point to Foyer Butler, cancel Stan Store yourself, directly on stan.store:

1. Sign in to your Stan Store account.
2. Click **Settings** in the bottom-left menu.
3. Open the **Billing** tab.
4. Click **Cancel Subscription** and follow Stan's steps.

You'll keep access to your Stan account until the end of your current billing cycle, so there's time to double-check everything moved over correctly first. If you'd rather delete your whole Stan account instead of just canceling the subscription, that's a separate step under Stan's **Settings > Security > Delete My Account**.

Full details straight from Stan: [How to Cancel Your Stan Subscription](https://help.stan.store/article/102-how-to-cancel-your-stan-account).
