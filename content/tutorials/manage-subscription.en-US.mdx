## Where to manage your subscription

Everything about your plan lives in your online dashboard. Sign in with the same email your license is on:

[influencerbutler.com/dashboard/subscription](https://www.influencerbutler.com/dashboard/subscription)

There you can see your current status, your renewal date, and your license key, switch between monthly and annual, update your payment card, view past invoices, and cancel.

You can also open the same billing screen straight from the app: go to **Settings > Subscription** and click **Manage subscription**. It opens your secure billing page in your browser, so you do not need to sign in again.

## How to cancel

1. Open [influencerbutler.com/dashboard/subscription](https://www.influencerbutler.com/dashboard/subscription) and sign in, or click **Settings > Subscription > Cancel subscription** in the app.
2. Choose **Cancel subscription** and confirm.
3. That is it. There is no support ticket and no back-and-forth.

## What happens after you cancel

You keep full access until the end of the billing period you already paid for. Nothing shuts off early. On the last day of that period your subscription ends and you are not charged again.

Your data stays put. Settings, keywords, templates, earnings history, and your devices are all kept, so if you resubscribe later you pick up right where you left off.

## Cancelling is not a refund

Cancelling stops the next charge and lets your current period run out. If you also want a refund for the period you are in, that is a separate request. See our [refund policy](https://www.influencerbutler.com/legal/refund) or email [hello@influencerbutler.com](mailto:hello@influencerbutler.com).

## Changing plan instead of cancelling

If the plan is more than you need right now, you do not have to cancel outright. From the same billing screen you can switch between monthly and annual, which changes what you pay without losing your setup.
