## What Delete Posts & Comments does

Deal posting leaves a trail: expired discounts, dead promo codes, and price drops that snapped back. This workspace bulk-removes your own Facebook posts and comments so your groups and profile stay fresh, without you clicking through Facebook one post at a time.

Deleting on Facebook is permanent, so the tool is built preview-first: it always shows you what would be removed before anything is, and an unattended run can never delete more than your per-run cap.

## Before you start

- A Facebook login in Influencer Butler (the same profiles Deals Influencer Butler posts with).
- Nothing else. The destinations list is copied straight from your Deals Influencer Butler groups and pages.

## Choosing what to clean

Two dropdowns decide the target:

- **Where to clean up**: **My timeline** is your personal Facebook profile. **Facebook groups or pages** covers the destinations your deal posts actually go to. Your Influencer Butler deal posts land in groups and pages, so pick that for deal cleanup.
- **Whose content**: **My own content** is the safe default. **Only posts Influencer Butler published** limits cleanup to posts this app created. **A specific person** and **Anyone, by keyword** are group-admin moderation tools: they remove other members' posts, need admin rights on the group, and apply to groups only.

With groups selected, tick the groups and pages to clean from the checklist (copied from Deals Influencer Butler), or paste more group or page URLs, one per line.

## Filters

- **Delete**: posts, comments, or both. Group cleanup removes posts; comment cleanup runs on your timeline.
- **Content type**: **Deal content only** matches posts and comments with Amazon links, promo codes, or #ad/#paidlinks. **Any content** matches everything.
- **Only if it contains / Never delete if it contains**: comma-separated keyword lists. The protect list always wins.
- **Only older than / Always keep newer than**: age limits in days, so fresh posts stay up.

## Safeguards

- **Preview matches** runs the whole scan and records every match as "would-delete" without touching anything. Always run it first.
- **Max deletions per run** caps how much a single run can remove (default 25).
- **Skip pinned / Featured posts** is on by default.
- Deletes are paced with a delay plus random jitter between each one.

## Running it

1. Pick your scope, author, and destinations.
2. Click **Preview matches** and review the Results table.
3. Tighten filters if the preview caught something you want to keep. The protect list is the strongest tool here.
4. Click **Start cleanup**. Turn on **Watch it run** to see the browser do the work.

The Results table tracks every item as matched, deleted, skipped, or failed, and the history sticks around between runs.

## Schedule

Set a start time and a repeat interval (6, 12, 24 hours, 7 days, or custom) and the cleanup runs itself. A scheduled run uses the same filters and never exceeds the per-run cap.

## Common gotchas

- **Preview shows my personal posts, not my deal posts.** Your scope is set to My timeline. Deal posts go to groups and pages, so switch the scope and tick your destinations.
- **A post was skipped with "No Delete option".** Facebook does not offer a delete action on that post for your account (for example a post cross-posted by a Page you do not manage). The runner skips it and moves on.
- **"A specific person" could not find the member.** Paste their profile link instead of typing the name. A name is matched against the group member list, which is less reliable.
- **Removing other members' posts needs admin.** The person and anyone filters only work in groups where you are an admin or moderator.
