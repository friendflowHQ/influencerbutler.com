## Who the Beginner plan is for

The Beginner plan is for creators who are new to Influencer Butler, or new to the Amazon Influencer Program, and want a focused set of tools instead of all 50+ butlers at once. You get the butlers that earn first, a guided fast track that sets them up in the right order, and AI included on our key so you never have to create an OpenAI account to get started.

Nothing is locked forever. Every Power User butler is one click away (see the last section), and your settings, keywords and earnings history carry over when you upgrade.

## What you see in the app

The left menu shows the starter butlers only:

- **Amazon Deals Butler**, with **Posted Deals Log** and **Expired Butler**: find deals from your keywords and post them to your Facebook groups and pages on a schedule.
- **Message Brands** and **AI Keyword Generation**: reach out to brands in Creator Connections with AI-written keywords and messages.
- **Daily Commission Butler**: collect your commission numbers every day.
- **Video Reload** and **Photo Reload**: re-upload content that Amazon removed.
- **Foyer Butler** and **Deeplink Butler**: your link-in-bio page and your branded short links.
- Every free butler: **CC Check**, **Orders Butler**, **Storefront Butler**, **Campaign Alert Butler**, **Brand Release Butler**, **Shopping Butler** and the **Like Butlers**.
- **Help & Tutorials**, the **AI Assistant**, the **Pro Lounge** community, **Settings** and **AI & Data Keys**.

An orange **Unlock Power User tools** pill sits at the bottom of the menu. Click it any time to see exactly which butlers the Power User plan adds.

Inside the starter butlers, a few advanced panels (for example the campaign value filters in Message Brands) are also part of Power User. They show a short note instead of the panel.

## AI is included

Your plan includes **150 AI actions a month** on our OpenAI key. One action is one AI request: a batch of keywords, a post caption, a set of hooks, a voiceover script. The counter resets on the 1st of each month, and the **AI & Data Keys** panel shows how many you have used.

You do not need to add an OpenAI key. If you ever want more than 150 actions, you can add your own key in **AI & Data Keys** and the app uses it instead of the included allowance. Image generation and audio transcription always use your own key, on every plan.

## Your fast track

The first time you open the app, the walkthrough offers **I'm new: set up the essentials**. Pick it and the app walks you through, in order:

1. **Storefront IDs**: your on-site and off-site tracking IDs, so every link you post is yours.
2. **Creator API credentials**: a free, five-minute setup on Amazon that lets the butlers search products. If Amazon does not let you create credentials yet, the step offers our backup credentials so you can keep going.
3. **Deals quick setup**: keywords, your Facebook groups and a posting schedule.
4. **Deeplink Butler**: your first branded short link.
5. **The Chrome extension**: so Creator Connections, Deals and your links work from your browser too.

You can rerun it any time from **Settings > Onboarding**.

## Support

Beginner plans get the same chat support, bug reporting and Pro Lounge community as everyone else. Booking a 1:1 call with the team is a Power User perk.

## Moving up to Power User

When you are ready for more, click **Unlock Power User tools** in the left menu, or **Settings > Subscription > Upgrade**. The sheet shows the live price and what unlocks. One click switches your plan; you only pay the difference for the rest of the current billing period, and your license key stays the same. See [Upgrade to Power User](/help/tutorials/upgrade-to-power-user) for the details.
