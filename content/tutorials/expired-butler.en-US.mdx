## What Expired Butler does

Every deal you post has a shelf life. Once the sale ends or the promo code stops working, the post keeps running, sending shoppers to a price that no longer exists. Expired Butler finds those posts for you and cleans them up.

It reads the deals your Butler actually posted, works out which ones have ended, and then removes the expired posts across your social platforms. The one exception: a post whose deal had a creator campaign attached is edited instead of deleted, so the link you submitted to the campaign stays live while the post stops advertising an offer that is gone.

## How it decides a deal is expired

A post is flagged when either is true:

- The deal's end time has passed.
- A fresh check shows the product is no longer on sale or no longer available.

That combination catches both the deals that ran their full window and the ones that died early.

## Delete or edit

- **No campaign attached**: the post is deleted.
- **Campaign attached**: the post is edited, not deleted. You choose what the edit does:
  - Add an EXPIRED banner to the top of the caption.
  - Remove the promo code and the dates from the caption.
  - Both (the default).

You can change the banner wording in Settings.

## Before you start

- A Facebook login in Influencer Butler (the same profiles Deals Influencer Butler posts with).
- Some posted deals to work on. Expired Butler reads them straight from your Deals Influencer Butler history.

## Running a cleanup

1. Pick your Facebook profile and click **Scan for expired**. Expired Butler lists every expired post it found.
2. Review the list in card or table view. Filter by platform, by delete vs edit, or by whether a campaign is attached, and search by title or ASIN.
3. Tick the posts you want to handle (everything supported is selected by default), then click **Clean up selected**.
4. Watch it work, or turn off **Watch it run** to let it work in the background.

Nothing is removed until you approve it, and every run stays within your per-run cap.

## Keeping a record and reposting

The **History** section keeps a permanent log of everything Expired Butler deleted or edited, so you always know what happened. If a deal comes back on sale, use **Post again** on any row to drop it back into Deals Influencer Butler as a fresh draft.

## Running it automatically

Turn on the daily schedule to have Expired Butler sweep for expired posts once a day. By default a scheduled run only reports what it would do; switch on **Auto-act** in Settings when you want it to clean up on its own.

## Platform coverage

Facebook groups and pages are handled today. Telegram, Reddit, Threads, and Instagram posts show up in the scan and are added as those platforms come online.
