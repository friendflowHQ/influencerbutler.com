## What Photo Reload Butler does

Photo Reload Butler is the photo counterpart to Video Butler. It takes the shoppable photos already on your Amazon Creator storefront and re-posts them to other marketplaces (Canada, the UK, Australia, Singapore) with fresh tags, so the same image can earn in every storefront you are eligible for. It can also reload photos that were removed from Amazon, because every delete saves the original to your computer first.

There is no re-encoding, joining, or duration logic here. Photos do not need any of that. The settings are the ones that make sense for images: where the photo comes from, which marketplaces it posts to, a few timeframe and duplicate filters, an availability check, and how to handle the original after a re-post.

## Before you start

- **Storefront Butler has synced your storefront at least once**: Photo Reload Butler reads from that catalog.
- **A signed-in Amazon Creator Hub session** in the Chrome window the butler opens. Each destination marketplace has its own "Log in to" button.
- Photo Reload Butler appears under **Amazon Butler** once the **Amazon influencer** platform is enabled in Settings.

## Running your first reload

1. Open **Photo Reload Butler** under Amazon Butler.
2. **Step 1, Source and destinations**: pick the **Photo source** (the marketplace your photos live on, usually US) and tick the **Upload destinations** you want to fan out to. Sign in to each destination with its "Log in to" button.
3. **Step 2, What to process**: set a **Timeframe** (or leave the defaults), a **Max photos per run**, and any **Skip duplicates** rules so you do not re-post the same image twice.
4. **Step 3, Reload behaviour**: choose a **Run mode**. Start with **Post and keep the original** so you can verify the new posts look right before letting anything go.
5. Click **Run**.

A Chrome window opens, reads each eligible photo from your storefront, and posts it to the destinations you picked. The **Photo Log** tab tracks every post with its destinations and status.

## One-click Preferred Formulas

You do not have to set the filters by hand. At the top of Step 1, **Preferred Formulas** gives you three one-click mixes for the most common reload paths. Clicking one fills in the source, destinations, and filters for you, and the active formula highlights in orange. Everything stays editable afterward and saves automatically.

- **Fan out US photos → CA + UK + AU + SG**: takes your US photos and posts them to Canada, the UK, Australia, and Singapore, deduping on title and ASIN so the same image never posts to a marketplace twice. Use it once you have posted to US and want the same photos earning everywhere.
- **US re-upload + fan-out → US (delete first) + CA + UK + AU + SG**: refreshes your US photos and fans them out at the same time. It deletes the US original first so the re-post is not rejected as a duplicate, then posts to US and the four other marketplaces. It skips unavailable products and photos added in the last 45 days. Run [Retag Butler](/help/tutorials/retag-butler) first so stale product tags get fixed before the re-post.
- **In-stock products only (skip unavailable)**: turns on both availability checks in one click and leaves the rest of your filters alone.

The middle formula deletes the US original before re-posting, so prove it on a small batch first (see "Test on a small batch first" below).

## Fan out to other marketplaces

The point of Photo Reload Butler is reach. A photo you posted in the US can usually be posted again in Canada, the UK, Australia, and Singapore, because each is a separate storefront. Tick those destinations in Step 1 and a single run posts the photo to each one it can.

Each marketplace is a separate Amazon storefront, so you sign in to each one separately and a re-post to a different marketplace is not a duplicate.

## Skip products that are not available

Posting a photo to a product that is out of stock earns nothing and can drag down your storefront metrics. Turn on **Skip products that are currently unavailable** in Step 1 to enable two checks:

- **Check 1 (before posting)**: asks the Amazon Creators API about each product's stock and skips posts to unavailable ones. It is fail-open, so an API error never blocks a post.
- **Check 2 (while tagging)**: a final safety net for products that still show "Currently unavailable" in Amazon's Add products window at tag time. Choose to keep them on the post or drop them so viewers do not get a dead link.

## Delete the original before re-posting

Amazon's duplicate detection is per-storefront, so re-posting a photo to the same marketplace it already lives on can be rejected as a duplicate. To refresh a photo on its own storefront, turn on **Delete the original before re-posting** in Step 3. Your photo is safe: the original is downloaded to your computer first, and only then deleted. If a re-post fails after the delete, the photo lands in the **Deleted from Amazon** tab with its saved file so you can put it back.

Other marketplaces are separate storefronts, so they never delete anything.

### Test on a small batch first

If you turn on delete-before-repost, prove it on a couple of photos before running your whole library. Set **Max photos per run** to a low number, turn **Watch it run** on, and run once. Check that the original is deleted before its re-post and that the photo looks right on your storefront. Then raise the max.

## Reload photos that were deleted

The **Deleted from Amazon** tab lists photos that were removed from your storefront, each with its locally saved original. Click **Reload Deleted Photos** to put them back on the marketplace they came from. Turn on **Skip photos already reloaded (by ASIN)** if you run this regularly and do not want to reload the same photo twice.

## Find a match by title when the ASIN doesn't exist abroad

International uploads normally match a product to its listing by ASIN, but the same physical product often lives on a foreign marketplace under a different ASIN. When none of a photo's ASINs exist on a destination marketplace, Photo Reload Butler searches that marketplace by the product title (using the Amazon Creators API, falling back to a marketplace search) and scores each result against your product title. It also compares each candidate's photo to your original product image and ranks the closest looking listings first, which matters on non-English marketplaces where the title is translated but the product photo is the same.

Matches are not posted on a guess. Each one shows up in the **Photo Log** as a **match-suggested** row with the candidate's product photo, ASIN, parent ASIN, and price. Review it, then click **Confirm** to upload the photo to that listing (this re-downloads the original and posts it straight away) or **Reject** to discard the suggestion. In pick-from-a-list mode you choose the right listing first; the photo preview updates as you change the choice.

## Tuning over time

- **Timeframe**: limit a run to recent photos, or turn on **All time / all photos** for a full sweep (this overrides the date limits and the per-run max, so use it deliberately).
- **Skip duplicates**: match by title, ASIN, title and ASIN, or "already posted to that marketplace" so a run never re-posts the same image.
- **Keep original if it was added in the last N days**: with delete-before-repost on, this protects freshly added photos from being deleted.

## Where the data goes

Per-run logs land in the Photo Log. Original photo files are saved to your computer before any delete, so a reload can always put a photo back.

## Common gotchas

- **"Creator Hub session expired"**: sign in again from the Chrome window the butler opened, then run again.
- **A re-post is rejected as a duplicate**: you are posting to the same storefront the photo already lives on. Turn on **Delete the original before re-posting** to refresh it, or post it to a different marketplace instead.
- **Nothing posts**: make sure at least one destination marketplace is ticked and signed in.
