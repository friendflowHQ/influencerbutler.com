> **Beta.** AI Influencer Butler is brand new, and a few steps have not been proven against the live Amazon and Walmart sites yet. We point those out below so there are no surprises.

> **Heads up on costs:** the AI parts run on your own keys (fal.ai for images, OpenAI for captions), so you pay those services directly. Setup shows an estimated daily cost and your daily limit before you start. New to OpenAI keys? [Set one up in 5 minutes](/help/tutorials/openai-api-setup).

## What Setup does

AI Influencer Butler used to be one long page of settings. Now it is a small group of pages, and **Setup** comes first:

- **Setup**: the questions, plus a quick look at what is ready.
- **Fashion Looks**: make looks, review them and run autopilot.
- **Your Clone**: train an AI version of you from your photos.
- **Milestone Video**: make a celebration clip. See the [Milestone Video tutorial](/help/tutorials/ai-influencer-milestone).

Setup asks what you want to do and turns your answers into settings across those pages, so you do not have to hunt through them. You can run it again any time with **Edit my answers**, and a setup you close halfway picks up where you left off.

## Start here

On the Setup page, the **Where you are** card shows whether setup is finished, how many clones are ready, and whether your AI keys are in. Below it, under **Start here**, you choose how to go:

- **Fast track** picks sensible defaults and only checks what you need to start.
- **Guide me** walks through every choice and explains it.
- **Edit my answers** (or **Continue setup**) reopens the answers you already gave.

### Question 1: what do you want to make?

Pick everything that applies:

- **Post outfit looks of my AI model**: finds products, dresses your model, builds a collage and posts it on a schedule.
- **Make product collages**: branded product grids from a keyword search, with no model.
- **Make a milestone video**: a celebration clip, like a champagne pop for 1k members.
- **Make photos of me with a product**: one-off photos of your AI clone with a product you choose.

The outfit looks goal has the most questions. The other goals finish quickly and point you to the right page when you are done.

### Fast track or Guide me

With **Fast track** you skip the detailed questions and start with these defaults:

- Your AI clone as the model.
- Products from Amazon and Walmart.
- Seasonal and classic everyday outfits.
- Highest commission first.
- Posting to Instagram, your Facebook Page and Pinterest.
- 2 looks a day, between 9 and 20 o'clock, with a daily limit of 5 USD.
- A list built first, with the link in the first comment.

You still get the key check and the sign-in check, because looks cannot run without them.

With **Guide me** you answer each of these yourself:

1. **Who should wear the outfits?** Your AI clone looks the most like you. A generic model needs no training. If you have more than one clone, you pick which.
2. **Dress your clone in the real products?** Optional and experimental. Your clone is drawn first, then one more image edit puts the real product photos on her. It costs about 0.05 USD more per look, and it has not been tested at scale. If it does not work, you still get the plain clone image.
3. **Which stores?** Amazon and Walmart, Amazon only, or Walmart only. A single store keeps every look to that store.
4. **What kinds of outfits?** Trending seasonal, classic everyday, loungewear and pajamas, workwear, or date night. Pick as many as you like.
5. **Any style to lean into?** Optional: luxury, New England preppy, southern, coastal, boho, western or trendy.
6. **Which products come first?** Highest commission, special price offers (SPCC), Creator Connections, or best value. Walmart products are ranked by commission. Creator Connections is checked by whether a campaign exists for the product, not by whether you have accepted it.
7. **Highest price for one item.** Enter 0 for no limit.
8. **Which collage designs to cycle through?** Optional. Pick several and each look uses the next design in turn.
9. **Where should looks be posted?** Instagram, Facebook Page, Facebook (your profile), Pinterest or TikTok. TikTok needs a video.
10. **Build a list first?** See list-first posting below.
11. **Where should the link go?** In the first comment or in the caption. Instagram and TikTok use the first comment or your bio whatever you pick, because captions cannot hold a clickable link there.
12. **How many looks per day, and between which hours.** The hours use your computer's clock.
13. **Daily spending limit.** Autopilot stops for the day at this amount.
14. **Also make a short video for each look?** About 1.60 USD more per look.

## Photos for a great clone

If you chose your AI clone, train it on the **Your Clone** page. The better and more varied your photos, the better your clone looks in every outfit. Aim for about **20 clear photos of only you**:

- **8 face close-ups**, from different angles and in different light.
- **6 waist-up** photos.
- **6 full-body** photos, head to toe.

Your Clone shows a counter for each group (for example "3 of 8"), so you can see what is missing. To train you need **at least 12 photos in all, with at least 3 of them full-body**. The Train button stays off until you have them. Full-body photos matter most for outfit looks.

Use sharp photos with no sunglasses, no filters and no other people.

If you do not have a clone yet, looks use a generic model until you train one, and you can switch any time.

## Check your AI keys and sign-ins

The last questions are checks:

- **Check your AI keys.** Looks need your fal.ai key for images and an OpenAI key (or the hosted AI on your plan) for captions. If one is missing, you are told which, and where to add it: **AI and Data Keys**.
- **Check your sign-ins.** Looks use the accounts you are signed in to for product lookups and posting: Amazon, Walmart, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, depending on your answers. If one is missing, a sign-in window opens. Sign in there, then check again. Pinterest cannot be checked ahead of time, so it is checked the first time it posts.

## List-first posting

With **Build a list first** on, each look follows the same order:

1. The products are added to an **Amazon Idea List** or a **Walmart collection** first.
2. Then the social posts go out, with the link to that list placed where you chose.
3. Captions are written to say where the link is, for example "link in the first comment", and use hashtags that fit the store.

If the list cannot be made, the look is **held** and is not posted. A held look shows a "needs attention" note on the Fashion Looks page with the reason, so you can fix it and try again. We hold it rather than post a plain product link, so the post never goes out without the list you asked for.

What is not proven yet, so please watch your first few looks:

- **Walmart collections.** The collection is created, but reading its link back from the page is best effort. If the link cannot be confirmed, the look is held.
- **Amazon Idea Lists** need at least 2 products.

## Approval first, always

The first time you finish Setup, nothing posts by itself. Autopilot is left off and every platform is set to wait for your approval. You review the first looks on the Fashion Looks page, and when you like them you turn on **Autopilot** there and choose which platforms post automatically.

The summary at the end of Setup shows the estimated cost per day against your limit. If your limit is lower than the estimate, some looks will be skipped, and Setup tells you so.

## After Setup

Setup takes you to the first page your goals point at:

- Looks: **Fashion Looks**, to create and approve your first look.
- Photos with a product: **Your Clone**.
- Milestone video: **Milestone Video**.
- Collages: **Collage Butler**.

## Common gotchas

- **"Setup is not finished yet" banner on Fashion Looks.** Open Setup and finish the questions, or run it again with **Edit my answers**.
- **My looks use a generic model.** No clone is ready. Train one on Your Clone, then run Setup again if you want it picked.
- **Train is greyed out.** Check the counters: you need 12 photos in all, with at least 3 full-body.
- **A look is marked "needs attention".** The list for that look could not be made or its link could not be confirmed. Sign in to the store, then try again.
- **Some looks are skipped.** Your daily limit is lower than the estimated cost. Raise the limit or lower the looks per day.
