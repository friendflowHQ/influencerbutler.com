Threads Butler posts to your Threads feed. Threads has no public posting API, so it posts by driving Threads in a browser using your Instagram login, the same login the Instagram butlers use.

## Connect (one time)

Threads uses your Instagram login. Open **Connect & Settings** and click **Sign in**. A window opens to Instagram/Threads. Sign in there once and Influencer Butler reuses that session.

## Compose a post

Open **Compose** to build your post the same way as Deals Butler's Build Your Post:

- **Write your text** (up to 500 characters) in the post box. Type freely, or drop in **Library pills** (Hook, CTA, Hashtags, Urgency, and hook styles). Click a pill to edit its lines; each post uses a different line, so your captions vary automatically.
- **Quick-start layouts** fill the post with a starting structure you can then edit.
- **More versions** lets you keep several variations of the caption; each post uses one, cycled.
- **Image**: drop the Image pill and pick a source. Paste an image URL, upload a photo from your computer, or generate one with AI. Or choose No image to post text only.
- **My Caption Voice**: paste 2 to 5 of your own posts and AI writes more versions in your voice, added to your list below.

Keep **Watch it run** on to watch the browser post it, or turn it off to run in the background. When it looks right, click **Post to Threads**.

## Schedule for later

Pick a date and time and click **Schedule**. Scheduled posts appear in the list below, each with a **Cancel** button, and post automatically at their time. A paused app will not fire scheduled posts.

## Manage posts

Open **Manage posts** to see what you have sent from Compose and delete any post. Threads only allows editing within about five minutes of posting, so Manage is delete only.

## Notes

Because Threads posting runs in a real browser, keep the app open and signed in for scheduled posts to go out. If Threads asks you to sign in, do it under Connect & Settings.
