## What the affiliate dashboard does

Once you are approved as an Influencer Butler affiliate, **Affiliates** in the left menu shows your branded promo code, your tracked share link, your earnings and your payouts. You earn recurring commission on every subscription you refer, and we pay you directly by PayPal.

The page has four tabs: **Overview**, **Promote**, **Performance** and **Get paid**.

## Where your money is

The **Overview** tab shows four stages for your commission:

1. **Upcoming**: future months of annual plans you referred. Each month is paid as it comes due.
2. **Clearing**: earned, but in a 14-day safety hold so refunds and disputes can settle.
3. **Payable**: cleared and ready. This is what the next monthly run sends you.
4. **Paid**: already sent to your PayPal.

We pay monthly, on or around the 1st, once your payable balance reaches **$10**. Anything under $10 rolls into the next month. PayPal fees are not covered, so the amount that lands can be slightly less.

## Get paid: tax form and PayPal

You can share your link and earn before you finish this, but we cannot send money until both are done.

1. Open the **Get paid** tab (or click **Add now** in the checklist at the top).
2. Fill in your tax form: **W-9** if you are in the United States, **W-8BEN** if you are an individual outside it, or **W-8BEN-E** for a business outside it. It takes about two minutes.
3. Add the **PayPal email** where you want to be paid.
4. When the checklist at the top turns green, payouts are set up.

## Why a payout can be on hold

Larger payouts, and every payout while we are still reviewing them by hand, get a quick check from our team before release. Nothing is wrong with your account. It usually adds a few days and your balance stays safe.

## Promote your link

The **Promote** tab has your branded code (15% off for the customer), a pre-filled share link, a QR code you can download at print size, per-channel tagged links, one-click social sharing and a media kit of ready-made graphics. The **Content Planner** and **Competitor Playbook** in the Affiliates menu give you captions, a 14-day calendar and comparison talking points.

## VIP Guest Passes

If your account has guest passes turned on, the Promote tab lets you hand a warm prospect a free single-seat Pro workspace for a set time. Each pass carries your referral link, so you earn commission if your guest upgrades. If you do not see the card, it shows a short note instead. Ask the affiliate team to enable it.

## Leaderboard

Under **Affiliates > Leaderboard** you can choose to appear on the public Top Affiliates board by handle. It is off by default (you show as initials) and only your referral count is ever shown, never earnings or contact details.

## Need help?

If a payout looks wrong or your tax form was not accepted, use the contact link at the bottom of the Affiliates page and pick the affiliate subject.
