## What changes when you upgrade

@youtube(JSw7LUAt3E4)

- Every butler appears in the left menu, including the ones behind the **Unlock Power User tools** pill, and the advanced panels inside the starter butlers open up.
- AI switches to your own OpenAI key with no monthly cap. Add the key in **AI & Data Keys** (see [Set up your OpenAI API key](/help/tutorials/openai-api-setup)). Until you do, AI buttons show a lock instead of using the included allowance.
- You can book 1:1 calls with the team from your online dashboard.
- Your license key, your devices, your keywords, templates and earnings history all stay exactly as they are. You do not sign in again.

## Upgrade from the desktop app

1. Click **Unlock Power User tools** at the bottom of the left menu, or open **Settings > Subscription** and click **Upgrade to Power User**. The same sheet also opens when you click a locked butler or hit the monthly AI cap.
2. The sheet shows the live Power User price for your seats and billing cycle, and what you pay today.
3. Click **Upgrade to Power User**. The switch happens on your existing subscription, and the app unlocks everything within a few seconds.

You only pay the difference for the rest of the current billing period. The next renewal is at the full Power User price.

## Upgrade from the Chrome extension

Open the extension popup. Beginner accounts see a **Plan: Beginner** line with an **Upgrade to Power User** button and the live price. Click it once; the extension and the desktop app pick up the new plan on their next check (a few seconds for the extension, within a minute for the app).

## Upgrade from the phone app

In the phone app, open **More**. Beginner accounts see the plan line with an **Upgrade** button. Your computer must be on with Influencer Butler open, because the switch runs through the desktop app. If it is not, the phone says so and you can do it from the computer later.

## On a free trial

If you are still on the free trial and have not added a card, the sheet offers **Switch my trial to Power User** instead of a charge. Your trial keeps running, with every butler open, and you pick a plan when the trial ends.

## Prices

As of October 2026, Power User Solo is $39 a month or $390 a year, and Power User Trio is $69 a month or $690 a year. On **November 28, 2026** these go up to $49 / $490 (Solo) and $89 / $890 (Trio); Beginner Solo goes from $24.99 to $29 a month on the same date. Anyone already on a plan keeps the price they signed up at, so upgrading before November 28 locks in today's Power User price. The sheet always shows the current figures from our billing system, so trust it over this page if they ever differ. Full details are on the [pricing page](https://www.influencerbutler.com/pricing).

## Going back to Beginner

The in-app sheet only moves up. To move back to Beginner, open [influencerbutler.com/dashboard/subscription](https://www.influencerbutler.com/dashboard/subscription) and switch plans there. Butlers that are not in the Beginner set disappear from the menu; their settings are kept in case you upgrade again.
