## Where Black Friday Butler went

The old Black Friday radar, which watched your storefront on a refresh cadence, has been retired. Everything it did, and a lot more, now lives in the **Deal Finder**.

From November 10 through Cyber Monday, open **Amazon Butler**, then **Black Friday Butler**, then **Black Friday Deal Finder**. It checks your storefront content, the products you bought (from Orders Butler) and Amazon's Deals Hub, then ranks every deal by estimated earnings.

## Quick start

1. Run **Storefront Butler** and **Orders Butler** first, so the Deal Finder knows your content and the products you own.
2. Open **Black Friday Deal Finder** and click **Run now**.
3. Leave **Only Black Friday deals** ticked to see just the deals Amazon has tagged for Black Friday Week. Untick it to see every deal. Early in November the list can be short, because Amazon tags event deals as it announces them.
4. Tick the deals you want and send them to your **Black Friday Deals** queue to post.

If you don't see Black Friday Butler in the menu, open **Settings**, then **Show / Hide Butlers**, and switch it on.

Outside Black Friday season the same tool is called Prime Day Butler. The full walkthrough, filters and tips are in the [Prime Day Butler tutorial](/help/tutorials/prime-day-butler).
