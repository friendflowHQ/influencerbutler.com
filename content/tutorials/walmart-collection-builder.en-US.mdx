> **You stay in control.** Collection Builder never creates anything on Walmart until you have reviewed the picks and clicked **Create collection**.

## What Collection Builder does

If you already have a great Amazon list, you should not have to rebuild it by hand on Walmart. Collection Builder reads your list and does the legwork:

- Reads the **name, description and every product** on your public Amazon list. No Amazon sign-in is needed.
- Finds a **Walmart product for each item**. When the identical product exists on Walmart it uses that. When it does not, it picks the closest style that shoppers actually buy (strong ratings and lots of reviews).
- Writes a **similar collection name and description** (up to 300 characters) so your Walmart collection feels like the original without being a copy.
- Builds the collection on your **Walmart Creator storefront** for you.

## Before you start

- Sign in to Walmart Creator once (Walmart Butler, then use the **Sign in to Walmart** button). Your session is saved.
- For the best picks and wording, connect OpenAI in **API Integrations**. Without it, Collection Builder still works using a simpler ranking.

## Step by step

1. Open **Collection Builder** under Walmart Butler.
2. Paste your Amazon list link. It looks like `amazon.com/shop/yourname/list/` followed by letters and numbers.
3. Choose **Matching**:
   - **Both (recommended)** uses the identical Walmart product when it exists and fills the rest with similar picks.
   - **Same product only** keeps only identical items, so some rows may be empty.
   - **Similar picks only** always searches for the closest popular item.
4. Click **Import and match**. The summary tiles show how many items matched exactly, how many are similar picks, and how many found nothing.
5. Review the picks. You can edit the **name** and **description**, untick any product, or use **Swap for another** to choose a different Walmart product for a row.
6. Click **Create collection**. Turn on **Watch it run** in the top bar if you want to see your browser build it.

## Tips

- If a product has no good Walmart match, leave it unticked or swap in one of the alternatives.
- Changed your mind about matching? Change the **Matching** setting and click **Match again**. Your name and description edits are kept.
- Collection Builder remembers which lists you have already turned into collections and warns you before making a second one.
- Turn on **Keep my Amazon wording** if you would rather copy the name and description exactly.
