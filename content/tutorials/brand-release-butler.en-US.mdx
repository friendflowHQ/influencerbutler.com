## What Brand Release Butler does

New products convert best when you are early. Brand Release Butler watches the
Amazon brands you follow, notices when they list a product it has not seen
before, and alerts you so you can promote it before anyone else. Each new release
can be handed to Build Your Post with one click, or auto-posted for brands you
trust. Everything you set here saves automatically.

## Before you start

- New products are checked a few times per day on their own. You do not need to
  keep the app open on this panel.
- The first check after you follow a brand quietly records its current products
  as a starting point. It does not alert on those, only on products that appear
  afterward.
- Coverage is a sample of each brand's catalog, so a brand-new item can take a
  little while to show up.

## How to use it

1. In the left menu, open **Amazon Butler**, then **Brand Release Butler**.
2. Under **Follow a brand**, type a name in the **Search brands** box and click
   **Add** on the brand you want. It joins your **Followed brands** list below.
3. Watch the totals strip at the top: **Brands followed**, **Products tracked**,
   **New releases**, and **Unread alerts** update as the Butler works.
4. To promote automatically, use the **Auto-post new releases** column in the
   **Followed brands** table. Turn it on, then choose **Review first** to
   approve each release before it posts, or **Post right away** to publish
   automatically through your Deals Butler settings.
5. Under **Alert methods**, choose how you want to hear about new releases:
   **Show a popup in the app** (on by default) and **Send me an email**. Both
   save on their own.
6. When a followed brand lists something new, it appears under **New releases**.
   Click **Build a post** on a release to send it to Build Your Post, or open
   **Deals Butler** where it has been added for you.
7. Keep the feed tidy with **Mark all read** and **Clear all**, or **Mark as
   read** on a single release.
8. To force a fresh look right now, click **Check now** in the hero. The status
   line shows when the last check ran.
9. To stop watching a brand, click **Unfollow** in its row.

## Notes

- Brand Release Butler is free forever and always visible under Amazon Butler.
- Watching for new products is separate from Creator Connections campaigns. For
  campaign alerts, use Campaign Alert Butler.
- "Post right away" publishes through your existing Deals Butler settings, so set
  those up first if you plan to auto-post.
