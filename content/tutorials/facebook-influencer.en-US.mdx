## What Facebook Inviter does

You post a deal in a Facebook group and forty people react or drop a comment, then nothing happens, because none of them follow your Page. Facebook Inviter chases them up: it walks the groups you belong to, finds the posts your own Page made in each one, and invites everyone who engaged with them to follow your Page. That means people who reacted (invited with one click) and, as a best effort, people who left a comment.

Those engagers are the warmest audience you will get that day. This tool turns them into followers who see your next deal without you re-posting into the group.

A quick note on commenters: Facebook gives a clean one-click invite for people who reacted, but the invite option for someone who only commented is tucked away and does not always appear. So Facebook Inviter invites every reactor and picks up commenters wherever Facebook offers the option, quietly skipping the rest rather than stalling.

## Before you start

- **You're signed in to Facebook in the app.** Facebook Inviter uses the same Facebook accounts as Deals Butler. If you haven't signed in yet, open Deals Butler first and sign in to Facebook there once.
- **Know your Page or profile ID.** The one required field is the numeric identifier of the Page or profile you want people to follow. You can find it in your Page's About section or in its URL.
- **Every invite is real.** There is no preview, no dry run, and no confirmation step. Pressing Start sends real Facebook invites that cannot be recalled. Start small.

## Setting it up

Under **Run settings**:

- **Invite everyone who engaged with my posts**: the master switch, on by default. Leave it on for the normal behavior (invite every reactor and commenter on your posts). Turn it off when you want to sit a run out; with it off, pressing Start does nothing.
- **Profile or Page ID**: the numeric identifier people will be invited to follow. Required; Butler won't start without it.
- **Facebook account**: which signed-in Facebook account sends the invites. It defaults to your Default account. The list matches the accounts you set up in Deals Butler, so pick the one that belongs to the Page.
- **Groups to skip**: any groups you belong to but would rather not be seen inviting people from. One per line.
- **Max invites per post**: the cap that matters most. Zero means unlimited, which is not what you want on your first run. Start with a small number.
- **Scroll depth**: how far to dig into each group's feed. Ten scrolls is plenty.
- **Watch it run**: leave it on to bring the automation browser to the front and watch invites go out, or turn it off to let it work in the background.

Everything auto-saves as you type. There's no Save button.

## Running it

1. Fill in the Page or profile ID and set a small per-post cap.
2. Click **Start Facebook Inviter**.
3. The status pill flips to Running, and the activity log shows what it's doing, group by group.

You can stop at any time with **Stop**.

## Running it on a schedule

1. Pick a **Start time** for the first automatic run.
2. Choose how many days to wait between runs.
3. Click **Enable Schedule**. It won't arm without the profile ID filled in.

The card shows when it last ran and when it runs next. **Stop Schedule** disarms it and is safe to press at any time. The app has to stay open and signed in to Facebook for scheduled runs to fire.

## Common gotchas

- **It says you're not signed in, but you are**: make sure the **Facebook account** you picked is one you actually signed in to in Deals Butler. Invites are sent from that exact account.
- **Go slow at first**: set a small per-post cap, do one run, then go look at your follower count before you widen anything. High volume is what trips Facebook's limits.
- **Zero means unlimited**: an empty-feeling "0" in the invites field is not "off", it removes the cap entirely.
