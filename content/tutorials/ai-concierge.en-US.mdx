## What Butler AI is

Butler AI is an on-demand concierge inside your dashboard. Instead of booking a call and waiting, you can talk to it right away and get a live demo, ask how a butler works, or walk through setting something up. It knows the product and pulls its answers from the same tutorials you are reading now, so the steps it gives you are the real ones.

It is an AI assistant, not a person. It is great for "how do I..." and "show me..." questions. When you want a human, it will point you to book a real demo or support call.

## How to start

1. In the dashboard, open **Book a Call**.
2. At the top, click **Talk to Butler AI now**.
3. Pick how you want to talk:
   - **Start voice chat**: your browser asks for microphone access. Allow it, then just start talking. Butler AI talks back.
   - **Type instead**: a normal chat box, good for quiet rooms or if you would rather read.
4. Tick the box to confirm you understand it is an AI and the conversation may be saved so the team can follow up.

Voice works best in a recent Chrome or Edge on a computer with a microphone. If the microphone is blocked, use **Type instead**.

You can also chat from the desktop app: in the left menu, click **AI Assistant**. It is the same brain, with the same answers.

## What it can do

- **Give a live demo**: ask it to show you what a butler does and it will walk you through it.
- **Answer setup questions with exact click paths**: for example, "how do I set up Goldmine Butler?" or "what do I put in API Integrations?" It looks up the real steps and tells you exactly where to click, starting from the left menu.
- **Show you the screen**: when a tutorial has a screenshot of the screen it is describing, the answer includes the screenshot so you can see where you are going.
- **File a bug or feature request for you**: if you mention something broken or wish for a feature, it offers to send the report to the team right from the chat. It shows you a short draft first and only files it after you say yes. The team replies by email.
- **Talk about pricing and plans**: what is free, what Pro unlocks, how the trial works.
- **Use your account details**: when you ask, it can check your own plan or your affiliate earnings summary. It only shares your data with you.

## Your chat history (desktop app)

In the desktop app's AI Assistant, your conversations are saved on your computer and come back after a restart. The panel keeps a history list on the right side, grouped by day. Click an old chat to reread it or keep the conversation going, use the search box to find a chat by keyword, and use **New chat** to start fresh.

## What it cannot do

- It cannot open or control your desktop app, and it cannot log into your Amazon or Instagram. It gives you the exact click path: you do the clicking.
- It does not give financial or investment advice.
- If it is not sure, it will say so and offer a tutorial link or a human call rather than guess.

## When you want a person

At any time, click **Book a human call instead** to schedule a demo or support call with the team. Butler AI will also offer this whenever it reaches the limit of what it can help with.

## A note on privacy

Butler AI is an assistant that answers your questions. Your Amazon and Instagram logins are never involved. A short summary of the conversation may be saved so the team can follow up if you asked for something they need to handle. Voice sessions have a time limit, shown as a countdown, so a session cannot run on forever.
