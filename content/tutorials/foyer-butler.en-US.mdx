## What Foyer Butler does

Foyer Butler builds you a single, shoppable page, your own "link in bio," made from the products you've already tracked with Link Butler and your live deals. Share one link everywhere (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, email) and shoppers land on a page with your picks, your storefront's deals, and a way to join your list. Every tap is a Butler Link tap, so it shows up in your Link Performance dashboard like any other click.

Your page and your subscriber list are yours to keep. They stay live and fully usable no matter what plan you're on, now or later.

## Building your page

1. Open **Foyer Butler**, under the Link Butler group.
2. Set your **title**, **handle** (this becomes part of your page's web address), **bio**, and an optional **avatar**.
3. Pick a **theme** from the swatch grid, or open the custom panel to set your own colors.
4. Choose a **page width** to fit how many products you want to show per row.
5. Add products by pasting a product link into **Add a product**, or add a plain heading to organize your page into sections.
6. Add a **Booking link** tile if you take calls or consultations, pointing at your Calendly, Cal.com, or other scheduler. It shows with its own calendar icon.
7. Add an **Announcement banner** for a sale, a new drop, or any time-limited message you want pinned above your name, with an optional button and an optional date to stop showing it. Pick a **Banner pack** (Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days, Black Friday, and more) to fill in the message, color and end date in one click, then edit anything you like.
8. Reorder or remove any product with the arrows and remove button on its row.

Everything saves as you type, no Save button. The preview pane on the right shows exactly what shoppers will see while you edit your page. On the Email tab it switches to a live preview of your next email, and on Analytics and Settings it steps aside so you get the full width.

## Today's Deals: your storefront on the page

Turn on **Today's Deals** to show your live Amazon, Walmart, and TikTok Shop deals on the page automatically, the same deals you post with your Deals Butlers. Choose which stores to include, how many picks and catalog deals to show, a minimum percent off, and how the grid is laid out (columns, rows, and how many days of past deals to include). Click **Refresh now** to push your latest deals immediately.

**Choose your More deals.** Under Today's Deals, pick **Deal Packs** (like Pets, Kitchen or Halloween) so More deals only shows the kinds of products you want. Add a **Deal source** filter to show only Creator Connections or SPCC deals, turn on **Best sellers only**, and set **Deal priority** to decide what ranks first (SPCC, Creator Connections, highest commission, best sellers, biggest discount). **Mix of stores** lets you alternate Amazon and Walmart deals. Walmart deals are found using your packs, so pick at least one pack to include Walmart. Leave everything at the defaults and More deals shows today's biggest discounts first.

## Growing your list

Turn on **Email capture** to show a sign-up form on your page, with your own headline and button text, positioned at the top or bottom. Every new subscriber lands in **Your subscribers**, where you can search, filter by status or tag, tag or untag in bulk, unsubscribe someone, or export the whole list to CSV.

## Sending newsletters (Pro after your trial)

Write a **subject** and a short **intro** under **Send to your list**, click **Send now**, and Foyer Butler builds the rest of the email from your live deals, the same picks shown on your page, and delivers it to everyone on your list. Turn on **Send automatically** to have it go out on a recurring schedule (daily, every 2 or 3 days, or weekly) at a time you choose, using whatever subject and intro you've last saved.

**Pick a send time after your deals are fresh.** Under Email, Compose, the "Send automatically" section tells you when your Deals Butler search starts, when it usually finishes, and when your page is updated. Choose "Use this time" to schedule the newsletter just after that. If you run more than one Deals Butler, pick the one that feeds this page under Today's Deals ("Which Deals Butler feeds this page"), and the timing follows it.

Sending is free during your trial. Once your trial ends, sending new newsletters needs an active Pro subscription; your page and subscriber list are not affected either way, only new sends pause until you upgrade. You'll get an email heads-up a few days before that happens, and another if it takes effect, so it's never a surprise. Every email includes an unsubscribe link automatically.

**Amazon items in your newsletter.** Amazon's Associates rules restrict showing prices outside Amazon's own tools, so Amazon deals in your newsletter show the percent off but never a price. Walmart deals still follow your Price display setting. When an email includes Amazon items, a short Associates disclosure line is added to the bottom automatically, in your app language (and a Walmart line when Walmart items are included). If you customized your disclosure wording on your page, that wording is used.

**Send from your own domain (paid plans).** By default your newsletter goes out from a shared Influencer Butler address. On a paid plan, your sender name shows as just your page name with no "via Influencer Butler". To go further, open Email, Compose, "Send from your own domain", enter a domain or subdomain you own (a subdomain like mail.yourdomain.com keeps your regular email separate), and click Add domain. Copy each DNS record shown into the DNS settings at the company where you bought the domain, then click Check my DNS. It can take a few minutes, sometimes longer. Once every record shows Confirmed, your newsletter sends from the address you choose (hello@yourdomain.com by default) and you can set where replies go. Until then, emails keep going out from the shared address, so nothing breaks while you set it up. Remove the domain at any time to go back.

## Digital Products (beta)

Sell an ebook, template, or preset straight from your page, or give one away free in exchange for an email, from the **Digital Products** card. Set a title and a price, upload a file, and click **Add to page** to place it as its own tile.

Free ($0) products work end to end today: a shopper enters their email, gets an instant download link, and can always get back to it later with the "resend my access link" option on the download page. Checkout for priced products is coming soon; until it ships, a priced product shows as "Coming soon" on your page instead of a broken buy button.

## Page Analytics

Every tile on your page is already a tracked Butler Link, so the **Analytics** tab shows how your page, links and emails perform with no extra setup. Pick a date range (today, 7, 30, or 90 days) and filter by real people only, or focus on a single tile. You get page views, taps, tap-through rate and net subscribers compared with the period right before, a daily chart, a view-to-tap-to-subscriber funnel, your top tiles, where taps come from (countries, devices, operating systems and traffic sources), the busiest times of the week, subscriber growth, and open and click rates for your recent emails. **Export CSV** downloads the current view.

## Socials and disclosures

Add your **social links** (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Pinterest, your Amazon storefront, Walmart, and email) to show a row of icons on your page, positioned at the top or bottom. Turn on the **Amazon** and/or **Walmart affiliate disclosure** if your page includes their affiliate links, required wording that keeps you compliant.

## Publishing

Click **Publish** to make your page live at `links.influencerbutler.com/@yourhandle`, or turn on **Auto-publish** so every edit goes live on its own. Changing your handle is throttled to once every 14 days; your page stays live at your current handle until then. The first time your page goes live at a new address, Foyer Butler reminds you to add it to your Amazon Associates site list, since Amazon requires every site you run affiliate links on to be declared there.

Pro accounts publish without the "Powered by Influencer Butler" footer.

On a paid plan, connect your own domain once in [Link Butler > Settings](/help/tutorials/link-butler#use-your-own-domain-paid-plans) and this page also goes live at that domain's main address, in addition to `links.influencerbutler.com/@yourhandle`, which keeps working too. The "Your own domain" card at the top of Foyer Butler shows the current status and a shortcut back to that setting.
