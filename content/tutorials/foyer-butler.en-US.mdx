## What Foyer Butler does

Foyer Butler is your link-in-bio page: the single link you put in your Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube bio that shows all the products you are recommending right now. You build it from tracked Butler Links, so every card on the page opens the Amazon shopping app (not a browser tab where the affiliate cookie can be lost) and every tap flows into your Ledger.

This is the same job Linktree or a Link in Bio page does. Foyer Butler goes further: because each card is a Calling Card, the page and its analytics are the same thing. You never have to reconcile a separate bio-link tool against your earnings.

## Building your page

1. Open **Foyer Butler**, under the Link Butler group.
2. Fill in **Your page**: a display name, a handle (this becomes your public address), a short bio, and a theme.
3. Under **Add a product**, paste a product link and click **Add to page**. Foyer Butler mints a tracked Calling Card for it and drops a card onto your page with the product photo and title.
4. Use **Add section** to group products under a heading, like "This week" or "Kitchen."
5. Reorder any card with the up and down arrows, or remove it with the x. Everything saves as you go, no Save button.

The **Live preview** on the right shows your page exactly as a shopper will see it, updating as you edit.

## Publishing your page

When you are ready to share it, click **Publish page**. Your page goes live at:

`links.influencerbutler.com/@yourhandle`

Put that link in your bio. To change your page later, edit it and click **Publish page** again; the same address updates in place. Your handle is yours: once you publish it, no one else can take it.

## Why every tap is tracked

Every card on your Foyer is a Butler Link, so:

- **It opens the app.** On a phone, a card opens the native Amazon app instead of the in-app browser, which converts far better.
- **It shows up in your Ledger.** Taps from your Foyer are labeled so you can see how much of your traffic and sales your bio page drives, next to every other place you post.
- **It never goes stale.** Because each card routes through a Calling Card, you can re-point a product later and the page keeps working.
