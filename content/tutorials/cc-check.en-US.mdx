## What CC Check does

CC Check is where you act on Creator Connections opportunities. It lets you:

- **Check ASINs** against the live Creator Connections + SPCC catalogs to see which products have active campaigns or commission offers.
- **Accept campaigns** that match your storefront.
- **Submit content** and **extract brands** from the results.
- **Import ASINs** to check in bulk.

Everything lands in one **results table** you can sort, filter, and act on.

> **Catalog refresh moved.** Downloading and scheduling the Creator
> Connections + SPCC catalogs now lives entirely in **Data Refresh Butler**.
> CC Check reads whatever those catalogs currently hold — it no longer has its
> own "download catalog" or "refresh schedule" sections. See the
> [Data Refresh Butler tutorial](/help/tutorials/data-refresh-butler) to set
> how often catalogs refresh.

## Keeping catalogs fresh

CC Check checks your ASINs against catalogs that **Data Refresh Butler** keeps
current. The default flow:

1. During first-run setup, the catalogs are harvested in the background.
2. After that, **Data Refresh Butler** refreshes them on a daily schedule (4:00 AM by default).

You don't run a catalog download from CC Check anymore. If a check looks like
it's working against stale data, open Data Refresh Butler and click one of its
**Refresh** buttons.

## Checking and accepting

1. Open **CC Check** in the left nav.
2. **Import ASINs** (or use the ones already loaded).
3. Click **Check ASINs** to match them against the current catalogs.
4. Review the results table, then **Accept Campaigns**, **Submit Content**, or **Extract Brands** as needed.

## Automating acceptance

The **Schedule** ("Check ASINs") form lets you run the check/accept pipeline on
a recurring cadence — handy if you want new matching campaigns accepted
automatically. This acceptance schedule is separate from catalog refresh:
Data Refresh Butler refreshes catalogs but never accepts campaigns on your
behalf.

## Gotchas

- **No results after a check**: confirm the topbar **CC Storefront ID** is set, and that Data Refresh Butler has harvested the catalogs at least once.
- **Results look stale**: open Data Refresh Butler and refresh; the last-harvest date/time on its CC and SPCC cards tells you how current the data is.
