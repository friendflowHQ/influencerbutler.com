## What CC Check does

CC Check is where you act on Creator Connections opportunities. It lets you:

- **Check ASINs** against the live Creator Connections + SPCC catalogs to see which products have active campaigns or commission offers.
- **Accept campaigns** that match your storefront.
- **Submit content** and **extract brands** from the results.
- **Import ASINs** to check in bulk.

Everything lands in one **results table** you can sort, filter, and act on.

## Where the catalog data comes from

You don't download or refresh a catalog anymore. CC Check checks each ASIN
against the **live** Creator Connections + SPCC catalogs, which are hosted
centrally and kept current for you. There's nothing to schedule and no
multi-hundred-megabyte download on your machine: every check reads the latest
data on demand.

## Checking and accepting

1. Open **CC Check** in the left nav.
2. **Import ASINs** (or use the ones already loaded).
3. Click **Check ASINs** to match them against the live catalogs.
4. Review the results table, then **Accept Campaigns**, **Submit Content**, or **Extract Brands** as needed.

## Automating acceptance

The **Schedule** ("Check ASINs") form lets you run the check/accept pipeline on
a recurring cadence, handy if you want new matching campaigns accepted
automatically. The app must stay open for scheduled sweeps to fire.

## Gotchas

- **No results after a check**: confirm the topbar **CC Storefront ID** is set. CC Check needs it to know which storefront the campaigns should match.
- **An ASIN you expected isn't matched**: it simply has no active CC or SPCC offer in the live catalog right now. Checks read the latest data, so try again later if a campaign launches.
