## What CC Check does

CC Check is the data-refresh runner that keeps two catalogs current:

1. The **download-all** catalog of every Creator Connections campaign visible to you.
2. The **SPCC** (Sponsored Products for Creators) commission catalog.

Most other workspaces - Daily Commission Butler, Goldmine Butler, Ads Goldmine Butler, Earnings Intelligence - depend on these catalogs being fresh. If you don't run CC Check, the rest of the app makes decisions on stale data.

## When to run it

The recommended setup is **automatic**, not manual:

1. Open **Data Refresh Butler** in the left nav.
2. Toggle on **Refresh CC & SPCC catalogs 1h before each search**. This couples CC Check to your Daily Deals cadence so it runs as a side effect.
3. If you don't run Daily Deals, set a daily refresh cadence in Data Refresh Butler instead (24h is plenty).

## Running manually

If you need a one-off refresh:

1. Open **CC Check** in the left nav.
2. Click **Run now**.
3. Wait for the run to complete - it usually takes 1–3 minutes depending on catalog size.

You'll see a progress badge in the CC Check topbar and the **Last refreshed** timestamps update in Data Refresh Butler when it finishes.

## Gotchas

- **Catalog stays empty after a run**: check the topbar **CC Storefront ID** field. If it's blank or wrong, CC Check has nothing to download against.
- **Run completes but downstream workspaces don't update**: they cache reads. Restart the workspace (close + reopen the app) to force a re-read.
