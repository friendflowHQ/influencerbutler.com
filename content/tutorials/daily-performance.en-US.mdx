## What it does

Influencer Butler gives you one clear daily readout of your verified Amazon performance: your last 30 days versus the previous 30, plus what happened yesterday. You get it in two places: a card on your **Dashboard** and on the **Amazon Butler** hub, and an optional daily email.

The card and email show five numbers for the last 30 days, each with how it moved versus the previous 30 days:

- **Earnings** and **Revenue**
- **Clicks**, **Ordered**, and **Shipped**

## Where to find it

- **Dashboard**: the performance card sits right under your earnings totals.
- **Amazon Butler hub**: the same card appears under Today.

The card only appears once Daily Commission Butler has harvested a few days of data, so if you just installed, give it a day or two. Until then it fills in as more days are collected, and it tells you how many days it is working from.

## Reading a down month

When your 30-day earnings are lower than the previous 30, the card does not just show a red number. It tells you what likely happened and one thing to try:

- **Traffic dipped**: fewer clicks reached your links. Reshare your top links and post a fresh roundup to bring shoppers back.
- **Clicks held, earnings cooled**: your links still got clicks, so check price and availability on your top products and refresh anything out of stock.

A dip from one month to the next is normal, and your most recent days are still settling. Amazon finalizes clicks, shipments, and returns over the following days, so fresh numbers usually firm up.

## The daily email

By default, Influencer Butler emails you this readout once a day so you never have to go looking for it. To turn it off, open the **Amazon Butler** hub and clear the **Email me this every day** checkbox under the performance card. You can turn it back on the same way.

## Prerequisites

- **Daily Commission Butler** has run at least once so your numbers are harvested. It runs daily by default.
- You are signed in, so the email has an address to reach.
