## What Launch Butler does

Launch Butler takes you from nothing to a running deals community. You answer one questionnaire about your niche, and it builds your Facebook Page and deals group, writes the welcome and weekly posts, sets up a posting schedule on its own Deals Butler, adds your Amazon disclosure, links your channels together, and connects a Foyer shop with a newsletter sign-up. Every customer gets one free launch, on any plan.

## Before you start

- Sign in to **Facebook** in the app so Launch Butler can act as you. It opens a sign-in window when it needs one, and it works even when Google sign-in is blocked.
- Connect the **Amazon Creators API** in **Settings > AI & Data Keys** so your launch can find deals.
- Have your niche and a name in mind. You can also point the launch at a Facebook Page or group you already have.

## Run a launch

1. Open **Launch Butler** in the left menu and start the **questionnaire**.
2. Tell it your **niche**, your **community name**, and whether to build fresh or use a Page or group you already run.
3. Pick the **channels** you want linked: Instagram, Telegram, your Amazon storefront list, and comment-to-DM funnels.
4. Choose your **posting schedule** and how often deals go out.
5. Review the plan. Launch Butler shows every channel it will set up, with your own niche on the covers.
6. Start the launch. It works through the steps in Mission Control: Page and group setup, bios and disclosures, the welcome and pinned posts, friend invites, the deals schedule, and the Foyer shop.

## After the launch

- Your launch runs on its own **Deals Butler**, with a name that fits, so it never changes a setup you already had.
- **Running-low reminders** tell you when the year's posts are getting thin, with a one-click **Schedule next year**.
- A full **product funnel** and a year of product posts and emails are set up, with a daily newsletter capture on your Foyer page.
- Everything a launch touches is additive. It never edits your existing butlers or settings.

## Good to know

- A launch adds your **Amazon disclosure** to the Page bio and puts #ad before deal links, so your community is compliant from day one.
- Reports Amazon refuses are flagged at the moment you add them, so you can swap them out before they go live.
