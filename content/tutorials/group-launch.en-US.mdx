## What Launch Butler does

Launch Butler takes you from nothing to a running deals community. You answer one questionnaire about your niche, and it builds your Facebook Page and deals group, writes the welcome and weekly posts, sets up a posting schedule on its own Deals Butler, adds your Amazon disclosure, links your channels together, and connects a Foyer shop with a newsletter sign-up. Every customer gets one free launch, on any plan.

## Before you start

- Sign in to **Facebook** in the app so Launch Butler can act as you. It opens a sign-in window when it needs one, and it works even when Google sign-in is blocked.
- Connect the **Amazon Creators API** in **Settings > AI & Data Keys** so your launch can find deals.
- Have your niche and a name in mind. You can also point the launch at a Facebook Page or group you already have.

## Run a launch

1. Open **Launch Butler** in the left menu and pick how to start: **Start a new deals business** (a new group and Page), **Use my Facebook group** (keeps the group you already have), or **Complete my existing setup** (for a group one of your Deals Butlers already posts to).
2. Tell it your **niche** and your **community name**. On the Stores step you can pick your own **Amazon tracking ID** for this business. Don't have one? Don't worry about it: your account's tracking ID is used. If you started from your own group, paste its link on the Names step.
3. Pick the **channels** you want linked: Instagram, Telegram, your Amazon storefront list, and comment-to-DM funnels.
4. Choose your **posting schedule** and how often deals go out.
5. Review the plan. Launch Butler shows every channel it will set up, with your own niche on the covers.
6. Start the launch. It works through the steps in Mission Control: Page and group setup, bios and disclosures, the welcome and pinned posts, friend invites, the deals schedule, and the Foyer shop.

## One Launch page per business

**Launch Butler** has its own spot in the left menu. Click it to see every business at a glance: whether it was launched, and its posts, clicks and earnings. Under it, each of your Deals Butlers has its own Launch page (for example **Gymnastics Launch**).

- **Re-run** checks that business again and adds only what is missing, with the answers its last launch used.
- **Start fresh** asks the questions again (your last answers are filled in) and builds what is missing for that Deals Butler.
- **Performance** shows posts, clicks, earnings and orders for the last 7, 30 or 90 days, what's working (top groups, deals and posting times) and what needs a look. Give each business its own tracking ID to see exactly what it earns.
- **Past launches** keeps every run, so you can open its full summary any time.

A launch on a Deals Butler you already have never replaces your settings. Your keywords, schedule, posting spots, tags and automations stay as they are. On your own group, join approval and moderation alerts only change if you tick **Change my group's join and alert settings**.

## Use your own landing page

Already have a landing page? On the Names step, tick **Use my own landing page instead of a Foyer page** and paste its address. The launch then makes no Foyer page:

- Your page is linked from your group, your Page, the pinned START HERE post, your Instagram bio, your Amazon site list and the comment-to-DM messages.
- You get a **newsletter signup form** to paste into your page. Find it on that business's Launch page under **Newsletter signup for your website**, then click **Copy the code**.
- AI reads your page once, finds your products, and writes a year of emails about them for everyone who signs up. You approve the first email before they start.

## After the launch

- Your launch runs on its own **Deals Butler**, with a name that fits, so it never changes a setup you already had.
- **Running-low reminders** tell you when the year's posts are getting thin, with a one-click **Schedule next year**.
- A full **product funnel** and a year of product posts and emails are set up, with a daily newsletter capture on your Foyer page.
- Everything a launch touches is additive. It never edits your existing butlers or settings.

## Good to know

- A launch adds your **Amazon disclosure** to the Page bio and puts #ad before deal links, so your community is compliant from day one.
- **More launches:** your first launch is included. Each launch after that runs on its own Deals Butler add-on ($24.99/month). In the ready check, click **Buy a Deals Butler add-on**, finish checkout, and come back: the launch unlocks by itself as soon as the purchase goes through. Already have a key? Click **I have a key** and paste it.
- Reports Amazon refuses are flagged at the moment you add them, so you can swap them out before they go live.
