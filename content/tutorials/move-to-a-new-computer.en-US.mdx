## What you get

Moving to a new computer takes about ten minutes. You save **one file** on your old computer and open it on the new one. The file is locked with your license key, so only you can open it. Nothing is changed or deleted on your old computer.

**What comes with you:**

- Your settings, templates, keywords, queues and schedules
- Your images and saved lists
- Your saved connections, like your OpenAI key and connected accounts, so you do not have to type them again
- Your progress and tallies

**What you set up again on the new computer** (a checklist walks you through each one):

- **Browser logins** to Amazon, Instagram, Facebook, Walmart and other sites. For your safety these never leave the computer where you made them.
- **Your license seat.** You sign in to the new computer with your license. If your plan has only one seat, you free the old one (see Step 3).
- **Your Chrome extension and your phone.** They pair with one computer at a time, so you pair them again.
- **Videos.** They are large, so they stay where they are. You can copy those folders over yourself if you want them.

**Your schedules start paused.** Your schedules come along, but they stay paused on the new computer until you turn them back on at the end of the checklist. That way nothing runs before you have signed in, and your old and new computers never post from the same accounts at the same time.

## Before you start

- Update Influencer Butler on **both** computers to the latest version. Open the app and it updates itself, or download it again from the website.
- Have your license email handy for signing in on the new computer.

## Step 1: save the file on your old computer

1. Open **Settings** and go to **Data & Backup**.
2. Find **Move to a new computer** and click **Save a move package** under **On your old computer**.
3. Choose where to save it. Pick somewhere outside the Influencer Butler folder, like your **Desktop** or a **USB drive**.
4. Wait for the message that says how many files were saved. It can take a minute.

When it finishes you also see anything that was left behind, like videos or very large files.

**Do this first.** If your plan has one seat, you need to be signed in on this computer to save the file, and you remove this computer from your plan in Step 3.

## Step 2: bring the file to the new computer

Copy the file over any way you like: a USB drive, AirDrop, or a cloud folder you trust. Keep it private. It is locked, but it holds your whole setup.

## Step 3: sign in on the new computer

Install Influencer Butler on the new computer and sign in with your license email. If it says your plan is full, open **Settings, Devices & Seats** on your **old** computer, remove the old computer there, then try signing in again on the new one. You have already saved the file, so you do not need the old computer signed in anymore.

## Step 4: open it on the new computer

1. On the new computer, open **Settings**, then **Data & Backup**, then **Move to a new computer**.
2. Under **On your new computer**, click **Choose a move package** and pick the file.
3. Check the line that says where it came from, then click **Import and restart**.

Influencer Butler checks the whole file first, and nothing changes if anything looks wrong. This can take a few minutes for a big setup, so leave the window open. It then restarts and puts everything in place. Anything it replaces is saved to a backup folder first, so nothing is lost.

## Step 5: finish the checklist

After it restarts you see a **Finish your move** card at the top. It only lists what could not travel, and most steps tick themselves off when they are done:

- **Sign in to your sites.** Click **Sign in** next to each site and sign in like you normally do.
- **Install a regular browser.** If you do not have Chrome, Brave or Edge, install one. Amazon, Instagram, Facebook and Walmart sign-ins need it.
- **Pick your folders again.** If a butler used a folder or file that is not on the new computer, it is listed here. Open that butler and choose it again.
- **Check your connections.** Open API Integrations and press **Test Connection** on each service you use.
- **Pair your extension and phone again.** Do this from Settings.
- **Bring your videos** if you want them, by copying the folders yourself.
- **Close Influencer Butler on your old computer.** Once the new one is working, quit the app on the old one so the two never run the same schedules.
- **Turn your schedules back on.** Click **Turn schedules back on** when everything above is done.
- **Remove your old computer** under Settings, Devices & Seats when you are finished with it, so it does not use up a seat.

When you are done, click **Hide this checklist**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This file was made with a different license key."** You signed in to the new computer with a different license than the old one. Sign out and sign in with the same license.
- **"This file is incomplete" or "damaged."** The copy did not finish. Copy the file again, or save a new one on the old computer.
- **"Sign in with your license first."** Sign in on the new computer, then try again.
- **"There is not enough free space."** Free up some space on that computer and try again. Importing needs about two to three times the size of the file.
- **A few files could not be copied.** The checklist tells you how many. Everything else came across, and you can use the app normally.
- **The app does not reopen after "Import and restart."** Open Influencer Butler again. It finishes the move when it starts.
- **Still stuck?** Click the chat bubble in the app, choose **Report a bug**, and tell us which step you were on.

Your old computer keeps working the same until you decide to stop using it.
