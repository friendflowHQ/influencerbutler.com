## What you get

Moving to a new computer (a new laptop, Mac or Windows PC) takes about ten minutes. You save **one file** on your old computer and open it on the new one. The file is locked with your license key, so only you can open it. Nothing is changed or deleted on your old computer.

**What comes with you:**

- Your settings, templates, keywords, queues and schedules
- Your images and saved lists
- Your saved connections, like your OpenAI key and connected accounts, so you do not have to type them again
- Your progress and tallies

**What you set up again on the new computer** (a checklist walks you through each one):

- **Browser logins** to Amazon, Instagram, Facebook, Walmart and other sites. For your safety these never leave the computer where you made them.
- **Your license seat.** You sign in to the new computer with your license. If your plan has only one seat, you free the old one (see Step 3).
- **Your Chrome extension and your phone.** They pair with one computer at a time, so you pair them again.
- **Videos.** They are large, so they stay where they are. You can copy those folders over yourself if you want them.

**Your schedules start paused.** Your schedules come along, but they stay paused on the new computer until you turn them back on at the end of the checklist. That way nothing runs before you have signed in, and your old and new computers never post from the same accounts at the same time.

Inside the app, **Settings, then Data & Backup, then Move to a new computer** has a **Guide me through it** button that walks you through the same steps, and **Show me where** points at each button.

## Before you start

- Update Influencer Butler on **both** computers to the latest version. Open the app and it updates itself, or download it again (see Step 3).
- Have your license email handy for signing in on the new computer.
- You do not need to rush. Influencer Butler keeps working on your old computer until you stop using it. On a Mac it needs **macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer**.

## Step 1: save the file on your old computer

1. Open **Settings** and go to **Data & Backup**.
2. Find **Move to a new computer** and click **Save a move package** under **On your old computer**.
3. Choose where to save it. Pick somewhere outside the Influencer Butler folder.
4. Wait for the message that says how many files were saved. It can take a minute.

**On Mac:** save it to your **Desktop** or straight onto a **USB drive**. The file is named like `InfluencerButler-move-2026-10-13.ibmove`.

**On Windows:** save it to your **Desktop** or straight onto a **USB drive** (it shows up in File Explorer as a drive letter). The file is named like `InfluencerButler-move-2026-10-13.ibmove`.

When it finishes you also see anything that was left behind, like videos or very large files.

**Do this first.** If your plan has one seat, you need to be signed in on this computer to save the file, and you remove this computer from your plan in Step 3.

## Step 2: bring the file to the new computer

Keep the file private. It is locked, but it holds your whole setup.

**On Mac:**

- **USB drive:** copy the file onto the drive in Finder. When you are done, click the eject button next to the drive in the Finder sidebar before you unplug it.
- **AirDrop (Mac to Mac only):** in Finder, click **AirDrop** in the sidebar, then drag the file onto the other Mac. You can also right-click the file, choose **Share**, then **AirDrop**.
- **A cloud folder you trust** (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox) works on both Mac and Windows. If you are moving from a Mac to a Windows PC, use a USB drive or a cloud folder, because AirDrop does not work with Windows.

**On Windows:**

- **USB drive:** copy the file onto the drive in File Explorer. When you are done, click the **Safely Remove Hardware** icon near the clock (it may be under the small up arrow) and choose the drive before you unplug it.
- **A cloud folder you trust** (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox) works on both Windows and Mac.

## Step 3: sign in on the new computer

First install Influencer Butler on the new computer.

**On Mac:**

1. Go to `influencerbutler.com/dl/mac` for a Mac with an Apple chip (M1, M2, M3 or newer) or `influencerbutler.com/dl/mac-intel` for an older Intel Mac. Not sure which you have? Click the **Apple menu**, then **About This Mac**. If it says **Chip: Apple M...** choose the Apple chip link. If it says **Processor: Intel** choose the Intel link.
2. Open the downloaded file, then drag **Influencer Butler** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from **Applications**. If macOS asks whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet, click **Open**. (If you open it from the download window instead, the app offers to move itself to Applications. Say yes.)
4. You need **macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer**.

**On Windows:**

1. Go to `influencerbutler.com/dl/win` to download the installer.
2. Run **Influencer Butler Setup** and follow the prompts. If Windows asks whether to allow the app to make changes, click **Yes**.

Then, on either computer, sign in with your license email. If it says your plan is full, open **Settings, Devices & Seats** on your **old** computer, remove the old computer there, then try signing in again on the new one. You have already saved the file, so you do not need the old computer signed in anymore.

## Step 4: open it on the new computer

1. On the new computer, open **Settings**, then **Data & Backup**, then **Move to a new computer**.
2. Under **On your new computer**, click **Choose a move package** and pick the file.
3. Check the line that says where it came from, then click **Import and restart**.

Influencer Butler checks the whole file first, and nothing changes if anything looks wrong. This can take a few minutes for a big setup, so leave the window open. It then restarts and puts everything in place. Anything it replaces is saved to a backup folder first, so nothing is lost.

## Step 5: finish the checklist

After it restarts you see a **Finish your move** card at the top. It only lists what could not travel, and most steps tick themselves off when they are done:

- **Sign in to your sites.** Click **Sign in** next to each site and sign in like you normally do.
- **Install a regular browser.** If you do not have Chrome, Brave or Edge, install one. Amazon, Instagram, Facebook and Walmart sign-ins need it.
- **Pick your folders again.** If a butler used a folder or file that is not on the new computer, it is listed here. Open that butler and choose it again. If you moved between a Mac and a Windows PC, every folder location needs to be picked again.
- **Check your connections.** Open API Integrations and press **Test Connection** on each service you use.
- **Pair your extension and phone again.** Do this from Settings.
- **Bring your videos** if you want them, by copying the folders yourself.
- **Close Influencer Butler on your old computer.** Once the new one is working, quit the app on the old one so the two never run the same schedules. See the next section, because closing the window is not the same as quitting.
- **Turn your schedules back on.** Click **Turn schedules back on** when everything above is done.
- **Remove your old computer** under Settings, Devices & Seats when you are finished with it, so it does not use up a seat.

When you are done, click **Hide this checklist**.

## How to really quit Influencer Butler on the old computer

Clicking the close button on the window only **hides** Influencer Butler so your schedules keep running. To stop it:

**On Mac:** click **Influencer Butler** in the menu bar at the top of the screen, then **Quit Influencer Butler** (or press **Command+Q**). You can also click the Influencer Butler icon in the menu bar and choose **Quit**. If a run is in progress it asks you to confirm.

**On Windows:** find the Influencer Butler icon in the notification area next to the clock (click the small up arrow if you do not see it), right-click it, and choose **Quit**. If a run is in progress it asks you to confirm.

## If your new Mac already copied everything over

When you set up a new Mac, macOS often offers to copy everything from your old one (Apple's **Migration Assistant**, or a Time Machine backup). That brings Influencer Butler and its data along, so you may not need the move file at all. Here is what to expect:

- **Your settings, templates and queues are there.** Browser logins usually do not carry over to a new computer, so you sign in to each site again.
- **Your schedules are paused.** Influencer Butler notices that its data came from another computer, pauses your schedules, and shows a **Finish setting up this computer** checklist at the top. It is the same checklist as in Step 5.
- **Check which version you copied.** If your old Mac had an Intel chip and your new Mac has an Apple chip (M1, M2, M3 or newer), the copy is the Intel version. It works, but the Apple chip version is faster. The checklist has a download button, or go to `influencerbutler.com/dl/mac`. Open the new one from **Applications**, then delete the old copy.
- **One seat at a time.** If your plan has one seat and the new Mac says your plan is full, open **Settings, then Devices & Seats** on your **old** computer, remove it there, then try again on the new one.
- **Quit the app on your old computer** (see the section below), so the two never run the same schedules.
- **Turn your schedules back on** from the checklist when everything above is done.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This file was made with a different license key."** You signed in to the new computer with a different license than the old one. Sign out and sign in with the same license.
- **"This file is incomplete" or "damaged."** The copy did not finish. Copy the file again, or save a new one on the old computer.
- **"Sign in with your license first."** Sign in on the new computer, then try again.
- **"There is not enough free space."** Free up some space on that computer and try again. Importing needs about two to three times the size of the file.
- **A few files could not be copied.** The checklist tells you how many. Everything else came across, and you can use the app normally.
- **The app does not reopen after "Import and restart."** Open Influencer Butler again (from Applications on a Mac, or the Start menu on Windows). It finishes the move when it starts.
- **Still stuck?** Click the chat bubble in the app, choose **Report a bug**, and tell us which step you were on.

Your old computer keeps working the same until you decide to stop using it.
