## What Orders Butler does

Orders Butler is the upstream data feed for almost every Amazon-side workspace. It logs into Amazon in your existing Chrome session, walks your order history page by page, and stores the result in a local database. Downstream:

- **Amazon Butler** uses it to find brands you sell.
- **Daily Commission Butler** uses it to match accepted campaigns to actual sales.
- **Earnings Intelligence** uses it to compute lifetime tallies.

If Orders Butler is stale, every Amazon workspace is making decisions on stale data.

## First sync

1. Open **Orders Butler** in the left nav.
2. In **Run settings**, choose a scope. For a fresh install pick **All years (full catch-up)** so Orders Butler walks your whole history once. This first sync is the slowest because nothing is cached yet.
3. Click **Sync now**.

The runner opens a Chrome window, logs in (if needed), and walks the orders pages. A full first catch-up can take a while on an account with years of history; a single recent year finishes much faster.

## Ongoing syncs

After the first catch-up, leave the scope on **Only new since last run** (the default). Orders Butler walks newest-first and stops as soon as it reaches orders you already have, so a routine sync finishes in seconds instead of re-walking your whole history. Set it to **run daily** in the Schedule card and it adds new orders incrementally. Reach for **All years (full catch-up)** only when you want a full reconciliation.

## Print a checklist PDF

Click **Print checklist (PDF)** in the results toolbar to turn the table into a printable, ADHD-friendly filming checklist with Influencer Butler branding: big checkboxes, one clear action per product (take a photo, film a vertical video, film a horizontal video), commission info, and a "top 3 by payoff" callout so you know what to film first.

- **Scope**: if any rows are checkbox-selected, only those print; otherwise the current filtered view prints.
- **Style**: pick **Rich cards** (photo, checkboxes, notes line, a few per page) or **Compact list** (one line per product). Your last choice is remembered.
- **Voiceover scripts**: tick **Include voiceover scripts** to print each product's script from Voiceover Butler under its card. If some products don't have a script yet, the app sends them to Voiceover Butler, walks you through generating and starring scripts, and a floating **Done, print my checklist** button brings you back and creates the PDF. Starred scripts win; your text edits are what print.

## Where the data lives

Pulled rows live in `{userData}/InfluencerButler/workspaces/preseed/data/orders/*.json`. You never need to touch these files directly, but they're inspectable if something looks wrong.

## Gotchas

- **"Login required" banner stays up**: Amazon asked for re-auth. Open the Chrome window the runner spawned, sign in, and click **Resume**.
- **Sync looks frozen on "Page 47"**: Amazon throttled the request. Orders Butler retries automatically with backoff; give it 1–2 minutes before clicking Stop.
- **Missing recent orders**: the most recent 24h sometimes don't appear in Amazon's UI until they ship. That's an Amazon-side delay, not a Butler bug.
