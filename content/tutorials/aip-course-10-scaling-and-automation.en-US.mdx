## When this module applies

Come back to this one when the Module 9 loop is boring: you film weekly without drama, your library is growing past 50 to 100 videos, and earnings are small but real. Scaling before the basics work just multiplies zero.

- [ ] The weekly loop runs without willpower; I am ready to add a layer {#loop-is-boring}

## Lever 1: Creator Connections (brands pay you extra)

Inside your influencer dashboard, Amazon runs **Creator Connections**: brands offer creators bonus commission rates on their products for a campaign period. Stacked on your normal rate, the difference is real money for the same work.

The catch is volume: campaigns are worth joining when they match products you already cover, and finding, evaluating, and messaging brands is repetitive. Start manually: join campaigns for brands whose products you already reviewed, and learn what a good offer looks like.

- [ ] Open Creator Connections in my dashboard and join one campaign that matches my content {#join-first-campaign}

## Lever 2: deal content and deal audiences

Shoppers love discounts, and deal-focused audiences (Facebook deal groups, Telegram channels, deal pages) are some of the highest-converting affiliate traffic that exists. Creators who build or post into deal communities with their affiliate links add a second income stream that does not require filming at all.

Manual version: when a product you have reviewed goes on a serious sale, share it with your link and say why it is worth it at that price. Genuine "I own this, it is finally cheap" posts convert absurdly well.

## Lever 3: automate the robot work, keep the human work

By month three you will notice your week splits into two kinds of tasks:

- **Human work that grows the business**: choosing products, filming honest reviews, judging what your audience wants. Never outsource the trust part.
- **Robot work that just eats hours**: checking which campaigns are worth joining, messaging brands one by one, hunting daily deals, formatting and posting them, re-checking links, pulling earnings numbers into a spreadsheet.

## The one metric for this phase

**Videos published per week, sustained.** Every scaling lever amplifies the library; none of them replace it. If a new tool or tactic reduces your filming output, it is costing you money no matter what it earns.

## Do this now

Pick ONE lever and add only that this month. Stacking all three at once is how the whole system collapses back to zero.
