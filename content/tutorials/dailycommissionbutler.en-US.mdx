## What it does

Daily Commission Butler reads your daily sales, finds the Creator Connections campaigns that match those products, and accepts the campaigns automatically. It also checks your storefront's top-selling products and accepts campaigns for those, so a best-seller with an open campaign is never left unclaimed even when it wasn't in yesterday's sales. It runs once a day by default - set it and forget it.

## Prerequisites

- **cc-check** has run at least once today so the campaign catalog is fresh.
- **Orders Butler** synced at least once in the last 48 hours.
- **CC Storefront ID** set in the topbar.

If anything's missing, the workspace shows a banner telling you which step is incomplete.

## First run

1. Open **Daily Commission Butler** in the left nav.
2. In **Acceptance rules**, leave the defaults on for the first run:
   - **Minimum commission rate**: 5%
   - **Minimum recent sales**: 1 unit in the last 30 days
   - **Skip campaigns ending in less than**: 48h
3. Click **Run once** to do a single dry pass - it shows you what *would* be accepted without committing.
4. Review the candidate list. If it looks right, click **Accept all**. If you want it to run daily on its own, switch to the **Schedule** card and set a daily cron.

## Tuning over time

After a week or two, look at the **Earnings Intelligence** workspace - it shows which auto-accepted campaigns actually generated commissions and which didn't. Raise the minimum commission rate or sales threshold to filter out duds.

## Affiliate+ and Earn on Clicks on the same product

Creator Connections has two campaign types: **Affiliate+** (Amazon labels it Bonus Commissions, a percentage on top of your normal commission) and **Earn on Clicks** (shown as Sponsored Products for Creators, which pays per qualifying click). If the same product has both, Amazon pays you from **Bonus Commissions only**. The two do not stack, and accepting Affiliate+ takes precedence over the click earnings for that product. This is confirmed in Amazon's Creator Connections Getting Started Guide (page 21).

So if you expect a product's click earnings to beat its commission, don't let the Butler accept that product's Affiliate+ campaign. Use **Run once** to preview the candidates, skip that one, and accept it yourself on Amazon after you decide. In **Daily Deals**, the same choice is the **Auto-accept CC campaigns before posting** toggle under **Auto-accept campaigns**. Your content links are still submitted either way.

## Gotchas

- **No campaigns to accept** usually means cc-check is stale - run cc-check and try again.
- **All campaigns rejected** typically points at thresholds being too aggressive. Drop the minimum commission rate to 3% for a run and see what shows up.
