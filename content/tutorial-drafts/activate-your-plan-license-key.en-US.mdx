## How your purchase reaches the app

When you buy a plan, you get an email with your **license key**. The app needs that key (or the email address the plan is on) before it knows what you bought. Until then it keeps showing whatever it had before, such as your free trial.

There are two ways to connect it. Use whichever is easier.

## Option 1: sign in with your email

1. Open Influencer Butler. If the sign-in window is showing, type the **same email you used at checkout**.
2. Check that inbox for the sign-in link and click it. The app opens and connects your plan.
3. You should see a message that your plan is active.

## Option 2: paste your license key

If you are already signed in (for example, on a free trial) and then buy a plan, paste the new key in the app:

1. Go to **Settings > Subscription**.
2. Find **Change or apply a license key** and paste the key from your purchase email.
3. Click **Test**. It shows the plan and the number of seats that key unlocks.
4. If that matches what you bought, click **Apply**.

To sign in with a completely different email or key, use **Sign out / Switch account** in Settings, then sign in again.

## What the plan names mean

Your plan has two parts.

**Plan level** decides which butlers you see:

- **Beginner**: the starter butlers, with AI included (150 AI actions a month on our key).
- **Power User**: every butler, with AI on your own OpenAI key.

**Seat tier** decides how many computers can use the plan at the same time:

- **Solo**: 1 computer
- **Trio**: 3 computers
- **Team**: 10 computers
- **Agency**: 25 computers

So "Power User Solo" means every butler, on one computer.

## Check which plan the app sees

Go to **Settings > Subscription**. It shows your status, your **Plan level**, and your license key. Click **Refresh** if you just bought or changed something.

## The app still says I am not on my new plan

Work through these in order:

1. **Did you apply the new key?** A new purchase can come with a new key. Paste it under **Change or apply a license key**, then **Test** and **Apply**.
2. **Click Refresh** in **Settings > Subscription**.
3. **Are you in Beginner Mode or a Beginner preview?** Settings has a **Switch to Beginner Mode** option and a **Preview Beginner plan** option. Both hide butlers without changing your plan. If either is on, click **Show everything again** or **Switch back to my plan**.
4. **Did you sign in with a different email?** If the plan is on another email address, use **Sign out / Switch account** and sign in with the email from your receipt.
5. **Still stuck?** Quit the app fully and open it again. If it still shows the wrong plan, send us a note from the chat bubble with what **Settings > Subscription** shows.
